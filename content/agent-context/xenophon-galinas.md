**Stats**: 26 findings, 10 connections, 8 entities

> Galinas is an example of a peripheral figure drawn into the Epstein record through a routine commercial transaction rather than any network participation. His significance lies in what the failed lease negotiation reveals about Epstein's operational methods in the mid-1990s: using government-custodied property as a profit vehicle, exercising contractual rights to block competing lease agreements, and misrepresenting State Department authorization to subsequent tenants. The Galinas episode also establishes that Epstein's 34 East 69th Street property was known in elite NYC real estate circles connected to the Onassis Foundation and Greek shipping networks.
