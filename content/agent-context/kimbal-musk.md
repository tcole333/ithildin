# Kimbal Musk
**Stats**: 9 findings, 4 connections, 0 entities
**Dossier**: /dossiers/kimbal-musk

> Kimbal Musk functions as a board-level governance presence within the Musk corporate family (Tesla, SpaceX) and as an independent operator in food-system philanthropy and commercial drone entertainment. Within the Epstein network documentation, he is recorded as a secondary contact whose primary significance is as a social conduit to his brother Elon Musk, with Boris Nikolic acting as the operational intermediary.

Nova Sky LLC registered TX Nov 2024 with Eli Mohs as Managing Member at 5900 Balcones Dr Austin TX. Abu Dhabi multi-year partnership for 10,000-drone shows, Jeffrey Katzenberg partnership, drone shows in 40 countries. (Finding #4484)
