> xAI is Elon Musk's artificial intelligence operating company — the entity that develops the Grok model, operates the Colossus supercomputer facility, holds the Pentagon's classified AI contract, and served as the AI business being absorbed into SpaceX through the February 2026 all-stock merger. It is the node that connects the DOGE-era federal data access episode, the government contracting pathway, the Musk private entity infrastructure managed by Jared Birchall, and the regulatory landscape preceding the merger.
