**Stats**: 46 findings, 34 connections, 0 entities

> Barrack illustrates how a culturally bilingual private-equity operator can sit at the junction of Gulf sovereign capital and U.S. political access. During the 2016&ndash;2018 window covered by his indictment, Colony NorthStar took in roughly a quarter of its $7 billion from UAE and Saudi sources, Barrack introduced Paul Manafort to the Trump campaign and chaired the $107 million inaugural committee, and Epstein routed political intelligence about Barrack to Gulf contacts and Western journalists. The 2021 EDNY indictment charged him as an unregistered foreign agent of the UAE; the jury acquitted him on all counts in November 2022 [Finding #2803].
