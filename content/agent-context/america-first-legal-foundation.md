# America First Legal Foundation
**Stats**: 21 findings, 20 connections, 0 entities
**Dossier**: /dossiers/america-first-legal-foundation

> America First Legal Foundation is a 501(c)(3) legal advocacy nonprofit founded in 2021 by Stephen Miller, operating primarily in immigration enforcement litigation, voter registration challenges, and anti-DEI corporate campaigns. Three structural features define its role in the broader conservative policy network. First, personnel overlap between AFL and the second Trump administration is unusually dense: five of eight known board and leadership members entered federal government positions by mid-2025, including Miller as Deputy Chief of Staff, Gene Hamilton as Deputy White House Counsel, Reed Rubinstein as State Department Legal Adviser, Matthew Whitaker as NATO Ambassador, and Russ Vought as OMB Director. Second, AFL's funding flows primarily through donor-advised fund intermediaries (DonorsTrust, Bradley Impact Fund) whose structures shield ultimate donor identities, producing revenue that fluctuates sharply with electoral cycles. Third, AFL's litigation and administrative petitions frequently parallel executive orders issued by the administration, with AFL continuing to pursue the same policy goals through independent legal channels. The Conservative Partnership Institute incubated AFL and shares board members, connecting it to a network of affiliated nonprofits including the Center for Renewing America and the American Accountability Foundation. Blake Masters's board seat links the organization to Silicon Valley capital via Peter Thiel, while Elon Musk's $50 million contribution to the AFL-controlled Citizens for Sanity in 2022 represents another technology-sector funding connection.

## Open Questions
- Who are the ultimate donors behind the Bradley Impact Fund's $27.1 million 2022 grant and DonorsTrust's $21.3 million 2024 transfer to AFL? Both intermediaries are donor-advised funds that legally shield contributor identities. DonorsTrust's 990 filings show eight unnamed donors provided $162.4 million of $284.1 million in 2024 grants, but no state or federal disclosure requirement currently applies.
- What communications, if any, exist between AFL leadership and White House personnel regarding the timing and content of EAC rulemaking petitions and executive orders on voter registration? The sequence—AFL NVRA lawsuits in September 2024, Trump EO 14248 in March 2025, AFL EAC petition in July 2025—is temporally suggestive but no direct evidence of coordination has surfaced. FOIA requests targeting White House counsel and EAC correspondence could clarify.
- Did AFL sign the DOGE-SSA 'voter data agreement' identified by congressional investigators, and if so, what Social Security Administration data was shared? Representatives Larson, Garcia, and Morelle sent records demands to fifteen organizations but no responses have been publicly disclosed as of March 2026.
- What is FlexPoint Media's (also operating as PG Placements) ownership structure and relationship to AFL leadership? The concentration of roughly $80 million in combined AFL and Citizens for Sanity advertising spending with a single vendor in 2022 is documented in 990 contractor disclosures but the vendor's corporate structure and any personnel overlap with AFL remain unexamined.
