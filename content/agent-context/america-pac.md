# America PAC
**Stats**: 11 findings, 16 connections, 0 entities
**Dossier**: /dossiers/america-pac

> America PAC is a federally registered Super PAC (Independent Expenditure-Only Committee) that served as <a href="/dossiers/elon-musk">Elon Musk</a>'s primary political spending vehicle in the 2024 election cycle, disbursing over $261 million almost entirely funded by Musk's personal contributions and routed through a network of Musk-controlled Texas LLCs.

## Key Findings
- **[financial/confirmed]** America PAC (C00879510) is a Super PAC (Independent Expenditure-Only) headquartered in TX. Treasurer: Chris Young. Founded 2024-05-22. 2024 cycle: raised .5M, disbursed .3M, made .7M in independent expenditures (66.5% of spending). 2025/2026 cycle (through Dec 2025): raised .2M, disbursed .2M, operating expenditures .4M, only K in IEs. Cash on hand at end of 2025: K. Key donors: Elon Musk (.8M), United States of America Inc (.9M). Key disbursement recipients: United States of America Inc (.3M in-kind), three M spokesperson consultants (Diestler, Jacobs, Ainsworth). IEs supported Trump (.3M) and opposed Harris (K) in 2024. (2024-05-22) (Finding #5601)
- **[financial/high]** Four Musk personal LLCs funneled ~80M in services to America PAC for Trump 2024 campaign: Europa 100 LLC (oldest Musk entity, also paid PAC treasurer Chris Young up to 1M salary), United States of America Inc (voter outreach with 47-dollar checks for petition signing), Group America LLC (campaign expenses), Excession LLC (family office, retirement plan contributions). The 47-dollar payment scheme operated as petition signing for First/Second Amendment support. Europa 100 previously used to pay nannies, repurposed for massive political spending. This structure routes campaign spending through personal LLCs rather than direct donations. (Finding #4384)
- **[legal/high]** America PAC voter payment scheme and legal challenges: PAC offered 47-dollar then 100-dollar payments to registered voters in swing states who signed petition supporting free speech and gun rights. Also ran 1M lottery-style giveaways. Philadelphia DA Larry Krasner sued alleging illegal unregulated lottery under PA law. PA judge declined to enjoin, allowing final giveaway to proceed. PAC defense: winners are spokespeople who 'earn' money, not lottery. Forum shopping: America PAC sought to move Krasner suit to federal court. Total ~80M funneled through Europa 100 LLC, United States of America Inc, Group America LLC, and Excession LLC. Chris Young (PAC treasurer) earned up to 1M from Europa 100. (Finding #4404)
- **[financial/confirmed]** America PAC (C00879510) operational scale: 2024 cycle receipts 263.5M, disbursements 261.3M, independent expenditures 173.7M. 99.25% individual contributions (virtually all Musk). Only 20 dollars in unitemized contributions — single-donor vehicle. 2026 cycle already 48.2M receipts, 50.2M disbursements, 37.4M operating expenses but only 87K in IE — building midterm infrastructure. Georgia State Election Board reprimanded PAC for sending pre-filled absentee ballot applications violating GA election law. PAC formed May 2024, treasurer Chris Young (Texas-based GOP campaign finance lawyer Chris Gober formed it). Musk total 2024 election spending: 290M+ per CNN. (Finding #4421)
- **[financial/confirmed]** America PAC vendor network: 2024 cycle disbursements dominated by USA Inc (11.2M in-kind), Blitz Canvassing (54M+ canvassing), Red Elephant Strategy (35K), IMGE LLC (21K consulting). 2025-2026 cycle: USA Inc (27.3M in-kind), Lex Politica PLLC (217K legal), IMGE LLC (92K digital), Rightside Compliance (30K), ALX (20K social media), CSC LLC (5K strategy), Excession LLC (3.3K in-kind). Key canvassing vendors: Blitz Canvassing LLC, Patriot Grassroots LLC, Echo Canyon Consulting, Synapse Group. (Finding #4430)
- **[relationship/confirmed]** America PAC shell entity hierarchy confirmed via TX Comptroller: United States of America Inc (TID 32097038932, SOS 0805733050, formed 10/2/2024) — Musk as President, Birchall as Secretary, address 2025 Guadalupe St Suite 260 Austin TX. USA Inc is sole member of Group America LLC (TID 32097028388, formed 10/2/2024). Europa 100 LLC (TID 32075024482, formed 7/14/2020) and Excession LLC (TID 32075024201, formed 7/14/2020) are older Birchall-managed entities at PO Box 341886 Austin TX. All use Registered Agent Solutions Inc. Europa 100 pays Chris Young; Excession provides his retirement plan. (Finding #4431)
- **[legal/high]** America PAC legal challenges: (1) PA Commonwealth Court: Phila DA Larry Krasner sued over 1M/day sweepstakes as illegal lottery. Judge Angelo Foglietta allowed it to continue Nov 2024 after Young testified winners were pre-selected, not random. (2) GA State Election Board: formal reprimand for sending pre-filled absentee ballot applications violating O.C.G.A. 21-2-381. Counties: Chattooga, Cherokee, Coweta, Floyd, Whitfield. (3) NC/MI investigations into voter registration fraud. (4) Federal cases: Doe v. America PAC (E.D. Pa. 2:25-cv-01691), Henderson v. America PAC (E.D.N.C. 4:25-cv-00012), Crawford v. America PAC (D. Ariz. 2:25-cv-00338). (Finding #4433)
- **[financial/high]** America PAC (C00879510) raised $263.5M and spent $261.3M in 2024 cycle. Made $78.8M in in-kind contributions to United States of America Inc (Musk entity, TID 32097038932). Treasurer: Chris Gober. Director/Treasurer also Chris Young. PAC replaced September Group canvassing vendor (paid $1.4M) with GP3 subsidiary Blitz Canvassing in Sept 2024. Also paid Synapse Group LLC $6M+ for canvassing and $1M each to 10+ spokesperson consultants. (Finding #4467)
- **[financial/confirmed]** America PAC receipts show Excession LLC (,324 in-kind contribution, Jun 2025), Group America LLC (,752, 4 contributions, Dec 2024), Europa 100 LLC (,811, 2 contributions, Dec 2024), 333 Main LLC (,840, Apr 2025), and Elon Musk Revocable Trust (,425, Apr 2025) as donors. Excession LLC is Musk's personal holding company; its appearance confirms direct financial linkage. America PAC disbursed ,324 back to Excession LLC and ,425 to Elon Musk Revocable Trust as in-kind contributions. These in-kind flows suggest the PAC used Musk-controlled entities for operational expenses that were then classified as in-kind donations. (Finding #5607)

## Open Questions
