# Delta Galil Industries
**Stats**: 29 findings, 8 connections, 0 entities
**Dossier**: /dossiers/delta-galil-industries

> Delta Galil Industries represents the commercial supply-chain layer of the Wexner network -- a major Israeli manufacturer deeply embedded in Victoria's Secret's intimate apparel production, co-located with Limited Brands in Karmiel, and led by a founder connected to Ehud Barak and the Israeli political establishment. The company is entirely absent from the Epstein document corpus, which suggests the relationship was managed through corporate procurement infrastructure rather than through Epstein's personal financial operations.

Isaac Dabah served as Director and Vice Chairman. Lautman was 71 years old, served as Chairman. Delta Galil was listed on TASE and had ADRs on Nasdaq Global Market (CIK 0001081022) before delisting from US exchanges in 2008. (2008) (Finding #2422)

Revenue from VS was growing: 'The increase in 2006 sales in North American is mainly due to the increase in sales of ladies fashionable intimate apparel products to Victoria's Secret.' Total 2006 North American sales were M (59% of .7M total), with VS among the five customers representing ~79% of that figure. (2006) (Finding #2404)
