# Javelin Advisors LLC
**Stats**: 23 findings, 1 connections, 0 entities
**Dossier**: /dossiers/javelin-advisors-llc

> Javelin Advisors LLC is a lobbying and advisory firm founded by former White House and Trump Organization officials who registered simultaneously under FARA (for Pakistan) and LDA (for six domestic and foreign-linked clients), generating approximately $3.4 million in its first year across foreign government representation, critical minerals diplomacy, executive clemency advocacy, and regulatory lobbying -- with a contracting structure that routes foreign principal payments through a co-owner's law firm.

Seiden (Managing Director). Primary address 433 Plaza Real Suite 275 Boca Raton FL 33432 at registration; changed to 1701 Pennsylvania Ave NW Suite 200 DC 20006 by Nov 2025.
