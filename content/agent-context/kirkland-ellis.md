# Kirkland & Ellis
**Aliases**: Kirkland Ellis, Kirkland and Ellis
**Stats**: 88 findings, 79 connections, 0 entities
**Dossier**: /dossiers/kirkland-ellis

> A U.S. law firm whose alumni have held senior Department of Justice and White House positions across multiple administrations, with a corporate criminal defense practice that handled several of the largest DPA and plea resolutions of the 2010s and 2020s and confirmed outside-counsel relationships with Apollo Global Management and Cerberus Capital Management; represented Jeffrey Epstein in the 2008 federal matter.

## Key Findings
- **[legal/confirmed]** K&E served as counsel to Smart & Final Stores Inc (the target company) in the SC 14D9 tender offer response during Apollo's acquisition (May 2019, $6.50/share). K&E attorneys: Michael A. Woronoff (LA), Philippa M. Bond (LA), Joshua M. Zachariah (SF), Joseph Halloum (SF). In this transaction K&E represented the target, not Apollo (the acquirer). This shows K&E operating on both sides of Apollo deals -- as Apollo's own counsel in some, and as target company counsel in others. (2019-05) (Finding #3169)
- **[legal/confirmed]** Epstein emailed Kathy Ruemmler on Feb 24, 2019 (5 months before death) questioning why K&E was not defending the NPA they negotiated: 'why isnt kirkland and ellis defending this npa in front of marra, it is their agreement that i followed? pled, registered and paid money. do they have an obligation to defend it?'
