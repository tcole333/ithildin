## My Recommendation

**Hero:**
> **Ithildin**
> A systems investigation of elite networks through primary evidence.
> 
> *Continuously mapped by autonomous agents. 800K+ entities. 204K+ documents. All cited.*

**Nav subtitle:** "Archive" or remove it entirely

**Cards:** Use the revised versions above — shorter, more direct, less prose-y

**Why:** 
- States what it actually is (systems investigation)
- Acknowledges the agent infrastructure without making it the headline
- Gives the scale numbers (substantiates the claim)
- "All cited" hits the forensic rigor note without being flowery
- Removes the "quiet index" type language that feels like LARPing
