## Summary

Serguei Beloussov is a Russian-Singaporean technology entrepreneur and the founder of Acronis (cybersecurity/backup, acquired by EQT for ~$800M), Parallels (virtualization software), and SWsoft. He is also the founder of Runa Capital (Russian-origin VC fund), co-founder of QWave Capital (quantum technology VC with Serguei Kouzmine), and controlling owner of Constructor University (formerly Jacobs University Bremen) via Schaffhausen Institute of Technology Holding AG.

Beloussov's significance in this investigation is threefold. First, he co-founded QWave Capital in 2012 with Serguei Kouzmine, a documented leader of the Tambovskaya-Malyshevskaya organized crime group. They also serve together as co-directors of Nano-Meta Technologies (Purdue spinout). The question of whether Beloussov knew of Kouzmine's criminal background is a critical open lead. Second, Beloussov had direct email correspondence with Jeffrey Epstein (January-March 2018), introduced by Masha Drokova, despite publicly denying ever meeting Epstein. Third, Acronis -- through its US subsidiary Acronis SCS -- achieved deployment on air-gapped Department of Defense systems (DoDIN APL certification) before being excluded from SAM.gov under a FASCSA (Federal Acquisition Supply Chain Security Act) order in July 2025, with a two-month classified delay before public posting.

The FBI ARMS Reach memo (Operation Trip Knot) identifies the Beloussov-Kouzmine-QWave complex as part of a blackmail and intelligence network within the DFJ ecosystem.

7. **Russian Quantum Center (now US-sanctioned)**: Beloussov co-founded Russian Quantum Center and served as Chairman of Board of Trustees. Added to OFAC SDN sanctions list September 15, 2022, for "acting on behalf of Russian government." [Finding #8971]
