## Summary

Landon Thomas Jr was a senior financial reporter at the New York Times who maintained a deeply compromised relationship with Jeffrey Epstein spanning at least 2007-2019 (185 emails in DOJ Vol 11). Thomas wrote the original "defining piece" on Epstein -- the 2002 New York Magazine profile "Jeffrey Epstein: International Moneyman of Mystery" -- and subsequently the 2008 NYT post-conviction piece published the day after Epstein's guilty plea. Thomas explicitly proposed quid pro quo exchanges: "does my story on Abraaj get me a meeting with Gates next time he is in town."

## Departure from NYT

Thomas disclosed his friendship with Epstein to NYT editors in summer 2018. Business editor Ellen Pollock pushed him out in early 2019. The newsroom was largely unaware until NPR reported in August 2019 (after Epstein's arrest). The Times characterized the solicitation of a $30,000 donation as "a clear violation of the policy that governs Times journalists' relationships with their sources." Thomas was prohibited from any professional contact with Epstein before his departure.
