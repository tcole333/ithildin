## The META OCTAV / International Fiduciaries Connection

**International Fiduciaries Limited (IFL)** was established in 2011 by **Carolyn Butler** (MD/Founder). It held HK TCSP licence **TC007309** and was a member of the HK Trustees' Association. Butler had previously been CEO of Hong Kong Trust Company Group (HKTC), which was co-founded by Kenneth Raymond Deayton.

## The Critical Conflict of Interest

**Kenneth Raymond Deayton sits on both sides of the Swiss Commodity Re formation:**

1. **Director of META OCTAV GROUP (HK) LIMITED** (formerly IFL, TCSP TC007309) — the entity that provided nominee subscriber services for Swiss Commodity Re's HK incorporation
2. **Controller of Swiss Commodity Re Limited** (per CRML SEC F-3) — the entity that was formed using those nominee subscriber services

Under Hong Kong's AMLO TCSP licensing framework, TCSP licensees are required to perform customer due diligence (CDD) on their clients. If a director of the TCSP is simultaneously the beneficial controller of the client entity, the CDD independence is structurally compromised.

Cecile Civiale Vuillier, as co-director with Deayton at Meta Octav/IFL, was a party to the institutional arrangement that enabled this formation — though no evidence suggests she had personal knowledge of or participated in the CRML transaction itself.
