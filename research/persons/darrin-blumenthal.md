## Key Analytical Note

Blumenthal's function at Jett Capital is identical to what Stuart Macliver provides at Amvest Capital: a person with genuine ABN AMRO/Macquarie pedigree and deep Australian mining advisory network who can credibly pitch Perth ASX junior miners to US institutional capital. The most important open question is whether Blumenthal introduced Macintyre to the CRML orbit, or whether Macintyre's connection to the copper deal predates Blumenthal's arrival at Jett Capital. If the latter, Macintyre's connector was someone else — possibly Matthew Jurjevich (who built Jett Capital's Perth network before Blumenthal arrived) or a third party in the ABN AMRO/Capella Capital orbit.
