### Critical Timing Anomaly

The Swiss Commodity Re NNC1 (October 23-30, 2025) was filed under the **old name** "International Fiduciaries Limited" — even though the HK entity appears to have already been renamed to META OCTAV GROUP (HK) LIMITED by that date. Possible explanations:

1. The engagement with Deayton's network predated the rename; Deayton used a pre-existing relationship/retainer with "IFL"
2. The name change had not yet propagated to the nominee subscriber agreements or the Companies Registry filing system
3. The NNC1 was prepared before the name change; filed under the outdated name in error

**This is an open question and a compliance issue worth flagging:** a TCSP appearing under its superseded name on an incorporation document may be technically incorrect.
