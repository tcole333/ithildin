## Summary

Frank Moyle Creer is a DFJ network venture capitalist who serves as the critical nexus entity connecting Tim Draper's Silicon Valley venture capital empire to Serguei Kouzmine's organized crime-linked investment vehicles. Creer co-founded Zone Ventures (a DFJ affiliate fund) with Tim Draper and Darius Sankey in 1998, served as an officer at DFJ Capital, was CEO of Draper Hero Institute (Draper's nonprofit, 2021-2024), and simultaneously held the position of Managing Director at QWave Capital -- the quantum technology fund co-founded by Serguei Beloussov and Serguei Kouzmine (documented Tambovskaya-Malyshevskaya organized crime leader).

The connection is confirmed through primary sources: SEC filings show Creer and Kouzmine as co-directors at Centice Corp (CIK 1415617, Delaware), a QWave Capital portfolio company specializing in Raman spectroscopy for drug identification. Other Centice directors include Vladimir Yakushev and Aidar Gabidullin — both now identified as co-officers at Advanced Diamond Technologies Inc (CIK 0001376372, Romeoville IL, 2013 Form D), confirming they are a persistent investment group with Kouzmine.

The FBI ARMS Reach memo (EFTA00129096, Operation Trip Knot, April 2025) contains the most serious allegations against Creer: that he was an active member of SNCTM (Sanctum, a high-end sex/swingers club), "collected information on members for blackmail, similar to Jeffrey Epstein," and "actively blackmailed fellow DFJ CAPITAL managing partner STEVE JURVETSON." The memo further alleges Creer "funneled money into quantum computing research through QWAVE CAPITAL" for political access, paralleling Epstein's MIT funding pattern. Both Creer and Draper categorically denied all allegations. Creer stated he had never heard of Sanctum and that he and Jurvetson are close friends.

Creer's career began at Bonneville Pacific Corp, one of Utah's biggest fraud scandals (four executives indicted, president pleaded guilty), where he performed financial analysis on projects later found to be fraudulent. No evidence of Creer's involvement in the fraud.
