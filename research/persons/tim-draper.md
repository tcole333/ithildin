## Summary

Third-generation Silicon Valley venture capitalist. Son of William Henry Draper III (SBIC pioneer, Marshall Plan administrator, UNDP administrator). Co-founded Draper Fisher Jurvetson (DFJ) with John Fisher and Steve Jurvetson. Notable investments include Hotmail, Skype, Tesla, Baidu, SpaceX, Robinhood, Coinbase. Major Bitcoin advocate -- purchased ~30,000 BTC from US Marshals Silk Road auction (2014). Investor in Theranos. Known libertarian/Republican donor.

Draper's significance in this investigation stems from an FBI ARMS Reach memo (EFTA00129096) filed under Operation Trip Knot by WFO Squad CR05 (Transnational Organized Crime, Eastern Hemisphere). The memo reveals that Frank Creer (CEO of Draper Hero Institute, former DFJ Capital officer) served as a bridge between the Draper/DFJ network and QWave Capital -- a quantum technology fund co-founded by Serguei Beloussov and Serguei Kouzmine, the latter being a documented leader of the Tambovskaya-Malyshevskaya organized crime group. Masha Drokova, simultaneously Epstein's publicist (2017-2019) and a publicist for QWave Capital and DFJ Financial, further links these networks. Draper denies all allegations.

The Draper empire operates from 55 East 3rd Avenue, San Mateo, which hosts approximately 45 entities with 32 still active, including Draper Associates, The Draper Foundation, Zone Ventures, JABE LLC, Draper Franchise LLC, DraperDragon funds, DNX fund family, Draper Oakwood entities, Brave VC, and multiple Riskmasters and Rhino SPV entities.

7. **Tezos ICO litigation**: Draper and Draper Associates named defendants in Tezos ICO securities fraud class action ($232M ICO, N.D. Cal. 3:17-cv-06779). Named as promoter/facilitator. Dismissed 2020. [Finding #8926]

8. **AllRounds v eShares/Carta**: Six DFJ/Draper entities plus Draper personally named defendants in trade secrets case (N.D. Cal. 3:20-cv-07083). [Finding #8927]

9. **Draper Oakwood SPAC**: Draper was Senior Advisor only -- no officer, director, or fiduciary role. Core team from 3i Group plc (UK). Pivoted from tech to Singapore luxury e-commerce (Reebonz). Director Abduwahab Al Betairi connects Draper brand to Gulf state capital (Saudi/Bahrain). [Findings #8928, #8921]

## Hypothesis Assessment

### H1: Draper-Epstein relationship was investment-transactional
**Supported.** No direct communications found. All contact mediated through Andra Capital/Haba's fundraising pitch, routed through Lesley Groff. Epstein declined. However, the FBI memo reveals deeper institutional connections (Foresight Institute, Drokova, Creer) that suggest proximity beyond a single fundraising pitch.

### H2: The Draper -> Drokova -> Epstein connection was not coincidental
**Strongly supported.** Drokova was simultaneously: working as Epstein's PR agent (2017-2018), employed by Draper (DFJ Financial per FBI memo, corroborated by LittleSis), publicist for QWave Capital and Runa Capital (Beloussov/Kouzmine firms), introducing Beloussov to Epstein, founding Day One Ventures with $450M in sensitive tech, and a former Nashi leader with Putin connections. This positions Drokova as a bridge between Russian tech networks and the Epstein network, with Draper's organizations as the Silicon Valley anchor point.

### H3: Frank Creer ran a parallel blackmail operation
**Unverified.** FBI memo allegations only. All named parties deny. Creer's only corpus appearance is in the FBI memo itself. However: Draper Hero Institute (Creer was CEO) received $805K from Draper Foundation, was incorporated Mar 2018 and terminated Jul 2024.

### H4: Ukraine/Russia sanctions angle
**Unverified independently.** FBI memo only. Case filing under OCDETF Eurasian Organizations is significant. Michael Caputo later became Trump's HHS Assistant Secretary of Public Affairs.
