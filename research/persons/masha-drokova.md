## Summary

Masha Drokova is a Russian-born venture capitalist who served as a connective node between Vladimir Putin's political network, Jeffrey Epstein's social/intelligence network, and Silicon Valley's DFJ venture capital ecosystem. She appears 1,627 times in DOJ Epstein file releases -- an extraordinary volume indicating she was not a peripheral contact but an active operational participant in Epstein's network over a 27-month period (March 2017 to June 2019).

Before entering the VC world, Drokova was a leader of Nashi, Putin's pro-Kremlin youth movement, and was famously photographed kissing Putin on the cheek (the "Putin's Kiss" documentary). She subsequently became VP Communications at Acronis and Head of Communications at Runa Capital -- both companies founded by Serguei Beloussov, whose QWave Capital fund was co-founded with Serguei Kouzmine, a documented leader of the Tambovskaya-Malyshevskaya organized crime group. Drokova simultaneously served as publicist for QWave Capital, DFJ Financial (per FBI), and as Epstein's publicist, creating an operational overlap that the FBI classified under Transnational Organized Crime (Eastern Hemisphere).

In January 2018, during the peak of her Epstein relationship, Drokova founded Day One Ventures, which now manages approximately $450M in assets across 14 SEC-registered entities (3 main Cayman LP funds + 11 syndicate sub-funds) focused on strategically sensitive technology sectors including quantum computing, nuclear energy, brain-computer interfaces, and AI security. The fund has 94% non-US funding and is Cayman-registered. An FBI CHS (Confidential Human Source) assesses that "Day One Ventures exists to steal technology."
