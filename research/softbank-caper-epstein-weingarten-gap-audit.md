# Epstein / Weingarten involvement in the Misra–Benedetti campaign: gap audit

**Date:** 2026-07-09  
**Profile:** `softbank-caper`  
**Analysis run:** #64 (`generate-hunches`)

## Bottom line

The current record supports **adjacency and post-exposure legal-intelligence
positioning**, not demonstrated Epstein or Weingarten participation in the
2015–2017 campaign against Nikesh Arora and Alok Sama.

The broad name-and-content search is mature. Findings #11690 and #11789 are the
controlling negatives: the main Epstein corpora contain no campaign work
product, Arora/Sama banking records, honey-trap material, Barkmere transaction,
or pre-publication communication with the operational cast. Another generic
name sweep is unlikely to add much. The remaining work should use matter
identifiers, payment records, redacted identities, document fingerprints,
personnel rosters, and repositories created by the targets or their lawyers.

Evidence capable of changing the assessment would include a pre-March-2018
campaign artifact in Epstein's possession; a pre-publication tasking message;
a matching payer, beneficiary, account, matter number, invoice, or traveler;
or a named operator shared by the two systems. Shared cities, firms, conference
lists, offshore jurisdictions, or similar tradecraft are hypothesis generators,
not corroboration.

## Corrections made during the audit

1. **The Tokyo dates now diverge, and Epstein's trip was canceled.** Epstein's
   staff booked a May 22–30, later May 25–June 2, 2015 trip at the Andaz Tokyo.
   The planned roster included three assistants, although released hotel records

   booking, or vendor match. The unresolved hotel is retained as a searchable

2. **`DB` is unresolved but relevant, with a probabilistic ranking.** In
   `HOUSE_OVERSIGHT_028577`, Epstein wrote that Karp represented SoftBank rather
   than Misra and referred to a prior-day conversation "re DB." The grammar
   favors Karp having spoken to Misra the prior day, and the surrounding corpus
   gives a moderate lexical/contextual lean toward **Deutsche Bank**. **David
   Boies** remains a material alternative because Epstein also used `DB` for
   Boies (`EFTA01051768`, `EFTA01051770`) and was discussing the Ransome/Boies
   settlement in this period. The abbreviation is neither resolved nor

3. **Karp did not answer the Misra question with "Indeed."** Karp's "Indeed"
   answered Epstein's separate statement about Carlos Ghosn. His answer to
   "Rajeev misra do you represent?" was: "Sort of. Spoke to him yesterday."

4. **The Sprint share chronology is probabilistically bounded, not sequenced
   to the Barkmere wire.** CH Communication admitted in March 2016 that it
   could not satisfy the one-year holding-duration requirement. An unidentified
   September 2015 UBP statement and the later CH-specific UBP letter each show
   exactly 10,600 Sprint shares. The leading inference is that these were the
   same CH position and that it was acquired after approximately February 19,
   2015 and by September 12, 2015. The documents do not prove that sequence:
   an older beneficial position with a custody/documentation break, an in-kind
   transfer, or an unidentified supporter followed by an identical CH position
   remain live alternatives. Nothing proves purchase after the April 2015
   Barkmere payment. Findings #11810, #11975, and #11983.

5. **The December 2018 Epstein-to-Boies payment is affirmatively allocated.**
   The Ransome settlement agreement required Epstein to wire $500,000 to

   and the parties dismissed the case on December 20. This closes that proposed
   funding bridge to the 2016 shareholder letter without claiming that every
   possible bridge is disproved (`EFTA00283429`, `EFTA02612964`,
   `EFTA01288073`, SDNY Doc. 189; finding #12007).

6. **The Cyprus Barkmere was a false merge, and the BVI vehicle predates the
   campaign.** Audited O1 Properties accounts and the Cyprus Gazette identify
   Cyprus Barkmere Limited HE338259 as an inactive O1 subsidiary later merged

   LTD**, no. 1010725, gives a February 14, 2006 registration date. That date

   controlled it in 2015. No public director, agent, address, status, charge,
   ownership, or Barkmere–Epstein service-provider match was found; the
   registered-agent test remains unperformed, not disproved. Findings #11992,
   #12000, and #12008–#12009.

## New falsifiable hypotheses

### #301 — `DB` may mean David Boies or a Boies-linked matter

This is a live alternative, not a conclusion. The same corpus explicitly uses
subject `DB` for David Boies; Epstein and Karp were discussing the Boies
settlement in late 2018; and Boies Schiller sent the January 2016 anti-Arora
letter. If adjacent call, calendar, billing, or email records instead identify
Deutsche Bank personnel, an account, compliance issue, or litigation, this
hypothesis is falsified. Lead #51816.

### #302 — the January 2019 exchange was routine prospective-client vetting

Weingarten asked Epstein "should I take the huawei cfo?" in
`HOUSE_OVERSIGHT_030799`, less than a month before asking for "special jeffrey
insights" on Misra. The Misra question came after public exposure, and "get the
book on him" concerned prospective hire Ron Soffer. A denominator of similar
intake questions and a January-2019-or-later matter opening would support this
null. A pre-March-2018 engagement, bill, tasking, or nonpublic artifact would
falsify it. Lead #51818.

### #303 — a lower-level K2 personnel or vendor bridge may exist

Finding #11783 documents a September 2015 K2 London pitch from Rosamund de Sybel
to Epstein via David Stern. The best current attribution is the unrelated
Boreh/Djibouti matter, but the subject is unnamed. The campaign side reportedly
included Matteo Bigazzi and David Robertson, while finding #11788 shows a
separate Epstein/Karp London-investigator channel in the same month. The test is
not the K2 name; it is a shared operator, supervisor, technical vendor, counsel,
matter code, invoice, or tasking phrase. Distinct teams and a confirmed Boreh
matter would harden the negative. Lead #51820.

### #304 — one master campaign packet can be fingerprinted across channels

The allegations propagated through an anonymous Boies Schiller letter, a
repeated Mintz & Gold cadence, media placements, and an Indian regulator
complaint. Text/name sweeps do not test attachment provenance. Original files
should be compared for hashes, filenames, PDF/Word author fields, creation
software, embedded images, EXIF, fonts, dimensions, OCR defects, typos, and
distinctive 8–15-word phrases. A pre-March-26-2018 nonpublic copy in an Epstein
archive would be probative; post-publication copies show monitoring only.
Lead #51822.

## Negative controls and stop rules

- March 26, 2018 is the public-knowledge boundary. Later mentions are presumed
  reactive unless they contain nonpublic material.
- Count the same EFTA/House document once across OCR corpora.
- Compare the Misra exchange with Weingarten's other client-intake questions.
- Compare the K2 pitch with confirmed Boreh and other unrelated K2 matters.
- Establish base rates for shared service-provider addresses and common investigators
  before treating an overlap as unusual. The Italian-passport-traveler test is now a
  worked example of why cross-production identifiers must precede timing inferences.
- A matching payment requires beneficiary/account/reference evidence, not only
  amount and month.
- The K2 subject now leans Boreh, the traveler resolved to an unrelated person,
  the campaign's April Tokyo setup diverges from Epstein's canceled late-May
  itinerary, the Arora emissary strongly favors Pozhidaeva without yielding a
  campaign link, and no pre-2018 retainer or campaign artifact appeared. The
  pre-publication operational hypothesis is therefore downgraded; it should not
  be replaced with a new associative theory absent transaction-, tasking-, or
  artifact-level evidence.
