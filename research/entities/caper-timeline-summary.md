# SoftBank Caper — Scheduling / Travel / Meeting Timeline Digest

Raw dataset: `research/entities/caper-timeline.jsonl` (60 events). Built from the
DOJ Vol 11, LMSBAND Epstein Files, Unified DB, and House Oversight 20K corpora
(2026-07-02). Every event traces to a real corpus document; nothing is fabricated.
This is the **shadow** of the operation — scheduled calls, itineraries, assistant
logistics, and off-email "move-to-voice" tells — not its substance, which is
mostly redacted or was never on email.

## The single most important structural finding

**The Epstein corpus does not contain the SoftBank Vision Fund operational cast.**
Across all four corpora, `Rajeev Misra`, `Nikesh Arora`, `Alok Sama`, `Marcelo
Claure`, `Bertrand des Pallieres`, `Giannakopoulos`, `K2 Intelligence`, and
`Barkmere` return **zero real hits** — only false-positive name collisions
(Shalini/Jayadev Misra, Fabrizio Benedetti the neuroscientist, WEF/Davos delegate
rosters, Marcelo Gleiser/Odebrecht, etc.).

What the corpus *does* contain are **two adjacent layers**:

1. **The Benedetti / Avenue Foch / Exor-CNH social layer** (2010–2014, dense in
   Paris). Alessandro **de Benedetti**, Eduardo **Teodorani-Fabbri** (CNH
   Industrial/Agnelli), Wojtek **Fibak** — Epstein's actual Paris circle, years
   before Misra hired Benedetti.
2. **The Nardello / London op-adjacent layer** (2015) — Epstein's own
   surveillance apparatus run through Paul Weiss (Karp), operating in the same
   London geography and same year as the K2/Arora op, but with **no documentary
   link** between the two.

The direct SoftBank-cast material is a **2017–2019 phenomenon** (Vision Fund
didn't launch until 2017). So the timeline is a barbell: a dense **2013 Paris
social cluster**, a thinner **2015 Nardello/London cluster**, and a dense
**Jan 2019 "caper" 72-hour cluster** — with op-critical 2015–2016 (when the
smear campaign actually ran) largely *dark* on the cast.

## Conspicuous silences (dense-elsewhere periods that go dark)

- **No Tokyo itinerary for Epstein.** Exact-phrase searches for "depart for tokyo"
  return **0** scheduling/travel docs. Given the honey trap was staged in a Tokyo
  hotel, Epstein's own calendar shows no Tokyo travel — his Tokyo access is entirely
  **proxied** through David Stern (Jun 2018). Epstein was never on the ground.
- **2015–2016 op window is dark on the cast.** The smear campaign ran Jan 2015
  (K2 hired) → Jan 2016 (BSF letter) → Jun 2016 (Arora departs). Across that window
  the cast (Misra/Arora/Sama/Benedetti) is **absent** from Epstein's scheduling
  stratum. What fills 2015 instead is the Nardello/London material (op-adjacent
  geography, unproven link) and one redacted Italian-passenger London ticket (Feb 2016).
- **2013 Paris is dense with Benedetti; 2015–2019 Paris scheduling drops the name.**
  Benedetti appears as a recurring calendar reminder in 2013, then vanishes from the
  scheduling stratum until the single Jan 28 2019 one-line "Allesandro benedetti?"
  probe. The 2011 and 2013 records establish the relationship; the operational years
  show only the reactive 2019 inquiry.
- **The substance is almost always redacted or absent.** The Oct 2015 Four Seasons
  meeting exists only as a transcript *cover email* — the transcript PDF itself is not
  in corpus. The 2015-09-19 and 2016-02-04 London travelers are redacted. The Jan 25
  2019 Karp↔Misra call left no content. Repeatedly we see the *logistics* of a contact
  with the *content* stripped.

## Coverage / corpus observations

- **2015 email is genuinely thin on the cast**, but *not* thin overall — 2015 has 8
  events, all in the Nardello/London-logistics register. The thinness is
  cast-specific, not corpus-specific: Epstein simply wasn't transacting with the
  SoftBank principals in 2015.
- **2019 is by far the densest** (15 events), concentrated in the Jan 21–28 window.
  **2013 is next** (14 events), the Paris/Benedetti social cluster.
- **Heavy cross-corpus overlap.** The Jan 2019 caper chain is triplicated
  (`HOUSE_OVERSIGHT_028576-028581` + rescans `028589/028590/028601`, plus Unified
  `hf_notesbymuneeb`/`hf_to-be` copies). The Benedetti Paris calendars appear in both
  DOJ and LMSBAND. Bates IDs are recorded in every `source_ids` for dedupe; the
  merged file collapses same-date/same-bates duplicates (60 unique of ~72 raw).
- **Date provenance:** where email headers were OCR-garbled, plist
  `date-received` unix timestamps were used and are flagged in `notes`. All
  header/plist dates cross-checked consistent.
