## Role in Swiss Commodity Re Formation

**International Fiduciaries Limited appeared as the sole founder member/subscriber on the NNC1 incorporation form for Swiss Commodity Re Limited (HK Company No. 79047415), dated October 23 / October 30, 2025.**

### Interpretation

Under standard HK company formation practice:
1. A TCSP-licensed firm (here: IFL) acts as **nominee subscriber** — it subscribes for the initial share(s) of a newly incorporated company on behalf of the actual client
2. On or shortly after incorporation, IFL transfers the shares to the actual client/beneficial owner
3. The nominated subscriber's name therefore appears on the NNC1 even though it has no ongoing economic interest

This is a **routine and legal TCSP function in Hong Kong**, analogous to the role that Deayton's HKCS Group would itself perform for its own clients. The distinction here is that Deayton (via HKCS Group) appears to have used IFL rather than HKCS Group itself as the NNC1 subscriber — either because:
- HKCS Group does not hold an independent TCSP licence (it provides services through its principals' qualifications), or
- Deayton preferred to separate his own firm's name from the incorporation record, and
- IFL, as a separately-licensed, independent TCSP, was a logical service provider given the prior institutional relationship through HKTC Group
