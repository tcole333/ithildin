## The Copper Powder Transaction

**Date:** Asset Sale Agreement signed November 21, 2025; closed December 16, 2025

**Transaction:** Swiss Commodity Re sold 40kg of ultra-high-purity (UHP) copper powder (99.96% purity) to Critical Metals Corp (NASDAQ: CRML) for **2,000,000 CRML ordinary shares** — 1,800,000 to Swiss Commodity Re and 200,000 to Malcolm Scott Macintyre.

**Valuation claimed:** US$20,000,000 ($10/share, at a ~40% premium to market)
**Valuation booked:** US$15.8M (CRML Dec 31, 2025 balance sheet — non-current inventory)

**Red flags:**
- No independent valuation or appraisal obtained
- No custody or storage location disclosed
- No supply chain provenance for UHP copper powder
- No 13D/G filing (shares kept below 5% threshold at 1.5%)
- Not disclosed as related-party transaction in SEC filings
- Entity incorporated 22 days before deal announced
- CRML is foreign private issuer exempt from Section 16 — insider sales not reported on Form 4
- 40kg is trivial in global context; market pricing for UHP copper powder at $200–$600/kg wholesale → deal implies ~$400,000–$500,000 wholesale value vs. $20M claimed

## Key Structural Assessment
Swiss Commodity Re Limited is a **22-day-old HK shell company** created specifically for the CRML copper powder transaction. It:
1. Used its controlling entity's (HKCS Group) office address as its own
2. Has no independent web presence, no public corporate registry entry, and no supply chain
3. Was incorporated by a professional corporate services provider (Deayton's own firm) 22 days before the deal was announced
4. Received 1.8M shares immediately registered for resale — consistent with a short-term liquidity vehicle
5. Kept share ownership below 5% (13D/G reporting threshold) — deliberate structuring to avoid disclosure

The 200,000 shares to Macintyre suggest he played a role in facilitating the deal — his infrastructure finance background and $32B+ track record at Capella Capital makes him an unusual co-recipient unless he has a personal relationship with Deayton or acted as an Australian-side intermediary for the Perth mining promoter network around CRML.
