## Connection to CRML Investigation

### Phase 1: Sizzle SPAC / European Lithium (2022–2024)
Jett Capital served as **exclusive financial advisor to European Lithium Limited** (ASX: EUR; Executive Chairman **Tony Sage**, Perth-based) for the Sizzle Acquisition Corp SPAC merger that created CRML. Deal announced October 2022; closed February 27, 2024.

**Compensation:**
- ~3% advisory fee on transaction value (~$750M transaction)
- 6% of PIPE capital raised pre-closing (~$10M Empery PIPE → ~$600K)
- Vendor shares: Jett received Ordinary Shares at closing as part of the 2,215,000 aggregate vendor/service provider shares

**Stockhead (Jett Capital quote on EUR/Sizzle):** *"European Lithium is one of the most interesting developers our firm has ever advised. In its Wolfsberg asset, you have a lithium developer that checks each of those three boxes [economics, permitting, strategic investment]."*
