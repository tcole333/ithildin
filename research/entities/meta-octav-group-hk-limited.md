## Connection to Swiss Commodity Re Limited

Swiss Commodity Re Limited is a **distinct entity** from META OCTAV GROUP (HK). The connection is:

1. **NNC1 Nominee Subscriber:** When Swiss Commodity Re was incorporated (October 30, 2025), "International Fiduciaries Limited" (IFL's pre-rename name) was listed as the sole founder-member/subscriber on the NNC1. Standard TCSP practice: TCSP subscribes initial shares, then transfers to actual client.

2. **Shared Controller:** Kenneth Raymond Deayton controls Swiss Commodity Re (per CRML F-3) AND is now a director of META OCTAV GROUP (HK) (formerly IFL). Deayton was thus on both sides of Swiss Commodity Re's formation: director of the TCSP that incorporated it, and controller of the entity itself.

3. **Shared Address:** Both entities registered at 6/F Wyndham Place — Swiss Commodity Re as HKCS Group client; META OCTAV GROUP (HK) as co-located TCSP.

4. **SEC Filing Status:** The CRML F-3 (Feb 23, 2026) and 424B3 (Mar 4, 2026) still refer to "Swiss Commodity Re Limited" as selling securityholder. No SEC filing has been updated to reflect IFL's rename to META OCTAV GROUP (HK). These are legally distinct entities.
