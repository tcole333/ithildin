## Relationship Types to Track

### Direct Relationships
- **EMPLOYED_BY**: Person worked at Organization
- **FOUNDED**: Person founded Company
- **OFFICER_OF**: Person is officer/director
- **COUNSEL_FOR**: Lawyer represented client
- **FINANCED_BY**: Company funded by Drexel
- **INVESTED_IN**: Entity held position in Company

### Investigation Relationships
- **CO_CONSPIRATOR**: Named in same case
- **COOPERATED_AGAINST**: Testimony against
- **PARKING_FOR**: Held shares for another
- **COORDINATED_WITH**: Acting in concert (alleged)

### Indirect Relationships
- **COMMON_ATTORNEY**: Share same counsel
- **COMMON_INVESTOR**: Both invested in same target
- **TEMPORAL_OVERLAP**: Active same period
- **ADDRESS_MATCH**: Same address appeared
