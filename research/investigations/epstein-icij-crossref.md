## Analysis

### Why No Direct Epstein Matches?

1. **Jurisdiction Differences**: Epstein's shell companies appear to be primarily US-based (Delaware, US Virgin Islands, New Mexico). ICIJ leaks focus on traditional offshore jurisdictions (BVI, Panama, Bahamas, Cayman, etc.)

2. **Service Provider Gap**: ICIJ leaks came from specific service providers (Mossack Fonseca, Appleby, Asiaciti Trust, etc.). Epstein may have used different corporate service providers not captured in these leaks.

3. **Time Period**: Much of Epstein's financial activity (documented 1999-2014) may have occurred through different channels than those exposed in the Panama/Paradise Papers.

4. **US Focus**: Many of Epstein's entities were domestic US LLCs and trusts, which wouldn't appear in offshore leaks focused on international jurisdictions.

### Significance of Brunel Finding

The Jean-Luc Brunel connection is significant because:

1. **Confirms offshore structure** used by Epstein network for model scouting operations
2. **Same service provider** (Portcullis TrustNet) handling multiple related entities
3. **BVI jurisdiction** commonly used for opaque corporate structures
4. **Timeline alignment** (2003 incorporation) with Brunel's model agency expansion
