## Key Allegations

### From the Expert Report:

1. **Volume of Accounts is Suspicious**: 134 accounts with no apparent business justification used to "shuffle funds to affiliated entities and individuals"

2. **Overly Complex Structure**: 10 different entities holding 6 aircraft with no clear commercial purpose; aircraft frequently transferred between entities "for unstated reasons"

3. **Payments Inconsistent with Entity Purpose**:
   - Aircraft expenses paid from personal account despite separate aircraft entities
   - C.O.U.Q. Foundation made payments "unrelated to charitable organization's purpose"
   - NES, LLC credit card activity "consistent with personal use - not business use"

4. **Shell Company Operations**:
   - Financial Trust Co. did not appear to "advise private clients with $1 billion or more in assets" as claimed
   - Southern Trust records show it was not engaged in "cutting edge consulting service" as stated

5. **Human Trafficking Indicators**:
   - JPM due diligence noted one individual was "reportedly 'purchased' at 14 from her parents in Yugoslavia"
   - Payments to women (many with Eastern European surnames) flagged
   - Wire transfers to high-risk countries for human trafficking

6. **Highbridge Capital Connection**: Epstein "appeared to play an active role in JPMorgan's acquisition of a majority interest in Highbridge Capital Management" (deal valued at stake increase to 77.5%, $21B AUM)

7. **Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation**: Epstein attempted to develop a "very HIGH profile" donor advised fund projected to raise "tens of billions of dollars"
