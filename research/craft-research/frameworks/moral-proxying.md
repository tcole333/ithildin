## Definition
Moral proxying is the practice of using a figure of unquestioned professional or moral standing (the "Moral Proxy") as the primary face of a transaction or social gathering to "wash" the involvement of a radioactive principal. The "Vouching Pattern" is a sub-mechanism where the broker uses the *presence* or *endorsement* of an existing elite contact to entice a new, high-value target to the table.

## Mechanism
1. **The "Anchor" Contact**: The broker cultivates a high-reputation figure (e.g., Bill Gates, Reid Hoffman, Larry Summers).
2. **The Lure**: The broker approaches a new target (e.g., Josh Harris, Elon Musk) and mentions that the Anchor Contact is already "on board" or will be attending the meeting.
3. **Moral Safe-Harbor**: The new target assumes that if the Anchor Contact has "vetted" the broker, they don't need to do their own due diligence. The Proxy’s reputation provides a buffer against the broker's radioactive history.
4. **Proxy Dragging**: The broker may physically bring the Anchor Contact to a meeting (e.g., bringing Gates to a bank meeting) to serve as a silent "vouch" for the broker's legitimacy.
5. **The Handoff**: Once the new target is "in the room," the broker works to establish a direct relationship, eventually making the Proxy's presence redundant.

## Detection Markers
- Introduction emails that lead with "X (famous person) is also coming" or "I was just with Y (famous person)."
- Planning documents for meetings that explicitly curate a "high-repute" list to impress a specific new target (e.g., Harris dinner planning).
- High-reputation figures appearing in meetings where their expertise is not directly relevant to the topic (e.g., a philanthropist at a commercial banking pitch).
- The use of terms like "Super Menchy" to describe individuals being used to "buffer" a more controversial request.
- Evidence of a target expressing hesitation that is overcome specifically by the mention of another elite's participation.

## Canonical Examples
- **Gates/JPM**: Bringing Bill Gates to JPMorgan meetings to provide legitimacy to the "scientific philanthropy" narrative while Epstein was still under probation.
- **Musk/Hoffman**: Using Reid Hoffman's name and presence as a hook to entice Elon Musk into meetings.
- **Josh Harris**: Carefully planning attendees for an initial meeting to create an environment of "peer validation" that lowers the target's guard.
