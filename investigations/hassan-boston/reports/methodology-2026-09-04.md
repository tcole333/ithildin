# Investigation methods learned

## Learnings

- [Methodology] Store exact corporate IDs, raw legal names and record dates; distinct Silverstone legal entities used the same name historically, so brand-name joins would conflate ownership and transactions.
- [Methodology] Search recorded-name abbreviations and full original instruments together. ABDUL R in the Suffolk index led to a deed spelling Abdul Rahman Ali Hassan in full beside Hicham and Zouhair.
- [Source quality] The Massachusetts individual-officer index omitted two companies whose official summaries showed the exact searched name; bounded zero results cannot establish absent roles or holdings.
- [Methodology] Keep assessor parcels, condominium masters, parking units and historical other-owner rows distinct; no personal wealth total follows from raw assessment sums.
- [Methodology] Link deeds to their capacities and predecessor references. Brimmer's foreclosure price was subject to an older mortgage, and later sub-100-dollar consideration did not describe market value or beneficiary interests.
- [Source quality] Bankruptcy schedules disclose the debtor's position at filing and may be amended; shared creditor obligations across related entities must be deduplicated before any debt total.
