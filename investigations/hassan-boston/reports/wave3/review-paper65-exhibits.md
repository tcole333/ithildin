# Paper 65 exhibit audit

Reviewed September 5, 2026. This is an independent page-scope audit of
`evidence/wave3/local-courts/family-exhibits-paper65.pdf` (40 pages). Every
page was visually inspected from a full-page render, and the complete 40-page
OCR set was checked for document boundaries and settlement-related terms. The
native text layer was used only as a secondary cross-check because most exhibit
pages are scans.

## Agreement-presence conclusion

The PDF does **not** contain the executed December 14, 2000 settlement
agreement. None of its 40 pages is that agreement, bears that date as an
agreement, or contains its alleged beneficial-interest provisions and party
signature set. The packet consists of 2021-2022 discovery orders, deadline
motions, discovery correspondence, and a February 2022 affidavit.

## Business-record evidence for source-owner review

- Page 34 contains an accountant's statement about the scope and gaps of the
  real-estate-entity bookkeeping materials then held and produced, including
  the absence of files for two named entities and the absence of audits. This
  is the sender's statement in an email chain, not independent proof that no
  other records or audits existed.
- Pages 37-40 are the complete affidavit, rather than pages 37-39 alone. They
  support Hicham's stated use of the name Sam and his statements about rent
  collection into business accounts and buildings owned through trusts he
  controls. Page 40 is necessary to establish that the affidavit is signed;
  its exact February 2022 day is not filled in.
- Exhibit A is a procedural discovery order requiring production and sworn
  certification. Exhibits F-H discuss or transmit categories of financial
  records but do not contain the underlying account statements, ledgers, tax
  returns, or the 2000 agreement. They should not be used as proof of balances,
  transaction amounts, beneficial ownership, or source of funds.

Incidental personal, account, contact, and medical details were excluded from
this audit.
