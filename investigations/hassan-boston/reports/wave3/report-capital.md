# Capital transaction and bankruptcy follow-up — September 5, 2026

This wave identifies a primary 2018 merger record linking Concepts International LLC to an Amazon-addressed merger vehicle and an exact closing date in the same period as the reported Concepts/Amazon-Zappos relationship. The filing does not establish that every reported characterization referred to this merger. It also does **not** resolve the purchase price, membership percentage, seller proceeds or any flow into Hassan-family real estate. Six new findings were added: **15629–15634**.

## The 2018 transaction now has a primary corporate record

Massachusetts Corporations Division entity **001148501** is Concepts International LLC. Its official summary says the company merged with **JORDAN MERGERSUB, LLC** on **August 9, 2018**. All three pages of original filing **201826378650** were visually read in the public registry viewer.

Jordan Mergersub was a Delaware LLC formed July 17, 2018. The certificate gives its office as **c/o Amazon.com, Inc., 410 Terry Avenue North, Seattle, Washington 98109**. Jordan Mergersub merged **into** Concepts International LLC, and Concepts survived. The merger took effect when filed on August 9. The certificate initially names Arun Rajan and Scott Schaefer as the survivor's managers, corporate-filing signers and real-property instrument signers. Tarek Ali Hassan signed the certificate for Concepts as `Duly Authorized` on August 9. Finding **15629**.

The certificate says an Agreement and Plan of Merger had been adopted and executed. It says that agreement was kept at Concepts' 4 Brattle Street, Suite 308 office and could be furnished to a member; the agreement is not attached. No contact or record request was made. The certificate does not identify Jordan's ultimate parent or equity owner, the consideration, form or percentage of an investment, recipients, post-closing cap table or use of proceeds. The c/o Amazon address is explicit primary evidence, but it is not by itself an ownership disclosure.

The sequence becomes clearer one month later. Massachusetts amendment **201832541000**, executed September 5 and filed September 10, states: `Add Tarek Hassan as a manager.` The November 2 annual report **201843509600** then lists Tarek Hassan, Arun Rajan and Scott Schaefer as managers while retaining only Rajan and Schaefer as corporate-filing and real-property signers. These are authority roles, not membership shares. Finding **15630**. This sharpens the prior November manager snapshot in finding 15610 without turning a manager title into an ownership claim.

The new filing is the strongest documentary bridge yet to the reported 2018 transaction. It names an Amazon-addressed merger entity and exact closing mechanism. It still does not substantiate a public claim that Zappos itself was the legal acquirer, that Amazon acquired a specified percentage, or that Tarek personally received a particular sum.

## Ownership evidence is now dated rather than assumed

The original 16-page Nanterre RCS packet for deposit **2019/71332** was obtained, rendered, OCRed and visually reviewed page by page. Its English Limited Liability Company Agreement says it was entered into on **October 2, 2014** by **Tarek Ali Hassan as sole member**. It vests management in the Member, permits contributions and transfers, and directs ownership-interest distributions to the Member, but it records no contribution amount. Tarek signed as Sole Member. Finding **15631**.

That instrument is direct ownership evidence for its 2014 date. It is not a 2018 closing cap table. The French filing cover describes translated statutes in force at the November 2019 deposit, but the attached English agreement remains dated 2014 and includes no later amendment, merger schedule or capitalization table. I therefore do not carry Tarek's sole-member status through 2018, 2019 or the present without the missing transaction documents.

The same packet contains the October 2019 French-branch consent. Kedar Deshpande and Scott Schaefer signed one English counterpart on October 14; Tarek signed a separate counterpart on October 17. Thus all three board managers executed across counterparts. The consent establishes a Clichy branch and appoints Joel Dion and Raman Rekhi as branch managers. It refers to an Exhibit A on banking, contracting, financing, management and sub-delegation authority, but Exhibit A is absent from the entire 16-page packet. Its detailed terms were not reviewed. The footer `Managers Consent re Merger` is not treated as evidence of a separate merger because the visible body concerns the branch and delegation policy. Finding **15632**.

## Trademark assignments define a separate asset sequence

The public USPTO Assignment Center supplies two additional dated intellectual-property events:

* **March 11, 2015:** reel/frame **5475/0588** records Paloma Tannery, LLC assigning its entire interest in CNCPTS serial **86458475** to Concepts International LLC. Finding **15633**.
* **July 31, 2025:** reel/frame **9039/0339**, recorded October 20, records Concepts International LLC assigning its entire interest in five marks to the distinct Delaware entity **CNCPTS LLC**: CC **86524050**, CC CONCEPTS **86554656**, CNCPTS **86458475**, and WILDERNESS WORKSHOP **87616181** and **87616168**. Finding **15634**.

The official index states no consideration for either assignment. The 2025 document link opened a PDF viewer that reported `Failed to load PDF document`; the nine source pages were not read. USPTO also cautions that assignment recordation is ministerial. The evidence establishes recorded IP transfers, not transaction validity beyond recordation or a monetary amount. The 2025 transfer is not evidence of what happened in 2018.

Existing canonical entities were reused: Tarek **7171**, CNCPTS LLC **7193**, Paloma Tannery LLC **7197**, and Concepts **7246**. Concepts was enriched with Massachusetts ID 001148501. New entity **7248**, JORDAN MERGERSUB, LLC, was created with Delaware jurisdiction and merged status. Profile connections **6804–6806** preserve the merger and trademark-transfer sequence.

The Massachusetts certificate says the plan is kept at the company, but it is not in the public filing, and this investigation does not contact subjects or request nonpublic records.
