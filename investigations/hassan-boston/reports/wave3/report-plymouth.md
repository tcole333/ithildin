## 53 Beach Avenue: Hicham collateralized Abdul Rahman's 1992 note

The clearest new capital connection is in the original **10797/121**, a $200,000 Mortgage and Security Agreement executed February 29 and recorded March 3, 1992. Hicham Ali Hassan, also identified as Hicham Abdul Hafiz Ali Hassan, is the sole defined and indexed mortgagor and the only signer on the reviewed signature page. On page 1, the printed word `Mortgagor` is visibly struck through in the note-maker line and **Abdul Rahman Ali Hassan** is typed in its place. The resulting operative text says the Hull mortgage secures a $200,000 same-date promissory note made by Abdul Rahman to USTrust.

This supports a direct financial relationship: Hicham supplied his 53 Beach Avenue real-estate collateral for Abdul Rahman's note. It does not make Abdul a co-mortgagor, co-owner or signer of the mortgage. Finding **15644** and financial connection **6807** preserve that narrower distinction. Ten of thirty pages were reviewed: pages 1, 2, 10, 20 and 25–30, covering the grant, sampled covenants, signature and property/security exhibits. This instrument remains `original_partial_review`; unreviewed boilerplate may contain more detail.

USTrust's separate **11586/222** says it “acknowledges satisfaction” of 10797/121. The discharge is dated and acknowledged January 8, 1993 and was recorded January 15. It does not identify the amount actually paid, payer or source. Adjacent **11586/221**, dated and recorded on the same dates, separately satisfies Hicham's older December 1986 mortgage at 7361/58. Finding **15645** covers the two original one-page satisfactions. The older underlying mortgage itself was outside this bounded page-priority review.

## 400 Boylston trust: $1 million Hull mortgage fully reviewed

Wave 3 completed pages 2–11 of **11586/233**, so all fourteen pages have now been reviewed. The source status is upgraded from the Wave 2 `original_partial_review` row to `original_image_reviewed`; finding **15598** and its evidence assessment were corrected accordingly.

The January 15, 1993 instrument is a $1 million mortgage from Hicham, Abdul Rahman and Zouhair Ali Hassan as trustees of 400 Boylston Street Realty Trust to First Trade Union Savings Bank, F.S.B. Page 8 states that the mortgagor's principal office is **402 Boylston Street, Boston**. The remaining covenant pages cover taxes, insurance, leases and rents, other liens, transfers, defaults and remedies. All three men sign as trustees and not individually. Exhibit A describes only the two Hull lots and associated beach rights.

The full review found no allocation of the loan proceeds to a Boston purchase, no Suffolk real estate in the collateral exhibit, no Suffolk mortgage cross-reference, no equity-funding source, and no payoff or current balance. The same-day trust declaration, deed and mortgage and the Boston office address are documented context. They do not prove that the Hull loan funded a Boston acquisition.

## 400 Boylston trustee chronology remains internally unresolved

Original **28008/229** says Hicham was “the sole Trustee” and removes Abdul Rahman under beneficiary directions and Section Seven of the trust declaration. Original **28008/230** records Zouhair's resignation under Section 7.1. Both instruments were executed and acknowledged on December 14, 2000, but recorded together on April 21, 2004. They name no agreement or cause, appoint no successor and include no separate delayed-effective-date language.

The Wave 3 event rows and corrected findings **15623–15624** use the **2004 recording date** and retain the 2000 execution date in notes. Signing alone is not treated as an established legal-effective cessation date without the declaration's operation and other records. A direct chronology conflict remains: all three men later signed Plymouth **19629/323** as trustees on March 30, 2001. No invalidity or unbroken sole-trustee status is inferred. The documents do not refer to the same-date family settlement agreement alleged in later litigation; temporal coincidence is not evidence of a connection.

## Hassull Realty Trust and Unit 307

All sixteen pages of the original **8177/4** declaration are now reviewed. It names Gregory V. Sullivan and Hicham as original trustees, permits one trustee to execute deeds, mortgages, notes, assignments and releases, and provides recorded mechanisms for trustee resignation, appointment and removal under beneficiary direction. A separately kept Schedule of Beneficial Interests identifies the beneficiaries; it is not in the sixteen recorded pages. Trustee status therefore does not establish beneficial shares. Finding **15625** reflects the complete review.

The same-day acquisition and financing records show a conventional secured-borrowing component:

| Recording date | Original instrument | Event | Amount | Finding |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| 1987-12-10 | 8177/20 | Unit 307 acquisition by Gregory and Hicham as Hassull trustees | $195,000 stated price | 15604 (Wave 2) |
| 1987-12-10 | 8177/24 | Hassull trustees' mortgage to Granite Cooperative Bank | $156,000 face principal | 15641 |
| 1993-11-04 | 12359/222 | FDIC, in its Granite receiver/conservator/liquidating capacity, assigns the mortgage and note to State Street as trustee for a Ryland mortgage-security vehicle | no price/balance stated | 15642 |
| 2002-06-27 | 22333/213 | State Street says it received full payment and satisfaction and discharges 8177/24 | payoff unstated | 15643 |

The $156,000 face loan equals 80 percent of the deed's $195,000 stated price, leaving a mathematical $39,000 difference. The records reviewed do not identify the source of that difference, actual cash at closing, costs, interest rate, later balance, payoff payer or payoff funding. The FDIC assignment is a transfer of the lender's interest; it does not itself show missed payments or borrower distress. The discharge was recorded about five weeks after Hassull's deed to Gregory individually at 22123/80, but the records do not explain the timing.

Three original certificates clarify capacity while leaving beneficiaries undisclosed:

- **22123/77**, recorded May 23, 2002, is signed by both Gregory and Hicham. They certify that they are the sole Hassull trustees and that all adult beneficiaries directed them to sell, mortgage and encumber Unit 307. It immediately precedes the trust-to-Gregory deed. Finding **15638**.
- **27625/56**, executed February 25 and recorded February 27, 2004, is signed by both men. They call themselves incumbent trustees, say the trust remains in force and owns Unit 307, and say the adult competent beneficiaries consented to the related transfer. Along with the declaration's one-trustee clause, it explains how Gregory could sign the following deed alone; it does not show Hicham had ceased serving. Finding **15639**.
- **48873/282**, signed only by Gregory on August 14 and recorded August 31, 2017, calls Gregory the current trustee and says Hicham will serve as successor if Gregory fails or ceases to serve. The related deed 48873/284 was executed later, on August 30, and names and bears signatures from both men as trustees. Finding **15640** preserves the capacity discrepancy; the records reviewed do not resolve an appointment, resignation or effective date.

## Pinecrest Road financing

The five specified Pinecrest originals are now resolved, and lead **96320** is complete.

Mortgage 34623/310 was executed May 30, 2007. Six operative, signature and exhibit pages of seventeen were reviewed, so it remains `original_partial_review`. The fixed/adjustable-rate rider prints a final-payment date no later than June 1, 2037. Mortgage 34676/138 was executed June 11; all six pages were reviewed. Its $300,000 figure is a maximum aggregate principal limit, not evidence that Houssam drew the full amount. Both instruments use the same Lot 2 and Lot B description and cite the 2006 acquisition deed 32556/341. The first mortgage was recorded more than a year after that $1.3 million acquisition and is not contemporaneous purchase financing.

The two one-page releases link conclusively to their mortgages through exact book, page and document numbers. Each says MERS as holder “acknowledge[s] satisfaction” of the specified mortgage, without giving the prior balance, cash paid, payer or source. Release 34869/46 prints the underlying recording date as `04/06/2007`; that numeric form can represent June 4 under day/month formatting, so without an established convention it is treated as an ambiguity rather than a definite conflict with the official June 4 detail. Release 38241/147 prints `07/15/2007`, which remains a distinct conflict with the official June 15 detail. The exact identifiers resolve which mortgages were released.

The credit-line mortgage also calls itself subordinate to a prior $100,000 mortgage dated June 1, 2007 to Countrywide Home Loans Inc. The amount and timing resemble 34623/310, but that original is dated May 30 and names Countrywide Bank, F.S.B., with MERS as mortgagee. Because the subordinate clause gives no book/page, the instruments are not silently equated.

The 2010 Secretary certificate was recorded immediately before Pinecrest Road, LLC's deed to the Aveni buyers. It lists **Houssam Hassan** as the only manager and only person authorized to act concerning real property, and Anthony Summers plus Houssam as persons authorized to execute Corporations Division documents. That proves filed manager/authority capacities. It does not prove membership, equity shares or a separate personal title interest.

Miller, also known as Irene Sylvia Miller, and Esther Miller conveyed the same lots 1626 and 1625 and associated beach rights to Amine Ali Hassan for stated **$100,000**. The deed was executed June 2 and recorded June 10, 1981; page 80 identifies the house lot as 53 Beach Avenue. Amine later conveyed the same parcels to Hicham for stated $1 in 1984. Finding **15595** and lead **96318** were audit-corrected; finding **15651** and financial connection **6808** preserve the predecessor acquisition and later direct property transfer. Lead 96318 is complete. The records do not identify the 1981 purchase-money source, beneficial partners, reason for the nominal 1984 transfer or kinship, and the broader capital question is carried on lead **95686**.
