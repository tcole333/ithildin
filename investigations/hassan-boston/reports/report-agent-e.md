Completed initial wave on 2026-09-04. All findings are explicitly scoped to `hassan-boston`. This is bounded public-record research, not a certification of all litigation. No paid PACER purchase, records request, contact, or headless dispatcher was used.

## Main results

1. The 2021 Massachusetts AG consent judgment is an actual later outcome of the article's pending enforcement case. It permanently restricts Hicham's operation of Massachusetts public accommodations and imposes $220,000, with $110,000 suspended. It expressly avoids admission/adjudication of liability. The signed order, including the operative scanned pages, was OCRed and visually checked.
2. The family-property opinion provides a primary judicial account of Tarek's asserted interests and identifies an alleged December 14, 2000 family buyout agreement. The opinion assumes allegations true at the pleading stage; it does not prove the asserted equity or identify Tarek's father. The underlying agreement is more important for capital/ownership reconstruction than an inference from a shared surname.
3. Nader's complete initial bankruptcy schedules establish a specific operating-company balance sheet and documented codebtor relationships. They do not establish personal net worth or property-company insolvency. Later reopening/amendments mean the 2017 values must be dated, not described as final claims.
4. Tivoli litigation now bridges the property entity to the 2026 registry attachment independently read by the deeds agent. Three fully read judicial opinions establish awards against the LLC; the attachment is overlapping security and must not be added as another debt.

## Learnings

- The RECAP party index can miss a known litigant; combine exact document text and opinion searches with docket/party work and state sources.
- Docket `short_description` often carries the decisive event while `description` is empty. An available metadata event is not an available underlying PDF.
- Reopened corporate bankruptcies require later amended schedules/final account before calling initial figures final.
- Publicly reproduced trial-court opinions can supply a much better financial/ownership lead than broad personal-name matches; an original agreement still outranks an opinion summarizing allegations.
- The AG judgment's scanned operative pages illustrate why successful text extraction alone does not establish that the entire document was read.
- A concurrent `lead_tracker` schema probe blocked a final note with `database is locked`; parent identified an overlapping auto-lead preview. This is repository lock contention, not an external source/access limitation.
