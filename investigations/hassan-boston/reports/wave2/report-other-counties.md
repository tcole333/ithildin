## Access and Learnings

- Norfolk permits free marked copies; six originals were downloaded from links generated by individual public lookups. These are not certified copies. Stable manifest links lead to the document-image list; generated PDF names can be transient.
- Norfolk's Modify Search can preserve an old first-name term when the box is emptied. Two attempted entity/surname queries therefore still showed ABDEL*; these are marked invalid-scope in coverage and were rerun with explicit wildcard*. Do not treat them as useful negatives.
- Norfolk's registered-result Modify Search unexpectedly returned a recorded-land form. Subsequent registered queries used the explicit Search Land Court Records link and verified returned headings.
- Middlesex South public viewing/printing is free. The ordinary print workflow generated a browser PDF, but browserDOM did not expose the native download control. Direct retrieval of the already-observed image returned403; no bypass was attempted. A screenshot preserves the reviewed operative portion, with the stable book/page route for reopening.
- CUA native app selection stalled roughly13minutes and finally showed another research window; no native action was taken. This delayed additional coverage; the unexecuted items above are preserved rather than called negative. Papercut logged separately.
- Original language, not an index role label, governs lender/borrower capacity. Repeated surname-only no-match results must be interpreted against actual observed hyphenated indexing.
