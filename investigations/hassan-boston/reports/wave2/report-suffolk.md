The conveyed interest then automatically reverts to the LLC. The LLC expressly retains possession, operation, rents, mortgage/lien powers, transfer of its reversion and reserved rights, improvement rights, insurance and condemnation proceeds, permits and tax appeals. Keep the FY2026 assessor owner label as a separate dated observation.

For repeated rows covering three parcels, the 1993 consideration is the aggregate transaction amount; do not sum it three times. A declaration or trustee certificate is a separate event type and does not start a title-transfer interval by itself. Parcel IDs are joined from the established Boston assessment baseline; these originals describe addresses and legal parcels rather than supplying modern PIDs.

## Learnings

Critical handwritten sums, date digits and clauses must be enlarged before extraction. The original 1993 consideration was audit-corrected from an initial thumbnail misreading; the current finding, ledger and wave-2 exports use $1,925,000. The 2026 maximum term was verified at enlarged scale as June 30, 2055 before persistence. Registry index party roles and consideration codes can differ materially from original deeds. A linked original discharge can turn a speculative current-debt lead into a documented historical financing event.

Do not repeat the prior 524-second asset-bundle export. The supported fallback is precise registry locators, viewed page counts, exact excerpts and explicit artifact limitations.
