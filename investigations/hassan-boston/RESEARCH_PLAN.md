# Research plan — September 4, 2026

## Objective and competing explanations

Build a dated, sourced map of the six named people, business vehicles, Boston-area
property, secured financing and litigation. Test whether documented growth is
explained by retail earnings, ordinary mortgages/refinancing, outside partners,
family capital or other identifiable transactions. None is established at setup.

Expected reports: A–F (six). No case-specific corpus is configured. Each source
has one persistence owner. Agents may relay pivots to one another but should not
duplicate findings. Person coverage is checked across all six tracks; a failed
endpoint or access restriction is distinct from a zero-result search.

exact search scope, access gaps and prioritized next records. Parent reviews
