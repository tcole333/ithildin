## 17988/312 — deed, three pages read

Recorded January 15, 1993 at 13:47; document 357. Executed and acknowledged December 23, 1992. All pages 312–314 examined, with page 1 consideration and page 2 predecessor reference enlarged.

Boylston Boston Corporation conveys to “Hicham Ali Hassan, Abdul Rahman Ali Hassan and Zouhair Ali Hassan, Trustees of 400 Boylston Street Realty Trust” under a declaration dated January 15, 1993, recorded prior hereto. Page 1 handwritten consideration is **$1,925,000**, also spelled “One Million Nine Hundred Twenty Five Thousand Dollars.” The typed “Two Million Dollars ($2,000,000.00)” is struck through. This corrects the initial wave's mistaken thumbnail reading; finding 15578 and first-wave artifacts have been audit-corrected.

Three parcels: buildings 392 and 394 Boylston Street (page 1); 396–398 Boylston Street (page 1); 400 and 402 Boylston Street (page 2). Page 2: “For title see deed of Rosalind E. Gorin et al., Trustees dated May 7, 1992, recorded with Suffolk Deeds at Book 17463 Page 182.” Page 2 assigns the grantor's interests in existing leases. Page 3 identifies the acknowledging corporate president as Susan A. Feig. This is a recorded title transfer, not evidence of where the grantees sourced the purchase funds.

Official index references subsequent deed 56448/321 (2016). Source reference `SUFFOLK-DEEDS:17988/312`; finding 15578.

## 17988/306 — 400 Boylston Street Realty Trust declaration

Official index: agreement, January 15, 1993, 13:47, document 356, six pages. Indexed grantors: ALIHASSAN HICHAM A; ALIHASSAN ABDUL R; ALIHASSAN ZOUHAIR A; BOYLSTON STREET REALTY TRUST 400. Later reference 56448/325 certificate (2016).

Page 1 names Hicham Ali Hassan, Abdul Rahman Ali Hassan and Zouhair Ali Hassan as trustees. They declare $1.00 and additional acquired property/interests held for “the sole benefit of the individuals or entities listed in the Schedule of Beneficiaries in the proportions stated in said Schedule, which Schedule has this day been executed by the Beneficiaries and filed with the Trustees.” Section 1.1 calls it a nominee trust to hold record legal title. Section 2.1 permits any two trustees to act where there are three or more; one may act where there are two. Page 2 section 3.2 makes beneficiary decisions unanimous. Section 3.3 permits a trustee to be a beneficiary; that provision alone does not establish any named trustee's beneficial percentage.

Pages 4–5 govern termination, amendment and successor trustees: section 7.2 calls for an instrument signed by all beneficiaries and recorded trustee certificate/acceptance, with title vesting in succeeding trustees without a separate conveyance. ## 56448/325 — trustee certificate, two pages read

Recorded July 19, 2016 at 09:33:38, document 63968. Executed and acknowledged July 13, 2016 (page 2). Hicham Ali Hassan signs as trustee of 400 Boylston Street Realty Trust, created January 15, 1993 at 17988/306.

Page 1: “I am the only Trustee of the Trust.” The trust is in force and not revoked or terminated. “The undersigned Trustee has been authorized and directed by all of the beneficiaries of the Trust” to execute the quitclaim deed recorded herewith for less than $100 to 392-402 Boylston Street Realty, LLC covering three parcels, 392 and 394, 396–398, and 400 and 402 Boylston Street. The certificate supplies a sole-trustee assertion on July 13, 2016; it does not supply dates or instruments by which the other original trustees ceased holding office, or beneficiary identities/percentages.
