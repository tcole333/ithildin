## Prior-mortgage clause and discrepancy

Page 3 says the mortgage is subordinate to a prior mortgage dated “JUNE 01, 2007,” given to COUNTRYWIDE HOME LOANS INC as mortgagee, in an original amount of $100,000. The clause gives no book/page. Mortgage 34623/310 has a May 30, 2007 execution date and identifies Countrywide Bank, F.S.B. as lender and MERS as mortgagee/nominee. Because the dates and named mortgagee differ, this review does not silently equate the page-3 prior mortgage with 34623/310.

The $300,000 figure is a maximum line amount, not evidence that the full line was drawn, a later balance, or equity.
