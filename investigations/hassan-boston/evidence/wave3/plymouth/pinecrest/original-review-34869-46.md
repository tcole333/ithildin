## Numeric recording-date ambiguity and limit

The discharge prints the underlying mortgage’s recording date as “04/06/2007,” while the official detail record for document 56456 reports June 4, 2007. The numeric text can represent June 4 under day/month formatting; without an established convention for this printed field, it is treated as a format ambiguity rather than a definite conflict. The exact book, page and document number identify the released mortgage.

The operative excerpt is: “Book 34623, Page 310, Doc # 56456 acknowledge satisfaction of the same.” This establishes a release of that specified mortgage. It does not establish the cash paid, the balance immediately before release, or any broader payoff beyond the obligation and collateral identified in the instrument.
