# Pinecrest Road mortgage, release and authority follow-up

Status: bounded original-document review complete. Registry navigation was explicitly returned to the parent investigator after review.

## Scope

This follow-up covers recorded-land instruments 34623/310, 34676/138, 34869/46, 38184/288 and 38241/147 for the shared property key `plymouth-hingham-116-0-47`. It records mortgage borrower/lender/nominee roles, the exact mortgage identified by each discharge, and the corporate capacities certified before the 2010 deed. A discharge is not described as a payoff, and a mortgage face or line limit is not treated as a balance or equity measure.

## Findings

1. **Mortgage 34623/310 (document 56456).** The mortgage was executed May 30, 2007 by Houssam Ali Hassan as borrower/mortgagor in favor of Countrywide Bank, F.S.B.; MERS acts solely as nominee for the lender and as mortgagee. The reviewed original states $100,000 principal and a June 1, 2037 final-payment date. Exhibit A covers 4 Pinecrest Road, Lot 2 and Lot B, 129,679 square feet, citing deed 32556/341. The official record was entered June 4, 2007. Review was partial: pages 1, 10, 11, 12, 16 and 17 of 17.

2. **Line-of-credit mortgage 34676/138 (document 60943).** The six-page instrument was executed June 11, 2007 by Houssam as mortgagor/borrower for Countrywide Bank, F.S.B., with MERS as nominee/mortgagee, and recorded June 15. It secures a revolving home-equity line up to $300,000 against the same described parcel. All six pages were reviewed. Page 3 identifies a prior $100,000 mortgage dated June 1, 2007 to Countrywide Home Loans Inc., but gives no book/page. Its date and named mortgagee differ from mortgage 34623/310, so the records are not silently equated.

3. **Discharge 34869/46 (document 76368).** MERS, as holder, acknowledged satisfaction of mortgage 34623/310, document 56456. Roxanne Bermea executed the one-page instrument as Assistant Secretary on July 17, 2007; it was recorded July 27. Its printed underlying recording date, “04/06/2007,” can represent June 4 under day/month formatting. Without an established convention for that field, it is treated as a format ambiguity rather than a definite conflict with the official June 4 record. The exact book/page/document reference resolves the instrument link. This shows release of that mortgage, without stating the cash paid or prior balance.

4. **Secretary’s certificate 38184/288 (document 7939).** The Massachusetts Secretary of the Commonwealth certificate is dated January 21, 2010 and was recorded January 28 immediately before deed 38184/289. It reports Pinecrest Road, LLC’s October 29, 2007 organization, identifies Houssam Hassan as its only listed manager and only person authorized for real-property acts, and lists Anthony Summers and Houssam as authorized to execute Corporations Division filings. The certificate establishes those filed capacities, not LLC membership or equity.

5. **Discharge 38241/147 (document 12805).** MERS, as holder, acknowledged satisfaction of line-of-credit mortgage 34676/138, document 60943. Tracy Duran executed the one-page instrument as Assistant Secretary on February 5, 2010; it was recorded February 16. Its printed underlying recording date, “07/15/2007,” conflicts with the official June 15 record, but its exact identifiers resolve the link. This shows release of that specified obligation and collateral, without stating the drawn balance or cash paid.
