## Judgment and post-judgment entries

The public docket text reproduces a July 18, 2023 amended/corrected judgment entry stating:

> Judgment For: Tivoli Inc Judgment Against: 216-218 Newbury Street Realty LLC ... Interest Begins: 05/10/2021 ... Interest Rate: .18 Daily Interest Rate: .000493 Damages: Damage Amt: 58548.00 Crt Ord Atty Fee: 241620.30 Judgment Total: 323,201.90.

This is a judgment against the LLC. It does not state a judgment against Hicham Ali Hassan personally. The USD 323,201.90 docket total overlaps the USD 58,548 damages, USD 241,620.30 fees and interest components; those figures must not be added together again as separate debts.

The September 5, 2025 docket entry reproduces a memorandum and order dated July 25, 2025. It says the LLC's motion for relief from judgment was denied and Tivoli's request to clarify fees and interest was allowed in part:

> the judgment shall be amended to add an additional $100,000 in fees and expenses that 216-218 Newbury street Realty LLC shall pay to Tivoli Audio, Inc.

The USD 100,000 is an addition to the existing judgment. The later USD 405,000 writ of attachment recorded at Suffolk 72957/242 is an attachment cap against the LLC's real estate, not another damages award and not proof of the unpaid balance.
