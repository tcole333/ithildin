# Hassan family-business public docket review

Reviewed September 5, 2026 through the unauthenticated Trellis page reproducing Suffolk Superior Court docket **2084CV00531**, *Tarek Ali Hassan v. Hicham Ali Hassan et al.* The page identifies a February 26, 2020 filing date, Suffolk Superior Court, and a July 7, 2026 last refresh. Trellis is a secondary host reproducing court docket text; it is not the official Massachusetts court docket.

Case page: https://trellis.law/case/25025/2084cv00531/hassan-tarek-ali-vs-hassan-hicham-ali-et-al

## Disposition

The public docket reproduces an August 18, 2022 entry stating that the parties filed an August 17 stipulation dismissing the plaintiff's claims against the defendants with prejudice, without costs, and with all appeal rights waived, followed by Rule 58 judgment entry. It also reproduces an October 7, 2022 entry canceling a scheduled Rule 56 hearing because the case had settled. This corroborates the previously preserved public preview of the signed stipulation, finding #15579. It does not disclose settlement consideration, payment, beneficiary shares, or any adjudicated ownership allocation.

## Original complaint and agreement limit

The public original-complaint page is https://trellis.law/doc/57968280/original-civil-complaint-filed. Trellis exposes three preview images and text from only the opening complaint pages. The preview covers the parties and the complaint's allegations concerning three trusts, but it does not expose the December 14, 2000 agreement or the complaint exhibit that allegedly set out ownership interests. Download and print links require signup, so neither was used.

A focused review of public docket document titles found no separately labeled "December 14, 2000 Agreement" or "Exhibit A to Complaint." The public document at https://trellis.law/doc/270319978/exhibits-appendix-exhibit-a-exhibit-i is not the agreement: its preview begins with the court's December 2, 2021 discovery order. The docket also describes provisionally impounded material and disputes involving bank records. No impounded or access-restricted material was accessed.

The original agreement, complete complaint package and beneficiary schedules therefore remain unavailable in the reviewed public materials. The same-date December 14, 2000 Plymouth trustee removal and resignation instruments do not name this agreement and are not treated as proof of its terms or purpose.
