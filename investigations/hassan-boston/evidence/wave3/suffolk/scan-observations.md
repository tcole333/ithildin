## 18 Brimmer Street financing and foreclosure

### 19143/176–184 — mortgage, all nine pages read

Recorded June 20, 1994 at 15:15, document 567. Index consideration/face amount $618,000. The original is dated June 15, 1994. Alexander Randall, 5th and Cameron Hall, husband and wife, are borrowers; Interstate National Mortgage Corporation is lender. Page 1 states: “Borrower owes Lender the principal sum of Six Hundred Eighteen Thousand and no/100 Dollars (U.S. $618,000.00).” Security is 18 Brimmer Street. Page 6 is signed by both borrowers and checks an Adjustable Rate Rider. Pages 7–8 are the rider: initial annual rate 6.625%; first change July 1, 1995; changes every 12 months; index is the average interbank offered rates for six-month U.S. dollar deposits in the London market; margin 2.875 percentage points; one percentage point limit per change; lifetime ceiling 12.625%. Page 9 legal description is lot 5 on C. H. Davies's Plan of 5 lots on Brimmer Street, Suffolk Deeds book 913/page 218, referring for title to book 16167/page 262.

The index links assignment 19143/185, later assignment 19491/107, and release 24001/64. A mortgage face amount is not a later balance.

### 19143/185 — assignment, one page read

### 19491/107–108 — assignment, both pages read

### 21956/120–123 — foreclosure deed, all four pages read

Recorded December 2, 1997 at 13:25, document 846, indexed consideration $325,000. Institutional Asset LLC, holder of a mortgage from Cameron Hall and Alexander Randall, 5th through Chapter 11 trustee Janet E. Bostwick (case 97-10296-JNF), dated October 3, 1997 at 21879/264, conveyed to “ZOUHAIR ALI HASSAN, Trustee of Eighteen Brimmer Street Realty Trust u/d/t dated December 1, 1997” for $325,000. Page 1 says the property is “subject to a mortgage dated June 15, 1994, recorded with said Deeds in Book 19143, Page 176,” plus outstanding taxes, tax titles and liens. The printed deed is dated and acknowledged November 3, 1997. Because that date precedes the December 1 trust declaration named in the grantee capacity, the facial chronology is preserved as printed and remains unresolved.

Page 3 is the foreclosure affidavit. It says the mortgagor defaulted, the statutory notice ran in the Boston Globe, required notices were mailed, and Institutional Asset LLC sold at public auction to Zouhair as trustee for $325,000, “being the highest bid made therefor at said auction.” Page 4 is the published notice. It describes the scheduled sale for October 30, 1997 on the premises, subject to the first mortgage to Interstate National Mortgage Corporation originally $618,000, and calls for a $5,000 cashier's or bank treasurer's check at sale with balance in 30 days. The document does not state the then-outstanding senior mortgage balance, the trust's cash source, or any refinancing at acquisition.

### 21956/113–118 — declaration of trust, all six pages read

Recorded December 2, 1997 at 13:25, document 844. Zouhair Ali Hassan of North Reading declared the Eighteen Brimmer Street Realty Trust by an instrument executed December 1, 1997. Page 1 says the trust estate is held “solely as nominee, for the sole benefit of the individuals listed in the Schedule of Beneficial Interest in the proportions stated in said Schedule,” and says the schedule was filed with the trustee. The recorded six-page declaration does not identify those beneficiaries. Page 2 defines beneficiaries through that separate schedule and provides that decisions and actions, including amendments, appointment or removal of trustees, directions, notices, and document execution, must be made by all beneficiaries. Page 3 states that the trustee has no power to deal with the trust estate except as directed by all beneficiaries. Page 5 provides that appointment or removal of a trustee is effected through written beneficiary action followed by a recorded trustee certificate. This is record-title and governance evidence; it does not establish the unrecorded beneficial ownership.

## Evidence-boundary notes

The original page reviews above are not a complete title examination. A trustee signature or certificate establishes the stated record-title capacity for that instrument, not beneficial ownership. A mortgage amount is original face principal, not equity or an outstanding balance; continuation, assignment, termination and discharge rows carry no loan amount unless they state a new advance. Same-session recordings establish timing and related collateral but not settlement disbursements. Modern parcel IDs in the event export come from the separate Boston assessment baseline and are joined to the addresses and legal descriptions stated in these originals.
