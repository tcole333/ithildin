## Interpretation boundary

The agreement directly establishes Tarek Ali Hassan's sole-member status under the instrument dated October 2, 2014. The later filing title says the statutes were in force when deposited, but the attached English instrument remains dated 2014 and supplies no 2018 merger agreement, amendment, cap table, ownership percentage after closing or consideration. The 2019 board structure establishes manager authority, not the members or distribution recipients in 2019. No ownership or proceeds inference is made from the manager signatures.
