## Certificate of Merger — filing 201826378650

All three original pages were visually read in the registry's public PDF viewer.

* Filed August 9, 2018 at 4:00 p.m.; effective upon filing.
* Constituent `JORDAN MERGERSUB, LLC` is described as a Delaware LLC formed July 17, 2018, with an office `c/o Amazon.com, Inc., 410 Terry Avenue North, Seattle, WA 98109`.
* Jordan Mergersub merged **into** Concepts International LLC, and Concepts was the surviving Massachusetts LLC.
* The certificate says an Agreement and Plan of Merger had been duly adopted, approved and executed by the constituent entities. It says the executed plan was on file at Concepts' 4 Brattle Street, Suite 308, Cambridge office and that a member could request a copy. The agreement is not attached to this public filing. No contact or record request was made.
* The filing names Arun Rajan and Scott Schaefer as the surviving entity's managers, corporate-filing signers and real-property instrument signers.
* Tarek Ali Hassan signed the certificate for Concepts as `Duly Authorized` on August 9, 2018. That capacity does not itself establish post-closing membership or proceeds.
* Page 3 is the Secretary filing cover dated August 9, 2018.

The certificate does not identify a parent or ultimate equity owner of Jordan Mergersub, consideration, the form or percentage of any investment, seller recipients, or use of proceeds. Its Amazon c/o address is an explicit filing fact, not proof by itself of Amazon's ultimate ownership.
