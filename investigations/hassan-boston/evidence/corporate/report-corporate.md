Hachim is the initial president; Harriet is the initial treasurer and clerk. Page 2 authorizes **7,500 common shares without par value**. It does not state issued shares, stock subscriptions, actual paid-in capital, investor contributions or where funding came from. The $125 fee on page 5 is a filing fee. Finding #15571 preserves this narrow distinction; screenshots of all pages and exact transcriptions are in `report-hicham-supplement.md`.

## Learnings

Entity IDs and record text must control matching. A single name can appear in several legal-entity histories, and one legal entity can appear under several historical names. Officer searches are useful candidate generators but have demonstrated omissions and honorific/tokenization artifacts. Current summaries should not supply presumed historical tenure dates. The source can retain dissolution flags alongside later reports, requiring explicit status uncertainty. Reported real-property authority is separate from recorded title; registered-agent/officer roles are separate from equity.
