The B.I./GEO incumbent channel

Confirmed · FPDS-NG

The single most important finding. ICE ran a $1.44B competition for skip tracing and then bought the service, at 4.5× the scale, from the company that already monitors the same people — through a pre-priced line item inside B.I.'s sole-source ISAP V electronic-monitoring task order.

08

Refuted / ruled out

Do not re-derive

Dead theories, recorded so they are not re-opened. Each was checked against primary data and did not survive.

✗Separation-of-duties failure

Single-user create-and-approve is the norm at ICE office 70CDCR — 37.4% of 1,249 baseline actions, 48.7% contemporaneous, 42.5% on an independent pre-program sample. Both programs sat below the surrounding rate. It tracks warranted-vs-unwarranted role, not program. FPDS fields are data-entry, not the FAR approval chain.

JABYAD7012 100% solo on non-program work · Somppi solo since 2018 · Boudreaux clean date-break
