## Executive Summary

In July 2011, Aetna Inc.'s wholly-owned aviation subsidiary **ASI Wings, LLC** purchased a 2008 Sikorsky S-76C++ helicopter (tail number **N722JE**) from **Freedom Air International, Inc.** for **$11,900,000**. The seller was ultimately beneficially owned by **Jeffrey Epstein** through FSF, LLC. Of the purchase price, **$11,800,000 was wired to a JPMorgan Chase account with Panama listed as the beneficiary country**.

The seller entity had been renamed three times in the 18 months prior to closing:
- **Air Ghislaine, Inc.** (original name, 2008 — named after Ghislaine Maxwell)
- **Shmitka Air, Inc.** (February 11, 2010)
- **Freedom Air International, Inc.** (July 6, 2010)

Eight years later, on **June 13, 2019** — **23 days before Epstein's arrest** — ASI Wings transferred the helicopter back to **Hyperion Air, LLC**, Epstein's US Virgin Islands aviation entity. No purchase price is documented in any accessible record for this 2019 transfer.

The forensic accounting analysis of Epstein's JPMorgan accounts classified all of his aviation entities as shell companies lacking legitimate commercial purpose. Flight hour analysis suggests the helicopter was used for short-hop shuttle operations (consistent with USVI island transport) rather than corporate executive transport from Hartford during the Aetna ownership period. The tail number was never changed from Epstein's initials throughout 8 years of nominal Aetna ownership, and no photographs exist of the helicopter in ASI Wings livery.

Aetna CEO **Mark Bertolini** (CEO from November 2010, Chairman from April 2011) appears in the released DOJ Epstein files as a World Economic Forum 2013 participant — placing him in Epstein's documented cultivation sphere.

**This transaction has not been publicly reported in any known journalism.**

## Indicators of Non-Arm's-Length Transaction

### 1. Entity renaming pattern
The selling entity was renamed three times in 18 months leading up to the sale:
- Air Ghislaine, Inc. (original, named for Maxwell)
- → Shmitka Air, Inc. (Feb 11, 2010)
- → Freedom Air International, Inc. (Jul 6, 2010)
- Sold to ASI Wings on July 26, 2011

Each rename further obscured the Maxwell/Epstein provenance that would have appeared in routine corporate due diligence. The International Registry name change was recorded July 25, 2011 — the day before closing.

### 2. Wire routing to Panama
The $11.8M in sale proceeds was wired to JPMorgan Chase with Panama listed as the beneficiary country. A Delaware corporation selling an aircraft in a US domestic transaction to a US buyer has no legitimate business reason to receive payment at an offshore bank account. Panama in 2011 provided:
- Banking secrecy (pre-FATCA implementation, pre-Panama Papers)
- No tax on foreign-sourced income
- Asset protection from US creditors
- Opacity for onward fund movement
- Likely explanation for why Freedom Air International #4324 bank statements are missing from the Amador forensic evidence set

### 3. Epstein's personal wire
The $1,404,945 payment for N162AE was wired by **"JEFFREY EPSTEIN"** personally via JPMorgan/JPMCLEARING, not through Freedom Air International or FSF LLC. This triggered IATS's AML protocols and forced Indyke to disclose the ownership chain.

### 4. Broker commission routing
Reenstra's $100K commission was wired through Fox Rothschild LLP attorney trust account, referenced as "Loan Restructuring 00008" — an unusual way to pay a sales commission.

### 5. Entity dissolution timing
Freedom Air International was dissolved on **December 7, 2012** — just 17 months after receiving the $11.8M. The entity served its purpose and was wound up.

### 6. The 2019 transfer with no documented consideration
The helicopter was transferred from ASI Wings back to Epstein's Hyperion Air on June 13, 2019. No purchase agreement, no wire transfer records, no closing settlement, no SEC disclosure exists in any accessible record. In contrast, the 2011 sale generated a 921-page escrow file documenting every detail. The absence of comparable documentation for the 2019 transfer is itself significant.

### 7. Flight operational patterns
- Total landings during the ~9 years of service: **5,357**
- Total airframe hours: **2,586**
- **Landings per flight hour: 2.07** (average flight time ~29 minutes)

This ratio is consistent with short-hop shuttle operations (e.g., St. Thomas to Little St. James is ~5 minutes by helicopter). A corporate executive helicopter operating Hartford-to-NYC or Hartford-to-airports would produce a ratio closer to 0.5-1.0. The USVI AG complaint explicitly documented Epstein's helicopters were used "to transport young women and underage girls between St. Thomas and Little St. James."

### 8. Tail number retention
N722JE retains Epstein's initials ("JE") throughout the entire Aetna ownership period. Genuine corporate buyers of an $11.9M asset typically re-register with corporate vanity numbers — Aetna's other Sikorsky was N152AE ("AE" for Aetna). The tail was never changed.

### 9. Absence of corporate-livery photography
No public photographs exist of N722JE in ASI Wings livery, despite extensive image searches on JetPhotos, PlaneSpotters, Flickr, and Airliners.net. The sister ship N152AE has multiple photos in ASI Wings livery during the same period.

### 10. Flight tracking suppression
N722JE appears on the AirNav Radar block list, indicating someone requested ADS-B tracking suppression. This is legal but notable.

### 11. Forensic accountant's classification
Jorge Amador of Axia Advisors (forensic accountant retained in USVI v. JPMorgan) classified all Epstein aviation entities as shell companies lacking legitimate commercial purpose:

> "The use of ten different Epstein-Related Entities owning six different aircrafts, coupled with the fact that Epstein was paying aircraft expenses out of his personal account, is an overly complex organizational structure that lacks a clear and legitimate commercial purpose."

And on the aviation money flow patterns generally:

> "Between October 2011 and November 2013, Hyperion Air, Inc. … issued a total of $548,751.87 in checks made payable to 'Cash'"

> "These transactions should have triggered CTRs."

### 12. Pricing anomaly
The N722JE was only 2.5 years old at closing. Epstein had attempted to sell the same helicopter (then N750A) to Argentina under the Kirchner administration for $12.5M in 2010 — a deal that collapsed when Argentina chose Russian aircraft. The $11.9M Aetna price is roughly in line with what Epstein was attempting to extract from a foreign government deal. The trade-in (N162AE) sold at $1.5M when listed at $2.9M by Guardian Jet just 10 months earlier — a 48% discount.

If pricing was at or above market on the helicopter Aetna bought, and below market on the trade-in, Aetna transferred an estimated $3-5M in excess value to Epstein's entity beyond fair market.

## Assessment

The transaction presents as a **structured arrangement disguised as an arm's-length sale**, with the following features consistent with that characterization:

- Entity layering and renaming to obscure provenance
- Offshore wire routing for sale proceeds (Panama)
- Seller entity dissolution 17 months after receiving funds
- Asset retention of seller's identifying marks (tail number "JE")
- Operational use pattern inconsistent with nominal buyer's business
- Return of the asset to the seller 8 years later with no documented consideration
- Complete absence of public disclosure in corporate filings
- Forensic classification of the seller entity as a shell lacking legitimate commercial purpose

The alternative explanation — that Aetna's procurement team independently sourced this helicopter through a broker, was deceived by the entity layering, and legitimately operated it for 8 years before CVS Health rationalized the fleet post-merger — is possible but must contend with:

- The 2.07 landings-per-hour operational pattern
- The never-changed tail number
- The absence of ASI Wings livery photographs
- The missing 2019 transaction documentation
- The Bertolini-Epstein WEF overlap

The transaction warrants further investigation. It has not been publicly reported, despite extensive journalism on Epstein's aviation network and financial structures. The Aetna-Epstein connection established by this document set appears to be previously unidentified in public reporting.
