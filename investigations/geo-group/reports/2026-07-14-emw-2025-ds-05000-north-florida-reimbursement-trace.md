## Executive verdict

The public evidence now supports an exact **state-paid** amount: Florida recorded five fiscal-year 2025-26 operational disbursements totaling **$28,897,523.51** to **GEO|CDR BAKER, LLC** from the Emergency Preparedness and Response Fund's illegal-migration appropriation. This materially converts PO-010844 from a **$100,357,654.76 authorized ceiling** into documented cash payments. The paid total equals **28.7945%** of that ceiling, leaving **$71,460,131.25** of authorized value not shown as paid in this ledger.

The public evidence does **not** allocate any portion of DHS/FEMA award **EMW-2025-DS-05000** or its **$58,292,145 award-wide outlay** to those vendor payments, PO-010844, mission 00354, or the North Florida facility. The federal-to-state edge and the state-to-vendor edge are each proven, but the public records recovered here do not join them at the transaction or cost-allocation level.

The defensible chain is therefore:

```text
DHS/FEMA --$608.4m cost-reimbursement award--> FDEM
                                              [allocation gap]
FDEM illegal-migration fund --$28,897,523.51 state payments--> GEO-CDR BAKER
FDEM --PO-010844, $100,357,654.76 authorized--> GEO-CDR BAKER / North Florida
```

This does **not** yet add a proven DHS pass-through context to the six-chain Tier 2 topology. It does establish a specific state-mediated detention-procurement context and a precise set of state documents that a later FEMA cost schedule or reimbursement request could match.

## FEMA award structure and initial holds

The official FEMA award package appears as Exhibit 3 to Eleventh Circuit case 25-12873, Document 91. The [filed primary document](https://earthjustice.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2026-01-07-91-25-12873-motion-to-supplement-the-record-.pdf) establishes:

- Award number EMW-2025-DS-05000; FDEM recipient; UEI N6KVCUU7LC61.
- Effective date September 30, 2025; performance period June 1, 2025 through July 31, 2027.
- Federal amount $608,400,000; assistance arrangement **COST REIMBURSEMENT**.
- Object-class budget: $608,400,000 in “Other,” with $0 in “Contractual.”
- FDEM had to submit a revised budget with a burden rate per detainee/night and a detailed cost breakout.
- Article 55 placed the full amount on an environmental-planning hold for the “Per Diem” activity.
- Article 56 separately prohibited FDEM from obligating, expending, or drawing funds until FEMA approved a detailed cost breakdown and justification.

The package says the program would relieve CBP short-term holding overcrowding, further DHS immigration-enforcement plans, and complement ICE operational priorities. It does not name Baker, North Florida, GEO-CDR BAKER, PO-010844, or mission 00354. The package proves program scope and reimbursement method, not facility allocation.

1. The approved revised budget or award amendment identifying Baker/North Florida and its burden rate.
2. The FEMA-GO payment request underlying the $58,292,145 drawdown, including cost-category and facility schedules.
3. FDEM reimbursement workpapers matching statewide documents D6000294462, D6000330868, D6000512228, D6000601410, or D6000601421.

5. SF-425, grant ledger, cost-allocation table, or FEMA approval that maps a North Florida per-diem period to the five payments.

Until one of those records is obtained, **$28,897,523.51 is a state-paid vendor amount—not a FEMA-funded amount**.
