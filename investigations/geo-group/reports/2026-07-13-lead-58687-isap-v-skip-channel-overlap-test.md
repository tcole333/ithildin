# Lead #58687: ISAP V / multi-award skip-tracing channel comparison

**Research date:** 2026-07-13  
**Profile / thread:** `geo-group` / 109, Electronic Monitoring & ISAP  
**Scope:** public Layer-1 procurement evidence; no claim of favoritism, duplicate billing, noncompliance, or absence from missing records

## Disposition

The reviewed public record establishes ordinary parallel allocation **inside** the `26-SOL-DCR-01` vehicle, but it does not resolve case-level overlap **between** that vehicle and ISAP V pricing item 42.

- Every one of the 14 prime vehicle holders received one visible first task order. The orders total $19,032,607; BI received $1,624,500, or 8.54%, the fifth-largest allocation. Finding #12487.
- Thirteen orders were obligated on December 16 or 18, 2025. Twelve later received substantially identical 60-day, zero-dollar extensions on March 10 or 11, 2026. Finding #12488.
- ICE's final Q&A said the Government would track target allocation and that each vendor would receive unique cases with no vendor working the same case. That rule is explicit but bounded to vendors within the vehicle. Finding #12489.
- The two channels have different disclosed baseline scopes. ISAP V covers enrolled ATD participants, case management, monitoring and residence verification; the skip vehicle covers an approximately 1.5-million-person docket using government-furnished files, commercial verification and possible physical observation. Finding #12492.
- The missing ISAP V item 42 and first-task SOWs prevent a case-population, unit, deliverable, invoice or identifier-level crosswalk. The public documents neither prove overlap nor affirmatively exclude ISAP participants from the 1.5-million-case docket.

The complete 61-row parent/task/action ledger is [bi-ice-skip-channel-task-allocation-ledger-2026-07-13.csv](bi-ice-skip-channel-task-allocation-ledger-2026-07-13.csv).

## Scope, population, unit and data comparison

| Dimension | ISAP V disclosed baseline | `26-SOL-DCR-01` disclosed baseline | Cross-channel result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Population | Aliens eligible for and enrolled in ATD/ISAP; not subject to mandatory detention and in removal proceedings | Approximately 1.5 million individuals represented by Government-furnished case files | Skip documents do not say whether ISAP participants are included or excluded |
| Core service | Case management, office/home visits, residence verification, GPS/biometric monitoring | Automated/manual location research, commercial-data verification and optional physical observation | Different baseline service models; item 42 scope remains undisclosed |
| Residence work | For enrolled participants; within 75 miles of ISAP C/G sites; triggered after enrollment or reported address change | Nationwide best-address research; residence prioritized, employment used if residence cannot be verified | Operational distinctions are documented, but item 42 cannot be mapped to either model |
| Unit | Public pre-award ISAP pricing has 39 items using each/day/month; no public item 42 | Firm-fixed per case; one individual is one subject/file and may have multiple addresses | Item 42 unit and quantity remain unknown |
| Government inputs | Full ISAP participant case record, enrollment/service/EARM IDs and supervision history | Name, DOB, country of birth, last known address, photo, phone and email, with incomplete files possible | Several biographical fields overlap; no common-record identifier rule is public |
| Contractor workflow | Contractor case system and ICE-connected program operations | Vendor-owned research tools; no direct Government-system access | Different disclosed access model |
| Output | ISAP case updates and required reports in contractor/ICE systems | Excel, CSV or other secure structured exports with daily/status updates and supporting documentation | No public handoff or shared integration record between channels |
| Duplicate control | No item-42-specific public rule | ICE tracks assignments and bars overlap among vehicle vendors | No public cross-channel duplicate-control rule |

The case and pipeline facts are preserved in finding #12490. No public source reviewed supplies the population crosswalk needed to determine whether item 42 served enrolled ISAP participants, a non-ISAP docket, a transition batch, or another subset.

## Disconfirmation results

The investigation explicitly searched for evidence of ordinary separation:

1. **Allocation across peers:** confirmed. All 14 holders received first tasks; BI held 8.54% of first-task obligations.
2. **Same-case control inside the vehicle:** confirmed in the final Q&A. ICE said cases would be unique per vendor.
3. **Separate task accounting:** confirmed in the solicitation through independent COR, funding, invoicing and reporting requirements.
4. **Distinct publicly stated baseline functions:** confirmed, but insufficient. ISAP V and the vehicle describe different base programs; undisclosed item 42 prevents scope matching.
5. **Explicit cross-channel population exclusion or transfer:** not found. No reviewed document says the 1.5-million-case docket excludes ISAP participants, identifies item-42 cases, or describes a transition.

These results disconfirm BI-exclusive allocation within the multi-award vehicle and document ordinary parallel first-tasking. They do **not** resolve the narrower cross-channel overlap question.

## Public-record boundary and precise FOIA targets

Exact-ID searches of ICE public pages, the ICE FOIA library, SAM and indexed official pages did not locate the signed post-award packages. Finding #12493 records this as a bounded public-access gap, not an absence or compliance conclusion.

5. ICE program allocation logs showing which target identifiers went to each vehicle vendor and any cross-check against ISAP V item 42, with PII redacted but aggregate counts and data dictionaries retained.
