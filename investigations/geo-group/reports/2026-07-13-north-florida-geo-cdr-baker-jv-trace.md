# North Florida Detention Facility: GEO-CDR BAKER entity and funding trace

**Research date:** 2026-07-13  
**Lead:** 57834  
**Scope:** Resolve the unnamed North Florida management entity in GEO's SEC disclosure; identify its public corporate record, state procurement instrument, facility/operator roles, ICE/DHS relationship, and unresolved ownership and payment questions.

## Executive finding

The entity is **GEO-CDR BAKER, LLC**, an active Florida domestic LLC, document **L25000395700**, filed on **August 28, 2025**. That is the same date on which GEO says it agreed with another contractor to form a management entity for the state-owned, 1,310-bed North Florida Detention Facility. Florida's Division of Emergency Management later issued **PO-010844** to the entity on October 17, 2025. The order shows an authorized total of **$100,357,654.76**, emergency procurement method **O2**, mission **00354**, and the line descriptions “Monthly rate” and “Mobilization.” The PO total is not evidence that the full amount was invoiced or paid.

The entity's name and managers establish a GEO/CDR-side structure: the 2025 record listed GEO executives George C. Zoley and J. David Donahue and CDR-linked executives Carlos Duart and Tina Vidal-Duart. The April 29, 2026 annual report no longer lists Donahue as a current manager. The available Florida corporate summary, GEO SEC filings, PO page, and federal award record do **not** disclose the exact legal CDR member, ownership percentages, capital contributions, or profit allocation. Carlos Duart's role at CDR Maguire makes that company a strong affiliation lead; it does not prove that CDR Maguire Inc. is the legal co-owner.

## Facility, buyer, and operator roles

Florida identified the site as the former **Baker Correctional Institution** in Sanderson. GEO calls it the state-owned **North Florida Detention Facility** in Baker County. Its facility address is **20706 US Highway 90 West, Sanderson, FL 32087**. It should not be confused with the Baker County Detention Center in Macclenny.

The public records support the following allocation of roles:

- **Owner/site:** State of Florida; former Florida Department of Corrections institution.
- **State procurement buyer and bill-to:** Florida Division of Emergency Management, 2555 Shumard Oak Boulevard, Tallahassee.
- **Management supplier:** GEO-CDR BAKER, LLC.
- **Facility use:** GEO grouped the North Florida agreement among four 2025 contracts to house ICE detainees. Florida described the site as a detention-and-removal facility and said its costs would be reimbursed by federal partners.
- **Federal funding recipient recovered:** FDEM, not GEO-CDR BAKER.

Sources: [Florida governor announcement, 2025-08-14](https://www.flgov.com/eog/news/press/2025/governor-ron-desantis-announces-expansion-floridas-capacity-detain-and-deport), [GEO 2025 results/8-K exhibit](https://investors.geogroup.com/static-files/9f91bb8b-51ef-439e-ad13-b754c879ea7d).
