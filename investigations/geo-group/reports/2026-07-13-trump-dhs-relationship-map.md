## Scope and evidentiary boundary

This source-gathering pass maps documented relationships among The GEO Group and its affiliates, Trump campaign/transition/administration personnel, and DHS/ICE officials across both Trump administrations. It prioritizes official financial disclosures, Senate Lobbying Disclosure Act filings, DHS/ICE records, White House releases, DOJ biographies, and SEC filings. It classifies edges without treating proximity, lobbying, a financial holding, or a personnel move as proof of influence.

“Direct” means the named person and organization are joined by the record itself. “Institutional” means a filing names an organization or government office but does not identify the individual contacted. “Professional” denotes agency-side work. “Second-degree” requires an intermediary. Dates described as “current” are bounded by the retrieval date; a current web biography is not a substitute for an appointment instrument.

## Executive findings

1. **Thomas Homan has a documented direct compensated relationship with GEO Care and Guidepost Solutions.** His certified 2025 new-entrant OGE Form 278e lists both as sources of more than $5,000 in annual compensation for consulting services during the reporting period. The form does not disclose exact amounts, dates, scopes, contracts, or the legal suffix of “GEO Care.” It does not show that Homan’s Guidepost work involved GEO subsidiary B.I. Incorporated. Finding #12505; connections #6374–#6375.

2. **David Venturella is a current ICE leadership official after a long GEO tenure.** ICE’s current leadership page identifies him as “Senior Official Performing the Duties of the Director” and says he was a GEO Group senior vice president from 2012 through 2023. The page does not state his current appointment date or disclose any ethics waiver, recusal, screening arrangement, or procurement limitation. Finding #12506; connections #6378–#6379.

3. **The first Trump transition/administration had multiple documented personnel overlaps with later GEO roles.** An official ICE transition packet identifies Matthew Albence and Thomas Homan as subject-matter experts clearing an agency transition response and Daniel Ragsdale as approving official. It also states Ragsdale became acting ICE director on January 20, 2017; GEO says he joined the company in July 2017. Trump White House records separately identify Albence as performing ICE director duties in September 2019 and record Homan’s January 2017 acting-director appointment. Findings #12510–#12513, with existing GEO findings #12385–#12386 and #12456–#12457.

4. **GEO lobbying accounts included named people with Trump-network ties, but the Checkmate record is family-mediated rather than a single-person campaign-to-lobbying edge.** Ballard Partners’ Q3 2019 GEO filing named Pamela Bondi and Justin Sayfie as lobbyists, reported their Trump transition/commission positions, and listed DHS and the White House Office among contacted entities. Checkmate’s 2025 registration and amended Q1 2026 filing named **Christopher LaCivita Jr.** on detention-center-contract lobbying. Checkmate identifies Jr. as its Public Affairs Director; Axios identifies him as the son of Trump 2024 campaign adviser **Chris LaCivita**. The amended filing listed the White House Office but does not say which lobbyist contacted which office or official. Finding #12710 supersedes the collapsed identity framing in #12509.

5. **Trump’s official 2026 annual OGE disclosure reports a household-reportable GEO equity interest and transactions.** The annual form lists “GEO GROUP INC NEW REIT” at $50,001–$100,000 and $1,001–$2,500 in dividends, with ten purchases and four sales during 2025. A May 2026 periodic transaction report lists a March 4, 2026 GEO purchase of $15,001–$50,000 and marks late notification. OGE Parts 6 and 7 may cover the filer, spouse, or dependent children, while the public form suppresses family-member identity. The edge is therefore **Trump disclosure/household-reportable financial interest → GEO**, not a claim that Donald Trump personally selected or beneficially owned every reported position. Findings #12514–#12517.

## Chronology and relationship classes

### Transition and first administration

- **Pamela Bondi — direct lobbying and political-role overlap.** Ballard’s Q3 2019 GEO LD-2 named Bondi and reported her 2016–2017 presidential-transition-team and 2017 opioid-commission positions. The same filing covered immigration, homeland security, and criminal justice and listed DHS and the White House Office. DOJ says Bondi joined the White House in 2019 and became attorney general in 2025. This establishes overlapping disclosed roles, not a specific contact or decision. Finding #12507.
- **Justin Sayfie — direct lobbying and appointment overlap.** The same filing named Sayfie as a GEO lobbyist and White House Fellows commissioner. A Trump White House release had announced the president’s intent to appoint him. Finding #12508.
- **Daniel Ragsdale — direct revolving-door chronology.** ICE’s transition packet says Ragsdale became acting director on January 20, 2017. Homan replaced him as acting director after Trump’s January 30 appointment. GEO’s proxy says Ragsdale joined GEO in July 2017. The available primary records do not give Ragsdale’s exact ICE departure date or connect the move to a particular procurement. Findings #12512, #12386, and #12457.
- **Matthew Albence — direct government role, later GEO employment.** The ICE packet identifies Albence as an ERO assistant director clearing a transition response; a September 2019 Trump White House briefing identifies him as ICE deputy director and the official performing director duties. Existing SEC evidence places his GEO start on September 1, 2022 and discloses $137,531 paid in 2022 to his GrindStone consulting firm for pre-employment services. Findings #12510, #12513, #12385, and #12456.
- **Thomas Homan — direct appointment and professional transition role.** The ICE packet names Homan as an agency expert clearing transition material. A Trump White House statement records his appointment as acting ICE director in January 2017. A current White House release calls him Border Czar. Findings #12510–#12511.

The transition packet proves agency-side clearance and approval of responses. It does **not** identify Albence, Homan, or Ragsdale as campaign or transition-team members.

### Second administration and current roles

- **Homan — direct administration and paid-consulting edges.** His official OGE form identifies him as a non-career DHS border-czar appointee and separately lists GEO Care and Guidepost as consulting compensation sources. Exact service dates and whether the work continued after appointment are not disclosed in the cited sections. Findings #12505 and #12511.
- **Venturella — direct GEO-to-ICE leadership edge.** ICE’s biography alone proves GEO employment from 2012–2023 and his current acting-director-level title. Primary appointment and ethics records remain missing. Finding #12506; follow-up lead #58985.
- **LaCivita family/Checkmate — family-mediated campaign relationship; institutional White House contact disclosure.** Checkmate registered for GEO effective August 13, 2025 on “contracts related to detention centers,” naming Christopher LaCivita Jr. Its amended Q1 2026 LD-2 reported $80,000, again named Jr., and listed the White House Office. Jr.'s father Chris LaCivita—not Jr.—was the Trump campaign adviser. The engagement ended May 4, 2026 according to the complete six-filing reconciliation in #12718; #12719 records that the contacted-entity fields never list DHS or ICE. Findings #12710 and corrected #12509 preserve separate father and son nodes.
- **Trump household-reportable GEO position — direct disclosure edge, owner attribution unresolved.** The official OGE annual and periodic reports establish the reported asset and trades but not which covered household member owned or directed them. The SEC reporting-threshold context in #12517 does not contradict a small OGE-reported interest. No procurement or policy inference follows from the disclosure alone. Findings #12514–#12517.

### Julie Myers Wood and Guidepost

The primary evidence located in this pass does not place Julie Myers Wood in a Trump administration role. Existing SEC findings show that she headed ICE in the George W. Bush administration, that Guidepost Solutions had a consulting agreement with GEO subsidiary B.I. Incorporated before her 2014 GEO board appointment, and that she led Guidepost. Homan’s OGE form creates a separate direct Homan–Guidepost compensated-consulting edge. Thus the Wood-to-Trump/Homan path is second-degree through Guidepost; it is not evidence that Wood participated in Homan’s engagement or that his work concerned B.I. Findings #12387–#12388, #12450, and #12505.

## DHS component coverage

- **ICE:** strong primary coverage for Homan, Ragsdale, Albence, Venturella, Wood, transition-response roles, and current leadership.
- **DHS headquarters/White House:** Senate LDA filings identify DHS and the White House Office as institutional contacts on Ballard’s 2019 GEO account. Checkmate’s amended Q1 2026 filing identifies the White House Office, HHS/ACF, and BOP depending on issue code.
- **CBP, USCIS, FEMA, FPS, TSA, Secret Service, and other DHS components:** this bounded pass did not verify direct GEO personnel edges. That is a documentation boundary, not proof of absence. DHS-wide procurement and personnel review should test these components separately.

## Disconfirmation and quality checks

- A retry after the Senate LDA rate-limit failure recovered 36 GEO filings for 2024; the initial empty file was not treated as a negative result (papercut #814).
- FARA searches for GEO Group, Bondi, Homan, and LaCivita returned no matching registrant or foreign-principal rows. The CLI misreported empty response containers as one result (papercut #818); no FARA conclusion beyond the queried names was drawn.
- CourtListener keyword searches returned broad GEO litigation and false-positive result sets; manual title review did not establish an additional probative Homan, Venturella, Bondi, or LaCivita relationship edge.
- FEC donor/employer pulls were retained as discovery material, but common-name and identity-resolution problems prevent them from being used here without address/employer/committee verification. Campaign-finance attribution remains a separate workstream.
- ProPublica’s disclosure index was used to locate Homan’s record, but the certified government form and OGE instructions are the evidence. Duplicate hosts are not corroboration.
- Lobbying disclosure government-entity fields do not identify the contacted individual and do not prove a meeting, influence, procurement intervention, or policy outcome.
