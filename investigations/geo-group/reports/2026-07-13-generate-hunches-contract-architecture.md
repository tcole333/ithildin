## Candidate patterns tested and filtered

### 1. Guaranteed minimums cluster around a common share of capacity

**Result: rejected for insufficient comparable contexts and no observed cluster.**

Tacoma is the only GEO federal-contract context in the reviewed records with a same-record numeric capacity and minimum: 1,181 of 1,579 beds, or 74.8% (#12647–#12648). The non-GEO Torrance comparator supplies 714/842 and later 505/842, or 84.8% and 60.0%, but both values belong to one chain (#12653). LaSalle supplies a 0% local-scope control because its operating agreement says neither the State nor LEDD guaranteed a minimum across 1,160 beds (#12657); the missing federal IGSA could differ.

Golden State and Mesa Verde disclose numeric minima but not compatible capacities. Adelanto and Desert View disclose minimum tiers, but their above-minimum tier ceilings are not capacity definitions and were not converted into capacities. Aurora and South Texas redact their minimum quantities. The record therefore lacks three independent, same-scope GEO contexts with capacity and minimum defined in the same record.

### 2. Low utilization creates recurring paid-unused-capacity exposure

**Result: rejected as one period-total GEO dollar case and duplicate of #335/#336.**

Golden State is the only GEO case with a period-compatible numeric minimum, occupancy, and primary-source unused-capacity payment: 560 minimum beds, average daily population 136, 424 average unused guaranteed beds, and approximately $25.3 million paid for unused bed space (#12398, #12642, #12644).

The evidence supports a bounded Golden State fact, not a recurring cross-facility dollar pattern.

### 3. Contract vehicle determines idle-capacity risk

**Result: near-miss; rejected for an asymmetric and incomplete comparison.**

Three independent direct-prime contexts disclose numeric minimum architecture: Golden/Mesa under `70CDCR20D00000008`, Adelanto/Desert View under `70CDCR20D00000009`, and historical Tacoma. Aurora and South Texas add redacted guarantee-CLIN architecture.

The usable IGSA comparison is thinner. Joe Corley reserves 1,050 beds for ICE exclusive use (#12627) but says ICE is financially liable only for actual detainee days (#12629). LaSalle's local agreement expressly disclaims a State/LEDD minimum (#12657), but the federal IGSA is missing. Karnes requires funded orders but does not disclose the payment basis; Folkston, Clearfield, and Evangeline lack complete federal/local term sets.

A real vehicle effect would predict that complete federal IGSAs and local operator agreements repeatedly place unused-capacity liability differently from matched direct primes after controlling for facility, era, readiness, and ownership. The current record has only two usable IGSA comparators and one is scope-limited, so the innocent explanation—facility-specific and historical variation plus missing documents—cannot be separated.

Existing leads #60026, #60206, and #60208 already request the decisive task orders, guarantees, occupancy, invoices, and downstream terms. A new lead would duplicate them.
