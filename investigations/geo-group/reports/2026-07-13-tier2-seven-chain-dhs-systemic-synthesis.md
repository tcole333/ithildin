## Executive verdict

The verified denominator is now **seven independent DHS public-prime/private-operator detention chains**: the six ICE chains synthesized in finding `#12668`, plus the CBP → County of Val Verde → GEO chain established in findings `#12711`–`#12714`. East Hidalgo, Coastal Bend, Brooks County, and Maverick remain **four direct-GEO CBP comparators**. They are not added to the public-prime denominator.

The Val Verde sequence is a useful but bounded natural experiment. CBP first recorded a direct GEO award for detention space, fully deobligated it on 2025-03-21 with the instruction to replace it with another PIID, and began a County of Val Verde award for detention space on that same date. Independent county, facility, SEC, and operator evidence preserves the route to the same GEO-operated Val Verde facility. The observed change is therefore in the federal recipient identity, while the completed evidence does not show an operational facility/operator change.

That sequence demonstrates a **recipient-name visibility-loss mechanism**: a GEO-recipient search finds the predecessor but not the active county-prime award. It does not establish why CBP changed the recipient, whether the current county-GEO agreement preserved every economic term, or how prevalent this mechanism is across DHS. Current Val Verde downstream rates, invoices, remittances, public-body fees, and GEO margin remain missing. One CBP context cannot establish cross-component prevalence or a seven-chain dollar gap.

The ACH result must be expressed as least evidence against:

- Hypotheses `#333` and `#334` remain tied on least evidence against. Seven-chain recurrence establishes a topology, while recovered terms remain materially facility-specific.
- Hypothesis `#341` has less verified evidence against than `#342` for the existence of a recipient-attribution blind spot. The inconsistent evidence for `#342` reduces to two independent chain contexts—Joe Corley under ICE and Val Verde under CBP—not five independent observations.
- The financial-materiality clause in `#341` remains unresolved. The evidence supports the mechanism, not a system-wide magnitude.

## Val Verde natural-experiment test

### Observed treatment

1. Direct GEO award `70B03C25P00000029` obligated $9,899.85 for detention space.
2. Modification `P00001` on 2025-03-21 removed the full amount and stated: `DE-OBLIGATE FUNDS AND REPLACE WITH 03C25P0150`.
3. County-prime award `70B03C25P00000150` begins on 2025-03-21, names County of Val Verde, and describes detention space in Del Rio.

### Operational-continuity evidence

- The 2016 CBP order makes Val Verde detention a rider on USMS IGA `80-98-0061`.
- The 2015 county agreement says GEO operates the facility, invoices monthly in arrears, and receives county remittances.
- GEO's current facility page and 2025 10-K continue to identify the Val Verde facility as GEO-managed/owned under the USMS-IGA structure.

### Outcome and inference boundary

The observed outcome is a recipient-name change from GEO to the county while the completed evidence preserves the facility/operator route. That supports a medium-confidence inference of **visibility loss without a verified operational facility/operator change**.

It is not a clean randomized experiment. The public record does not supply the contracting officer's rationale, current county-GEO agreement, current price schedule, or paired invoices. The sequence cannot prove that every economic term remained constant, that the switch was designed to obscure GEO, or that similar switches are common.

## Systemic-analysis result

The only pattern meeting the three-context recurrence threshold is the already-known public-prime/private-operator topology, now present in seven independent DHS chains. A fee or retained-value record remains visible in at least LaSalle, Clearfield, and Montgomery, but the forms and periods differ and that phenomenon is already predicted by `#333` and routed through existing Layer-1 work. Val Verde does not add a verified current fee.

No evidence establishes lateral coordination among the seven public primes, a GEO-designed common template, a common guarantee/remedy rule, deliberate concealment, or uniform financial effect. Base-rate caution remains essential: four other CBP detention facilities in the same reviewed portfolio were contracted directly to GEO.

## Final caveats

Federal obligations are not downstream payments or GEO revenue. Local ledger totals, federal obligations, unit rates, fees, ceilings, and outlays are different accounting objects and must not be summed. The Val Verde sequence establishes an observable recipient-attribution mechanism; it does not quantify current GEO receipts, show intent, or prove prevalence outside one CBP chain.
