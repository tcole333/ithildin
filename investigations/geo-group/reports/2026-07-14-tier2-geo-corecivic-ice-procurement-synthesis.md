**Hypotheses revisited:** `#343`, `#344` (H0), `#345`

## Executive result

The completed California City audit strengthens two observations without supporting a causal allegation. First, CoreCivic's FY2025 direct-ICE increase was concentrated: California City and Midwest Regional contributed **$74,943,682.25**, or **59.33%**, of the exact **$126,323,664.15** increase. Second, federal-fiscal timing was material: **$80,338,431.54** was obligated from September 26 through September 30, equal to **63.60%** of the annual increase. These facts are consistent with hypotheses `#343` and `#345`, but they do not establish favoritism, political influence, illegality, or improper fiscal timing.

The new cross-company capacity proxy preserves hypothesis `#344`, the null. FY2025 obligations for CoreCivic's two new direct sites were **$20,858 per announced design bed**; GEO's Delaney Hall and North Lake direct sites were **$21,671 per announced design bed**, a **3.9%** spread. This is consistent with a coarse capacity-scaled explanation, but it is not a ready-bed test: Midwest had no intake by year-end, California City did not begin intake until August, and comparable rates, occupancy, geography, security level, medical/transport services, and activation dates remain missing.

ACH therefore has no winner. All three hypotheses still have zero inconsistent evaluations. The raw concentration threshold in `#343` is met, the simple design-bed proxy is consistent with H0 `#344`, and the fiscal/calendar comparison supports `#345`. None is confirmed or refuted.

One denominator correction is material. The prior report's **$811.729 million “GEO parent”** FY2025 timeline is a USAspending parent-hierarchy result that includes B.I. Incorporated. It decomposes into **$621.386 million** for the legal recipient The GEO Group, Inc. and **$190.342 million** for B.I. The hierarchy grew 8.61%, while the legal GEO recipient grew 35.35% and B.I. declined 33.98%. The earlier four-row 11.2% median is descriptive, not an independent peer median, because the hierarchy and B.I. rows overlap. Findings `#12413` and `#12449` were corrected and verified accordingly.

The audited GEO architecture now contains **seven verified DHS public-prime/private-operator chains**, six under ICE and one under CBP (`#12668`, `#12727`). Those chains demonstrate that recipient-name searches can omit operator economics, but they do not supply a uniform pass-through amount or prove that every company uses IGSAs at the same rate. Direct-prime, IGSA, local-payment, company-revenue, obligation, outlay, ceiling, and per-diem values remain separate accounting objects.

The procurement evidence is therefore mixed:

- **Fact:** CoreCivic California City and Midwest and GEO North Lake used sole-source urgency letter contracts.
- **Fact:** GEO Delaney Hall used full-and-open competition with two offers.
- **Fact:** CoreCivic Otay Mesa and MTC Imperial were facility-specific full-and-open IDIQs that each reported one offer.
- **Fact:** CoreCivic Dilley and GEO D. Ray James used IGSA channels that are not represented as direct operator awards.
- **Inference:** urgent demand was satisfied through several acquisition routes according to site and contract context.
- **Unsupported theory:** the completed record does not show favoritism, political influence, coordination, or illegality.

CoreCivic calendar-action totals were **$179,216,891.33** in 2024 and **$266,391,160.57** in 2025. GEO legal-recipient totals were **$469,578,873.88** and **$527,827,186.25**. MTC totals were **$44,630,623.44** and **$38,083,184.71**.

These comparisons support `#345` as a fiscal-boundary explanation across the cohort, not just at CoreCivic. They do not make calendar actions service-period aligned. Task funding can cover future months; transaction rows do not provide transaction-level outlays; and SEC revenue follows calendar-year accrual accounting.

For CoreCivic specifically:

- six actions from September 26–30 totaled **$80,338,431.54**;
- those actions equal **26.27%** of FY2025 obligations and **63.60%** of the annual increase;
- California City plus Midwest accounted for **$51,235,282.25** of the five-day amount;
- California City FY2025 obligations were **$49.153 million**, while company-reported calendar-2025 facility revenue was **$39.2 million** and year-end population was 1,436 of 2,560 design beds (`#12776`).

The last comparison is not a profit calculation or an outlay reconciliation.

## 4. Ready-capacity null: the test that is possible and the test that is not

Finding `#12782` records the only defensible local capacity normalization available after the contract audit:

| Company | New direct sites | Design beds | FY2025 direct obligations | Obligations / design bed |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| CoreCivic | California City + Midwest | 3,593 | $74,943,682.25 | $20,858 |
| GEO | Delaney Hall + North Lake | 2,800 | $60,678,872.24 | $21,671 |

GEO is 3.9% higher on this proxy. That is consistent with H0 `#344` at the coarsest capacity denominator and does not support the prediction in `#343` that CoreCivic remains an outlier after adjustment.

It is not sufficient to accept H0 because “design bed” is not “ready bed.” The calculation lacks:

1. ready and staffed beds on each award date;
2. actual intake and monthly occupancy;
3. guaranteed minimums, fixed monthly components, and per-diem/CLIN rates;
4. geography, security classification, medical and transportation service mix;
5. startup cost and activation-date alignment;
6. IGSA activations and downstream payments;
7. a new-site MTC denominator.

Midwest is the decisive warning: CoreCivic received FY2025 activation obligations while litigation prevented detainee intake. A per-design-bed result that treats all 1,033 beds as equally ready would overstate the operational denominator.

## 5. What the missing California City file would discriminate

The public record confirms the authority coding but does not expose the signed J&A, market research, alternatives analysis, price-negotiation memorandum, CLIN schedule, or exceptional-circumstances determination (`#12774`, `#12777`). Human action `#53` already requests the file with `related_lead_id` left NULL; no duplicate action was created.

The file could discriminate the live hypotheses as follows:

| Missing record | Result favoring H0 `#344` | Result adding weight to `#343` | Timing relevance to `#345` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Signed J&A and approval | Identifies a documented serious injury, urgent schedule, and acquisition duration tied to operational need | Shows a narrower-than-expected alternatives analysis or leaves the one-source choice unexplained; this would be a records gap, not proof of illegality | Dates show whether the long period was known at letter award or emerged during definitization |
| Market research / alternative-facility analysis | Compares available GEO, MTC, public, and CoreCivic sites and documents why California City best met capacity/location/readiness needs | Shows feasible alternatives were identified but rejected on terms requiring further review | Establishes actual availability dates and the time needed for competition |
| IGCE, CLIN schedule, price-negotiation memorandum | Benchmarks fixed, startup, per-diem, transport, and medical pricing to peer facilities | Shows unexplained price dispersion after comparable services and capacity are aligned | Separates startup and future-period funding from occupied-bed services |
| Definitization schedule and proposal | Explains the September 26 value increase and two-year structure with negotiated support | Documents unresolved cost/pricing issues or departures requiring a narrower follow-up | Allocates late-FY obligations to service months and option periods |
| Exceptional-circumstances documentation | Supplies the approval basis for an urgency period beyond one year | If absent from the produced complete file, creates a concrete compliance question for counsel/contracting review; absence must be established from the file, not inferred from web search | Clarifies whether the second year reflects urgency, successor competition timing, or an option structure |

The relevant comparator is not “sole source versus no award.” It is California City against GEO North Lake under the same urgency/letter-contract pattern and against GEO Delaney under full-and-open competition. That three-way file comparison would test whether site-specific readiness and geography explain the route and price.

## 7. Novelty gate

No new hunch passed the required gates.

- The near-parity design-bed proxy is novel analysis but is already a predicted test of H0 `#344`; it updates that hypothesis rather than creating a new one. It also covers only two direct new-site company contexts, below the three-independent-context threshold for a new causal hunch.
- The GEO parent-hierarchy/B.I. correction is a denominator repair, not a causal theory.
- Direct-prime versus IGSA visibility is already covered by findings `#12668` and `#12727` and hypotheses `#341/#342`.
- The letter-contract pattern is already within `#343/#344`, and the fiscal-boundary pattern is already `#345`.

Accordingly, run 93 created **one independently supported synthesis finding (`#12782`)**, **zero hypotheses**, **zero leads**, and **zero human actions**. Existing action `#53`, infra request `#152`, and the completed lead `#58082` cover the remaining files without duplication.
