## Bottom line

The March 17, 2025 record changes the narrow ACH result. It identifies David J.
Venturella as ICE **Senior Advisor to the Director** 45 days after the filed
GEO consultancy's scheduled January 31 end and two days after the prior GEO
award's scheduled March 15 vesting date. The old 472-day and 429-day intervals
measure only the later May 2026 top-leadership-page transition. They are not a
government-return boundary and were explicitly excluded from this run.

On the corrected evidence set, hypothesis #355 is the least inconsistent and
hypothesis #356 is second: 0.0000 versus 0.1111. Those figures are ordinal ACH
inconsistency scores within this bounded matrix, not probabilities, confidence
percentages, or proof. #355 is least inconsistent only in its
**unresolved-boundary** sense. The evidence does not establish an actual GEO
interest after the scheduled dates, a consultancy extension, actual share
vesting or retention, a missing or inadequate ethics screen, procurement
participation, a conflict, a recusal failure, appointment authority, or
procurement influence.

This does not reverse the broader July 14 Trump/DHS synthesis in finding #12939.
It strengthens the timing and ethics relevance of one political/access
relationship and removes an old separation argument from the innocent account.
It still supplies no relationship-to-decision bridge for a named GEO facility,
PIID, solicitation, source selection, price, modification, option, or capacity
decision. H0 therefore remains substantially less inconsistent for aggregate
DHS/GEO expansion, while vendor/facility selection and terms remain unresolved
with H0 only weakly less inconsistent across the full evidence base.

in the narrow set, but it does not establish adequate insulation by itself.

## Strongest innocent explanation

Venturella returned to ICE after his filed consultancy was scheduled to end and
after the scheduled award event. Ordinary agency onboarding identified any
remaining interests, imposed appropriate restrictions, and routed acquisition
work through M&A/OAQ. His later official-capacity and collective-litigation
appearances reflect office succession and government representation rather than
personal GEO participation. Agency-held personnel and ethics records, rather
than public records, account for the documentary gap.

This explanation is plausible and remains live. It is not established because
the actual termination, disposition, screen, delegation, routing, and
participation records have not been obtained.

## Falsification and missing-record boundary

Evidence that would materially falsify the stronger #355 formulation would
show, with dates, that all relevant GEO relationships and interests ended or
were disposed of; a comprehensive screen was in force before Venturella's ICE
work; and GEO matters were routed without his participation.

Evidence that would materially falsify #356 would show an extension or residual
GEO interest, a late or narrow screen, a waiver or authorization exposing a
specific boundary, or Venturella's personal participation or intervention in a
