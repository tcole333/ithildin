# Ballard Partners–GEO lobbying contact and work-paper audit

**Lead:** #59423  
**Profile:** `geo-group`  
**As of:** 2026-07-13

## Adjudicated result

The public record supports the lead's ordinary-aggregate explanation. Ballard Partners disclosed a GEO lobbying engagement from 2017-02-01 through 2021-01-31 and reported $1.8 million in income across the engagement ($1.23 million in 2018–2020). The 18 official filings identify firm lobbyists, issue categories, and government entities, but those activity-level fields do **not** say which lobbyist contacted which entity. No named personal GEO contact, GEO procurement intervention, acquisition work paper, directive, recusal, or award-selection communication emerged from the bounded primary-record checks.

This is a supported negative conclusion, not proof of absence. DHS's published no-record response ends on 2019-02-14, OMB visitor records omit client and purpose, the Trump White House did not publish a complete White House visitor-log series comparable to OMB's, and agency-held acquisition and correspondence records remain incompletely public.

### Attribution limit

The Senate API nests `government_entities` under a quarterly lobbying-activity row, alongside an array of `lobbyists`. The print form likewise reports issue activity, entities, and lobbyists at the activity level. It does not create person-to-entity pairs. Therefore:

- the Q3 2019 filing establishes that Bondi was listed on the account and that the activity named DHS and the White House Office;
- it does **not** establish that Bondi personally contacted either entity;
- the same limitation applies to Sayfie and every other listed lobbyist;
- government-entity fields cannot be treated as a meeting, communication, procurement intervention, or resulting decision.

The official Q4 2019 print filing is stronger on one narrow chronology point: item 23 identifies Pamela Bondi as a previously reported individual no longer expected to lobby for GEO. The filings do not state the exact dates of her work inside Q3 or the reason she left the account.

## Trump-administration role overlap

The overlap is real but limited by the filing's attribution structure.

- The Q3 2019 LDA filing reported Bondi's 2016–2017 presidential-transition role and 2017 opioid-commission role. Ballard Partners announced that she joined the firm on 2019-01-22. DOJ's official biography says she joined the White House in 2019 as a special adviser in the White House Counsel's Office. The Q4 filing then removed her from GEO's roster. This is a revolving-door chronology, but the public filing does not establish a particular contact or procurement act.
- Sayfie was listed from 2017 Q3 through termination, with the covered position “Commissioner, Commission on White House Fellows.” The Trump White House announced the intended appointment on 2018-04-24. Official White House Fellows materials describe a selection/advisory function, not a procurement role.
- The Trump White House announced Brian Ballard's Kennedy Center board appointment in December 2020. That establishes another administration connection, but the board role is not evidence about DHS or ICE acquisition activity.

## Calendar, visitor, and correspondence checks

### DHS no-record response

DHS FOIA tracking number `2019-HQFO-00438`, dated 2019-02-25, responded to a request for communications, calendar invitations, meeting background, and notes between DHS political appointees and Ballard Partners personnel from 2017-01-20 through 2019-02-14. The request expressly named Ballard, Lukis, McFaul, Benn, Sayfie, Bondi, and the firm's email domain. DHS reported that a comprehensive Headquarters/Office of the Chief Information Officer search was “unable to locate or identify any responsive records.”

The response is a meaningful bounded disconfirmation for DHS political-appointee records through 2019-02-14. It is not agency-wide proof of absence: it predates Bondi's Q3 2019 GEO listing and does not resolve later records, ICE/component files, career-staff contacts, or the appeal.

### OMB WAVES visitor records

All 41 OMB monthly/combined visitor-record PDFs linked from the archived official OMB FOIA page, covering January 2017 through December 2020, were downloaded and exact-name screened for the six-person GEO roster. Three appointment groups contained exact roster names:

| Date | Visitor(s) | OMB/EOP visitee | Location/group | What the record establishes |

| 2018-06-05 | Daniel F. McFaul | Kailey Pickitt | OEOB 252; U15647 | Appointment/entry record only |

No exact Brian Ballard, Pamela Bondi, Justin Sayfie, Rebecca Benn, or GEO Group entry appeared in those OMB files. None of the three exact roster events names GEO, ICE, DHS, a solicitation, an award, or an appointment purpose. These records therefore establish firm-level EOP/OMB access for McFaul and Lukis but cannot be attributed to the GEO account.

## Procurement work-paper screen

The six roster surnames were screened across the profile's DHS-wide GEO action ledger, direct-ICE USAspending action rows and award universe, BI/ISAP solicitation matrix, and top-cohort document audit. The screen produced zero matches. It is a bounded negative against a public award-document name trail, not a substitute for agency acquisition files.

Material procurement actions occurred during the engagement, including 2018 ISAP III task-order modifications, 2019 detention and ISAP task orders, and 2020 ISAP IV and Los Angeles detention actions. Timing alone is not evidence of lobbying influence. The LDA record names no PIID, solicitation, contracting officer, source-selection official, award decision, or intervention, and the procurement records screened name no Ballard roster member.
