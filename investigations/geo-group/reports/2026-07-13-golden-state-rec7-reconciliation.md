# Golden State Annex: OIG-24-23 Recommendation 7 contract reconciliation

**Research date:** 2026-07-13

## Bottom line

ICE retained Golden State Annex's **560-bed guaranteed minimum** after DHS OIG recommended that ICE determine an appropriate minimum and update the contract. The current Oversight.gov recommendation record is **Closed**, although the public record does not disclose the closure date or the ICE analysis OIG accepted.

The closed flag is procedural status evidence, not proof by itself that ICE reduced the minimum or saved money. It does not revise the historical findings: during OIG's April 20, 2022–April 19, 2023 window, the public record remains **560 guaranteed beds**, **136 ADP**, **424 average unused guaranteed beds** as a bed-count subtraction, and **approximately $25.3 million** as OIG's reported actual unused-bed payment. The later ICE statistics establish a different fact: ICE retained 560 after the review while average occupancy increased substantially.

The executed public contract trail is consistent with retention:

- `P00014`, signed May 30, 2024, changed the Golden State guaranteed-minimum bed-day **rate** effective December 20, 2023. The public copy redacts the rate, quantities, and amounts and does not state a new minimum count.
- `P00016`, signed December 18, 2024, exercised Option One for December 20, 2024 through December 19, 2029 and carried forward Golden State guaranteed-minimum CLIN `1001A`. The public copy redacts the bed count, quantities, rates, and not-to-exceed amounts.
- `P00018`, signed May 29, 2025, retroactively changed the CLIN rates effective December 20, 2024 and stated that all other contract terms remained the same. Its rate and retroactive-payment amount are redacted.
- ICE's own FY2025 facility statistics, based on a September 15, 2025 data pull, list Golden State's guaranteed minimum as **560**. ICE's FY2026 file, based on data through April 2, 2026, again lists **560**. These files resolve the redacted count in the exercised option: the minimum was retained, not reduced.

The historic monetary finding remains exactly what OIG reported: ICE paid **approximately $25.3 million for unused bed space** during April 20, 2022 through April 19, 2023. This is an OIG-reported approximate actual payment, not an amount reconstructed from a derived rate. No public monthly invoice, receiving-report, payment-voucher, credit, or facility-allocation package was found, so contract-rate savings or continued daily unused-bed payments after the review cannot be quantified from public records.

### Final verdict

- **Minimum:** not reduced in the available post-review snapshots. Two separate primary ICE workbooks—the FY2025 facility sheet sourced September 15, 2025 and the FY2026 facility sheet sourced April 2, 2026—each report **560**.
- **Rate:** changed or adjusted by `P00014` effective December 20, 2023 and by `P00018` effective December 20, 2024, but the public modifications redact the revised rate and retroactive-payment amount.
- **OIG closure:** procedural status only in the available public record. It does not show that OIG accepted a numeric reduction, and the closeout memorandum/analysis is not public.
- **Historical audited period:** **560 / 136 / 424 / approximately $25.3 million** remains valid, with 424 expressly a derived average unused-bed count and $25.3 million expressly OIG's reported actual approximate unused-bed payment.

## Recommendation 7 status and chronology

OIG-24-23 instructed ICE to:

> Review and update ICE's contract with Golden State by assessing housing requirements and determining an appropriate guaranteed minimum to avoid excessive payment for unused bed space.

ICE said the San Francisco field office would assess use and work with the operator, with an estimated completion date of November 29, 2024. OIG said it would close the recommendation only after ICE supplied the analysis and updated the contract accordingly.

On September 24, 2024, OIG-24-59 still identified Recommendation 7 as the one guaranteed-minimum recommendation that was “resolved and open.” The current Oversight.gov record now displays “Recommendation Status: Closed.” The public status page does not display the closure date or attach ICE's analysis, contract crosswalk, or OIG acceptance memorandum. Therefore:

- **Confirmed fact:** Recommendation 7 is currently closed.
- **Confirmed boundary:** closure occurred after September 24, 2024.
- **Unresolved:** exact closure date, OIG's rationale, the analysis ICE submitted, and which instrument OIG treated as the responsive contract update.
- **Inference, not fact:** `P00016` is a plausible update vehicle because it followed ICE's estimated completion date by 19 days and exercised the new five-year option. The public closure record does not identify it.

## Population implications, carefully bounded

OIG analyzed daily population counts and bed-rate costs for April 20, 2022 through April 19, 2023. It reported an average daily population of **136**, a 560-bed minimum, and approximately **$25.3 million** paid for unused bed space. Subtracting the reported ADP from the minimum gives **424 average unused guaranteed beds**; that is only a bed-count comparison and does not imply a bed-day rate or independently reconstruct $25.3 million.

ICE's later structured data show a major occupancy change:

- FY2025 classification-level ADP components sum to approximately **595.745**, about 35.745 above the 560 minimum.
- FY2026 components through April 2, 2026 sum to approximately **603.071**, about 43.071 above the minimum.

Those later annual/YTD averages do **not** prove there were no individual days below 560. They do show that the public evidence does not support carrying the OIG-period average-unused-bed pattern forward into FY2025 or FY2026. A defensible continued-payment calculation requires daily population, the applicable guaranteed-minimum and above-minimum rates, invoice line items, and adjustments.

These actions are irregular, combined-facility funding transactions, not a monthly invoice ledger. The FY2022 order's $314,767.05 closeout deobligation and the FY2023 order's later $295,815.67 closeout deobligation cannot be labeled Golden State credits, savings, or recovered unused-bed payments.

Money labels used in this report:

- **OIG-reported actual approximate payment:** $25.3 million for unused Golden State bed space during the reviewed 12 months.
- **USAspending transaction amount:** obligation or deobligation authority, not an invoice or payment.
- **USAspending outlay:** cash outlay at the shared task-order level, not a Golden State invoice allocation.
- **Contract rate / retroactive payment:** publicly redacted in `P00014`, `P00016`, and `P00018`.
- **Savings / continued unused payment:** not publicly quantifiable with the available rate, daily-population, allocation, and invoice record.
