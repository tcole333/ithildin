# Thomas Homan consulting compensation: GEO Care, Guidepost Solutions, and the unresolved payment path

**Lead:** 58983  
**Profile / thread:** `geo-group` / 112  
**As of:** 2026-07-13  
**Method:** Primary-record follow-up to finding #12505, with the Guidepost–B.I. chronology in #12388, #12450, #12452, and the existing Guidepost reports used as context. Public records were searched without contacting Homan, GEO, Guidepost, or any other subject.

## Bottom line

Homan's official White House OGE Form 278e proves four narrow facts about the two clients:

1. Homan personally provided consulting services to both **Guidepost Solutions** and **GEO Care**.
2. Each source paid more than $5,000 in at least one calendar year during the Part 4 reporting period.
3. For this report, that period was the preceding two calendar years and the current year through filing: **January 1, 2023 through February 10, 2025**, using the form's February 10 electronic signature as the filing date shown by the public record.
4. Part 4 permits a source to be reported whether it paid Homan directly or paid his employer for his personal services.

The public form does **not** identify which year crossed the threshold, the exact fee, the service dates, deliverables, contract, invoice, payment account, or whether the source paid Homan or Homeland Strategic Consulting, LLC. The separately disclosed **$360,000 salary from Homeland Strategic Consulting** cannot be allocated to GEO Care, Guidepost, or any other client from the form.

GEO's SEC filings establish a contemporaneous **entity-level** relationship between Guidepost and GEO subsidiary B.I. Incorporated: GEO disclosed $180,000 in Guidepost-B.I. consulting-agreement payments in each of fiscal 2023 and 2024. But no searched primary record names Homan as personnel on that engagement or links his Guidepost services to B.I., ICE, ISAP, or any GEO project. That proposed connection remains **unresolved and second-degree**, not a supported attribution.

The public ethics treatment is also narrower than a recusal record. Homan's form says an LLC would remain dormant during the appointment, records his withdrawal from a defined-contribution plan, and was reviewed and certified by agency ethics officials. No separate public recusal, screening arrangement, authorization, waiver, calendar, visitor-log entry, contract, SOW, invoice, or last-service certification tied to GEO Care or Guidepost was located in the official sources searched.

## What the OGE form establishes

### Reporting period and threshold

The official OGE Part 4 guide says a new entrant reports any source paying more than $5,000 for personal services in any calendar year during the preceding two calendar years and the current year through filing. Homan electronically signed the report on February 10, 2025. The resulting Part 4 window is January 1, 2023-February 10, 2025.

This is a per-source, per-calendar-year threshold. The entries establish that Guidepost and GEO Care each exceeded $5,000 in at least one covered year; they do not establish that either paid more than $5,000 in every covered year or identify which year did so.

### Personal performance, but ambiguous payment route

The form's instructions say a source is reported when the filer personally provided the services, including when the source paid the filer's employer rather than the filer. A client payment for another employee's services is not reportable in the filer's Part 4.

Therefore:

- **Established:** Homan personally supplied consulting services associated with each listed source.
- **Not established:** whether Guidepost or GEO Care paid Homan directly, paid Homeland Strategic Consulting, or used another disclosed employer/intermediary.
- **Not established:** the gross client fee, Homan's share, net income, invoice date, or payment date.

### Homan's consulting business and income

The same form lists:

- Homeland Strategic Consulting, LLC, Fredericksburg, Virginia;
- Homan as president from July 2018 through December 2024;
- salary of $360,000 from the company in Part 2;
- the company itself as a Part 4 source for “Services as President.”

Official FEC filings independently show **Homeland Strategic Consulting, LLC** at a Fredericksburg address receiving strategy-consulting payments from Jim Lamon for Senate in 2021-2022. Those transactions corroborate that the named business operated as a consulting payee, but they predate the Homan Part 4 period and do not identify GEO Care or Guidepost.

An exact-name search of the local SAM.gov public entity extract and USAspending returned no Homeland Strategic Consulting registration or federal prime award. That negative result does not bear on a private client payment and does not resolve the GEO/Guidepost payment route.

Virginia's official SCC search was reached, but its public search submission is reCAPTCHA-gated. The local unified registry had no responsive Virginia row. Secondary registry mirrors were not promoted as primary evidence, so formation documents, entity status, and the registered-agent record remain a registry follow-up rather than a finding here.

## “GEO Care” is not a sufficiently precise legal payer name

Homan's Part 4 uses the shorthand **GEO Care**, Boca Raton, Florida. Official records show that this label has covered a division, prior entities, and current subsidiaries:

- GEO's official site calls GEO Care a division of The GEO Group.
- GEO's fiscal-2024 SEC Exhibit 22 lists **GEO Care LLC** as a guarantor subsidiary.
- Florida Sunbiz shows foreign LLC M15000002176, now **GEO Care Services, LLC**, filed March 24, 2015, active, with a May 20, 2025 LLC name change and the GEO headquarters address at 4955 Technology Way, Boca Raton.
- A 2026 SEC credit-agreement amendment identifies **GEO Care Services, LLC (f/k/a GEO Care, LLC)**.
- GEO's fiscal-2025 Exhibit 22 lists both **GEO Care Services, LLC** and **GEO Care, Inc.** The current corporation was formed after Homan's reporting window; an older GEO Care, Inc. had converted in 2012.

The entity that existed under the GEO Care LLC name during the 2023-February 2025 reporting window is a plausible source behind the shorthand, and its Boca Raton address aligns with the form. But the 278e does not supply a legal suffix, UEI, EIN, contract number, or invoice. It is therefore not sufficient to assign the payment to GEO Care LLC, GEO Care Services LLC, GEO Care, Inc., B.I., or the public parent.

## Guidepost's B.I. relationship: strong corporate link, no Homan attribution

The primary SEC record shows:

- Guidepost's consulting agreement with B.I. existed by fiscal 2013, before Guidepost CEO Julie Myers Wood joined GEO's board in July 2014 (#12450).
- GEO paid Guidepost $180,000 under that agreement in fiscal 2023 and another $180,000 in fiscal 2024.
- GEO said the agreement was extended through December 31, 2025; the 2026 proxy reports $420,000 paid in 2025 and says the agreement ended at year-end.
- GEO described the relationship as a related-party transaction because Wood was both Guidepost's CEO and a GEO director.

The existing [Guidepost-B.I. chronology](guidepost-bi-chronology-2026-07-13.md) and [annual source matrix](guidepost-bi-source-matrix-2026-07-13.csv) preserve the full 2013-2025 payment series.

The Homan follow-up found no primary bridge from his Guidepost entry to that agreement:

- “Homan” does not appear in GEO's 2023, 2024, 2025, or 2026 proxy statements searched for this lead.
- The proxies do not attach the Guidepost-B.I. agreement, SOWs, work orders, personnel lists, invoices, deliverables, or committee minutes.
- Exact-phrase CourtListener/RECAP searches returned no record combining Thomas Homan and Guidepost Solutions. The two exact “Thomas Homan”/“GEO Care” RECAP hits were 2016 Flores materials, outside the reporting period and unrelated to consulting compensation.
- Guidepost's official site contains media pages discussing Homan and a broad immigration/border-services practice, but no located Homan staff profile, project page, B.I. assignment, or GEO deliverable.
- USAspending shows several Guidepost federal awards, including unrelated expert-witness, security-assessment, and investigation-service work. Those award records identify the entity but not Homan as personnel.

The fact that “Guidepost” appears in Homan's disclosure and in GEO's B.I. related-party disclosure is meaningful for follow-up, but common entity appearance alone is not proof that the two engagements are the same.

## Public ethics treatment

### What is documented

Homan's form records:

- non-career appointment as DHS “Border Czar” on January 20, 2025;
- an electronic signature on February 10, 2025;
- ethics review by Ashley Lam on April 10, 2025;
- an agency ethics official's certification by Michael O'Connor on May 8, 2025, concluding on the information in the report that Homan was in compliance with applicable laws and regulations;
- multiple data revisions between February 21 and May 7, 2025;
- no further participation in a listed Homeland Strategic Consulting defined-contribution plan;
- an arrangement stating that an LLC “will remain dormant during appointment.”

The dormancy line is internally inconsistent: Part 1, the salary line, and the 401(k) use **Homeland Strategic Consulting**, while the defined-benefit-plan and dormancy entries use **Homeland Security Consulting**. No primary registry evidence was found that permits those names to be merged confidently. The report therefore preserves the discrepancy instead of silently correcting it.

### Applicable framework, not a Homan-specific determination

OGE's official consulting-employment guidance says a former consulting client is a “covered relationship” for one year after the employee last provided services and while an outstanding fee remains unpaid. In a particular matter involving that client, the employee may need to inform the agency designee and obtain authorization if a reasonable person could question impartiality.

The public Homan form does not disclose his last service date for GEO Care or Guidepost, whether any fee remained outstanding at appointment, or whether either entity was a party or represented a party in a specific matter Homan handled. The one-year period therefore cannot be calculated from the form. The agency's certification shows review and a compliance conclusion based on the report; it does not itself establish a GEO/Guidepost recusal, screening arrangement, waiver, authorization, or participation history.

Bounded searches of official White House, OGE, DHS, and ICE FOIA-library pages located no separate Homan document naming GEO Care or Guidepost in a recusal, screening, waiver, authorization, calendar, visitor log, or ethics-agreement certification. This is an absence from the searched public record, not proof that no internal advice or screening occurred.

## Evidence conclusions

### Confirmed by primary records

- Homan personally provided consulting services associated with Guidepost and GEO Care.
- Each source exceeded $5,000 in at least one covered calendar year between January 1, 2023 and February 10, 2025.
- Homan was president of Homeland Strategic Consulting through December 2024 and reported $360,000 in salary from it.
- The 278e permits payment to have gone to Homan or to an employer for his services.
- Guidepost had a paid consulting agreement with B.I. during the same period, including $180,000 payments in both 2023 and 2024.
- The public form underwent ethics review and was agency-certified on May 8, 2025.

### Unresolved

- exact GEO Care or Guidepost fee;
- threshold year, payment date, invoice date, and service dates;
- direct payment versus payment through Homeland Strategic Consulting or another intermediary;
- exact “GEO Care” legal entity;
- project scope, contract, SOW, work product, and personnel;
- Homan's last service date and whether any payment remained outstanding on January 20, 2025;
- any Homan-specific recusal, authorization, screening arrangement, or covered-matter list;
- any connection between Homan's Guidepost work and Guidepost's B.I. agreement.

### Not supported by the public record reviewed

- that Homan worked on B.I.'s ICE/ISAP contracts for Guidepost;
- that the $360,000 Homeland Strategic Consulting salary came from GEO Care or Guidepost;
- that a GEO entity paid Homan directly rather than through an employer;
- that the ethics certification was a transaction-specific recusal determination;
- any claim of procurement influence, preferential treatment, or misconduct.
