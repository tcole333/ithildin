# GEO Group — Trump Administrations and DHS Investigation Plan

## Scope

Parent lead: **#58868**

This investigation tests documented relationships between The GEO Group, its
subsidiaries, executives, directors, political committees, lobbyists, investors,
lenders, advisers, and former officials on one side, and the first and second
Trump administrations and the Department of Homeland Security on the other.
The DHS scope includes headquarters and every component for which records show a
connection; it is not limited to ICE.

## Initial competing explanations

These are working hypotheses for later ACH analysis, not findings.

1. **H0 — ordinary sector alignment and access.** GEO's political money,
   lobbying, former-official hiring, and contract growth resemble the conduct of
   a large incumbent detention/monitoring contractor operating under an
   enforcement-oriented administration. Expected evidence includes bipartisan
   giving, comparable peer patterns, passive rather than strategic ownership,
   and awards traceable to established vehicles or documented procurement needs.
2. **H1 — unusually concentrated Trump-network access.** GEO-linked political
   money and intermediaries are disproportionately connected to Trump campaign,
   transition, White House, or DHS decision networks compared with peers and the
   company's own cross-party baseline. Expected evidence includes multiple
   independent direct edges, persistent timing overlap, and access not explained
   by ordinary trade-association or incumbent-contractor channels.
3. **H2 — relationship-linked procurement acceleration.** Lobbying, personnel,
   or political-money events precede or overlap specific DHS award activations
   more closely than normal procurement lead times and peer behavior would
   predict. This requires dated solicitation and acquisition records; temporal
   proximity alone cannot establish influence.
4. **H3 — direct Trump-linked financial interest.** Trump, a family member,
   appointee, adviser, or controlled entity holds or held a direct or beneficial
   GEO financial interest or strategic business relationship. Passive fund
   exposure and ordinary syndicated lending do not satisfy this hypothesis.
5. **H4 — the network extends materially beyond ICE.** Documented GEO ties to
   DHS headquarters or non-ICE components involve substantive programs,
   officials, or awards rather than administrative routing or generic lobbying
   references.

Every non-null hypothesis must be paired with the strongest innocent
explanation and a falsification test after the factual ledgers are complete.

## Interpretation rules

- A donation establishes a payment, not influence.
- A lobbying registration establishes a compensated representation and named
  issue/target, not a private conversation or policy result.
- Shared government service establishes professional proximity only when the
  overlap and organizational relationship are documented.
- A public-company shareholder position establishes ownership by the reported
  person or manager; it does not establish the beneficial owners of underlying
  diversified funds.
- A DHS award establishes a procurement relationship only at the legal entity,
  component, vehicle, action, and obligation levels supported by the record.
- Temporal overlap is a hypothesis generator, not proof of coordination or
  causation.
