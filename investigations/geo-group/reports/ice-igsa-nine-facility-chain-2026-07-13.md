## Outcome

All nine SEC facility-table rows classified as verified ICE IGSAs are now mapped to six independent federal/local/GEO chains, or bounded where the exact downstream instrument is still missing. The correct denominator is:

- 9 distinct SEC facility rows;
- 6 independent ICE → public-prime → GEO chains;
- 6 unique public primes; and
- 6 current PIID/predecessor families.

Three pairs share one chain and one set of chain-level dollars: Alexandria Staging/Central Louisiana, D. Ray James/Folkston, and Pine Prairie/South Louisiana. They must not be treated as six independent awards.

The only compatible numerical roll-up is the nine distinct SEC physical-capacity rows: **11,361 beds**. That figure is physical capacity, not an ICE guarantee. Joe Corley, for example, has 1,517 physical beds in the SEC table but the federal base reserves 1,050 for ICE exclusive use.

No defensible six-chain federal obligation total or downstream GEO-receipt total can be calculated from the acquired records. The available figures mix audited annual inflows/outflows, unit and monthly rates, one discrete task-order adjustment, administrative fees, and a zero-dollar base action.

## What this adds beyond the five-path baseline

The earlier five-path reports covered eight facility rows through five public-prime chains. This review adds the missing ninth row and sixth chain:

**ICE → County of Montgomery → GEO Corrections and Detentions, LLC → Joe Corley Processing Center**

New primary records beyond the earlier baseline:

1. ICE 70CDCR18DIG000013 base/P00001;
2. Montgomery County’s public copy of ICE modification P00040;
3. the 2013 Montgomery County-GEO operating agreement and first amendment; and
4. Montgomery County’s FY2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report.

New bounded dollar evidence:

- FY2025 Joe Corley Pass Through Fund revenue and operations expenditure were each $43,194,467, but the fund combines ICE and USMS reimbursements;
- effective November 1, 2025, the federal bed-day rate was $82.56 and transportation rate was $228,019.07 per month;
- P00040 approved a discrete $8,137.37 retroactive task-order payment;
- the 2013 local amendment required a $500,000 annual county fee, but no acquired later amendment proves that fee remained current in FY2025.

These figures describe different accounting objects and are not added together.
