### ICE title policy does not supply authority

ICE Directive 1052.2, effective November 1, 2023, governs use of `Director` in headquarters working titles. It says the policy applies to working position titles `without regard to the associated position descriptions` and contains no procedures, recordkeeping requirements, or attachments. The directive helps explain why a public or working title should not be treated as proof of a permanent office, personnel action, succession designation, or delegated function. It is not a Venturella-specific appointment instrument.

### Applicable rule boundaries

The archived July 10, 2026 eCFR issue provides the current rules:

- Section 2635.502 covers appearance concerns in particular matters involving specific parties. Its former-employer covered relationship lasts one year after resignation. An agency designee may authorize participation under subsection (d), with writing required at the designee's discretion or the employee's request.
- Section 2635.503 applies a two-year recusal only when the employee received a defined `covered payment` from a former employer. The rule defines thresholds and exclusions, including a qualifying-program exclusion, and permits a written waiver. It includes former consultants within `former employer`.
- Section 2634.201 requires a public new entrant to file within 30 days after assuming a public-filing position. Section 2634.202 defines public-filer categories. The present record does not identify Venturella's pay classification or filing category.
- 18 U.S.C. § 208(a) applies when an employee participates personally and substantially in a particular matter while knowing of a covered financial interest. A subsection (b)(1) waiver requires advance written disclosure and determination. The public record here does not establish the necessary current interest, participation, knowledge, or waiver facts.
- DHS supplemental § 4601.106 contains ICE-specific outside-activity and household-employment provisions. It is not a blanket former-employer restriction and should not be used as one.

These rules define the documents and facts to obtain; they do not prove that Venturella was subject to, violated, or was excused from a particular recusal.
