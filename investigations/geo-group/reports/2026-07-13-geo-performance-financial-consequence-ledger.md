## Public-record limits and exact acquisition needs

| Track | Unknown/not-public record | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| B.I. / ISAP | QASPs; monthly monitoring files; case-file audit closure evidence; discrepancy/cure/show-cause notices; invoice adjustments, credits, offsets, or deductions; releasable CPARS portions; ISAP V source-selection treatment excluding protected proposals | Needed to quantify whether GAO-documented failures produced payments, ratings, or recompetition consequences. FAR 42.1503(d) states completed performance evaluations are not released beyond government personnel and the evaluated contractor while usable as source-selection information, so CPARS itself may be restricted. |
| Golden State | OIG-24-23 recommendation 7 closure package; operational-readiness/housing analysis; revised guaranteed minimum and rate; signed post-OIG modifications; invoices and deductions | Needed to identify the actual post-OIG minimum, effective date, and savings/cost consequence. |
| Folkston | 2019 and 2021 discrepancy reports; proposed penalty calculations; contracting-officer decisions not to enforce/waivers; staffing plans and reports; invoices/deductions; recommendation 12 closeout evidence; P00023 and later modifications | Needed to explain why attempted penalties were not enforced and whether later corrective costs or payment changes occurred. |

These files are requested in infrastructure request **150**. The lack of a public file is not coded as “no remedy.”
