The same report identifies maximum capacity of 1,890, reports that South Texas could house 1,417 people at the pandemic-era 75-percent target, and says the population never exceeded the guarantee during November 2020–October 2021. Average unused guaranteed space was 36 percent, producing OIG's approximately $18 million unused-space payment finding.

## Folkston reconstruction

### OIG-period finding and current status

OIG-22-47 collectively treats Folkston IPC and the Annex. For FY2021 it reported ADP/capacity of 286/780 at the IPC and 88/338 at the Annex (#12652). Capacity minus ADP is not an unused-guaranteed-bed measure because no numeric guarantee was recovered.

The report says the contract required 95 percent of the approved staffing plan. ICE attempted financial penalties through 2019 and 2021 discrepancy reports but did not enforce them; the facility continued receiving full contract funding without consequence. ICE later described vendor-funded overtime as the interim staffing response (#12426). Oversight.gov now reports recommendation 12 closed with $0 questioned costs and $0 funds for better use. Those report fields do not reveal invoice deductions or prove that no later remedy occurred.

### Post-OIG county cash-flow evidence

Charlton County's December 2025 packet provides a complete January–December bank-account row. Monthly receipts sum to $53,776,553.79 and monthly expenditures sum to $51,801,922.78. The year-end account carried a $5,482,998.45 outstanding check; the February 2026 county report later shows $28,108,013.50 in receipts and $33,591,011.95 in expenditures, returning the account to $100. These timing swings demonstrate pass-through-account batching, not payment entitlement or remedy treatment.

The county's May 2025 financial discussion confirms that ICE money moved through the account and that the month involved 12 invoices. It does not disclose invoice values or recipients. The Charlton-GEO agreement gives the county audit rights, cure/termination provisions, and 30-day convenience termination, but the public copy redacts the operator rate and county fee (#12439).

### Shared-chain control

GEO reported that D. Ray James, capacity 1,868, was activated under the same IGSA in June 2025; it lists Folkston IPC capacity as 1,118. The county account is named for Folkston IPC, but later cash cannot be allocated among IPC, Annex, and D. Ray James without invoice- or facility-level records. The county flow is consequently recorded once at chain level.

## Evidence-class controls

- A guaranteed minimum is not physical capacity, emergency capacity, an above-minimum ceiling, or funded quantity.
- USAspending obligations authorize spending; outlays are federal account-level disbursement measures. Neither is a CLIN, gross invoice, deduction, credit, county pass-through, or GEO facility revenue.
- County receipts and expenditures are not added to USAspending amounts and are not attributed to GEO without a payee record.
- OIG's approximately $18 million South Texas figure is an actual-payment finding. No implied rate was calculated from an award total.
- OIG's Folkston non-enforcement finding is bounded to the period it examined. Later cash flow does not prove that the same remedy posture continued.
- Report-level $0 questioned-cost and funds-better-use fields are not substitutes for contracting-officer or invoice records.
