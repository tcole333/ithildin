## Bottom line

Primary FEC records support four legally distinct streams totaling **$1,426,296.28** in arithmetic context: **$450,000 from GEO PAC to Trump joint-fundraising committees; $225,000 from GEO Corrections Holdings, Inc. to the pro-Trump super PAC Rebuilding America Now; $250,000 from GEO Corrections Holdings to the 58th Presidential Inaugural Committee; and $501,296.28 in bounded individual receipts to Trump committees.** This context total must not be described as one corporate-donor stream.

The individual total itself has two audit layers: **$389,696.28** from direct individual receipts with exact GEO employer strings, plus **$111,600** from two George Zoley receipts resolved separately despite obvious employer typos. Employer aggregation is not evidence of corporate funding, reimbursement, coordination, or company policy.

The preliminary checkpoint total of **$1,314,696.28 is superseded**. It excluded Zoley’s $100,000 receipt filed with employer `THW GEO GROUP INC` and his $11,600 receipt filed with employer `TH GEO GROUP INC`. The final arithmetic context total is **$1,426,296.28**; the campaign/political-committee subtotal excluding the inaugural donation is **$1,176,296.28**.

## Corporate super-PAC stream and MUR 7180

Rebuilding America Now reported two receipts from GEO Corrections Holdings, Inc.: **$100,000 on August 19, 2016** ([FEC image `202003059203760355`](http://docquery.fec.gov/cgi-bin/fecimg/?202003059203760355), sub-ID `4031220201703004413`) and **$125,000 on November 1, 2016** ([FEC image `202003059203760558`](http://docquery.fec.gov/cgi-bin/fecimg/?202003059203760558), sub-ID `4031220201703004541`). A complete 1,059-row Rebuilding America Now Schedule B export for 2016–2017 contained no GEO-named refund. Official Schedule E rows identify the committee’s independent expenditures for Trump with support indicator `S`, establishing the pro-Trump classification.

The enforcement outcome requires precise phrasing. The [FEC’s MUR 7180 page](https://www.fec.gov/data/legal/matter-under-review/7180/) records a 5–0 reason-to-believe vote concerning the federal-contractor prohibition. In 2021, the Commission deadlocked 3–3 on probable cause and separately 3–3 on no probable cause, then voted 6–0 to close the file. Therefore, the record supports an investigation and reason-to-believe determination, **not** a final probable-cause finding, adjudicated violation, or civil penalty.

## Corporate disclosure boundary

GEO’s [2025 Political Activity and Lobbying Report](https://www.geogroup.com/geo-2025-political-activity-and-lobbying-report/) states that GEO PAC is funded solely through voluntary employee contributions, while corporate funds and facilities may provide lawful administrative support. It separately says corporate political contributions require legal review and, above $500 in 2025, CEO and CFO approval. The report also discloses $4.2169 million in 2025 corporate political contributions and $208,500 from GEO PAC, but does not itemize the federal/national corporate recipients in the report itself.

The disclosure corroborates why the PAC, corporate, and individual streams must remain separate. It does not independently validate every FEC transaction, and the company expressly says the report is unaudited.

## Method and exclusions

- Downloaded all itemized GEO PAC Schedule B rows for cycles 2016–2026 and retained actual transaction dates from 2015 onward.
- Downloaded all GEO PAC Schedule A line-16 refund rows for those cycles and preserved filed signs.
- Tested official committee IDs for Trump candidate, JFC, leadership, super-PAC, party, and inaugural entities.
- Counted individual receipts only when the recipient filed them on an individual-contribution line and the employer exactly matched the bounded GEO regex, except for the two separately documented Zoley typo resolutions.
- Did not count downstream JFC allocation/memo rows as new donations.
- Did not attribute personal donations to GEO or employer aggregates to corporate policy.
- Did not complete a state-by-state corporate/PAC ledger or a full common-name executive/director identity resolution; those are follow-up leads.
