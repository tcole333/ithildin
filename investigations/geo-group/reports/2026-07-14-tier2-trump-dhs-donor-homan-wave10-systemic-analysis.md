## Donor identity ledger: broad but not temporally or causally scoped

Finding #12984 is one identity-resolution context, not 19 independent mechanisms and not 1,335 votes. Its $1,632,401.60 gross total comprises $465,022.10 to the employee-funded GEO PAC and $1,167,379.50 to external committees. Six source-linked refunds total $8,476.90; the unmatched $2,200 refund is preserved without subtraction. These controls improve arithmetic and attribution, not influence inference.

The ledger is expressly **identity-scoped, not tenure-scoped**. Some retained receipts predate GEO employment or board service, so the total cannot be used as a measure of political activity while serving GEO. Cycle 2026 is year-to-date through July 14, 2026 and is not an equal-duration completed cycle. The $302,940 figure is only the gross subset whose official committee names literally match the ledger's Trump/Save America/Never Surrender/MAGA name rule. It is not a complete ideological category, an access measure, or a motive inference.

Finding #12985 reinforces the method boundary rather than adding scale: the $3,000 Los Angeles `GEO GROUP / PROFESSOR` Trump Victory receipt remains unresolved and excluded. Exact employer text did not override conflicting geography and occupation. #12985 is therefore an attribution-control row inside the same donor context, not a second independent political-access mechanism.

The donor ledger increases confidence that personal political participation around the leadership roster is broad and financially material. It does not place any receipt in a decision chain or show that the donor had access to, requested, or affected a DHS/ICE outcome.

## Homan: two official ledgers, no joined transaction

Finding #12983 replaces the older ambiguity with a more exact boundary:

1. Homan's OGE Part 4 lists `Guidepost Solutions` and suffix-free `GEO Care` as sources associated with his personal consulting services. The form's rule permits the source to have paid Homan's employer rather than Homan directly.
2. GEO's SEC filings separately report that **B.I. Incorporated** paid Guidepost $180,000 in 2023, $180,000 in 2024, and $420,000 in 2025 under a consulting agreement.

No reviewed primary record names Homan as personnel on that B.I.-Guidepost engagement or shows that B.I.'s payments reached him. The common Guidepost name is a valid investigative path but not a payment, personnel, or procurement bridge. The OGE label `GEO Care` is not merged with The GEO Group, B.I., or another legal entity without evidence. Corporate Audit and Finance Committee approval is not a DHS ethics determination.

## Final conclusion

The donor ledger broadens access-adjacent evidence; Homan's two-ledger result narrows a suspected bridge. Taken together, they do not materially increase the evidence that political access or relationships shaped a specific GEO DHS/ICE selection, expansion, term, or enforcement outcome. Finding #12939 remains the controlling broader synthesis: H0 is substantially less inconsistent for aggregate expansion; vendor/facility selection and terms remain unresolved, with H0 only weakly less inconsistent.
