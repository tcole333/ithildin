# Guidepost Solutions–B.I. relationship and former-ICE chronology

**Lead:** 57972  
**Profile:** `geo-group`  
**As of:** 2026-07-13  
**Method:** Layer-1 primary-source chronology; facts are separated from unresolved public-record gaps. Chronological proximity is not treated as evidence of coordination, procurement influence, or misconduct.

## Bottom line

The SEC record establishes that Guidepost Solutions had a consulting agreement with GEO subsidiary B.I. Incorporated during fiscal 2013, before Guidepost CEO and former ICE head Julie Myers Wood joined GEO's board on July 2, 2014. GEO then disclosed a Guidepost-B.I. payment in every fiscal year from 2014 through 2025. The exact disclosed total is **$2,394,106 for 2014-2025**, plus an unspecified fiscal-2013 amount that did not exceed the then-applicable $120,000 Item 404(a) threshold. The public filings state that GEO's Audit and Finance Committee reviewed and approved the related-party transactions, but the searched public record does not disclose the consulting scope, statements of work, invoices, deliverables, amendments, approval minutes, or a Wood-specific recusal/abstention.

The related former-ICE chronology is documentary but does not establish influence: Daniel Ragsdale joined GEO in July 2017 after an ICE career ending that year; Matthew Albence began at GEO on September 1, 2022 after leaving ICE in August 2020. GEO separately disclosed $137,531 paid in 2022 to Albence's GrindStone Strategic Consulting for work performed before he joined GEO and said GrindStone provided no services after he joined.

Checkmate Government Relations registered for GEO effective August 13, 2025, two days before ICE posted the ISAP V solicitation. Its LDA description, however, was limited to “contracts related to detention centers” and did not name ICE, B.I., ISAP, an award, or a procurement contact. The timing is therefore a research lead, not proof that the lobbying concerned ISAP V.

## Guidepost-B.I. payment series

The companion [source matrix](guidepost-bi-source-matrix-2026-07-13.csv) preserves the fiscal-year amount, stated extension, current/most-recent retainer, filing date, and SEC URL for every disclosure. Key accounting boundaries:

- 2014-2024 exact total: **$1,974,106**.
- 2014-2025 exact total: **$2,394,106**.
- Fiscal 2013: an unknown amount no greater than $120,000; excluded from both exact totals.
- The filing language changes from “has” a consulting agreement through the 2025 proxy to “had” in the 2026 proxy, which states it was not extended beyond December 31, 2025.

## Governance record and public-record gaps

GEO's proxies state that the Audit and Finance Committee had authority to review and approve certain transactions over $100,000 involving directors, officers, or employees and that the committee “reviewed and approved the related party transactions described above” (#12453). The 2015-2018 proxies also state that the disclosed relationships did not require separate approvals under applicable policies and procedures.

No Wood-specific `recus*` language was located in the 2013-2026 GEO proxy set. That text search is limited: generic proxy voting uses of “abstain” are not transaction-specific minutes, and the absence of a filing disclosure does not establish that no recusal, review, or approval process occurred. The following remain unresolved:

- Guidepost-B.I. agreement text and original execution date;
- scope of work, work orders, named personnel, and ICE/ISAP relationship, if any;
- amendments, including the date and basis for the 2025 retainer increase;
- invoices, deliverables, payment approvals, committee minutes, and Wood-specific recusal/abstention records;
- ICE source-selection records, B.I.'s proposals, evaluator identities, organizational-conflict disclosures, and post-award contract-administration files.

## Negative and disconfirming checks

- An exact-name scan across 16 public ISAP V solicitation attachments found no occurrence of Guidepost Solutions, Julie Myers Wood, Matthew Albence, Daniel Ragsdale, Checkmate Government Relations, or GrindStone Strategic. Proposals and source-selection/evaluation records were not in the public attachment set, so this is a bounded negative result, not proof of no role.
- CourtListener searches returned three Guidepost-name dockets, zero results combining B.I. and Guidepost, and no located pleading that supplied the B.I. agreement scope or a Wood recusal record. This does not establish that no responsive litigation record exists.
- Guidepost's official public materials and its DOJ FARA registration describe non-ICE work, including compliance, monitoring, investigations, security consulting, and work for the Republic of the Marshall Islands. This supports the ordinary-course alternative that Guidepost has a broad consulting portfolio; it does not identify the B.I. scope.
- Peer practice is not unique to GEO: CoreCivic public releases document hiring former U.S. Marshals Service Director Stacia Hylton to its board in 2016 and former Bureau of Prisons Director Harley Lappin as an executive in 2011. This is a qualitative benchmark only; it does not answer transaction-specific conflict questions.
- The LDA record's “detention centers” phrasing cuts against treating Checkmate's filing as affirmative evidence of work on ISAP, an alternatives-to-detention program.

- #12452 — 2014-2024 annual SEC payment series totals $1,974,106.
