## Policy and budget alternative

The White House's January 20, 2025 order directed DHS to allocate resources or establish contracts for detention facilities 205 days before Checkmate began. Public Law 119-21 then appropriated $45 billion for ICE single-adult detention and family-residential capacity on July 4, 40 days before the engagement. This public policy-and-funding environment is a strong alternative explanation for the scale of later incumbent funding actions.
