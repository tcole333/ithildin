# Trump-linked investment, ownership, and financial relationships involving GEO

Date: 2026-07-13  
Profile: `geo-group`  
Lead: #58874  
Threads: 111 (primary), 112 (context only)

## Bottom line

Primary federal ethics records establish a direct reportable securities relationship between the Trump disclosure household and The GEO Group. Donald J. Trump's 2026 annual OGE report lists `GEO GROUP INC NEW REIT` in Investment Account #7 at **$50,001-$100,000**, with **$1,001-$2,500 in dividends**. The same annual report lists **ten purchases and four sales during 2025**. A separate 278-T reports another **$15,001-$50,000 purchase on March 4, 2026** and marks that notification was received more than 30 days later.

This is not merely institutional-fund proximity. It is a specifically named GEO security in a presidential financial disclosure. It is also not evidence that Donald Trump personally selected or beneficially owned the position: OGE instructs filers to report Part 6 assets and Part 7 transactions for the filer, spouse, and dependent children, while public forms omit family-member names. The strongest supported formulation is therefore **Trump household-reportable GEO interest**, not **Donald Trump personally traded GEO**.

Separately, Thomas D. Homan's official White House disclosure lists **GEO Care** and **Guidepost Solutions** as sources of more than $5,000 in compensation in at least one reporting-period calendar year for consulting services. That is pre-government source compensation, not current GEO ownership. Homan's Guidepost entry must also remain distinct from Guidepost's separately disclosed consulting agreement with GEO subsidiary B.I. Incorporated: the records do not say Homan's Guidepost work involved GEO.

## Homan compensation edge

Homan's new-entrant report identifies him as Border Czar, appointed January 20, 2025, signed February 10, 2025, and certified May 8, 2025. Part 4 lists:

- `Guidepost Solutions — Consulting Services`
- `GEO Care — Consulting Services`

Part 4 says it covers sources that paid more than $5,000 in a calendar year for the filer's services during any year of the reporting period, including clients to whom the filer personally provided services even if payment went to the filer's employer. The form gives no client-specific amount or service date. The official copy is the [White House Homan Form 278e](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Homan-Thomas.pdf). This independently verifies existing finding #12505; it does not duplicate or expand the characterization.

The Guidepost paths are separate:

```text
Homan -- reportable consulting compensation --> Guidepost
Guidepost -- separately disclosed consulting agreement --> B.I./GEO
```

No primary record reviewed says Homan worked on Guidepost's GEO/B.I. engagement. Treating that as one contract would collapse a second-degree network path into an unsupported direct relationship.

## SEC ownership and related-party checks

The SEC review covered all twelve GEO 10-Ks and twelve DEF 14As filed from 2015 through 2026, plus 48 issuer-linked Schedule 13 filings from 2015 through 2024. The Schedule 13 set consisted of 11 SC 13Gs and 37 SC 13G/As, with no SC 13D and no Trump or Kushner family name in the filings. One hundred recent Forms 3/4/5 were identified; 41 full 2026 forms were fetched and screened alongside the current proxy.

GEO's 2026 proxy says its ownership table covers directors, named executive officers, the officer/director group, and each person or group known to own more than 5%. It lists BlackRock at **19,860,856 shares (14.97%)** and Vanguard at **13,575,361 shares (10.23%)**. No Trump is listed. That absence is expected for a $50,001-$100,000 position and does not contradict the OGE record.

The institutional positions are manager-reported Schedule 13G holdings. They are context only. They do not establish that Trump is a beneficial owner through BlackRock, Vanguard, an index fund, or any 13F manager.

The 2026 proxy's Item 404 discussion identifies compensation to George Zoley relatives and the Guidepost agreement, then states that, except for those relationships, there were no other material relationships or related-party transactions requiring disclosure for fiscal 2025. No Trump-family contract, advisory role, or related-party transaction appeared. That negative is bounded by Item 404 materiality and disclosure rules.

## Lender and Trump Organization checks

GEO's April 18, 2024 credit agreement names Citizens Bank, N.A. as administrative agent and Citizens, TCBI Securities, and Synovus Securities as joint lead arrangers/bookrunners. The January 20, 2026 third amendment again names Citizens as agent and increases revolving commitments to **$550 million**.

The public exhibits do not disclose a complete current lender roster: commitment amounts sit in lender addenda, and the agreement says the agent maintains the lender register. The named institutions do not overlap with Deutsche Bank, Ladder Capital, Royal Bank of Pennsylvania/Bryn Mawr, and Capital One, the lenders named in the reviewed official New York `People v. Trump` record. This is a bounded negative, not proof that no syndicate participant, assignee, or ordinary banking relationship could overlap.

Trump, 2025 NY Slip Op 04756](https://www.nycourts.gov/reporter/3dseries/2025/2025_04756.htm).

## Other official disclosure negatives and limits

- Eight downloaded Kristi Noem and Pam Bondi nominee, ethics-agreement/certification, and transaction documents produced no exact `GEO Group`, `GEO Care`, or `Guidepost` hit.
- Trump's official OGE reports still available for direct download from 2020, 2021 termination, 2025 annual, 2025-2026 278-Ts, and 2026 annual were screened. GEO appears in the 2026 annual and one May 2026 278-T; no exact GEO security hit appeared in the 2020 annual, 2021 termination, or 2025 annual.
- OGE's index lists three Trump candidate reports from 2023-2024 as request-only. They were not obtained in this workstream, so the candidate-report negative universe is incomplete.
- Stephen Miller's current new-entrant report and transaction filing were request-only and were not reviewed. No conclusion is drawn about his holdings.
- The reviewed New York Trump Organization decision contained no `GEO Group` or `GEO Care` match and no overlap with GEO's named credit-facility institutions. It is not a complete registry of Trump entities, contracts, lenders, or brokerage accounts.
- OGE value ranges do not disclose share counts, cost basis, broker, voting authority, investment discretion, or the covered household member. The records do not establish strategic intent or a quid pro quo.
