The new rates/NTEs are redacted and all added CLINs show **$0.00 obligated**. The old **$228,019.07 per month** rate is historical, not current.
- The latest acquired public instrument ends its period of performance on **June 14, 2026**. No later P00044 or extension appeared in the bounded 2014–2026 Montgomery County agenda search. This does not prove services ended; status after June 14 remains unestablished.
- The 1,050 ICE-exclusive beds do not create a verified guarantee in the acquired record. The base agreement limits ICE liability to actual detainee days under written funded orders, and P00043 leaves other terms unchanged.
- FY2025 Fund 222's **$43,194,467** revenue and equal operations expenditure are one mixed ICE-and-USMS pass-through cycle. They cannot be split by agency or identified as a payment to GEO from the acquired ACFR.
- The 2013 signed management agreement names **GEO Corrections and Detentions, LLC**. A 2014 county purchasing memo corrected renewal P.O. 2013455's payee to **GEO Group, Inc.** Later records show GEO personnel and GEO-submitted REAs, but no acquired primary record establishes the current county payee, a legal assignment, or the current administrative fee.

## Accounting taxonomy

The records contain five different financial objects. They are kept separate throughout the matrix.

| Object | Meaning | Can be treated as cash paid? |
|---|---|---|
| Federal obligation/deobligation | ICE funds added to or removed from a Montgomery County task order | No; it is award authority, not proof of disbursement or downstream payment |
| Authorized retroactive invoice/adjustment | Amount ICE permitted to be invoiced on a task order | No; authorization is not evidence of Treasury payment or county remittance to GEO |
| Unit/monthly rate | Price applied to measured performance | No; requires actual days, hours, miles, receipts, or covered months |
| County Fund 222 annual flow | Mixed ICE and USMS receipts and equal operations expenditure | No exact GEO attribution; the two sides are one cycle, not additive |
| County-to-GEO term | Downstream contract fee or pass-through formula | Only if a ledger/check/invoice proves a transaction; the current ledger is missing |

## Two-facility transportation topology

P00041 does not create a second Joe Corley detention prime. It creates a transportation-service connection between two separately named facilities:

**ICE → Montgomery County → Joe Corley service provider → consolidated transport → Joe Corley + Montgomery Processing Center**

Montgomery Processing Center is independently present in the direct-prime ledger under GEO's HSCEDM17D00009 vehicle, including FY2025/2026 task order 70CDCR25FR0000097. P00041 nonetheless leaves the Joe Corley federal prime as Montgomery County. The supported conclusion is cross-facility scope consolidation, not a merger of awards, beds, or facility identities.

## Capacity liability

The 1,050-bed term is an exclusive-use allocation, while the base payment clause says ICE is financially liable only for actual detainee days and separately bars performance before a written funded order. P00043's “all other terms and conditions shall remain unchanged” language leaves those provisions intact in the acquired document chain. No verified calculation therefore multiplies 1,050 beds by any rate or day count.

Any liability estimate must use verified actual detainee days, applicable rate periods, task-order funding ceilings, and invoice acceptance. None of the acquired records supplies a complete FY2025 actual-day series.
