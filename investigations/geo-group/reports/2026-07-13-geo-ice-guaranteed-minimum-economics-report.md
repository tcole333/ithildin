## Outcome

The cross-facility comparison is defensible only when the evidence classes remain separate. Eleven facility/source contexts were reconstructed from ICE contracts and inspections, DHS OIG reports, a local operating agreement, and the completed direct-prime award-action ledger. They do **not** support a single rate or unused-bed-cost benchmark across GEO facilities.

Golden State Annex is the only GEO facility in the reviewed records with all three inputs needed for a period-level result: a numeric minimum, period-compatible occupancy, and a primary-source payment explicitly attributed to unused capacity. DHS OIG reported a 560-bed minimum, average daily population of 136 for April 20, 2022 through April 19, 2023, and approximately **$25.3 million paid for unused bed space**. The compatible bed-count calculation is `560 - 136 = 424 average unused guaranteed beds`. The $25.3 million is OIG's actual-payment finding, not an analyst estimate; no implied rate was calculated.

Tacoma supplies a historical same-record control: in January 2012, a 1,181-detainee minimum and 1,358-detainee inspection population imply `max(1,181 - 1,358, 0) = 0` unused guaranteed beds at that snapshot. It does not establish a period invoice. The LEDD-GEO agreement supplies a structural disconfirmation: for a facility expandable to 1,160 detainees, it says neither the State nor LEDD guaranteed a minimum. That local clause does not prove ICE's unrecovered federal IGSA had no commitment.

## Monetary labels

- **Unused-capacity payment:** Golden OIG amount, approximately $25.3 million. This is the only period-total dollar in that category.
- **Fixed monthly contract charge:** Torrance's three disclosed monthly values. OIG linked the arrangement to unused space, but did not isolate the unused portion in dollars.
- **Per-diem rates:** Tacoma's historical $100.65 minimum-population rate and $62.52 above-minimum rate.
- **Award-action obligation:** the six unique current actions totaling $308,998,179.99. Obligations are not payments, outlays, invoices, company revenue, or guaranteed-minimum charges.
- **Potential/ceiling value:** not used in any unused-bed calculation or total.
- **Pass-through/local expenditure:** not added to this matrix because the relevant earlier audited amount was not independently re-read in this workstream.

## Remedies and controls

- South Texas contract authority distinguishes a recoverable withholding from a nonrecoverable deduction and permits invoice adjustments for deficient performance. No actual South Texas deduction record was found.
- Folkston is an adverse-remedy case: DHS OIG found attempted staffing penalties were not enforced and full funding continued without consequence.
- Torrance is the non-GEO consequence control: ICE assessed a 25% monthly billing reduction and reduced the guaranteed minimum from 714 to 505.
- Tacoma is a zero-unused snapshot control because the inspected population exceeded the minimum.
- LEDD/LaSalle is a no-local-guarantee control. It is disconfirming evidence against a universal GEO template, subject to the missing federal IGSA.
