## Direct evidence of overwritten resources

The intact linearization xref stream (object 405) decodes with Predictor 12,
Columns 5, and `W [1 3 1]`. It places original objects 399-404 at offsets
42,347; 59,528; 60,279; 61,266; 62,274; and 85,669.

Every marker is absent from the current file. Those declared ranges now contain
the aligned Address Book plists or the Base64 JPEG. None is relocated into the
later float/code sectors. Therefore the missing page-one background, alpha
mask, gradient functions, and large image are demonstrably overwritten, not
merely hidden by a broken xref. The machine-readable map is
`output/pdf/epstein-recovery/EFTA00597207/xref405-map.json`.

Object 398 is a damaged embedded TrueType font. Its first 10,000 compressed
bytes produce 18,628 partial font bytes, but the stream continuation is
overwritten; it is not a complete font artifact.

and date recovered from the damaged source. Its remaining pages contain basic
specifications, detailed specifications, two layout slides, three picture
slides, and a price/contact slide. It is a production-rendered copy rather than

copy lost.

`A6-DFR_BBJ_Specs_-_May_2016-rev3.pdf`. The emails concern Royal Jet offering
A6-DFR (MSN 30884) for sale.
