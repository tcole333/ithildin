page because nearby object 1844 contains the overlay `154 of 234`. Independent
testing retracted that interpretation before publication.

CCITT Group 4 stream. Its Base64 matches the printed object-1847 span at

1847 is not missing internal page 154. No 235-page artifact was created and no
bytes or legal text were inferred.
