## Hypothesis exports are disputed baseline records

The eight files under `ach-baseline/` retain 70 assessment cells for hypotheses
464–471 in four groups. Every hypothesis was proposed at export. These are
historical analytic artifacts, not endorsed rankings, findings or conclusions.
The collection wave did not rescore or promote them.

The known ranking limitations remain material. Budget comparisons use unlike
campaign/date/population denominators; generic proxy support does not establish
political deployment; a professional agency association does not exclude a CPT
production function; and a support alias or unnamed legal-party wording does
not resolve business control. Some alternatives are nonexclusive or omit real
partly used funding and mixed independent-clippers/shared-tools explanations.
Do not use a mechanically lower inconsistency ratio as evidence of a winner.

## Exclusions and provenance limits

Raw HTML, public form administrative/runtime fields, session-bearing pages,
full forum renderings, challenge pages, unrelated search results, executable
client bundles and unrelated concurrent-worker findings stay outside this
bundle. Public URLs, business-role selectors and source hashes are retained
only where they document the commissioned source chain. No source was newly
retrieved, contacted, authenticated to, or modified for this review.

The original local raw files remain temporary for reproducibility. A hash proves
byte identity, not that a testimonial, advertised service or changelog claim is
true. The video-caption/audio HTTP statuses in B's ninth artifact are explicitly
collector transcriptions of contemporaneous tool stdout, not retained raw HTTP
headers. Empty result files confirm missing comparison bytes, not those HTTP
statuses independently.

The snapshot uses an explicit seven-ID allowlist. Pre-wave findings 15444–15446
and all concurrent other-worker findings, including 15452–15454 and
15457–15458, are excluded from the new finding count. Source QA may refer to
pre-wave context without converting it into commissioned discoveries.
