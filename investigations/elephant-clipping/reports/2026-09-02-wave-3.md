## Specific additions beyond the saved article

### Political-specific content-generation software

A newly reviewed [published Monster Lab client](https://monsterlab.io/_next/static/chunks/2315-13f181a882d3b3ad.js)
contains a changelog entry headed by a claim that **Political Clip mode** launched.
It describes converting a long podcast or interview into vertical shorts with
hooks and captions. The display field says **May 21**; the internal identifier
is **2026-05-21**. That is a publisher-assigned history claim observed now, not
an independently verified launch date. Finding **15449**.

The same client describes watchlist-based generation from new X-profile videos
into a History view, sharing uploaded source material across a clipping team by
role, and downloading completed clips as MP4s. These are concrete product
descriptions, but no identified political campaign, source supplier, account or
payment was linked to their actual use. No generation or private-data request
was executed. The record is now more specific than generic commercial tooling;
it still does not establish political-campaign deployment.

The separate [auto-submit client](https://monsterlab.io/_next/static/chunks/1824-444bfb31f106dbaf.js)
describes polling accounts for posts already published, collecting only posts
after activation, and submitting them to a selected campaign. This is a
client-described reward-submission workflow, not evidence that it publishes to
social accounts. Other capabilities remain untested. Finding **15447**.

The saved Atlantic article already discusses Gemini/ChatGPT idea generation and
payment review after posting. Those broad concepts are not new. The named mode,
specific changelog, and integrated generation/source-sharing descriptions are
additional detail relative to that supplied text. This is not a claim of first
public disclosure elsewhere.

## Search coverage and interpretation

The agents used actual Bing, Brave and Yahoo interfaces, with a further bounded
Mojeek attempt by the provenance track. Bing returned off-target results despite
echoing precise queries; Brave relaxed operators or returned 429; Yahoo returned
500; Mojeek returned 403. Those are documented coverage limitations, not strict
negative searches or proof no public documents exist. Prior challenged
DuckDuckGo/Yandex routes were not repeatedly retried. The useful forum lead came
through the web reader/search route.

The current hypothesis matrices were exported and inspected. Their earlier
rankings still depend on disputed comparisons and nonexclusive alternatives;
they were not used to declare a winner, and no hypothesis was promoted or
rescored during collection. Clarifying notes were attached to leads 94992,
94994, 94996, 94998 and 95000 before dispatch. In particular, a support alias
does not identify a principal, absent names in unrelated training files do not
disprove campaign uploaders, and a registry/payee no-hit cannot establish
fictitious budgets.
