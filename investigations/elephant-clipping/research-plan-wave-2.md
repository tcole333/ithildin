## Competing explanations and tests

- Shared content could reflect paid central tasking, a shared source library or
  scheduler, copying, or independent selection from a viral original. Compare
  edits, caption residue, lag, earliest source availability, and uncoded controls.
  A shared code alone cannot identify a sponsor or human owner.
- Generic region/proxy training may be ordinary commercial audience targeting;
  its existence is not proof of deployment by any political account. Seek an
  account- or campaign-specific bridge, and report the lack of one explicitly.
- An uploader may be distributing routine client material without commissioning
  a campaign. An employment match or display-name match cannot establish
  authorship, access ownership, funding, or approval.
- A missing merchant name may reflect incomplete website legal drafting rather
  than concealed control. Require a first-party/official legal-entity bridge;
  do not select a registry homonym because its name sounds plausible.
- Configured budgets, available balances, approved rewards, and released payouts
  are different measures. No displayed cap becomes actual spending by inference.

## Execution and report contract

Four independent agents are launched before waiting. Each reads the profile
guidance and relevant previous report, checks `search_log`, and uses its assigned
prefix under the work directory for all outputs. Reuse same-day negative source
coverage unless a new selector, source, or time range justifies another search.
Record every mandatory source as newly checked, prior-artifact reused, unavailable,
or inapplicable with a reason; never silently skip it.

User addition during execution: use search engines besides Google. A fifth,
bounded discovery track is therefore added explicitly. Its output compares
queries, engine, access time, retrieved result URLs/snippets, unique new URLs,
and inaccessible/challenged searches. An unspecified search-tool backend is not
represented as a named engine. Multiple engines returning the same underlying
page are discovery redundancy, not corroboration. Track E passes candidates to
the owning A–D researcher for primary-source verification and does not duplicate
their factual records. Search interfaces requiring authentication, challenge
bypass, or private index access are not used.

Each report includes YAML front matter with agent/target/skill/status and counts;
Key Discoveries; Findings Added (IDs); Connections/Entities; Negative Results;
Sources Checked (command, scope, output artifact, result, coverage limit);
Source Gaps; Follow-Up Leads; and a heading exactly `## Learnings`.
Findings and quoted evidence go into the profile-scoped database before the
report. Source URLs, selectors, acquisition time, hashes, and limitations must
be reproducible. No quantity target for findings. Avoid duplicate baseline facts.

All conclusions use the appropriate claim type and confidence ceiling. Personal
information is limited to relevant public professional identity. No passwords,
session material, signed-media URLs, banking suffixes, or incidental order IDs
go into durable artifacts or narrative. Raw viewer HTML stays temporary; preserve
sanitized inert notes and hashes for reviewed public documents.

The coordinator reviews reports rather than full transcripts, checks liveness
after five minutes, routes specific follow-ups, checks competing hypotheses,
ingests Learnings, runs the required post-wave auto-lead scan, and obtains an
independent evidence/hygiene review. Final deliverable: a durable wave-two brief
that clearly distinguishes new evidence, unresolved questions, and the next
most discriminating tests. Open questions are not marked solved merely because
the wave has ended.
