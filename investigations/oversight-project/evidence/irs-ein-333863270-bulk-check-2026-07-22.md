## Interpretation limits

This is a bounded public-record negative, not proof that the organization failed to
notify the IRS, failed to file, or is taxable. A new section 501(c)(4) organization
must submit nonpublic Form 8976, but may operate without the optional Form 1024-A
application and resulting determination letter. EO BMF and TEOS determination-letter
absence therefore cannot establish the organization's substantive tax status or an
exemption effective date.

The March 2026 Florida packet identifies a submitted Form 8868 attachment labeled
for an extension through 2026-11-15. Form 8868 cannot extend Form 990-N, so the label
is consistent with a planned Form 990 or 990-EZ, but the public packet does not embed
the actual Form 8868 and does not identify the return type. No delinquency inference
is warranted while that documented extension remains open.
