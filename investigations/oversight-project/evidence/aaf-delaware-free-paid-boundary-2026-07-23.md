Delaware defines the file number as the entity's unique identifier and the
incorporation/formation date as the effective date of the certificate. Its field
guide defines `Corporation` as the entity kind and `Exempt` as a Delaware Title
8 tax classification for a corporation meeting section 501 requirements. The
`Exempt` registry value is not, by itself, a fresh IRS recognition
determination.

The two current names map to two different file numbers and formation dates.
That supports treating them as separate Delaware legal files. Similar names do
not establish a predecessor, conversion, name-change, or other continuity
relationship.

## What the free page does not establish

The Division's FAQ states: “Search results will return both active and inactive
entities, however, this does not provide entity status.”

Accordingly, the free records do not establish:

- current status or status-as-of date;
- good standing;
- incorporator or certificate signatory;
- initial charter language;
- prior legal names;
- amendments, mergers, conversions, dissolutions, revivals, or historical
  registered-agent changes;
- officers, directors, or principal-office information from annual reports.

The free page displays the current entity name. It cannot show whether that name
resulted from an amendment. A complete certified history or the underlying
filing images are required before asserting that no prior name or continuity
event exists.

## Official paid-record boundary

| Official route | Published price | What Delaware says it returns | Important limit |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Online status | USD 10 | Current status and status-as-of date | Not a certified statement of good standing |
| Status, Tax and History Information | USD 20 | Status, status-as-of date, last five filings, franchise-tax assessment, total authorized shares if applicable, and tax due | Only the last five filings; no document images or officer/director information |
| Short-form certified status | USD 50 | Entity name and status | Does not provide the filing history or formation document |
| Long-form certificate | USD 175 | Current status plus all documents filed, dates and times, and any name changes | Certificate index/history; underlying filing images still must be ordered |
| Document Filing and Certificate Request Service | Fees assessed during processing | Requested certificates and plain or certified document copies | Submission channel only; no direct retrieval or up-front fee calculation |

## Disconfirmation discipline

The result disconfirms neither a historical Klimon/Compass role nor a
name-change or predecessor relationship: the free record lacks those fields.
Conversely, no such role or continuity event may be inferred merely from the
entities' names, common registered agent, or related tax reporting. AAF's
Schedule R evidence that it controlled AAF Action is a separate federal
tax-record proposition, not a Delaware formation-history fact.
