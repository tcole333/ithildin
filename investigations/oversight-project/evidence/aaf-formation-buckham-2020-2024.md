# AAF formation-era record and Matthew Buckham role

Date reviewed: 2026-07-23  
Lead: 73131  
Profile / thread: `oversight-project` / 180

## Result

The public primary record confirms Matthew Buckham's historical AAF co-founder role. It does not show that he was an initial director, officer, or incorporator.

- AAF's initial full Form 990, tax year 2021, identifies `MATTHEW BUCKHAM` with title `CO-FOUNDER`, 40 hours per week, `KeyEmployeeInd`, and $154,276 in reportable compensation. It does not mark him as an officer or individual trustee/director. IRS object: `202223199349311477`.
- AAF's tax-year-2022 return identifies him as `COFOUNDER (LEFT SEPT 2022)`, 40 hours per week, and a highest-compensated employee, with $130,588 in reportable compensation and $5,181 in other compensation. It again does not mark him as an officer or director. IRS object: `202323199349319772`.
- AAF's own archived leadership pages separately caption Thomas Jones and Matthew Buckham `Founder` on 2021-07-17 and 2022-08-08. The earliest archived homepage capture located, 2021-03-09, says, "The team at AAF is led by Tom Jones and Matt Buckham."
- By the 2022-10-05 and 2022-11-06 archived leadership captures, the page identified only `Thomas Jones — President and Founder` and contained no Matthew/Matt/Buckham match. The current team page likewise says, "AAF is led by Tom Jones." This matches the IRS return's September 2022 departure notation. It does not negate the historical founder title.
