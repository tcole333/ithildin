# Thomas Binion / Heritage consultancy continuity, 2022–2025

## Question and answer

Did the Heritage Foundation consultancy announced for Thomas (“Tommy”) Binion
when he left for OnMessage Public Strategies continue through the Oversight
Project’s 2025 spin-out?

**Result: not established by the public record reviewed.** Heritage directly
announced on September 8, 2022 that Binion “will remain a consultant to
Heritage.” No later public record located in this review states that the
consultancy continued, ended, or generated a particular payment. This is a
bounded negative, not evidence that the consultancy terminated.

## What the primary records establish

1. **Consultancy at departure.** Heritage’s September 8, 2022 announcement says:
   “Binion, who worked at Heritage for eight years, including the last four as a
   vice president, will remain a consultant to Heritage.” It does not state a
   term, scope, compensation, contracting entity, or end date.
2. **2022 compensation is reported as officer compensation.** Heritage’s
   calendar-year 2022 Form 990 identifies Binion as “VP, GOV'T RELATIONS,”
   reports 40 hours per week, $218,622 in reportable compensation from the
   organization, and $55,535 in other compensation. Schedule J reports
   $276,074 in total compensation. The return does not separately identify a
   post-departure consulting payment to Binion or OnMessage.
3. **OnMessage affiliation continued through the spin-out period.** The first
   located OnMessage Public Strategies LD-1, effective January 1, 2023 and
   posted February 15, identifies “TOMMY BINION” as the firm contact and
   “THOMAS BINION” as a lobbyist. A first-quarter 2025 LD-2 posted April 21,
   2025 again identifies him as contact and lobbyist.
4. **Oversight role is distinct from Heritage consultancy.** Florida’s
   charitable-registration application signed April 24, 2025 identifies Thomas
   Binion as an Oversight Project director. It does not describe his
   compensation or any Heritage appointment.
5. **Heritage’s own 2025 annual report confirms the spin-out but names a
   different continuing link.** It says the Oversight Project “take[s] flight
   as an independent investigative organization” and that Mike Howell
   “continues to serve as a visiting fellow at Heritage.” It does not mention
   Binion or OnMessage.

Part VII, Section B names only the five highest-paid independent contractors.
Accordingly, omission of Binion and OnMessage does not exclude:

- payments below the fifth-highest contractor amount;
- payments of $100,000 or less;
- payments embedded in an aggregate expense line;
- a consulting arrangement paid through another entity; or
- an unpaid or expired advisory arrangement.

The 2022 return’s annual officer-compensation reporting also does not allocate
Binion’s compensation between his employment period and any later consultancy.

## Audited statements and accounts-payable limit

Heritage’s audited consolidated statements for 2022, 2023, and 2024 contain no
“Binion” or “OnMessage” text. They report aggregate consultants and independent
contractors expense of $9,758,573 in 2022, $14,770,992 in 2023, and $21,884,139
in 2024. Accounts payable and accrued expenses were $8,791,794, $9,322,138,
and $12,160,896 at the respective year ends.

Those statements are consolidated and counterparty-blind; they are not an
accounts-payable ledger. Heritage is a privately supported nonprofit, and no
public vendor ledger, invoice, purchase order, or contract naming Binion or
OnMessage was located. USAspending returned no federal award for “OnMessage
Public Strategies” or “OnMessage Inc.” That search does not reach Heritage’s
private payments.

## Disconfirmation search

Searches for an explicit termination, end date, “no longer consultant”
statement, later Heritage consultant biography, later Heritage/OnMessage
announcement, or dated payment produced no such record. Heritage’s current
profile labels Binion a former vice president and says he is no longer a staff
member. That is consistent with his 2022 departure but does not answer whether
he remained an outside consultant.

## Conclusion and reopening standard

The evidence confirms the **start** of the consultancy in September 2022 but
does not confirm its **duration** through the 2025 spin-out. Binion’s 2025
Oversight directorship and continuing OnMessage role show overlapping network
positions, not payment or control by Heritage.

Reopen this issue only for a counterparty-specific record, such as:

- a Heritage–Binion or Heritage–OnMessage contract, amendment, termination, or
  invoice;
- a Heritage accounts-payable/vendor ledger entry;
- a later first-party statement describing the consultancy as current or ended;
- a Form 1099 or other dated payment record; or
- a later Form 990 schedule that actually names Binion or OnMessage.

Heritage’s TY2025 Form 990 was not present in the official IRS 2026 e-file index
as of July 22, 2026 (existing finding #14215). For a calendar-year filer, an
extended return could be due November 16, 2026. Heritage’s already-published
2025 annual report is not a Form 990 and does not cure that tax-record gap.
