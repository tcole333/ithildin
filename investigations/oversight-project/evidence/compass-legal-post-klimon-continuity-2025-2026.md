# Compass Legal continuity after William Klimon's Holtzman Vogel move

Date reviewed: 2026-07-23  
Lead: 73734  
Profile / thread: `oversight-project` / 180

## Result

The public record supports continued activity by Compass Legal Group after William
M. Klimon joined Holtzman Vogel on April 1, 2025. It does **not** support the
stronger claim that Klimon personally continued operating Compass as a private
dual-hat vehicle.

These are separate propositions:

- **Practice continuity is confirmed.** Federal committee reports disclose two
  post-move payments to `COMPASS LEGAL GROUP` for `LEGAL CONSULTING`: $739.70
  from Never Surrender, Inc. on September 10, 2025, and $2,564.00 from Sarah PAC
  on November 4, 2025. Compass also maintained a live public website, web
  hosting, DNS, and Google Workspace mail routing.
- **Klimon continuity is not established.** Holtzman Vogel's dated announcement
  says that before joining the firm Klimon "was senior counsel at Compass
  Legal." Its current biography calls him a Holtzman Vogel partner, describes
  the boutique-firm role as prior employment, and gives only
  `wklimon@holtzmanvogel.com` and Holtzman Vogel's telephone number.
- **The post-move record points away from dual-hatting.** New Wyoming
  incorporations after April 1 use Holtzman Vogel's address and
  `compliance@holtzmanvogel.com`, while the older Compass-routed filings predate
  the move. The two IRS EO BMF care-of rows containing Klimon/Compass have 2024
  ruling months and first appear in 2024 archive coverage; they are not fresh
  post-move formations.
- **The January 2026 Oversight Project annual report is contact reuse, not a
  Klimon service record.** It retains `wklimon@compasslegal.org` and
  `(202) 927-2309`, but Michael Howell—not Klimon—certified the report on
  January 22, 2026.

The bounded conclusion is therefore: Compass Legal remained an operating legal
services payee and public-facing practice, but no first-party or official record
located identifies Klimon as a post-move Compass employee, counsel, signatory, or
service provider. This does not exclude an undisclosed relationship.

## Website and infrastructure

Post-move archived homepages advertise:

> Compass Legal Group offers experienced legal guidance to public officials,
> candidates, nonprofit organizations, and others

They list campaign finance, government ethics, and nonprofit tax/governance
services and link to "Meet the Compass Team." The current `/staff` route returned
404 during this review, and the homepage itself did not name Klimon or any other
individual. Domain checks returned Cloudflare A/AAAA and authoritative
nameserver records, Google Workspace MX records, and a Google SPF record.

Those facts show maintained infrastructure and marketing. They do not identify
the people operating the firm, establish delivery to
`wklimon@compasslegal.org`, or prove dual employment.

## Corporate record limit

IRS and Department of Labor records identify Compass Legal Group Inc / Compass
Legal Services Inc as a Delaware taxable C corporation under EIN 86-2833005.
The accessible public record still does not supply a current Delaware
good-standing certificate, exact formation history, current officers, or
ownership. That documentary ceiling does not prevent resolution of this lead:
even proof that the corporation remains legally active would not establish
Klimon's personal post-move role.
