## Evidence and Language Rules

- Start with `tools/ingest_kabasshouse.py` for released-document text. Use EFTA
  or HOUSE_OVERSIGHT canonical IDs and quote the exact page.
- The same released page appearing in Kabasshouse, LMSBAND, DOJ, FBI, or Unified
  is one source represented multiple times, not corroboration.
- Existing generated dossiers and narrative files are discovery aids. Trace
  every important assertion back to `investigation.db` evidence links and the
  underlying primary document before creating a Brad Karp finding.
- Do not describe material as "leaked privileged information" merely because a
  lawyer discussed a client-adjacent matter. Establish the client, mandate,
  content, source, recipients, confidentiality, and privilege elements first.
  Until then, use neutral terms such as "shared information" or "information
  flow" and label any breach theory as a hypothesis.
- A government-released email confirms what the page records; it does not by
  itself prove every interpretation, professional-duty claim, or reported motive.
- Contact, social attendance, political donations, referrals, or representation
  do not independently establish misconduct.
