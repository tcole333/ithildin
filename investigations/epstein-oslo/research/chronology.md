# Oslo Channel Chronology Spine (WP0)

STATUS: COMPLETE FOR WP1 CORRELATION — 2026-07-17. Hilde Henriksen Waage
enumerates 12 formal Declaration-of-Principles (DoP) rounds. Ahmed Qurei calls
the 19–20 August initialing session the thirteenth round and the 30–31 August
mutual-recognition session the fourteenth. The final 8–10 September Paris
session is retained separately rather than silently renumbered.

### Date Disputes

- **London precursor:** the registered anchor is 4 December 1992. Qurei's
  contemporaneous-style report is dated 4 December but explicitly dates the
  two London sessions to **3 December**. Waage gives the enclosing multilateral
  window as 3–4 December. WP1 window: **2–5 December**.
- **Round 1:** Waage's formal range is 20–22 January. Qurei documents arrival
  at Fornebu late on 20 January and calls 21–22 January the talks. This is a
  travel-versus-working-session distinction, not necessarily a contradiction.
- **Round 5 venue:** Waage lists Thomas Heftye Villa; Qurei says an MFA
  guesthouse near the Royal Palace. The exact property is unresolved. Do not
  normalize either to “Thomas Heftyes gate.”
- **Round 6:** Waage's list gives 21 May only. Qurei arrived on 20 May and says
  the round ended on 22 May; Waage separately says Savir made his first Oslo
  trip on 20 May. WP1 window: **19–23 May**.
- **Round 7 numbering:** Qurei's chapter heading calls Singer's entry the
  seventh round, but one sentence calls its first session the “sixth round.”
  Waage's sequence and Qurei's heading support round 7; treat the sentence as
  an internal memoir/editorial anomaly.
- **Round 8:** Waage gives 25–27 June. Qurei says Hirschfeld and Pundak arrived
  on 25 June but the working session with Singer occurred 27 June; Singer
  independently dates his own Oslo meeting to the night of 27 June. WP1
  should retain the entire 25–27 June movement window.
- **Round 9:** Qurei says the PLO team returned to Oslo on 3 July; Waage dates
  the talks 4–6 July. Use 3 July as travel day and 4–6 July as formal round.
- **Round 10:** Waage's consolidated list says 10–11 July, one narrative
  passage says 10–12 July, and a detailed passage plus Qurei give 11–12 July.
  Qurei records arrival on 11 July and adjournment on 12 July. WP1 window:
  **9–13 July**.
- **Round 11:** Waage's list says 25–26 July while her prose says 24–26 July.
  Qurei traveled on 24 July and labels the working days 25 and 26 July; the
  two accounts reconcile when travel is separated from sessions.
- **Round 12:** Waage's list says 13–14 August; elsewhere she calls the
  Sarpsborg round 13–15 August. Qurei records reunion on 13 August and the
  first formal session the next morning. WP1 window: **12–16 August**.
- **Round count:** Waage calls 13–14 August the twelfth and final formal DoP
  round. Qurei calls 19–20 August round 13 and the 30 August Oslo Plaza
  session round 14; he says Holst publicly announced 14 rounds while round 14
  was still underway. Both taxonomies are retained.
- **Initialing:** Qurei planned to initial on 19 August; the ceremony occurred
  after midnight on 20 August. Use 19 August for arrivals/plenary and 20
  August for the act of initialing.
- **Point Mugu:** Waage says the party set off for the United States on 27
  August; Singer's relative chronology can be read as a 27 August meeting,
  while Qurei says the Israeli cabinet received the report on 29 August from
  the meeting “the night before.” WP1 window: **26–30 August**.
- **Paris telephone session:** Waage places it on 3–4 September and says Holst
  returned to Oslo on 4 September. Qurei says Peres returned to Israel on the
  night of 5 September and Holst went to Oslo. Retain **3–5 September**.
- **Final Paris session:** Qurei and Singer date the face-to-face meeting from
  the afternoon of 8 September into the morning of 9 September; Waage says
  the Israelis returned on 9 September and agreement came at dawn on 10
  September. The recognition letters are dated 9 September and Rabin signed
  on 10 September. Do not average: search **7–11 September**.
- **Holst/Cairo, 6 September:** Savir's account puts Holst in Cairo with Arafat
  and Mubarak. Corbin puts him in Oslo; Waage reconstructs Paris→Oslo on 4
  September, a Norwegian interview on 5 September, and Oslo→Paris on 7
  September. The table uses Oslo but preserves the dispute.
