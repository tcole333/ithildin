### 1. Hilde Henriksen Waage

**Who she is.** Waage is a historian and source-critical scholar of Norway's
Middle East policy. PRIO currently lists her as a research professor and as a
University of Oslo professor of history, both since 2005. Fritt Ord identified
her as 66 when it awarded her its 2026 prize. She is in an active news cycle:
PRIO lists repeated February 2026 interviews about the missing Oslo-process
documents, academic freedom, and the Epstein disclosures, and Fritt Ord's prize
notice foregrounds her archival work. [PRIO current profile](https://www.prio.org/people/3470)
| [Fritt Ord 2026 profile](https://frittord.no/en/prizes/the-freedom-of-expression-prize/hilde-henriksen-waage)

**What she uniquely knows.** Waage received privileged access to relevant,
still-classified UD files for her 1993–96 study. Her 2004 finding was not that
every ministry folder was blank, but that the substantive back-channel record—
minutes, memoranda, letters, and records of specified ministerial
conversations—was absent. Her footnotes map surviving adjacent material in
`UD 25.11/19ø` binds 1 and 3, `UD 25.11/19n` bind 3, `UD 25.11/19s` binds 1–2,
`UD 11.7/4` binds 29–30, `UD 34.4/87` bind 6, and `UD 25.11/19å` bind 2. She
also personally saw the few Holst papers Marianne Heiberg held in 2002,
including copies of the four mutual-recognition letters. Finally, she is the
primary witness to the spring-2000 meeting in which she says Mona Juul tried to
halt funding or continuation of phase two; WP6 establishes that phase one was
published on schedule and phase two was commissioned in January 2001.
[WP6 findings 1–5](wp6-archival-void.md)

**Public contact surface.** PRIO and the University of Oslo are the relevant
public institutional affiliations. This dossier intentionally omits personal
telephone, email, and address details.

**Risk and sensitivity.** Waage is an independent, source-critical scholar in
the middle of a live Norwegian news cycle. Ask for scope, exact references, and
records rather than speculation about motive. She is not a charged party. Any
request must be human-approved, and no approach to the charged parties or their
circle for comment should occur without editorial and legal review.

1. **Question.** In the UD series you cite—especially `25.11/19ø`, `25.11/19n`, `25.11/19s`, `11.7/4`, `34.4/87`, and `25.11/19å`—and in the Holst papers Marianne Heiberg showed you in 2002, did any American private funding line or private intermediary appear in peace-process-adjacent material from 1991–93; if so, what is the exact file, date, or document description?
   - **Why answerable-narrow:** It is confined to records Waage personally reviewed and asks for a yes/no plus a document pointer, not an interpretation of the whole process.
   - **Update map:** A named, contemporaneous U.S. private funding line tied to channel costs could supply the missing test for rule 4 and justify reconsidering H1; a private intermediary without channel function would be an H2 lead; a no after a stated search scope would add only a bounded archival null; inability to recall or identify the file would leave coverage unchanged.

2. **Question.** Do you retain any contemporaneous note, calendar entry, email, or draft memorandum recording the spring-2000 meeting with Mona Juul, including who attended and the precise decision about funding the second study; if so, where is that record held?
   - **Why answerable-narrow:** It asks about one meeting, four document types, and custody of any surviving record.
   - **Update map:** A contemporaneous record would fix the participants, wording, and administrative effect; a record showing only a funding discussion would reinforce WP6's corrected formulation; a record showing a broader restriction would revise the episode's scope; no record would leave the account as retrospective testimony with Dan Smith's partial recollection.

3. **Question.** Across every pre-2000 document you have personally reviewed in this domain—including UD files, the cited adjacent series, and the Holst material shown by Heiberg—has the name Jeffrey Epstein, or a clearly identified Epstein entity or intermediary, ever appeared; if yes, what is the exact document pointer, and if no, what is the boundary of the reviewed set?
   - **Why answerable-narrow:** This is the plan-brief name test, limited to Waage's direct documentary knowledge and answerable with a citation or a defined-scope null.
   - **Update map:** A verified pre-2000 name hit would be a major H2 lead and would affect H1 only if the document showed channel funding, logistics, communications, or introduction; a defined no would strengthen a bounded external-archive null but would not overcome left-censoring elsewhere; an unidentified intermediary would require entity verification before any hypothesis update.

### 2. Joel Singer

**Who he is.** Singer served as legal adviser to Israel's Ministry of Foreign
Affairs from 1993–96 after directing the IDF International Law Department. His
current public biography places him in a post-government, post-law-firm career
stage: he retired from private practice in 2021 and publishes as an independent
scholar and commentator. His author site remains live and maintains a detailed
article/interview archive; the latest substantive item currently listed there
is an Israel Policy Forum video dated 19 September 2023, while the site's policy
pages were updated in 2024. [Singer biography](https://www.joelsinger.org/bio/)
| [articles and interviews](https://www.joelsinger.org/media/)

**What he uniquely knows.** Singer can speak from direct knowledge about his
own entry into the official-level talks, travel, authorization, document flow,
and drafting. WP0 places his entry at round 7 on 14 June 1993 and identifies him
as principal at round 8 on 27 June. His published account supplies
first-person movements and legal chronology, including the drafting and
negotiation of mutual recognition. His site demonstrates a willingness to
answer highly specific questions about text, sequence, and negotiating roles.
[WP0 findings 3 and 5](wp0-chronology-spine.md) | [Singer on mutual
recognition](https://www.joelsinger.org/the-israel-plo-mutual-recognition-agreement/)

**Public contact surface.** No current university or institute affiliation was
verified. The only appropriate public professional surface identified for this
prep is the author site. Personal contact details are deliberately omitted.

**Risk and sensitivity.** Singer is a participant with a stake in how legal
authorship and negotiating authority are narrated. Treat first-person answers
as high-value leads to records, not independent corroboration of his own role.
He is not a charged party. Human approval is required before any request, and
the charged parties' circle remains outside scope absent editorial and legal
review.

1. **Question.** For the trip on which you first joined the Oslo talks on 14 June 1993, who booked and paid the airfare, lodging, and local transport, and to which office or organization did you submit any travel or reimbursement record?
   - **Why answerable-narrow:** It concerns one dated trip and three expense categories from Singer's own travel record.
   - **Update map:** Israeli-government payment would close one logistics line; Norwegian MFA/Fafo payment would corroborate the existing public-funding map; ECF or FES payment would clarify the early-support mechanics; a named U.S. private payer would require contemporaneous documentation and could reopen rule 4 only if tied to channel costs; no retained record would define a custody gap.

2. **Question.** When you began converting the academic text into the Declaration of Principles, did any U.S.-based private lawyer, financier, or adviser outside the Israeli and PLO delegations and their governments review, transmit, or comment on a draft; if yes, who and which version?
   - **Why answerable-narrow:** It asks about Singer's own drafting chain, a defined actor class, and an identifiable draft version.
   - **Update map:** No would support a closed governmental/participant drafting chain; a named person with a draft pointer would create a targeted H2 or H1 lead depending on function; informal advice without a record would remain uncorroborated; documentary participation in drafting or transmission could justify reconsidering facilitation evidence but would not by itself prove funding.

3. **Question.** Which archive, institution, or personal custodian now holds your June–September 1993 draft versions, calendars, and travel records, and is there a file or collection identifier that a researcher can cite?
   - **Why answerable-narrow:** It requests custody and a finding-aid pointer rather than a narrative account.
   - **Update map:** A public or institutional identifier would create a primary-record route for travel and drafting tests; personal custody would define a preservation and access issue for human editors; destruction or non-retention would explain a coverage boundary; uncertainty would not change H1, H2, or H3.

### 3. Yair Hirschfeld

**Who he is.** Hirschfeld led the initial unofficial Israeli academic team in
the Oslo channel and co-founded ECF. The University of Haifa now lists his
senior-lecturer affiliation as 1989–2024 and separately identifies him as
professor emeritus. A 2025 Fathom profile identifies him as ECF director general
and currently teaching at Haifa. He remains publication-active at an emeritus
career stage: the university lists a 2024 book, *The Israeli–Palestinian Peace
Process: A Personal Insider's Account*, and Fathom published his diary in April
2025 and a book review in September 2025. [University of Haifa research
profile](https://cris.haifa.ac.il/en/persons/yair-hirschfeld/) | [Fathom author
profile](https://fathomjournal.org/author/yair-hirschfeld/)

**What he uniquely knows.** Hirschfeld is the strongest direct witness to ECF's
founding funders and the mechanics of early support. WP5 identifies FES
collaboration beginning in September 1991, an EC Commission research contract
for his September 1992 report, and Norwegian MFA/Fafo salary and logistics from
the 9 September 1992 commitment into the 1993 channel. The public record does
not identify amounts, payee accounts, grant numbers, or a complete 1991–93
donor schedule. No American private donor to ECF in the window was verified.
[WP5 findings 1–5 and 7](wp5-ecf-funding.md)

**Public contact surface.** The University of Haifa and the Economic
Cooperation Foundation are the relevant public institutional affiliations.
This dossier excludes personal contact information.

**Risk and sensitivity.** Hirschfeld is a participant and ECF founder with a
stake in the founding and Oslo narratives. Funding recollections should be
converted into grant numbers, ledger entries, or custodial pointers. He is not
a charged party. Any request requires human approval, and no approach to the
charged parties or their circle should occur without editorial and legal
review.

1. **Question.** ECF's first two years were supported by FES and an EC Commission research contract in the sources reviewed; were there any other funders of any nationality in 1991–93, and does ECF or another custodian hold the year-one ledger or donor schedule that names them?
   - **Why answerable-narrow:** It requests a finite funder roster and one accounting record for a three-year window.
   - **Update map:** A complete European-institutional roster would tighten the existing null; a named U.S. private donor would become a rule-4 lead but would require proof of receipt and channel use; an in-kind supporter would update the support map without necessarily updating H1; no surviving ledger would preserve the account-completeness limitation.

2. **Question.** In 1991–93, did FES pay ECF, reimburse you or Ron Pundak directly, or pay travel and service providers itself, and what grant, project, or ledger identifier records that arrangement?
   - **Why answerable-narrow:** The answer falls into three payment routes and asks for one identifier.
   - **Update map:** Payment to ECF would establish a direct early receipt; individual reimbursement would explain support without an ECF ledger entry; provider payment would explain an off-account support line; an identifier would enable T1/T2 document retrieval; inability to distinguish the route would leave WP5's qualified wording unchanged.

3. **Question.** For the EC Commission contract behind your September 1992 report, who was the contracting party and payee, what were the amount and payment dates, and did any of that contract pay for Oslo-channel travel or meetings through September 1993?
   - **Why answerable-narrow:** It is tied to one named contract and asks for standard contract fields plus a yes/no cost-allocation test.
   - **Update map:** Research-only spending would separate the EC line from channel costs; documented channel spending would revise the early-funding map but remain European public support; a different payee could explain why no ECF receipt is visible; an additional private co-funder would require its own provenance before any hypothesis update.

January 1993. WP6 further reports that Egeland delivered three home-held
documents during the later archive recovery, including an early
economic-cooperation draft. He can identify the budget post, the expected
accounting/travel series, and the provenance and current custody of those three
documents. [WP0 finding 7](wp0-chronology-spine.md) | [WP5 finding
5](wp5-ecf-funding.md) | [WP6 finding 5 and prepared target B](wp6-archival-void.md)

   - **Why answerable-narrow:** It is limited to three known documents and standard provenance fields.
   - **Update map:** Complete provenance would define what has been recovered and prevent overclaiming the archive's completeness; an original UD document would bear on official custody; a Fafo or personal copy would clarify mixed provenance; a record mentioning funding would create a targeted accounting lead; none of these outcomes alone would reopen H1.

### 5. Yossi Beilin

**Who he is.** Beilin, born in 1948 and now in his late 70s, is a former
Israeli justice minister, former deputy foreign minister, ECF co-founder, and
founder and Israeli chair of the Geneva Initiative. He remains publicly
active: ECF identifies him as a co-leader of the Holy Land Confederation work,
whose second edition was updated in July 2025; his memoir *Secrets I Won't Take
with Me* was published in 2025; and he gave a detailed interview in December
2025 about the two-state solution and Israel's 2026 elections. [Geneva
Initiative current media profile](https://geneva-accord.org/resources/) |
[ECF 2025 confederation edition](https://content.ecf.org.il/files/The%20Holy%20Land%20Confederation%20as%20a%20Facilitator%20for%20the%20Two-State%20Solution%20-%20English%20-%202nd%20edition.pdf)
| [2025 memoir record](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.book.epl?ISBN=9780817362027)
| [December 2025 interview](https://www.ips-journal.eu/interviews/the-two-state-solution-is-back-on-the-agenda-8713/)

**What he uniquely knows.** Beilin supplies the political layer above ECF's
academic work. ECF's current founders page names Beilin, Hirschfeld, and Boaz
Karni, while a participant retrospective expands the working founding circle
to include Nimrod Novik and Ron Pundak and identifies Karni with finance and
operations. Beilin can distinguish legal founders, political sponsors, and
people with actual financial authority. His participant account, *Touching
Peace*, is also the source cited for Norway meeting flights, accommodation,
materials, secrecy, and security costs. [WP5 findings 2 and 5](wp5-ecf-funding.md)
| [WP0 source-availability record](wp0-chronology-spine.md)

**Public contact surface.** The Geneva Initiative and ECF are the relevant
public institutional affiliations. No personal contact details are included.

**Risk and sensitivity.** Beilin is a senior participant with political and
authorial stakes in the founding and Oslo narratives. Use his answers to locate
founding records, ledgers, and source documents, then cross-check them against
Hirschfeld and institutional files. He is not a charged party. Human approval
is required before any request, and no approach to the charged parties or their
circle should occur without editorial and legal review.

1. **Question.** In ECF's 1991–93 founding period, which of Yair Hirschfeld, Boaz Karni, you, Nimrod Novik, and Ron Pundak had legal signature or financial authority, and where are the founding instrument, first bank mandate, and year-one ledger now held?

   - **Update map:** Identifying Karni or another officer would assign the best next accounting witness; a founding instrument would resolve the public roster conflict; a bank mandate or ledger could test direct receipts; no surviving records would preserve WP5's account-completeness limit; role descriptions without documents would remain participant testimony.

2. **Question.** Besides FES and the EC Commission research contract, which funders of any nationality supplied ECF with cash or in-kind support in 1991–93, and were any of those commitments obtained through your Labor Party or Foreign Ministry political network?
   - **Why answerable-narrow:** It asks for a bounded three-year roster, separates cash from in-kind support, and limits the political-mechanics inquiry to Beilin's own network.
   - **Update map:** Additional European institutional support would complete the roster without affecting rule 4; a named U.S. private commitment would become a rule-4 lead only after receipt and channel use were documented; political introductions without money would clarify mechanism but not funding; no additional funders would corroborate Hirschfeld's bounded answer rather than independently prove completeness.

3. **Question.** In *Touching Peace*, your account says Norway met flights, accommodation, materials, secrecy, and security costs; which contemporaneous records or personal notes supported that passage, and what archive or custodian holds them now?
   - **Why answerable-narrow:** It ties the request to one published passage and asks only for its documentary basis and custody.
   - **Update map:** A voucher, itinerary, or Fafo/MFA record would harden a T2 participant account into a primary-record route; notes naming the responsible budget line would cross-check Egeland; a recollection-only basis would keep the claim at participant-account strength; any private payer named in the source records would require independent provenance and allocation testing.
