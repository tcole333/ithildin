## Findings

### 1. Passport primary record corrects the launch fact

**Claim — T1; evidence dated 2019-07-16.** In *United States v. Jeffrey Epstein*, 19 Cr. 490 (RMB), the government said agents found what appeared to be an expired foreign passport in a safe at Epstein's Manhattan residence. It bore a photograph that appeared to depict Epstein but “lists a different name.” The government was still investigating “whether the passport is genuine or fabricated.” Citation: SDNY Dkt. 23 at 2, `EFTA00014494` (full filing `EFTA00014493`; duplicate extraction `EFTA00016749`, full filing `EFTA00016748`).

**Claim — T1; evidence dated 2019-07-18.** Judge Richard M. Berman's remand decision described an “expired Austrian passport bearing Mr. Epstein's photo but not his name” and stated: “The Austrian passport lists residence in Saudi Arabia.” Citation: SDNY Dkt. 32 at 24–25, `EFTA00039406`–`EFTA00039407` (full document `EFTA00039383`; clean extractions `DOJ-OGR-00000495`–`DOJ-OGR-00000496`).

**Claim — T1; evidence dated 2019-07-16 to 2019-07-18.** The defense said Epstein **acquired**, not that authorities issued him, the passport in the 1980s as protective identification “in connection to Middle East travel.” It later said a friend gave it to him, he “never used the document to travel internationally,” and the stamps predated his receipt. The government said the document contained 1980s entry/exit stamps for France, Spain, the United Kingdom, and Saudi Arabia. Citations: SDNY Dkt. 24 at 7–8, `DOJ-OGR-00000451`–`DOJ-OGR-00000452`; Dkt. 30, `EFTA00016172`; Dkt. 31, `DOJ-OGR-00000471`; Dkt. 32 at 25, `EFTA00039407`.

**Corrected launch fact — synthesis, T1 record basis; assessed 2026-07-18.** An expired Austrian passport bearing Epstein's photograph, another name, and a Saudi residence was seized in 2019. The July 2019 bail record left authenticity and procurement unresolved. “Fake,” “1980s-issued,” and “used by Epstein for Middle East travel” are not established by those filings; the last proposition was expressly denied by the defense. Later FBI/evidence records identify the displayed name as **Marius Robert Fortelni** (`EFTA01307727`, `EFTA00163566`, `EFTA01656317`), but those later records do not resolve when Epstein received or used the document.

### 2. Douglas Leese claim: T4 provenance, no independent introduction proof

**Claim — underlying source T4, interview in fall 2002; T2 publication dated 2003-03.** The earliest located publication of the claim is Vicky Ward's [March 2003 *Vanity Fair* article](https://www.vanityfair.com/news/2003/03/jeffrey-epstein-200303): “According to Hoffenberg, the two men were introduced by Douglas Leese, a defense contractor.” Ward immediately recorded a competing account: “Epstein has said they were introduced by John Mitchell.” Ward later dated her Hoffenberg prison interview to fall 2002. A DOJ-hosted court exhibit reproduces the article but is not independent confirmation: [*USVI v. JPMorgan*, SDNY No. 1:22-cv-10904-JSR, Dkt. 187-11](https://www.justice.gov/multimedia/Court%20Records/Government%20of%20the%20United%20States%20Virgin%20Islands%20v.%20JPMorgan%20Chase%20Bank%2C%20N.A.%2C%20No.%20122-cv-10904%20%28S.D.N.Y.%202022%29/187-11.pdf), filed 2023-06-15.

**Claim — T4; evidence dated 2019-08-10.** Hoffenberg later repeated that Douglas Leese introduced him to Epstein; the report said Leese did not respond to comment requests. [Washington Post analysis syndicated by *Portland Press Herald*](https://www.pressherald.com/2019/08/10/analysis-epstein-slipped-away-from-prosecutors-and-victims-for-30-years/). The repetition is the same narrator, not corroboration.

**Association evidence — T2; published 2025-12-16 about 1981 onward.** A [*New York Times* investigation](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/12/16/magazine/jeffrey-epstein-money-scams-investigation.html), drawing in part on Tom Pattinson's unpublished interview transcript with Douglas's son Julian Leese plus other interviews and records, reports that Paula Heil brought Epstein to the Leese family in 1981, Douglas took Epstein on as a consultant and mentored him, and Julian described Epstein as “part of the family.” The same report says **Julian introduced Hoffenberg to Douglas**, who then introduced Hoffenberg to prospective bond buyers. This is independent T2 support for early-1980s Epstein–Douglas Leese contact, not T1 evidence and not confirmation of Hoffenberg's claimed introduction direction. [Accessible syndication](https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/scams-schemes-ruthless-cons-the-untold-story-of-how-epstein-got-rich-125122500276_1.html).

**Verified later name-hits — T1; evidence dated 2011, 2015, and 2017.** Three underlying primary email threads survive in 16 duplicate/re-OCR rows:

- **2011-05-12:** Epstein wrote, “douglas Leese died last week” (`EFTA01777995`, `EFTA01865271`).
- **2015-12-27:** Epstein wrote, “ask him if he knew a Douglas Leese in london” (`EFTA02477052`).
- **2017-05-09:** Gia Leese referred to “the Douglas Leese mindset” in an email to Epstein (`EFTA02650447`; re-OCR `EFTA01046582`).

**Provenance verdict — synthesis; assessed 2026-07-18.** The located **introduction claim** originates with Hoffenberg, a designated T4 narrator, and is materially challenged by Julian Leese's participant account as reported at T2. The early-1980s **association** has T2 support but no located T1 contract, contemporaneous correspondence, deposition, corporate ledger, or court finding. No Robert Trivers primary assertion or independent T1/T2 confirmation was found for the Hoffenberg introduction, an Epstein arms or money-laundering role, or a Leese-mediated Khashoggi connection.
