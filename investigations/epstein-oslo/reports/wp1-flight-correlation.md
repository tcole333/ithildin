# WP1 — Flight-log correlation, 1991–1994

## Summary

**Rule-1 verdict: not triggered.** Across every widened round window and every Shuttle & Side Trips window, Rodgers' log contains **zero Oslo/Fornebu, London, Tunis, Stockholm, or other target European/MENA legs**.

The positive observation is a bounded null: 12 of 17 core windows contain 39 recorded legs, 37 wholly within the United States and two forming a Columbus–Montreal–Rochester return; none crossed the Atlantic. The structured passenger transcription names Epstein on 34/39 of those rows. The six-leg North American loop during the final Paris window is the densest high-value null.

The December 1992–September 1993 baseline contains 147 dated log-derived legs, 131 with Epstein named in the derivative passenger field. Movement is dense enough that the logged domestic pattern is evidentiary within Rodgers' instrument, not merely an empty corpus.

Epstein's window fleet was a Hawker Siddeley HS125-700 (first logged under N404CB, then N908JE, later N988JE). In 1994 Hyperion Air added Gulfstream G-1159B serial 151, formerly N979GA; FAA authorized its change to N908JE on 1994-04-20. N120JE and the Boeing 727's use of N908JE are later and out of scope.

This result does **not** establish Epstein's physical absence from Europe or MENA. Rodgers recorded only flights he flew; Visoski, charter, and commercial movement are not visible. Rule 1 supplies no negative-update formula, and the investigation's left-censoring warning bars converting a partial-instrument null into evidence for H3.

Of the 30 pages covering April 1991–October 1994, 17/30 met the explicit working-legibility threshold (at least 75% of rows with an unambiguous date/from/to reading); 377/538 rows (70.1%) met that row-level test. The remaining pages are included, with uncertainty marks preserved.

### Legibility and completeness

The 1991–94 extraction includes all 30 exhibit pages and all 538 transcribed rows, including repositioning, tests, simulator entries, and multi-stop rows. A row was counted as route-legible only when its date, origin, and destination were nonblank and did not contain `?`, `[illegible]`, or `[blank]`. On that strict reproducible rule, 377/538 rows (70.1%) were route-legible and 17/30 pages had at least 75% route-legible rows. The other 13 pages are **partially legible**, not absent; no page was discarded. The derivative endpoint expands some multi-stop rows, which explains its 557-flight count rather than 538 log rows.

## Update-Rule Triggers

> “Rodgers log shows Oslo/London/Tunis/Stockholm within ±3 days of a verified channel round → major update H1/H2. Single European hit without date correlation → weak; Epstein flew Europe socially. Require ≥2 correlated hits or a venue-specific hit (Fornebu) before treating as strong signal.”

**Trigger: none.** There is no single European hit, no correlated hit, and no Fornebu hit in 1991–94. Rule 1 therefore supplies no update to H1 or H2. It contains no pre-committed negative-update rule, so this report does not improvise one. The bounded Rodgers-log null is retained as evidence about this instrument only; it is not treated as evidence for H3.

## Limitations

- Rodgers' personal log covers only flights Rodgers flew. It is not a fleet-wide movement ledger.
- Lawrence Visoski's legs are separate and mostly unreleased for this period. A trip flown by Visoski would be invisible here.
- Charter and commercial travel are invisible. So are ground transfers and any travel in another person's aircraft.
- Rodgers testified that entries such as `one female` or `two females` meant he did not know the passengers' names. His passenger recording was therefore explicitly imprecise. See the [trial transcript, vol. 8, pp. 152–53](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/14/USA_vs._Maxwell_jury_trial_transcript_-_volume_8.pdf) and the [Law&Crime exhibit report](https://lawandcrime.com/live-trials/ghislaine-maxwell/jeffrey-epstein-flight-logs-showing-detailed-passenger-lists-entered-into-evidence-at-ghislaine-maxwell-trial/).
- Handwriting OCR is unreliable, especially on pp. 6, 8, 12, 20–22, and 26. Tail letters, airport codes, and years are recurrent failure points. The conflict table identifies every disagreement material to the correlation result.
- Only 17/30 pages met the 75% working-legibility threshold; 377/538 rows had an unambiguous date/from/to reading. Uncertain rows remain in the appendix rather than being silently repaired.
- Before April 1991 there are no pages in this log, and before the first Epstein-associated Hawker trip in July 1991 this instrument shows no Epstein-controlled aircraft. Pre-1991 movement cannot be tested.
- The current FAA registry is current-state and N-numbers are reused. It cannot by itself establish 1991–94 ownership.
- The same released exhibit appearing in DOJ-OGR, FBI, Maxwell-trial, Carstensen, and EpsteinExposed forms is redundancy, not corroboration.
- **Left-censoring warning:** the email corpus begins in 2008, financial transactions become substantive in 1999, and the few parseably dated early-1990s documents are overwhelmingly these flight logs. A late corpus terminus is uninformative, and this partial aviation null must not be generalized to H3.
