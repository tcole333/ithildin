## Summary

- **(a) Maxwell in the Owens circle — no documented connection. [T1/T2 sources dated 1989–2024; searched 2026-07-18]** Exact searches returned no Robert Maxwell name-hit with Wayne Owens, S. Daniel Abraham, David Jan Mitchell, Joyce Starr, the Center for Middle East Peace, or the Middle East Development Bank. H.J.Res. 213, the reviewed trip reporting, Center pages/returns, Starr texts, and Owens receipts also contain no Maxwell hit. This is a bounded source-set null, not proof of universal absence.
- **(b) Earliest documented Robert-related Epstein datapoint — November 1991, after Robert's death. [T1 visual / T2 caption]** A photograph shows Jeffrey Epstein with Ghislaine Maxwell and Tony Randall at a November 1991 YIVO event at the Plaza Hotel honoring the late Robert Maxwell. Craig Unger's 2021 account dates the event to November 24, nineteen days after Robert's death. It documents Epstein beside Ghislaine at Robert's memorial; it does **not** document Robert and Epstein together.
- **Pre-death Robert–Epstein contact — no verified T1/T2 connection. [T1/T2 records covering 1991–2025; assessed 2026-07-18]** In a 2025 DOJ interview, Ghislaine said she met Epstein sometime in 1991 and told her father about him before Robert died; she also said Robert and Epstein “never met” and had no connection. That is a late, self-interested participant account, confirmed only as her statement. Tom Bower, after reviewing Robert Maxwell's private papers, said Epstein was not listed among Robert's New York contacts. One quality reporter asserted a “close relationship” in 2021 without disclosing supporting evidence. No contemporaneous document, calendar, photograph, ledger, contract, correspondence, or independently sourced T2 account placing the living men together was located. The affirmative introduction/use/money narratives located are contested T4 claims only and appear exclusively in the quarantined ledger below.
- **(c) Maxwell in the Owens-circle lane — no. [T1/T2 sources dated 1988–1991]** Maxwell's verified Israeli lane was investment, media ownership, immigrant/humanitarian assistance, and access to Israeli political leaders. Primary securities records and contemporaneous reporting document Scitex, Teva, Ma'ariv, Keter, and Vremya. No T1/T2 source reviewed places him in an Israeli-Arab joint economic project, Owens's bank proposal, CMEP, or a documented Arab-facing negotiation. His reported politics were, if anything, closer to Shamir's line than to Owens's economic-cooperation design.
- **(d) Channel question — temporally foreclosed. [T2 death record, 1991-11-05; T1/T2 Oslo chronology, 1992-12]** Robert Maxwell died thirteen months before the Oslo channel opened. He could not have participated in that channel. This report tests only possible pre-death antecedents and finds no documented Owens-circle intersection.

### Test 2 — Robert Maxwell and Jeffrey Epstein before 1991-11-05

#### Ghislaine's move and the relationship onset

**[T2 contemporaneous | published 1992-11-15 about 1991]** Michael Robotham's *Mail on Sunday* profile said Ghislaine first arrived in New York in February 1991 to serve a social role around her father's *Daily News* acquisition and said Epstein had helped her establish herself in Manhattan “for over a year.” The article also referred to the prior year's Plaza Hotel tribute to Robert. This supports New York activity during 1991 and an Epstein association by late 1991; it does not establish an exact first-meeting date or Robert–Epstein contact. [Lawfully mirrored article scan](https://unlimitedhangout.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/The-Mystery-Of-Ghislaine-Maxwell_S-Secret-Love_Revealed-1.pdf).

**[T1 participant statement | interview 2025-07-24 about 1990–1991]** In an official DOJ proffer interview, Ghislaine said she was already going “backwards and forwards in America”; “in April of 1991, I came to New York” for “something to do with my dad”; a female friend proposed she meet Epstein; and “sometime in 1991” she met him at his office. She said that after Robert died she returned to New York for Macmillan and then stayed in America as the family businesses collapsed. The conservative chronology is recurring U.S. travel in 1990, New York work by February–April 1991, a meeting with Epstein sometime in 1991 on her account, and settled residence after Robert's death. Her account was given thirty-four years later by a convicted participant in a proffer interview and is not independent corroboration. [DOJ interview landing page](https://www.justice.gov/maxwell-interview); [Day 1 transcript, pp. 8–13](https://www.justice.gov/storage/audio-files/Interview%20Transcript/Interview%20Transcript%20-%20Maxwell%202025.07.24-cft%20%28Redacted%29.pdf).

**[T1 visual / T2 caption | 1991-11, more specifically dated 1991-11-24 by a later account] Earliest independently visible association.** A photograph caption reads: “(L to R) Jeffrey Epstein, Ghislaine Maxwell, and Tony Randall, who presided over a November 1991 YIVO Institute event at the Plaza Hotel to honor the late Robert Maxwell.” The released image is reproduced at `HOUSE_OVERSIGHT_008975`, `HOUSE_OVERSIGHT_010479` (bundle `HOUSE_OVERSIGHT_010477`), and `HOUSE_OVERSIGHT_010487` (bundle `HOUSE_OVERSIGHT_010486`); these are one photograph, not corroborating sources. Craig Unger's 2021 account dates the event to November 24. This is the earliest located visual Epstein–Ghislaine association and the earliest documented Epstein datapoint bearing directly on Robert: attendance at a memorial after Robert's death. [Released House Oversight reproduction](https://media-cdn.rollcall.com/epstein-files/House_Oversight_010477-010484.pdf); [Unger, *American Kompromat*, ch. 12](https://books.google.com/books?id=s80AEAAAQBAJ).

**[T1 trial testimony | 1991 recollection given 2021]** Epstein pilot Larry Visoski testified that he first met Ghislaine in 1991 while employed by Epstein. The testimony supplies only the year and does not place Robert with Epstein. Local released transcripts: `EFTA00068582`, `EFTA00068722`.

#### Direct-contact test and source classification

**[T1 participant statement | interview 2025-07-24 about pre-1991-11-05]** Asked whether her father and Epstein had a business relationship, Ghislaine answered: “They never met” and “I know they never met.” She said she told Robert in 1991 that she had met Epstein because she believed Epstein worked at Bear Stearns; Robert then called Jimmy Cayne and Ace Greenberg to ask what kind of person Epstein was. She later said, “he never had a connection with Epstein. He had a connection with the bank.” This account creates one **indirect, asserted pre-death datapoint**—Robert learned Epstein's name from Ghislaine and allegedly checked him through Bear Stearns contacts—but expressly denies a meeting, direct contact, or business relationship. The statement is T1 evidence of what Ghislaine told DOJ, not confirmation that the described calls occurred. [Day 1 transcript, pp. 14–17](https://www.justice.gov/storage/audio-files/Interview%20Transcript/Interview%20Transcript%20-%20Maxwell%202025.07.24-cft%20%28Redacted%29.pdf).

**[T2 archive-based assessment | published 2021-11-22 about Robert's papers]** Biographer Tom Bower said: “Jeffrey Epstein would have featured in some of Robert Maxwell's New York contacts, and I've got all those private papers, and he wasn't listed.” This is a useful archive-based negative, not a complete inventory or proof that no unrecorded contact occurred. [Broadcast transcript preserved by the University of Auckland](https://collections.library.auckland.ac.nz/tv-radio/title/VL_05131_01).

**[T2 assertion without disclosed support | published 2021]** Investigative reporter Julie K. Brown told the *Times of Israel*: “Epstein had a close relationship with Robert Maxwell.” The article provides no date, document, named witness, or disclosed evidentiary basis for that sentence. It is therefore recorded as a T2 **assertion of contact**, not as verified contact, and it is not staged. [*Times of Israel* interview](https://www.timesofisrael.com/for-writer-who-broke-epstein-case-a-rumored-mossad-link-is-worth-digging-into/).

**[T1/T2 source-set synthesis | coverage through 2026-07-18] Verdict on contact.** No reviewed T1/T2 source independently places Robert Maxwell and Jeffrey Epstein together while Robert was alive. No contemporaneous photograph, letter, call log, calendar entry, contract, payment record, corporate filing, court finding, or source-grounded quality biography was located. The earliest **documented Robert-related** Epstein evidence is the post-death memorial photograph. The earliest **pre-death** item is Ghislaine's 2025 account that she mentioned Epstein to her father, paired with her categorical denial that the men met. Affirmative introduction/use/money claims remain T4-only and are quarantined below.

#### Leadership access, politics, and funeral

**[T2 contemporary | 1988–1991] Maxwell had documented Israeli leadership access.** Preserved contemporary photo captions identify Shamir–Maxwell investment meetings; after Maxwell's death, Shamir described him as an investor who offered his international connections to Israel. *Washington Post* sources said Maxwell spoke with Shamir at least weekly, visited Jerusalem at least monthly, was friendly with Shimon Peres, and was particularly close to Ariel Sharon. These are contemporaneous sourced reports, not diplomatic minutes. [NLI 1988 retrospective caption](https://www.nli.org.il/en/newspapers/jweekly/1991/11/08/article/129.1); [NLI 1990 caption](https://www.nli.org.il/en/newspapers/jweekly/1990/03/16/article/121.1); [UPI, 1991-11-06](https://www.upi.com/Archives/1991/11/06/Maxwells-death-raises-questions-in-Israel/7466689403600/); [*Washington Post*, 1991-11-11](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/politics/1991/11/11/israel-gives-maxwell-farewell-fit-for-hero/1773995a-0eac-4a3e-abed-1d3254cc0baa/).

The event involved speeches and working groups supporting Israel; no reviewed report links it to Owens, Abraham, CMEP, or the development bank. [JTA preview](https://www.jta.org/archive/shamirs-solidarity-conference-winning-broad-backing-in-u-s); [JTA conference report](https://www.jta.org/archive/despite-differences-on-policy-world-gathering-achieves-unity).

This direct quotation places his public intervention closer to Shamir's line, not Owens's cross-border cooperation model.

**[T2 contemporary | 1991-11-05 context reported 1991-11-07]** *El País* reported that, just before his death, Maxwell was preparing a speech against the Madrid peace conference and President George H. **[T2 report of Maxwell self-claim | published 1991-11-06]** The *Washington Post* reported that Maxwell claimed to have mediated between Shamir and Bush during their 1991 dispute. [*Washington Post*](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/politics/1991/11/06/publisher-maxwell-dies-at-sea/f657456b-f987-43f1-8aa3-6131dfd77452/).

[UPI](https://www.upi.com/Archives/1991/11/10/Maxwell-buried-on-Mount-of-Olives/4340689749200/); [JTA](https://www.jta.org/archive/maxwell-colossus-even-in-death-laid-to-rest-on-mount-of-olives); [*Washington Post*](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/politics/1991/11/11/israel-gives-maxwell-farewell-fit-for-hero/1773995a-0eac-4a3e-abed-1d3254cc0baa/).
