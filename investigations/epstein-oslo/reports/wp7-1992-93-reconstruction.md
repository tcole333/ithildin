## Summary

- **1992-06-01–1993-12-31 — T1/T3:** A pilot log admitted as a court exhibit, accessed through a T3 normalized transcription, names Epstein on private-aircraft legs in every month of the period. It is substantial density evidence but is not a complete itinerary and cannot exclude commercial travel, other aircraft, or omitted passengers.
- **1992-12-04 and 1993-01-20/22 — T1/T3:** Named U.S. legs tightly bracket the first London meeting and Round 1. These windows are documented-domestic, with exact event-day gaps.
- **1993-08-17/18 — T1/T3:** Named legs place Epstein on a U.S. itinerary from New Jersey through Ohio, New Mexico, and Colorado during the Stockholm phone-marathon dates. August 20 itself has no Epstein passenger entry, although named legs bracket it.
- **1993-09-13 — T1/T2/T3:** Epstein's presence at the White House signing ceremony is **unknown**. The online Clinton Library provides finding aids, not scans, for the relevant attendee and WAVES records. The flight transcription places him in Canada on September 10 and next names him on a U.S. leg September 17.
- **1993-09-29 — T2 over T1 records/photos:** The well-documented 1993 White House appearance with Ghislaine Maxwell was a donor reception sixteen days after the signing, not the September 13 ceremony.
- **1993-02-25–03-19 — T1:** Zorro Trust contracted to buy the New Mexico ranch assets on February 25. Epstein executed a limited power of attorney in New York City on March 3 for the anticipated March 4–8 closing; it was recorded in Santa Fe County on March 19. The instrument does not state the consideration or place Epstein in New Mexico at closing.
- **1993-02-08 onward — T1:** The SEC's contemporary Towers Financial releases name Towers, Hoffenberg, Brater, and Ferro, not Epstein. They document the enforcement boundary but neither establish Epstein's exit date nor prove that he had no later role.
- **1992-06-01–1993-12-31 — T1–T4:** No named European or MENA leg appears in the reconstructed private-aircraft layer during the channel-round windows. That null is left-censored by the log's limited scope and must not be treated as a complete alibi.

### 2. Zorro Ranch transaction

**1993-02-22 — T1.** A separate New Mexico State Land Office file says a partial relinquishment of grazing lease GM-2583 was approved in favor of Zorro Trust for **1,158.97 acres** on February 22. A March 11 agency memorandum and transmittal record the transfer work. This is the state grazing-lease component, not the private-land closing and not a physical sighting of Epstein. [Official archived lease file](https://www.nmstatelands.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/GM2791-Expired-and-Archived-Complete-Electronic-File-Contents.pdf) (`EFTA00101481`).

**1993-02-25–03-19 — T1.** The limited power of attorney in `EFTA00030804` identifies a purchase agreement dated **1993-02-25** between purchaser **Zorro Trust** and sellers **King Brothers Ranch**, **King Land & Cattle Company**, and **Pine Canyon Ranch, Inc.** It authorized John J. Kelly to close the Santa Fe County real and personal property transaction “on or about March 4–8, 1993.” Epstein signed and notarized the instrument in **New York City on 1993-03-03** as trustee; Santa Fe County recorded it **1993-03-19 at 3:11 p.m., Book 7037, page 401**. [Primary document](https://www.justice.gov/epstein/files/DataSet%208/EFTA00030804.pdf).

A [2019 New Republic account](https://newrepublic.com/article/154761/jeffrey-epstein-zorro-ranch-new-mexico-history) describes February 22 as both the lease and land-purchase date. The primary instruments distinguish the **February 22 state-lease approval**, **February 25 private purchase agreement**, and **March 4–8 anticipated closing**, so this report preserves those separate dates. **T2 conflict resolved in favor of T1 records, checked 2026-07-17.**

The document calls Zorro Trust a New York trust. A later 2011 deed in `EFTA00310887` describes it as an Ohio trust under agreement dated 1993-03-01; that later characterization does not overwrite the contemporaneous instrument. **T1, later-document comparison dated 2011.**

The instrument contains no purchase consideration. A [2023 Wall Street Journal property graphic](https://www.wsj.com/public/resources/documents/YiuiC6meChTtdia7Jh0n-WSJNewsPaper-9-15-2023.pdf) labels Epstein's 1993 purchase price “Unknown”; the roughly $12 million figure in later coverage refers to the estate's 2023 sale and should not be back-projected to 1993. **T2, checked 2026-07-17.** The transaction is a strong dated U.S. asset activity, but only the **1993-03-03 New York execution** is a physical-placement anchor. The pilot-log transcription separately names Epstein on flights to Santa Fe on **1993-03-11** and away on **1993-03-14**. **T1 artifact/T3 transcription.**

### 4. Towers Financial record boundary

**1993-02-08 — T1.** The SEC filed its civil action against Towers Financial Corporation, Steven Hoffenberg, Mitchell Brater, and Arthur Ferro. The release alleged insider trading by Hoffenberg from January 1990 through April 1992. Epstein is not among the named defendants. [SEC News Digest, 1993-02-09](https://www.sec.gov/news/digest/1993/dig020993.pdf).

**1993-03-03–1994-11-03 — T1.** The amended action added subsidiaries and relief defendants; the SEC's later arrest and judgment releases continued to describe the Towers/Hoffenberg case without naming Epstein. [1993-03-15](https://www.sec.gov/news/digest/1993/dig031593.pdf), [1994-02-18](https://www.sec.gov/news/digest/1994/dig021894.pdf), [1994-11-03](https://www.sec.gov/news/digest/1994/dig110394.pdf). The federal criminal opinion says the government investigation began in 1991, the SEC action was filed on 1993-02-08, and the criminal investigation began in early 1993. [United States v. Hoffenberg](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/FSupp/908/1265/1457501/). A bankruptcy opinion arising from a 1993-10-04 order also contains no Epstein reference. [In re Towers Financial Corp.](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/BR/164/719/1858349/).

This boundary supports only a **medium-confidence synthesis**: the reviewed contemporary enforcement and bankruptcy opinions do not establish an Epstein role or exit date. Absence from those named-party records is not proof of absence from Towers. The widely repeated 1987–88 consulting narrative is retrospective reporting ([CBS, 2019](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/jeffrey-epstein-worked-at-towers-financial-with-stephen-hoffenberg-who-committed-ponzi-scheme-crimes/); [Vanity Fair, 2003](https://www.vanityfair.com/news/2003/03/jeffrey-epstein-200303)), while claims of post-1991 operational involvement rest substantially on Hoffenberg and remain **T4, verbatim-only, non-load-bearing**.
