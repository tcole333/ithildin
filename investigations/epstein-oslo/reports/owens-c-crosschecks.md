### 1. The 2018 “lists”: one unlabeled self-forwarded roster, released twice

**[T1 | 2018-01-08]** `HOUSE_OVERSIGHT_019871` and `EFTA02540965`–`EFTA02540967` are duplicate releases of one email from Jeffrey Epstein to himself, subject **“Fwd: radical breakthrough.”** Above idea fragments, the body contains an unlabeled run of names beginning:

That quotation follows the House rendering; the EFTA OCR represents the same separators with quoted-printable/OCR artifacts (`wayne owens= prince andrew`).

What it establishes: Epstein typed “wayne owens” into a private self-forwarded artifact on 2018-01-08, sixteen years after Douglas Wayne Owens died on 2002-12-18. What it does **not** establish: a contact record, encounter, trip, introduction, or ordered chronology. The “chronological early-contact list” reading remains a hypothesis, not a finding.

### 2. The 2009 email: one cold-fusion claim, rendered four times

**[T1 | 2009-10-01]** The relevant records are `EFTA02437604`–`EFTA02437607`, `EFTA00740161`, `EFTA00884559`, and `EFTA00770891`. They are overlapping renderings of one Jeffrey Epstein–Al Seckel thread, not independent corroboration. In response to a question about Fleischmann and Pons/cold fusion, Epstein wrote first that he had “killed pons years ago” and then:

> “wayne owens senator from utah,, i was there an argues against, it, had ot meet with the head of the mormon church.”
