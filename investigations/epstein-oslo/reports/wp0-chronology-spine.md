## Summary

Waage's archival chronology contains **12 formal Declaration-of-Principles
rounds**, 20 January–14/15 August 1993. Qurei then numbers the 19–20 August
initialing session as round 13 and the 30–31 August mutual-recognition session
as round 14; the final 8–10 September Paris session follows without a stable
round number. This reconciles the launch brief's “about 14 rounds” without
silently changing Waage's taxonomy. Qurei's reproduced 4 December report dates
the first Hirschfeld meeting to **3 December**, not 4 December; both dates are
retained. Fornebu is directly documented for several PLO arrivals, but most
other airports remain correlation aids rather than proven delegation fields.
The completed row-level table and widened dispute windows are in
`research/chronology.md`. WP0 establishes the correlation spine; it does not
test Epstein's presence, and no inference about Epstein was made.

## Findings

### 1. Twelve formal DoP rounds plus Qurei's rounds 13 and 14

**Claim.** Waage's consolidated lists identify rounds 1–5 on 20–22 January,
11–12 February, 20–21 March, 30 April–1 May, and 8–9 May, followed by rounds
6–12 on 21 May, 13–15 June, 25–27 June, 4–6 July, 10–11 July, 25–26 July, and
13–14 August. Her narrative supplies broader alternatives for the last three.
Qurei calls the 19–20 August initialing session the thirteenth round and the 30
August Oslo Plaza session the fourteenth. The two classifications describe
different boundaries rather than requiring an invented Waage round 13 or 14.

- Event dates: 1993-01-20 through 1993-08-31.
- Evidence dates/tier: Waage 2004 (T2); Qurei 2006 (T1-self for his own
  participation, T2 for the broader event).
- Citations: Waage, printed pp. 51 n.2 and 85 n.3; Qurei, pp. 245–67,
  especially his chapter headings “Thirteenth round” and his account of
  Holst's 14-round announcement.
- Confidence: high for the taxonomy; medium for disputed outer dates.

### 2. The London precursor was on 3 December in Qurei's own report

**Claim.** The registered anchor gives 4 December 1992. Qurei reproduces a
report dated 4 December whose memorandum explicitly dates the Hirschfeld
discussions to 3 December; Waage supplies the enclosing 3–4 December London
window. Qurei identifies the Cavendish Hotel, Afif Safieh as the third person
at the table, and a return to Tunis after the multilateral meeting. The
chronology retains the registered anchor but directs WP1 to search 2–5
December.

- Event date: 1992-12-03, disputed against 1992-12-04.
- Evidence dates/tier: Qurei 2006 (T1-self/T2 event); Waage 2000 and 2004
  (T2).
- Citations: Qurei pp. 41–45; Waage 2004 pp. 47–49; Waage 2000 PDF pp. 49–50.
- Confidence: high that the meetings occurred on 3 December; medium that any
  substantive session occurred on 4 December.

### 3. The travel dates are often broader than the working-session dates

**Claim.** Several apparent chronology conflicts reconcile when travel and
preparation are separated from substantive sessions. Examples: the PLO team
arrived at Fornebu on 20 January before 21–22 January talks; Hirschfeld and
Pundak arrived on 25 June before Singer's 27 June session; Qurei traveled on
24 July before 25–26 July working days; and the PLO team arrived on 19 August
before the after-midnight 20 August initialing. Other discrepancies remain
genuine and are not averaged: round 6 (Waage 21 May versus Qurei 20–22 May),
round 10 (10–11, 10–12, or 11–12 July), round 12 (13–14 or 13–15 August), and
the final Paris session (8–9 or 9–10 September).

- Event dates: 1993-01-20 through 1993-09-10.
- Evidence dates/tier: Qurei 2006 and Singer 2021 (T1-self/T2 event); Waage
  2004 (T2).
- Citations: Qurei pp. 54–56, 165–66, 210–11, 245–47, 269–74; Singer pp.
  13–20; Waage pp. 85, 103, 120, 131, 148–49.
- Confidence: high for the specific first-person movements; medium for the
  widened correlation bounds.

### 4. Fornebu is proven for several movements; most other fields are not

**Claim.** Qurei expressly names Fornebu for the PLO team's late-20-January
arrival, 10-February arrival, 30-April arrival, July departure, and 19-August
arrival. He describes the secret team's indirect European routings but rarely
names intermediate airports. Fornebu was Oslo's principal airport until 1998.
The table therefore uses ENFB as a documented or practical Oslo correlation
field while flagging every row where actual airport use remains unknown.
Rygge, Hamar/Stafsberg, and Eggemoen are geographical-nearest/local fields,
not claims that negotiators used them. Gardermoen is not substituted for
Fornebu.

- Event dates: 1993-01-20 through 1993-08-19.
- Evidence dates/tier: Qurei 2006 (T1-self); Norwegian reference/accident
  authority material accessed 2026-07-17 (T2/T1 administrative reference).
- Citations: Qurei pp. 54–56, 76–78, 115–16, 200, 245–47; Store norske
  leksikon, “Oslo lufthavn, Fornebu”; Norwegian Accident Investigation Board,
  1992-BUL04.
- Confidence: confirmed/high for the named PLO Fornebu movements; medium for
  practical-gateway assignments; low for unreported fields.

### 5. Participant changes and travel bases are now row-specific

**Claim.** Hirschfeld and Pundak were the only Israelis in rounds 1–5. Savir
entered the official-level sixth round in May; Singer entered round 7 on 14
June. In round 8, Qurei says Singer was principal, Hirschfeld/Pundak were
support, and Savir was absent. Abu Koush joined the Palestinian team at
Grefsheim/Gressheim, replacing al-Kurd. The PLO team took instructions from
and debriefed Tunis around every round. “Israel side” and “Tunis side” in the
spine denote these documented reporting bases unless a first-person source
actually gives the departure.

- Event dates: 1993-01-20 through 1993-09-10.
- Evidence dates/tier: Qurei 2006 and Singer 2021 (T1-self/T2 event); Waage
  2004 (T2).
- Citations: Qurei pp. 2–4, 54–59, 144–66, 178–80; Singer pp. 12–14; Waage
  pp. 60–62, 91–101.
- Confidence: high.

### 6. The venue record corrects two launch candidates and leaves one property unresolved

**Claim.** Qurei directly identifies Holmenkollen Park Hotel for round 4,
the Thomas Heftye House near Holmenkollen for round 6, the Fafo Centre for
round 8, Halvorsbøle Hotel and Conference Centre for rounds 10–11, and the
Bristol Hotel for the final Paris session. Oslo local history places the
Heftye-villa at Frognerseteren as a municipal representation property; it is
not “Thomas Heftyes gate.” No load-bearing source consulted placed a round at
Gamle Logen. Round 5 remains disputed between Waage's “Thomas Heftye villa”
and Qurei's “government guesthouse near the Royal Palace.” The exact official
guesthouse used for the 20 August initialing also remains unnamed.

- Event dates: 1993-04-30 through 1993-09-10.
- Evidence dates/tier: Qurei 2006 (T1-self/T2 event); Waage 2004 (T2); Oslo
  byleksikon accessed 2026-07-17 (T2 reference).
- Citations: Qurei pp. 115–16, 137–45, 165–66, 195, 245–47, 269–70; Waage pp.
  51, 85, 139–40; Oslo byleksikon, “Frognerseteren.”
- Confidence: high for named venues; medium for round 5; low for exact
  initialing-property name.

### 7. Norwegian public funding and Fafo cover supported the channel logistics

**Claim.** Waage reports that Rød-Larsen arranged time, place and transport;
Jan Egeland found money in the Norwegian Foreign Ministry budget; Fafo acted
as the logistical cover; and Hirschfeld and Pundak were put on Fafo's payroll.
This is a material WP4 fact because it identifies a documented public funding
route. It is not evidence of unexplained US private money.

- Event date: 1993 (setup in January).
- Evidence dates/tier: Waage 2000 and 2004 (T2).
- Citation: Waage 2000 PDF pp. 53–54; Waage 2004 p. 80.
- Source excerpt: “Jan Egeland approved the plan and found money on the
  Foreign Ministry's budget to pay the bill.”
- Confidence: high (paraphrase cap respected).

## Proposed Leads

1. **WP1 correlation:** use the widened windows in `chronology.md`, treating
   Fornebu as proven only where Qurei names it. Prioritize 20 January, 10
   February, 30 April, 19 August, 30 August, and 7–11 September. Search the
   London precursor across 2–5 December, not only the registered 4 December
   anchor.
2. **WP4 funding:** reconcile Norwegian MFA disbursement records, Fafo
   payroll, tickets, hotels, and transport against Waage's public-funding
   account. Look for deviations before asking whether any money is
   “unexplained.”
3. **Venue resolution:** identify the round-5 guesthouse and the 20-August
   official guesthouse from already-public Norwegian property/archival
   sources. Do not infer that the two were the same property.
4. **Source hardening:** obtain lawful publisher/library copies of Corbin,
   Savir, Beilin and Hirschfeld for page-level comparison, especially round 6
   dates, the Halvorsbøle ranges, and the Point Mugu itinerary.
5. **Grefsheim identity:** resolve the estate's precise locality and historic
   access route from public land/property history before assigning any local
   airfield beyond ENFB as a correlation target.
