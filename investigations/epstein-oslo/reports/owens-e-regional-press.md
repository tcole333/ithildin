# OWENS-E — contemporaneous regional coverage of the 1988–1990 Middle East trips

**Profile:** `epstein-oslo`  
**Lead / thread:** #72733 (OWENS-E) / 175  
**Research date:** 2026-07-18  
**Question:** Do regional press and government archives identify Owens's traveling party, the Saudi Red Sea host, or more precise dates and venues than the OWENS-A trip table?  
**Result:** Regional records refine three dated trip rows and fill one previously undated trip slot, but name no traveling companion, Epstein, American financier in the party, or Saudi Red Sea host.

## Summary

- **Answer:** No located regional source names a member of Owens's traveling party. Three official broadcasts refer to “the delegation accompanying him” or “his delegation,” but none supplies a name.
- **No Epstein / financier hit:** The issue-complete FBIS sweep returned no relevant `Epstein`, S. Daniel Abraham, Slim-Fast, American-financier, or American-businessmen reference near Owens. The single OCR token `EPSTEIN` was a corrupt table-of-contents artifact, not a person.
- **Saudi host unresolved:** No source identifies the “one of the world's richest men” who reportedly flew the party to a Red Sea palace near Jeddah. No regional source even repeats that anecdote.
- **1989-02-11/12 through 02-13 — T1/T2:** A PLO broadcast places Owens in Tunis by the night before its 12 February transmission and records an additional meeting with PLO Executive Committee member Yasir ‘Abd Rabbuh. An Arabic newspaper report and a French regional chronology separately date the Arafat meeting to 13 February and add its US–PLO-dialogue and human-rights agenda.
- **1989-03-30 — T1:** Amman radio and television fix Owens's Jordan meetings: Prime Minister Zayd al-Rifa‘i in his office that morning and King Hussein at the Royal Court that afternoon.
- **1988-12-14 — T1:** Iraqi state news records Owens, an unnamed accompanying delegation, and the U.S. ambassador meeting First Deputy Premier Taha Yasin Ramadan in Baghdad.
- **1989-12-14 through 12-16 — T1/T2:** Jordanian and Syrian broadcasts establish a previously undated trip slot: Royal Hashemite Court on 14 December, then meetings in Damascus with Vice President ‘Abd al-Halim Khaddam and Foreign Minister Faruq al-Shar‘ on 16 December. Contemporaneous U.S. Jewish press says a planned Assad meeting was canceled. The OWENS-A count-derived unresolved slots therefore fall from two to one.
- **Coverage caveat:** JPRESS/NLI has in-range *Ma‘ariv* and *Hadashot* issues, but public full-text execution was blocked by the archive's access controls and no interactive browser was available. That surface is reported as **not searched to completion**, not as a zero-hit archive null.

## Findings

### 1. Tunis: an earlier PLO meeting and a more precise agenda

#### Yasir ‘Abd Rabbuh meeting

**Event:** night before the 12 February 1989 broadcast (most naturally 11 February, but the translated relative date is retained rather than forced)  
**Evidence date:** 1989-02-12 broadcast; published in FBIS-NES-89-028 on 1989-02-13  
**Tier / claim:** **T1**, direct quotation of a contemporaneous U.S. government translation of a monitored PLO broadcast; confirmed that FBIS carried the report. The underlying Arabic audio/transcript is not reproduced in the scan.

The Baghdad Voice of the PLO in Arabic reported at 1922 GMT:

> “received Wayne Owens” ... “in Tunis last night.”

The report identifies Owens as a member of the House Foreign Affairs Subcommittee on Europe and the Middle East, says the discussion covered the Palestinian peace initiative and an international peace conference, and ends: “Owens is visiting Tunis for meetings with the Palestinian leadership.” It names no companion.

**Original / translation:** Arabic original unavailable in the FBIS item; the quoted English is FBIS's translation.  
**Citation:** *FBIS Daily Report: Near East & South Asia*, FBIS-NES-89-028, 1989-02-13, PDF leaf 17, item JN1202212089, [Archive.org scan](https://archive.org/details/micro_IA1176910_0238/page/n16/mode/1up).

**Trip-table addition:** Owens was in Tunis before the 13 February Arafat meeting and met another named PLO official. The defensible trip interval becomes **at least the night of 11/12 February through the reported 14 February return**, without converting “last night” into false precision.

#### Arafat meeting

**Event:** 1989-02-13  
**Evidence dates:** 1989-02-15 newspaper; FBIS publication 1989-02-17; summer 1989 regional chronology  
**Tier / claim:** **T1** for the FBIS government translation; **T2** for the contemporaneous regional chronology, which attributes its item to AFP and a Palestinian source.

Dubai's Arabic-language *Al-Bayan* reported:

> “PLO chief Yasir ‘Arafat in Tunis on 13 February met with Wayne Owens”

It says the meeting addressed the Palestinian view of peace and ways to develop the Palestinian–U.S. dialogue launched on 16 December. The underlying Arabic pages are identified as *Al-Bayan*, 15 February 1989, pp. 1 and 14, but are not reproduced in the FBIS scan.

**Citation:** *FBIS Daily Report: Near East & South Asia*, FBIS-NES-89-032, 1989-02-17, PDF leaf 17, item JN1702120889, [Archive.org scan](https://archive.org/details/micro_IA1176910_0242/page/n16/mode/1up).

The French-language *Revue d'études palestiniennes* chronology adds the subcommittee assignment and a human-rights topic:

> **French original:** “Yasser Arafat reçoit à Tunis un congressiste démocrate américain, M. Wayne Owens, chargé d’effectuer une visite d’information”
>
> **Translation:** “Yasser Arafat receives in Tunis an American Democratic congressman, Mr. Wayne Owens, assigned to conduct a fact-finding visit ...”

The remainder says the discussion concerned developing the dialogue established between the PLO and United States the previous December and respect for human rights in the Israeli-occupied territories. It cites a Palestinian source and AFP.

**Citation:** “Chronologie: 1 janvier–31 mars 1989,” *Revue d'études palestiniennes*, no. 32 (summer 1989), p. 154, [IPS PDF](https://www.palestine-studies.org/sites/default/files/jq-articles/1-janvier-31-mars-1989%20%282%29.pdf).

**Trip-table addition:** Confirms 13 February and adds the fact-finding characterization and substantive agenda. It names no delegation member.

### 2. Four-country bank trip: Amman fixed to 30 March

**Event:** 1989-03-30  
**Evidence date:** FBIS publication 1989-03-31  
**Tier / claim:** **T1**, direct English-language Amman radio/television reports preserved by FBIS.

Amman Domestic Service in English reported that Prime Minister Zayd al-Rifa‘i met Owens **in the prime minister's office that morning** to discuss Middle East developments, peace efforts, and U.S.–Jordanian relations. Amman Television Service in English reported:

> “His Majesty King Husayn received at the Royal Court this afternoon U.S. Congressman Wayne Owens”

It says the discussion concerned peace through an international peace conference. Neither item says Owens was alone, but neither identifies or even mentions a delegation.

**Original / translation:** The monitored broadcasts were in English; the quotation is original-language text.  
**Citation:** *FBIS Daily Report: Near East & South Asia*, FBIS-NES-89-061, 1989-03-31, PDF leaf 39, items JN3003142989 and JN3003202289, [Archive.org scan](https://archive.org/details/micro_IA1176910_0271/page/n38/mode/1up).

**Trip-table addition:** Fixes Hussein to **30 March 1989, Royal Court, afternoon**, and adds **Zayd al-Rifa‘i, prime minister's office, morning**. It neither refines nor contradicts the overall 23 March–approximately 5 April interval. The 11 available FBIS reports spanning 23 March–7 April contain no other Owens item for Cairo, Saudi Arabia, or Israel.

### 3. December 1988 trip: Baghdad delegation language and venue context

**Event:** 1988-12-14  
**Evidence date:** FBIS publication 1988-12-15  
**Tier / claim:** **T1**, direct English-language Iraqi News Agency dispatch preserved by FBIS.

Baghdad INA reported:

> “Wayne Owens, member of the U.S. Congress External Affairs Committee and the delegation accompanying him.”

The dispatch says the U.S. ambassador in Baghdad attended and that bilateral relations, Iraq's position on the Palestine question, and support for the PLO were discussed.

**Original / translation:** INA transmitted the item in English; the quotation is original-language text.  
**Citation:** *FBIS Daily Report: Near East & South Asia*, FBIS-NES-88-241, 1988-12-15, PDF leaf 25, item JN1412170288, [Archive.org scan](https://archive.org/details/micro_IA1176910_0197/page/n24/mode/1up).

**Trip-table addition:** Adds **14 December, Baghdad, Taha Yasin Ramadan**, plus an unnamed accompanying delegation and U.S. ambassador. It does not date the Shamir meeting or name a traveler.

### 4. A previously undated slot: Amman and Damascus, 14–16 December 1989

#### Amman

**Event:** 1989-12-14  
**Evidence date:** FBIS publication 1989-12-15  
**Tier / claim:** **T1**, U.S. government translation of Amman Domestic Service in Arabic.

> “His Majesty King Husayn this afternoon received U.S. Congressman Wayne Owens and his delegation at the Royal Hashemite Court.”

The chief of the Royal Hashemite Court and U.S. ambassador attended. No delegation member is named.

**Original / translation:** Arabic original unavailable in the FBIS scan; quoted text is FBIS's English translation.  
**Citation:** *FBIS Daily Report: Near East & South Asia*, FBIS-NES-89-240, 1989-12-15, printed p. 30, item JN1412172089, [Archive.org scan](https://archive.org/details/micro_IA1176910_0435/page/n33/mode/1up).

#### Damascus

**Event:** 1989-12-16  
**Evidence date:** FBIS publication 1989-12-18  
**Tier / claim:** **T1**, U.S. government translations of Damascus Domestic Service in Arabic. “Douglas Wayne Owens” is not a different person: the House's official biography gives that as Wayne Owens's full name.

> “received at 1100 this morning U.S. Congressman Mr Douglas Wayne Owens and his accompanying delegation.”

The meeting was at 1100 and concerned Lebanon and the Middle East. A second broadcast says Foreign Minister Faruq al-Shar‘ received Owens that morning, with the U.S. ambassador present, to discuss the international situation, Arab–Israeli conflict, and an international peace conference.

**Original / translation:** Arabic originals unavailable in the FBIS scan; quoted text is FBIS's English translation.  
**Citations:** *FBIS Daily Report: Near East & South Asia*, FBIS-NES-89-241, 1989-12-18, printed p. 50, items JN1612181989 and JN1612120489, [Archive.org scan](https://archive.org/details/micro_IA1176910_0436/page/n55/mode/1up); [official House biography](https://history.house.gov/People/Detail/19258).

The contemporaneous *Detroit Jewish News* adds an intended but canceled meeting: Owens met high-level Syrian officials and lectured them about the U.S. commitment to Israel; “as a result, Owens' meeting with the intractable Assad was canceled.” This is Capitol Hill-sourced reporting, not a Syrian primary document.

**Citation:** “Owens Alienates Syrians For A Change,” *Detroit Jewish News*, 1989-12-29, p. 37, [page image and OCR](https://digital.bentley.umich.edu/djnews/djn.1989.12.29.001/37) — **T2**.

**Trip-table addition:** Creates a discrete **14–16 December 1989 Jordan–Syria trip** and fills one of OWENS-A's two count-derived undated 1989–90 slots. The evidence does not establish the trip's start, end, funding, or other countries; one unresolved slot remains.

## Angles & Hypotheses (not findings)

> [!CAUTION]
> **UNVERIFIED EXPLORATION ONLY. Nothing in this section is a finding, and none of it is staged in the findings JSON.**

1. **The Saudi selector is now narrower.** With Owens still in Amman on the afternoon of 30 March and back in the United States around 5 April, the Jeddah/Red Sea weekend most plausibly falls in the immediately following weekend. That is a search-order hypothesis, not a date finding.
2. **“His delegation” should have generated protocol paperwork.** The exact Royal Hashemite Court and Damascus meeting times imply scheduled protocol events. Court guest lists, embassy cables, or official photographs may name people omitted from the short broadcast copy.
3. **The canceled Assad meeting implies an advance schedule.** If the contemporaneous Capitol Hill account is accurate, an earlier itinerary or Syrian protocol document may list the intended audience and party even though the meeting did not occur.
4. **Do not retrofit a Saudi magnate.** Wealth, a Jeddah palace, and access to aircraft fit many people in 1989. Naming a candidate without a source would violate the WP9 conspiracy-gravity guard; the correct next move is date-bounded Saudi press and aviation/protocol evidence.
5. **Delegation language does not establish the Epstein trip.** The December 1988 and December 1989 records prove that Owens sometimes traveled with unnamed delegations. They do not make Epstein, Abraham, Gordon, Yeager, or anyone else a member of those parties.
