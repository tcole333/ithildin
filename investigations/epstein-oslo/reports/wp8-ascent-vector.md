# WP8 — Mid-90s ascent vector

**Profile:** `epstein-oslo`  
**Lead / thread:** #72592 / 175  
**Research date:** 2026-07-18  
**Scope:** Organization-side admission and board records, dated self-descriptions, and the earliest documented Peres/Barak contacts. “No peace-process fingerprint” means none appears in the records reviewed; it does not prove that an undisclosed private sponsor had no such connection.

## Summary

- **1995 — T1 organization roster:** The Council on Foreign Relations’ 1995 membership roster marks “Epstein, Jeffrey E.” with the footnote “Elected to membership in 1995.” CFR’s own annual reports continue to list him through 2009 and omit him in 2010. His case-specific proposer and seconders remain unidentified.
- **1995–approximately 1998 — T2 archival reporting over Rockefeller records:** David Rockefeller’s prepared remarks place Epstein’s entry onto Rockefeller University’s board in 1995. The university later said that he served one three-year term in the 1990s, making 1995–1998 the best-supported tenure but an inference rather than an exact organization-published exit date. A later review memo records Leon Black’s understanding that David Rockefeller made the appointment.
- **By 1995-06-12 — T1 organization roster:** The Trilateral Commission claim is **verified, not disproved**. Its organization-published list names “Jeffrey E. Epstein, President, New Albany Property, Inc.” The document proves presence by that date, not the first admission year.
- **Fingerprint verdict:** The three 1995 institutional records do not document an Oslo participant, Norwegian intermediary, peace-process program, or Middle East sponsor as the admission vector. Rockefeller University is explicitly a science/philanthropy channel; the Trilateral roster identifies Epstein through Wexner’s New Albany enterprise; CFR’s private sponsors are unknown, while its visible record is membership plus sustained philanthropy. The documented mid-90s institutional vector therefore favors **Wexner/Rockefeller-linked finance, science, and philanthropy**.
- **1989 — T2 retrospective reporting, contemporaneous trip corroboration:** Epstein’s Israeli entrée was not 2002+. New York Times reporting based on participant interviews says he accompanied Representative **Wayne Owens** on a 1989 Middle East economic-diplomacy trip and attended meetings with Shimon Peres, Benjamin Netanyahu, and Saudi Crown Prince Abdullah. Contemporaneous reporting confirms Owens undertook 1989 travel to promote a Middle East development bank, although the underlying manifest and meeting records were not located.
- **2003 — T1 for Barak’s statement:** Ehud Barak now says Peres introduced him to Epstein at a Washington social event in May 2003 and that he did not meet Epstein in person until November 2003. His 2019 account had placed the introduction only at about 2002 and was uncertain between New York and Washington; the 2026 statement is more precise but remains participant testimony.
- **2004–2006 — T1 tax filings:** Three IRS Forms 990-PF report payments to Barak for “research”: $1,000,000, $505,000, and $800,000, totaling **$2,305,000**. No Israeli State Comptroller report containing these payments was found; the primary payment record is the U.S. tax filings, and Israeli reporting identifies the later inquiry as a Civil Service Commission matter. The same filings identify Epstein as a Wexner-foundation trustee or officer, but that does not prove he directed the Barak payments.

### 2. Council on Foreign Relations: 1995 entry and 2009 terminus

**1995 — T1 organization-published artifact.** The CFR roster preserved in the 1993–2000 annual-report compilation lists `Epstein, Jeffrey E. *`; the same roster defines the asterisk as “Elected to membership in 1995.” The compilation also contains 1993 and 1994 rosters without Epstein, so this is an entry-year marker rather than merely the first annual report obtained. [CFR roster compilation](https://www.scribd.com/doc/175393631/Council-on-Foreign-Relations-Rosters-1993-2000).

**1997 — T1 official annual report.** CFR’s official report lists Epstein twice: in the membership roster and among the **Harold Pratt Associates**, the $10,000-and-above annual-support category. [CFR Annual Report 1997, donor section and roster](https://www.cfr.org/sites/default/files/report_pdf/CFR_annual_report_1997_0.pdf). The 1998 and 2001–2006 reports likewise list him both as a donor and member; 1999 and 2000 list one of the two categories, depending on report layout. Multiple annual reports are a chronology, not independent corroboration of admission.

**2009–2010 — T1 official roster comparison.** The [2009 annual report](https://www.cfr.org/sites/default/files/report_pdf/CFR_annual_report_2009_0.pdf) lists “Epstein, Jeffrey.” The [2010 annual report](https://www.cfr.org/sites/default/files/report_pdf/CFR_annual_report_2010_0.pdf) lists Jason and Joshua Epstein but not Jeffrey. This establishes **last listed in 2009 / absent in 2010**. It does not by itself state the administrative reason.

**2019 reporting about 1995–2009 — T2.** CFR told the Washington Post that Epstein had been a member from 1995 to 2009, gave about $350,000, attended only two identified events—a 1998 donor dinner and a 2002 discussion with Treasury Secretary Paul O’Neill—and lost membership for nonpayment of dues. [Washington Post, 2019-09-10](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/council-on-foreign-relations-another-beneficiary-of-epstein-largesse-grapples-with-how-to-handle-his-donations/2019/09/10/1d5630e2-d324-11e9-86ac-0f250cc91758_story.html). This reporting matches the roster endpoints, but the reason and event count remain T2 institutional reporting rather than text printed in the rosters.

**Sponsor procedure, not sponsor identity — T1 dated 2000.** CFR’s FY2000 official report says that the then-current regular-member procedure required a written nomination by one member and seconds from at least two other people, at least one a CFR member; the average file contained four to five support letters. [CFR Annual Report 2000, pp. 140–141](https://www.cfr.org/sites/default/files/report_pdf/CFR_annual_report_2000.pdf). That proves CFR used sponsor letters by 2000; it does not establish that the procedure was identical in 1995 or identify Epstein’s proposer/seconders. Searches for `Epstein` near `recommend`, `nominate`, `second`, `sponsor`, `proposer`, and `application` in the local corpus produced no candidate-specific name.

**Program-participation null — T1 bounded to reports reviewed.** Across the official 1997–2009 annual reports, Epstein’s located occurrences are roster/donor listings. No committee, study group, task force, or program role was identified. CFR’s later identification of only two events is consistent with this report-level null.

### 3. Rockefeller University: 1995 entry; approximately 1998 exit

**1995 — T2 archival reporting over David Rockefeller papers.** A New York Times investigation reports that David Rockefeller’s archived prepared remarks welcomed Epstein to Rockefeller University’s board in 1995. The same investigation reports that Epstein’s foundation had already donated tens of thousands of dollars to Rockefeller University and the Trilateral Commission. [New York Times archive](https://archive.ph/XNZdB); [syndicated text](https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/scams-schemes-ruthless-cons-the-untold-story-of-how-epstein-got-rich-125122500276_1.html). This is the strongest start-year evidence located, but the prepared remarks themselves were not retrieved, so the claim remains T2 rather than T1.

**1990s three-year term — T2 transmitting an organization statement.** A Rockefeller University spokesperson told The Verge that Epstein’s board service consisted of one three-year term during the 1990s. [The Verge, 2019-07-13](https://www.theverge.com/2019/7/13/20692415/jeffrey-epstein-scientists-sexual-harassment). Combining that statement with the 1995 start yields **approximately 1995–1998**. Because the end date is arithmetic synthesis rather than a dated resignation or board roster, it is medium confidence.

**Appointment vector — T1 document dated 2021 for Leon Black’s reported understanding.** Dechert’s Apollo-commissioned review says Black “learned that David Rockefeller had appointed Epstein as a director to the Board of Rockefeller University” and viewed the appointment as consistent with Epstein’s professed science and technology expertise. `EFTA02730996`. This directly records Black’s understanding; it is not the university’s board minute and does not disclose the formal nominator or vote.

**Board leadership — T1 organization records.** Richard M. Furlaud chaired the board from 1990 until Richard B. Fisher took over on 1998-07-01. Rockefeller University’s contemporaneous announcement identifies Furlaud as chair in May 1996. [Rockefeller University, 1996-05-30](https://www.rockefeller.edu/news/4496-koch-and-massey-join-rockefeller-university-board-of-trustees/); [Rockefeller University, 1998-06-30](https://www.rockefeller.edu/news/4415-arnold-j-levine-named-president-of-rockefeller-university/). David Rockefeller left the executive-committee chair in June 1995 and became a life trustee and honorary chair, so “David Rockefeller welcomed/appointed him” and “Furlaud was formal board chair” are compatible roles, not competing claims. [Rockefeller University, 1996 institutional history](https://www.rockefeller.edu/news/4482-rockefeller-university-honors-brooke-astor-presents-honorary-degrees-to-sydney-brenner-and-viktor-hamburger-and-awards-25-doctorates-at-graduation-ceremonies/).

**By 2002 — T1 archived organization page.** Rockefeller University’s archived June 2002 board page does not list Epstein among trustees or trustees emeriti. [Wayback snapshot, 2002-06-16](https://web.archive.org/web/20020616015146/http://www.rockefeller.edu/corp/board.htm). This confirms only that he was gone by 2002; it does not independently fix 1998.

**Conflicting date resolved.** Vicky Ward’s 2003 profile retrospectively dated the board appointment to 2000. The later discovery of David Rockefeller’s prepared remarks and the university’s “three-year term in the 90s” statement contradict that date; this report uses 1995 and marks the exit as inferred.

### 4. Trilateral Commission: the membership claim is verified

**1995-06-12 — T1 organization-published roster.** The Trilateral Commission’s membership list, headed “as of June 12, 1995,” identifies David Rockefeller as founder and honorary chairman and lists: “Jeffrey E. Epstein, President, New Albany Property, Inc.” [Scanned roster, p. 2](https://swprs.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/trilateral-commission-members-1995.pdf); [alternate mirror](https://www.scribd.com/document/175392380/Trilateral-Commission-Membership-List-1995). This answers the “ever” question affirmatively. It proves membership **by** the roster date; without a 1994 comparison roster or election notation it does not prove that 1995 was the admission year.

**Fingerprint — T1 roster plus T2 archival reporting.** The organization itself assigned Epstein his Wexner-orbit role at New Albany Property, not a peace-process or diplomatic affiliation. The later New York Times archival investigation reports that Epstein-foundation donations to the commission preceded the membership. The visible vector is therefore finance/real estate/philanthropy. David Rockefeller’s founder status is relevant context but does not itself prove that he personally nominated Epstein.

**Self-claim adjudication.** Undated Epstein-affiliated biographies `EFTA00725190` and `EFTA00295224` call him a former Trilateral and CFR member; the 1995 organization roster confirms the Trilateral claim at least for that date. A 2012 USVI banking application (`EFTA01104771`, duplicate `EFTA01187889`) instead calls him a current member of both groups even though CFR’s official 2010 roster had already omitted him. A 2013 email distributing Epstein-foundation promotional copy (`EFTA00711844`) returns to “former member.” These artifacts are T1 for what his operation represented on those dates and are treated with T4-level suspicion for the truth of the affiliations. They demonstrate why the organization roster, not the bio, controls.

### 5. Israeli entrée chain

#### Shimon Peres: earliest documented contact is 1989

**1989 — T2 retrospective participant reporting.** The New York Times reports that Epstein accompanied Utah Representative **Wayne Owens** on a Middle East trip intended to promote commercial ties between Israel and neighboring states. Screenwriter Dan Gordon, part of the delegation, recalled Epstein’s presence in meetings with Shimon Peres, Benjamin Netanyahu, and Saudi Crown Prince Abdullah. Owens aide Michael Yeager recalled Epstein boasting about hidden-asset work and his close ties to Wexner. [New York Times archive](https://archive.ph/XNZdB); [syndicated text](https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/scams-schemes-ruthless-cons-the-untold-story-of-how-epstein-got-rich-125122500276_1.html).

**1989 contemporaneous corroboration of the trip program, not Epstein’s attendance — T2.** On 1989-03-23 the Deseret News reported that Owens was leaving for a week-long regional trip to promote a Middle East development bank using public/private finance for cooperative Israeli-Arab projects. [Deseret News, 1989-03-23](https://www.deseret.com/1989/3/23/18799842/owens-bound-for-mideast/). A 1990 AIPAC-related letter in an archival collection likewise describes Owens’ May 1989 development-bank bill and his friendships with Israeli leaders including Peres. [American Jewish Archives, MS-630](https://collections.americanjewisharchives.org/ms/ms0630/ms0630.001.003.pdf). These sources support the year and economic-diplomacy purpose, but the underlying delegation manifest, schedule, and Peres meeting record were not located. The exact trip month is therefore left as **1989**, not forced to March.

**1997 later co-presence — T2, locally preserved.** A 2015 Gawker interview archived in `EFTA01250666` quotes Alan Dershowitz: “My first substantive contact with him was to fly with him to Les Wexner’s house to attend dinner with Shimon Peres and John Glenn.” This corroborates a later Wexner-hosted Peres/Epstein venue but is no longer the earliest contact. [Gawker archive](https://www.gawkerarchives.com/flight-logs-put-clinton-dershowitz-on-pedophile-billio-1681039971).

**2010-09-24 — T1 correspondence, planned event only.** David Mitchell asked Epstein, “can you come with me for a very intimate dinner with Shimon Peres tonight at 7.30 6 people”; Epstein proposed using his own house. `EFTA02421857`. The exchange proves an invitation and plan, not attendance.

#### Ehud Barak: Peres introduction in 2003

**2019 statement about approximately 2002 — T1 for what Barak claimed.** Barak told the Daily Beast, “The man who introduced me to Epstein about 17 years ago was Shimon Peres,” while saying he was unsure whether the event was in New York or Washington. [Daily Beast, 2019-07-15](https://www.thedailybeast.com/israels-ehud-barak-i-visited-epsteins-island-but-never-met-any-girls/).

**2026 statement fixing May/November 2003 — T1 for what Barak claimed.** In a 2026-07-08 interview, Barak said he was “introduced to Jeffrey Epstein by no other than Shimon Peres ... only in May 2003”; he then placed their first in-person meeting in November 2003. [Piers Morgan Uncensored transcript](https://pod.wave.co/podcast/piers-morgan-uncensored/exclusive-netanyahu-is-fascist-ehud-barak-breaks-silence-on-shock-epstein-claims). The precise later account supersedes the rough 2019 estimate for chronology, while the discrepancy is preserved rather than hidden.

**2010-04-22–30 — T1 released-corpus floor, not relationship start.** Nili Priell told Epstein’s assistant, “We’d love to meet Jeffrey,” in `EFTA02427415`; Epstein’s 2010-04-30 schedule then recorded “5:00 Appt w/Ehud Barak” in `EFTA02424659`. This is the earliest direct Barak appointment located in the released local corpus. It is left-censored and does not contradict Barak’s claimed 2003 introduction. A schedule proves a planned appointment, not completion.

**Established later overlap.** Finding **#77** already documents Barak and Epstein on the 2014 IPI-administered Mongolia Advisory Board with Rod-Larsen and others. It is cross-cited only and not re-derived here.

#### Ordering test

The conditional premise “Israeli entrée is demonstrably 2002+” is false for **Peres** but approximately correct for **Barak**. The earliest documented Peres contact is 1989; Barak says his introduction occurred in 2003; the existing WP3 direct Norwegian terminus is 2010. The evidence therefore **refutes a Norwegians-before-Israelis ordering**. It does not establish that the 1989 Peres meeting produced a durable relationship, that Peres sponsored the 1995 memberships, or that Epstein participated in Oslo. It is qualitative H2 evidence, not H1 evidence.

## Update-Rule Triggers

**None fired in WP8.** The pre-committed Rule 2 is expressly limited to an Epstein↔Rod-Larsen/Juul communication terminus and is not broadened to Peres after seeing the evidence. The 1989 Peres meeting is nevertheless a material **qualitative update toward H2**, because it documents pre-2000 contact with a central Israeli peace-process figure; it does not update H1 without funding, facilitation, logistics, communications, or introductions tied to the 1992–93 channel.

H1 was already retired under Rule 6 in the status ledger. This work does not reopen it. The absence of peace-process fingerprints in the three 1995 institutional admissions is useful mechanism evidence but is not itself an update rule. The late 2010 Barak corpus floor also produces no H3 update under the censoring guard.
