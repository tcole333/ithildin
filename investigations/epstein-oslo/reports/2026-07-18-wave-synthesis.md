## Verdict

**H1 (Epstein played an active background role in the 1992-93 Oslo channel) is
RETIRED** under pre-committed rule 6: all five instruments returned nulls on
their positive triggers. **H3 (reverse acquisition) is the consolidated
finding** (#13902): no evidence of any Epstein contact with, funding of, or
role in the 1992-93 channel; the documented relationship begins in May 2010 —
with affirmative non-acquaintance behavior — and Epstein then acquired the
Oslo network's afterlife (IPI, the envoy circuit, the mythology itself),
consistent with his fiduciary-capture pattern elsewhere.

**H2 (pre-2000 contact with the channel's network)** ends materially below its
15-20% prior. Its hypothesized concrete surface — the PLO-adjacent finance
world (Samed / Abu Ala's economic circuit / Gulf-arms substrate) — produced
zero name-hits across every searched primary corpus (WP9). What remains is one
documented pre-2000 contact with a peace-process principal via a *different*
channel: the 1989 Wayne Owens economic-diplomacy trip (meetings reportedly
including Peres, Netanyahu, Crown Prince Abdullah; T2 participant reporting,
#13911). That contact is genuine H2-adjacent evidence but carries no channel
function and refutes rather than supports H1's required ordering (Israelis
years before Norwegians).
