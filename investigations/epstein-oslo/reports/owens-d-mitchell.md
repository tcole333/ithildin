# OWENS-D — David Jan Mitchell identity and bridge test

**Profile:** `epstein-oslo`  
**Lead / thread:** #72716 / 175  
**Research date:** 2026-07-18  
**Question:** Is the David Jan Mitchell credited by Wayne Owens in 1989 the David J. Mitchell of Mitchell Holdings LLC who offered Jeffrey Epstein access to a Shimon Peres dinner in 2010, and does that identity establish a bridge back to 1989?  
**Result:** The identity resolves to the **same person**. ## Summary

- **1989-01-12 to 1989-03-09 — T1/T2:** David Jan Mitchell was a New York investment banker, an Israel Bonds Manhattan New Leadership chair, and one of the people Owens publicly credited for ideas behind the Middle East Development Bank project. Owens did not say Mitchell helped incorporate the Center, held a Center office, traveled with him, or knew Epstein.
- **1989–1992 — bounded null:** No Mitchell name appeared in the reviewed early Center material or trip reporting. A full OCR review of Joyce Starr's 1989 *Development Diplomacy* acknowledgments found no Mitchell, Abraham, or Owens. No 1989 delegation list naming Mitchell was located.
- **2009-05-27 — T1:** The earliest dated target-context corpus item says Mitchell had seen Epstein “last week,” placing a meeting in late May 2009. This is the earliest located contact, not necessarily the first contact.
- **2009-10-07 — T1:** Epstein asked Mitchell to approach “Danny Abraham”; Mitchell offered to arrange a meeting at Abraham's house. This is the first located document that reuses a person from the 1989 Owens/Abraham circle inside the Epstein relationship. It remains twenty years after 1989.
- **2010-09-24 — T1:** Jonathan Leitersdorf invited Mitchell to meet Peres; Mitchell then invited Epstein. When the prospective count expanded from six to 12–18, Epstein replied “I'm out.” The records do not establish Epstein's attendance, that the dinner occurred as planned, or a complete guest list.
- **2013-11-14 — T1, collision/context caution:** One email contains both David Mitchell and Terje Rød-Larsen, but in separate clauses: David Stern reported lunch with Mitchell and separately said “PA is in Abu Dhabi”; Epstein replied that “him” should contact Rød-Larsen, who was also there. The pronoun most naturally refers to the PA contact, and the document does not establish a Mitchell–Rød-Larsen relationship.
- **Bridge verdict — bounded null:** No pre-2000 Mitchell–Epstein name hit was found. Identity resolution proves that one person appears in the 1989 economic-diplomacy record and the 2009–2010 Epstein record; it does **not** prove Epstein's 1989 presence, Mitchell's role on a 1989 trip, or transmission of a relationship from 1989.

## Findings

### 1. Who David Jan Mitchell was

**1989-03-09 — T1.** Owens's floor statement supplies the contemporary occupation and role: “Many of the ideas for this project” came from S. Daniel Abraham, “David Jan Mitchell, an investment banker in New York,” Joyce Starr, three House members, and others. The “project” is Owens's proposed Middle East Development Bank, discussed immediately after his description of organizing the Center with Abraham. The grammar credits Mitchell with ideas; it does not make him a Center founder, officer, donor, or traveler. [Official *Congressional Record*, pp. 3843–44](https://www.congress.gov/101/crecb/1989/03/09/GPO-CRECB-1989-pt3-9-1.pdf).

**1989-01-12 — T2 contemporaneous press.** A JTA brief reprinted in the *Rhode Island Jewish Herald* said the Sabra Society of State of Israel Bonds had elected Ron Silver president and that the announcement came from “David Jan Mitchell, chairman of the Manhattan New Leadership Division of Israel Bonds.” This independently places the full-name person in organized Israel-related activity immediately before Owens's statement. [Issue PDF, p. 2](https://www.rijha.org/wp-content/uploads/voiceandherald/1989/1989-01-12.pdf).

**2006 and 2009 — T1 SEC.** TSS/Fortress proxy statements identify David J. Mitchell as president of a New York merchant bank he founded in January 1991 and give the exact 1980s chronology. The 2009 proxy states that he was 47, making his 1989 age approximately 27–28, and says:

- Rodman & Renshaw, managing director and principal, 1988–1990;
- Laidlaw Adams & Peck, managing director, 1985–1988;
- earlier Bear Stearns and Oppenheimer service;
- Mitchell Holdings LLC founder/president from January 1991.

The separate 2009 insider filing uses the full reporting name **MITCHELL DAVID JAN** and his full-name signature. [2009 proxy](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1320760/000114420409023308/v147432_def14a.htm); [Form 4](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1320760/000114420409025294/xslF345X03/v148564_ex.xml); [2006 proxy](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1320760/000114420406054588/v060421def14a.htm).

**2001–2002 captures — T3, firm self-description.** The archived Mitchell Holdings site described Mitchell & Company / Mitchell Holdings as a merchant-bank and venture operation. Its biography says Mitchell had led the company since January 1991 and repeats the Rodman, Laidlaw, Bear Stearns, and Oppenheimer chronology. The site used 212-486-4444 and 212-588-0286, the phone/fax later printed in the 2010 Peres emails. [Biography capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20021208165752id_/http://mitchellholdings.com:80/David%20Mitchell.htm); [contact capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20010607190127id_/http://www.mitchellholdings.com:80/ContactUs.htm); [homepage capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20020205022552id_/http://mitchellholdings.com:80/index.html).

**2015-06-23 — T2 trade press.** The *New York Real Estate Journal* identified David Mitchell, incoming Gracious Home chairman, as president of Mitchell Holdings LLC, a New York investment and real-estate development company. This is later continuity, not a Middle East role. [Article PDF](https://nyrej.com/pdf/28524).

### 2. The documented 1989 role—and its limits

The affirmative record is narrow but clear:

1. Mitchell participated in Israel Bonds leadership by January 1989 (T2).
2. Owens publicly credited him as a source of ideas for the development-bank project on March 9 (T1).
3. His SEC employment chronology establishes that he was a New York investment banker at the time (T1).

No record reviewed upgrades that role to formal Center governance or travel:

- The Center's legal and institutional material reviewed in [OWENS-B](owens-b-center.md) identifies Abraham and Owens as organizers/founders. Mitchell does not appear in the reviewed early Center pages or later 990-PF officer/contractor layer.
- Full OCR of Joyce Starr's 1989, 46-page *Development Diplomacy: U.S. Economic Assistance to the West Bank and Gaza* found no **Mitchell**, **Abraham**, or **Owens**. Its acknowledgments list numerous officials and specialists, including advisers to Shimon Peres, but not Mitchell. [Washington Institute publication and PDF](https://www.washingtoninstitute.org/policy-analysis/development-diplomacy-us-economic-assistance-west-bank-and-gaza).
- Exact-name open-web searches did not find Mitchell in Starr's *The Politics of Scarcity* or her 1991 “Water Wars.” The complete 1988 book was not lawfully available in searchable full text, so no whole-book null is claimed.
- The March 23–approximately April 5, 1989 reporting reviewed in [OWENS-A](owens-a-primary-records.md) names Owens's meetings but no traveling companions. It does not name Mitchell. No other reviewed 1989 trip record names him.

**1989-trip answer:** no document located shows Mitchell on a 1989 Owens/Abraham trip. This is an unresolved participant question, not proof of absence. The Wayne Owens papers catalog provides direct future selectors: MS0108 box 128/folder 2 (“Owens Middle East Study Trip,” 1989), boxes 128/3–6 (trip notes, 1989–92), boxes 191/9 and 192/1–2 (1989 Israel/Middle East trips), and P0108 group-photo folders.

### 3. What the bridge actually documents

The defensible timeline is:

`1989 Mitchell idea-source / Israel Bonds role` → **20-year released-record gap** → `late-May 2009 Mitchell–Epstein meeting` → `2009-10-07 Epstein asks Mitchell to approach Danny Abraham` → `2010-09-24 Mitchell offers Peres dinner; Epstein opts out` → `2010-09-28/29 Epstein–Rød-Larsen content begins through a separately documented channel`

**2009-05-27 — T1, earliest dated target-context hit.** In `EFTA02443038`, an email from `djm@mitchellholdings.com` says: “When I saw JEE last week he wanted someone to see this apartment in New York.” The message asks what Mitchell should do next and carries his Mitchell Holdings signature. It proves an in-person encounter by the prior week. It does not identify how or when the men first met.

**2009-10-07 — T1, Abraham bridge.** In `EFTA02437722`, Epstein tells Mitchell that local prosecutor Michael McAuliffe was reportedly close to “Danny Abraham” and asks Mitchell to request a favorable word. Mitchell replies “pleasure” and asks whether Epstein wants him to arrange a meeting at “Danny's house.” Duplicate releases (`EFTA00740394`, `EFTA00740396`, `EFTA00771111`) are the same underlying exchange, not corroboration. This establishes:

- Epstein understood Mitchell to have a usable route to Danny Abraham;
- Mitchell represented that he could ask Abraham and potentially arrange a house meeting;
- the same Abraham node from the 1989 Owens/Center record was active in the Mitchell–Epstein relationship by October 2009.

It does **not** establish that Abraham acted, a meeting occurred, or Mitchell and Epstein knew each other in 1989.

**2010-10 to 2011-02 — T1, later access-broker pattern.** Separate Silverstone correspondence shows Epstein forwarding Mitchell's team material to Peter Mandelson, and Mitchell later meeting Mandelson at Epstein's house (`EFTA02416520`, `EFTA02001404`, `EFTA01809767`). This supports a general 2009–2011 pattern in which Mitchell and Epstein sought introductions or credibility for business projects. It is later than the bridge cutoff and does not connect to Owens or the Center.
