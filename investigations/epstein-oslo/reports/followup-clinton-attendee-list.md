## Findings

### A. September 13 ceremony: the released record is actual-attendance data, but the pages are not online

**Claim — T1 archive metadata; event 1993-09-13; release notice/finding aid dated 2017–2018; checked 2026-07-18.** The [current Clinton Library finding aid for 2018-0024-F](https://www.clintonlibrary.gov/research/archives/finding-aids/israel-palestinian-declaration-principles-white-house-ceremony) describes “a list of attendees present” at the September 13 ceremony and says the responsive records were printed from the WHODB. It identifies:

- **Box 1**
- **Clinton Presidential Records: White House Database (WHODB)**
- **[Event Search]**
- **[1993-09-13]**
- **Id Number: 78 [OA/ID 160000]**
- **Id Number: 160 [OA/ID 160000]**

The finding aid describes WHODB as a database of White House events and the people who attended them, maintained by the Office of Correspondence, Social Office, and Office of Public Liaison. That makes 2018-0024-F the closest located record to an event-attendance answer. It is distinct from:

1. an **invitation list** (who was invited or expected);
2. a **clearance list** (who was submitted or approved for security processing);
3. a **WAVES record** (an access appointment/admission record); and
4. a press estimate of the crowd.

The [official NARA Presidential Records Act release notice RN-LPWJC-2018-019](https://www.archives.gov/files/foia/pra-notifications/pdf/clinton/rn-lpwjc-2018-019.pdf) says all **112 pages** were processed, restricted in part, and proposed for opening in part. Thus the records were opened with redactions; the problem is public digital delivery, not an indication that the entire file remains closed.

**Result:** the current site returns one exact finding-aid hit for `2018-0024-F`, but its exact-ID + “Digitized Content: Yes” search returns **0**. The page contains no record-file attachment. The [legacy item 57483](https://clinton.presidentiallibraries.us/items/show/57483) expressly says no items in the collection have been scanned or made available online. Its identifier browse contains only the finding-aid item. **Epstein's presence remains unknown.** Absence from other ceremony-planning or invitation material would not substitute for a negative search of both Event IDs 78 and 160.

### The invitation/clearance records are a separate collection

**Claim — T1 archive metadata; target event 1993-09-13; checked 2026-07-18.** [FOIA 2008-0728-F, Oslo Accords Signing](https://www.clintonlibrary.gov/research/archives/finding-aids/oslo-accords-signing), is an 87-folder, approximately 3,935-page collection of planning records. Its scope includes invitation lists. The most precise container is:

- **Box 2**
- **Clinton Presidential Records: White House Staff and Office Files**
- **National Security Council / Staff Director / Soderberg, Nancy**
- **[Middle East Peace Process Signing Ceremony] Invitees Contact Numbers and Clearance Information [OA/ID 1406]**

This is useful for identifying invitees or people submitted for clearance, but it is not the WHODB attendance output. The legacy exact-identifier browse produces a finding aid and [one digitized PDF item](https://clinton.presidentiallibraries.us/items/show/14616). The attached PDF is two pages: a withdrawal/redaction sheet and an unrelated June 1993 Stanley Sheinbaum presidential-mail page. It contains no invitation list and no `Epstein` hit.

The GovernmentAttic mirror of the Clinton FOIA logs describes the original 2018-0024-F request as seeking attendees for a “9/15/93” event. That is an administrative-log date error. The later official NARA release notice and the processed finding aid consistently identify the responsive ceremony as **September 13, 1993**.

### B. WAVES: open in part, but no public export or Epstein row was located

**Claim — T1 archive metadata; records 1993-01-20 through 1995-12-31; release notice/finding aid dated 2018; checked 2026-07-18.** The [current finding aid for 2016-0726-F](https://www.clintonlibrary.gov/research/archives/finding-aids/worker-and-visitor-entry-system-waves-1993-1995) identifies:

- **Box 1**
- **Clinton Presidential Records: Worker and Visitor Entry System (WAVES)**
- **Electronic Records**
- **WAVES [OA/ID 180000]**
- **[01/20/1993 – 12/31/1995] [disc]**

The Secret Service used WAVES to manage appointments and access. The responsive records were isolated and copied into Microsoft Excel sheets. The release contains one electronic file, estimated as **136,437 electronic pages**. The NARA release notice says all 136,437 pages were opened in part.

This corrects the shorthand “primary scans”: the primary public-release object is an Excel-derived electronic export. The finding aid lists visitor name, access, appointment time, authority surname, room, caller surname, time of arrival, and badge date among its possible fields. It also warns of data-entry errors and says multiple fields have no data for 1993–1995, including visitor appointment date, authority first name, meeting time/location, caller first name, and time of departure. A surname in an authority or caller field cannot safely be expanded to a person without additional evidence.

The exact-ID search returns one finding aid and **0 digitized exact-ID results**. [Legacy item 57482](https://clinton.presidentiallibraries.us/items/show/57482) expressly says no collection items have been scanned or posted. The NARA notice and FOIA logs are administrative records only. They contain none of the Epstein rows.
