# WP6 — Norwegian MFA archival void mapping

Research completed 2026-07-18 for lead #72588, thread 173. Catalog and public-journal results are snapshots as of that date. No archive, person, or institution was contacted; no innsyn/offentleglova request was submitted.

## Summary

- Waage's 2004 formulation is specific: she found no UD documents **on the negotiations in the Oslo Back Channel**—no minutes, memoranda, or letters—and no official record of important foreign-minister conversations or copies of certain Holst letters quoted elsewhere. The later **January–September 1993** bound comes from Waage's 2026 interviews, not the 2004 gap paragraph.
- “No paper in any folder” must not be read literally across all UD business. Waage's own footnotes cite surviving UD memoranda and cables from May–July 1993; she repeatedly says those records do **not disclose the secret channel**. The verified void is the substantive channel record, not every UD document created in those nine months.
- The launch-brief claim that “Juul shut down Waage's research in 2000” is materially misleading. The first MFA-commissioned study was completed on schedule and published as Evaluation Report 9/2000. Waage says Juul tried to stop funding/continuation in spring 2000; no contemporaneous meeting record has surfaced, and the second study was commissioned in January 2001 and published in 2004.
- Waage's stated explanation is that the missing record would expose Norway as operating on the strong Israeli party's terms and damage the peace-nation brand. That is Waage's reasoned thesis, not proof of who removed or retained particular documents. It is a sufficient non-Epstein explanation under the WP6 guard.
- A small official Holst accession survives at Nasjonalarkivet: **RA-S-7027, Utenriksdepartementet, Johan Jørgen Holst (1993–94)**, four boxes dominated by reaction, correspondence, speeches, clippings, and requests. It is not a comprehensive negotiation archive and has zero digitized documents in Arkivportalen.
- Public catalogs expose no comprehensive Holst personal fonds, no Marianne Heiberg fonds, and no NUPI accession covering her 1992–2004 period. These are catalog nulls, not proof that family- or institution-held papers do not exist.
- The parliamentary track is moving decisively and has already passed the “proposal” stage. A unanimous committee initiative produced a commission, an Oslo-process mandate, and **Law 23 June 2026 no. 75**, which lets the commission compel any person to provide relevant documents or objects. The commission met on 5 May and reports by 31 January 2028.

## Findings

### 1. The verified void: exact scope, bounds, and limits

**Claim — T2 direct research statement and synthesis; event January–September 1993; evidence published 2004 and 2026; reviewed 2026-07-18.** In the introduction to *Peacemaking Is a Risky Business*, Waage says she received privileged access to all relevant, still-classified ministry files for the 1993–96 study. Her operative wording is:

> “Absolutely no documents were found in the ministry’s files on the negotiations in the Oslo Back Channel. No minutes, no memos, no letters…”

That sentence defines the object of the absence: **documents on the back-channel negotiations**. Waage adds that important conversations in which the Norwegian foreign minister discussed the negotiations with the Israeli foreign minister, the US secretary of state, or the PLO chairman appeared not to have been recorded on official paper. Letters by Holst that were excerpted in participant books could not be found in UD or among the few private Holst papers she examined. See Waage, [PRIO Report 1/2004, p. 11](https://cdn.cloud.prio.org/files/dc704558-ffa6-40f9-bf49-9f90ba1588b7/Waage%20Hilde%20Henriksen%202004%20Peacemaking%20is%20a%20Risky%20Business%20PRIO%20Report%201-2004.pdf?inline=true).

Waage did **not** put January–September bounds on that paragraph in 2004. She supplied them in interviews published on 12 and 20 February 2026:

- In [Forskning.no, 2026-02-12](https://www.forskning.no/israel-og-palestina-krig-og-fred-politikk/forsker-om-terje-rod-larsens-private-arkiv-de-savnede-dokumentene-fra-oslo-avtalen-vil-dukke-opp-i-jerusalem/2616891), she said no paper existed in any folder from January through September 1993.
- `I alle departementets mapper og serier manglet dokumenter fra perioden januar til september 1993.` (“Across all the ministry's folders and series, documents from January through September 1993 were missing.”) [Aksess, 2026-02-20](https://www.aksess-tidsskrift.no/aktuelt/diplomati-nasjonalarkivet-oslo-avtalen/261092).

The literal, all-subject reading of those later sentences conflicts with Waage's own 2004 footnotes, which cite several UD records created inside the period. The defensible reconciliation is: January–September is the bounded channel window, while the absent class is the **substantive negotiation record**. Peripheral official records survive but omit the secret channel.

| Document class | What the sources establish | What they do not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Minutes, memoranda, letters on the negotiations | Explicitly absent in Waage's 2004 formulation. | No document-by-document destruction or removal mechanism is proven. |
| Records of Holst's channel discussions with Peres, Christopher, or Arafat | Waage says no official-paper account appeared for the examples she checked. | Not proof that every diary entry, itinerary, telephone log, or foreign counterpart copy is absent. |
| Holst letters quoted in memoirs/books | Copies were not found in UD or the limited Holst private papers Waage saw. | Not a complete inventory of all Holst papers then or now. |
| Rød-Larsen reports | The 2004 report does not identify “Rød-Larsen reports” as a discrete missing UD series. The 2006–26 archive dispute establishes that he acknowledged a private archive and resisted transfer. | It is unsafe to relabel the whole void as missing Rød-Larsen reports. |
| Juul notes/calendars | In 2006 Juul reportedly said she had no official papers but might have notebooks, calendars, or personal notes in boxes; UD's 2026 account says it has no proof those were delivered. | Waage's 2004 report does not identify a specific missing “Juul notes” series, and current contents have not been publicly inventoried. |
| Cable traffic | Surviving cables exist before, during, and after the gap. The 28 May 1993 Washington cable omits the secret channel; Waage says Egeland/Rød-Larsen used the US Embassy's secure telephone until a leak. | The sources do not prove wholesale deletion of a cable series. This is why the prepared request below targets the traffic and metadata precisely. |

The bound maps directly onto the documented chronology: the first Norway round began on 20 January 1993; formal and recognition sessions continued through early September; the Declaration of Principles was signed on 13 September. The void therefore covers the channel's operative documentary spine, not simply an arbitrary archival year.

### 3. Waage's explanation is a documented thesis, not a proven removal chain

**Claim — T2 attributed inference; event explanation concerns 1993 and later archive stewardship; statements published 2026-02-12 and 2026-02-19; reviewed 2026-07-18.** Waage says the documents would show that Norway was a useful `løpegutt for Israel` (“errand runner for Israel”). In a later interview she connected the absence to the strong party setting the rules and to the protection of Norway's peace-nation image. [Forskning.no](https://www.forskning.no/israel-og-palestina-krig-og-fred-politikk/forsker-om-terje-rod-larsens-private-arkiv-de-savnede-dokumentene-fra-oslo-avtalen-vil-dukke-opp-i-jerusalem/2616891) and [Forskerforum](https://www.forskerforum.no/forsker-mener-savnede-ud-dokumenter-ville-svekket-bildet-av-norge-som-fredsnasjon/).

Her explanation coheres with the substantive finding in the 2004 report: Israel controlled what Norway relayed to Washington, Norway's role moved from facilitation toward mediation, and the official success story understated the asymmetry. It does **not** identify who removed a given file, when, or under whose instruction. The March 2026 official review instead describes a likely mixture of UD, Fafo, and personal material and acknowledges that the 2006 inquiry should have made a deeper archival assessment. [UD letter to the Control and Constitution Committee, 2026-03-10, pp. 15–18](https://www.regjeringen.no/contentassets/f1ed05fd7d6b43e4abe7a78ad5b54bee/260310-brev-fra-utenriksministeren-til-kontroll-og-konstitusjonskomiteen.pdf).

Under the pre-committed WP6 sufficiency guard, this is evidence about **Oslo's character, state documentation, and later brand protection**. Nothing in the archival pattern independently places Epstein in 1992–93.

**Verdict:** the first report was neither stopped nor buried. The best-supported account is an **alleged political attempt to deny continuation/funding for phase two in spring 2000**, partly supported by Smith's memory of what Waage told him at the time. The attempt did not persist: a new UD agreement followed in January 2001. There is no evidence that Juul restricted Waage's archive clearance; the later report says the opposite. Waage attributes the reversal to political pressure, including Kåre Willoch's intervention, but no primary paper trail for that causal claim was found.

The corrected chronology strengthens the non-Epstein explanation: the alleged intervention predates any documented Epstein–Rød-Larsen/Juul contact and concerned criticism of Norway's Oslo narrative.

The current recovery effort is real but incomplete:

- UD's 2026 review says its 2006 division of work asked Arkivverket to pursue Rød-Larsen's claimed private archive while UD asked Juul and Egeland about official documents. - Økokrim told UD on 2026-03-06 that it had seized Oslo-related documents, some marked `strengt fortrolig` and one folder marked `Hemmelig`. UD and Nasjonalarkivet retrieved five additional boxes from the couple's cellar on 2026-03-14 for ownership review. [UD letter, 2026-03-10](https://www.regjeringen.no/contentassets/f1ed05fd7d6b43e4abe7a78ad5b54bee/260310-brev-fra-utenriksministeren-til-kontroll-og-konstitusjonskomiteen.pdf); [Storting answer 15:1904, 2026-03-16](https://www.stortinget.no/no/Saker-og-publikasjoner/Sporsmal/Skriftlige-sporsmal-og-svar/Skriftlig-sporsmal/?qnid=116155).
- No later public inventory, ownership decision, archival signature, or finding aid was located through 2026-07-18. It would be premature to say the January–September gap has been recovered.

Rød-Larsen and Juul are charged, not convicted, and deny criminal wrongdoing. The location or mishandling of archival documents is not itself proof of the charged conduct.

## Stortinget status

**Claim — T1 parliamentary and statutory records; events 2026-02-05 through 2026-06-23; status checked 2026-07-18.** The premise “Rødt representantforslag for a special archive-handover law” combines several distinct steps.

1. **Dokument 8:111 S (2025–2026), 2026-02-05**, was sponsored by Arild Hermstad and Julie E. Stuestøl (MDG) and Guri Melby (V)—not Rødt. It proposed a government-appointed independent Epstein commission and an end-2026 report, not a named law compelling Juul/Rød-Larsen to hand archives to Nasjonalarkivet. The sponsors later withdrew it. [Proposal](https://www.stortinget.no/no/Saker-og-publikasjoner/Publikasjoner/Representantforslag/2025-2026/dok8-202526-111s/) | [case status](https://www.stortinget.no/no/Saker-og-publikasjoner/Saker/Sak/?p=200025).
2. Rødt did push the archive issue politically. Bjørnar Moxnes raised the missing private archive in the 11 February debate and filed written questions about the Waage episode and the seized/cellar records. That pressure is documented, but it is not authorship of Dokument 8:111 S.
3. The operative track was the Control and Constitution Committee's own unanimous **Innst. 160 S (2025–2026), 2026-03-03**. It demanded a parliamentary commission and special law. It specifically said the commission needed power to obtain documents and other evidence from private legal subjects when relevant material was believed absent from the administration. [Innst. 160 S](https://www.stortinget.no/no/Saker-og-publikasjoner/Publikasjoner/Innstillinger/Stortinget/2025-2026/inns-202526-160s/?all=true).
4. Storting adopted decisions 555 and 556 on **2026-03-17**. On **2026-04-15** it appointed the seven-member commission through [Innst. 200 S](https://www.stortinget.no/no/Saker-og-publikasjoner/Publikasjoner/Innstillinger/Stortinget/2025-2026/inns-202526-200s/?all=true). The mandate expressly includes the process leading to the 1993 Oslo Agreement, archive routines, and related matters. The commission must report by **2028-01-31** and may issue interim reports.
5. The commission held its first meeting on **2026-05-05**. Secretariat leader Aina Mee Ertzeid takes office on 2026-08-10 and was already leading recruitment in June. [Storting status update](https://www.stortinget.no/no/Hva-skjer-pa-Stortinget/Nyhetsarkiv/Pressemeldingsarkiv/2025-2026/aina-mee-ertzeid-blir-sekretariatsleder-for-granskingskommisjonen/).
6. **Law 23 June 2026 no. 75** entered into force immediately. Section 6 authorizes the commission, by ruling, to order anyone to appear, explain, and provide access to documents or objects believed relevant; sections 7–8 provide enforcement and Supreme Court appeal. [Lovvedtak 87](https://www.stortinget.no/no/Saker-og-publikasjoner/Vedtak/Beslutninger/Lovvedtak/2025-2026/vedtak-202526-087/) | [Lovdata](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2026-06-23-75).

**Status verdict:** yes, it is moving—and has already produced an operational commission and an in-force compulsory-evidence law. The law does not order a named archive's immediate transfer to Nasjonalarkivet; it gives the commission a general, reviewable power that can reach Juul, Rød-Larsen, or any other private holder if the records are relevant.

**Standing-monitor lead proposed, not created:** monitor the commission's public journal, section 6 document orders/appeals, interim reports, any published inventory of Oslo-process material, changes to the 2028-01-31 deadline, and the final archive transfer under section 15.
