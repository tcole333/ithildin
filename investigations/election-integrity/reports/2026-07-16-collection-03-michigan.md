## 3. Key claims analysis (WH page vs. documents)

**(a) Canvasser admissions + incentives — yes, present, from a minority, as recorded witness assertions.** Direct large-scale admission: "Widespread" (~100 fake apps; signed 20 of 22 shown; "just fill them in" instruction at a 100+ meeting; made-up SSNs; quota 7–8/10). Small-scale: "Sit In Car" (names of friends/relatives from his car). Qualified: "Probably Forged" ("probably did at some point"). Instruction to falsify with redacted subject: "Other States." Others-did-it: "Paid By Registration" ("Most of the employees submitted voter registrations with false information because it was 'easy money'"), "Pot" (marijuana/money traded for registrations). Denials: `196`, `233`, and the 2025 clerk. **"Gift cards"**: no document says gift cards — the instruments are payroll **"blue cards"/prepaid debit/Visa** cards; volume-linked pay appears for some (per-signature for two canvassers; $50 bonus per the clerk; per-completed-form per the supervisor's first story) and is denied by others (hourly/no incentives × 4); the case agent himself wrote "**it doesn't appear [redacted was] paying on a per-registration basis**." The page compresses a mixed record into its strongest form.

**(b) Registrations vs votes — the collection contains registration fraud only; no document records a fraudulent vote cast.** All checks target application forms; the 100-app batch was "reportedly **not processed by the clerk**"; the agent's own manual quote concedes bounty-hunting fraud "usually does not result in fraudulent votes actually being cast"; the "Widespread" witness says fabricated forms "were sent to the main office and then shredded" (supervisor's claim to her) and that she doesn't know whether the goal was the election or the paycheck.
