# Collection 2 Review: "China's Acquisition and Exploitation of American Voter Data"

**Reviewer scope:** All 23 PDFs (167 pp) in `datasets/wh_election_integrity/extracted/02-china-voter-data/`, with full text-sidecar reads, full visual page-by-page reads of the four thin-OCR files, and visual page-1 marking checks. Review date 2026-07-16 (subagent). No database writes.

**Bottom line up front:** The collection contains genuine, significant primary records — most importantly a same-day FBI HQ-driven recall of an election-related IIR with contemporaneous "contradicts Director Wray's testimony" emails, an NSA analyst's written statement that NSA "deliberately massaged our one pending PDB to avoid any direct links to the election," and SIGINT-derived reports showing PRC entities analyzing, purchasing, and downloading large volumes of U.S. voter registration data (2018–2023). But the White House page's framing overstates the documents on every load-bearing claim: "220 million" appears in no document (it is the arithmetic sum of four line items on a list of *already-leaked/compromised* datasets a PRC entity possessed as of 2019, dominated by one 2016-vintage 204.8M-record national voter file of a type publicly circulating since 2015–2017); the only unredacted acquisition verbs anywhere in the collection are "purchased," "downloaded" from "commercial websites"/"publicly available" sources, and "likely leaked"; the phrase "data exploitation unit" appears nowhere in unredacted text; and the "cover-up" emails document an analytic-objectivity dispute that was contemporaneously escalated to the IC Analytic Ombudsman — the same dispute litigated publicly in Jan 2021 — rather than a concealed one. The FBI Albany material was substantially disclosed by Chairman Grassley in 2025 (the released IIR carries Senate Judiciary Committee Bates numbers `FBI-SJC-IIR-000001–000006`).

## 2. Key claims analysis

**(a) "220 million voter files."** The figure appears in **no document**; the WHTF statement says "more than 200 million." The source is the 200M-list's voter table: 204,822,241 + 1,746,069 + 7,893,248 + 5,578,302 = **220,039,860** — the WH page's "220 million" is an unlabeled sum of four rows (five more state DBs are "Unknown"). What the document states is possession of "a **list** of Likely Leaked, Compromised Data" as of 2019, data "primarily dated from between 2009 and 2018," with reportorial sourcing ("was in possession," "Apparently Include," "potentially indicating") and no IC confidence language. It does not establish PRC bulk acquisition of the underlying data (one narrow exception: a downloaded file "indicated his possible possession of this data as well" — a single individual). The dominant 204.8M/2016/45GB row matches the profile of national commercial/aggregated voter files publicly exposed in 2015–2017; the same list catalogs public credential dumps (Exploit.in, Hive), torrent sites, medical and defense-contractor breach data. **Voter files per se vs aggregation:** more aggregation-of-circulating-data than documented voter-file theft. **Bought vs stolen vs hacked:** the documents' unredacted verbs are "purchased" (1.22), "downloaded"/"publicly available"/"commercial websites" (1.21), "likely leaked, compromised" (1.2), "obtained [method redacted]" (1.1/1.13), "collected publicly available information" (1.16). No unredacted passage attributes hacking or theft of voter data to China.

**(b) "18 states."** From the 18 States Memo (1.1): PRC **analysis** of "U.S. voter registration data from 18 states" from "the [redacted] Mid-term Elections" (acquisition clause redacted), fields down to party affiliation, vote history, donation expenses; North Carolina "over 8 million voters"; purposes = "mining PII," identifying "important U.S. targets," "General Election [opinion] analysis," intent "to continue [with] state voter registration data." The WH's "bought, stolen, or hacked" is imported; the memo says none of those words in clear text, and no state-system intrusion is claimed anywhere.

**(c) "Data exploitation unit."** The phrase (and any near variant) **appears nowhere in unredacted text** — verified by search across all sidecars plus the visual reads. PRC organizational identities are uniformly redacted. The 2024 report's caveat cuts the other way: "It is unknown if [redacted] were instructed to target unidentified 2024 US elections by [redacted]." Either the claim rests on redacted passages (unverifiable) or it is interpolation.

**(d) Suppression / "Deep State cover-up."** The emails document, in sequence: (1) a sharp but procedurally normal Sep 2020 coordination fight (INR: "we actually do possess positive evidence about China's intent—and it suggests China probably is not interfering"; NIC: "is it just me, or are they basically saying we should self-censor…"); (2) an Oct 2020 clearly-labeled **alternative analysis** NIC memo with the mainline view included — the opposite of suppression procedurally; (3) the Nov 2020 NSA line — real and damning-sounding: "We have deliberately massaged our one pending PDB to avoid any direct links to the election" — with two contemporaneous readings *inside the documents*: benign classification-management (NIC: "NSA has been fantastic about making election-relevant [reporting] usable"; "If this is off, why does [redacted] say, 'ok'?") vs suppression (NIO Cyber: "the IC is deliberately avoiding mentioning a connection to elections for non-substantive reasons"; DNI official: "I would be concerned as to why they are 'deliberately massaging' analysis away from elections"). Critically, the NIO Cyber escalated it same-day to the **IC Analytic Ombudsman**, who replied "we see an opportunity to prevent an analytic objectivity mistake from happening" — i.e., the dispute fed the Ombudsman's politicization review made public in Jan 2021. The emails corroborate a real, contentious dispute; they do not show leadership directives to conceal intelligence, and they show oversight engaging in real time. (4) Dec 2021: the NIO Cyber's labeling-inconsistency complaint ("It's the same personnel doing the same kind of activity") — an after-the-fact consistency argument by the same dissenter, and this collection's best support for "the 2020 mainline was too conservative."

**FBI Albany IIR / Grassley:** the IIR is raw, uncorroborated ("CORROBORATION… None"), sub-sourced to "unidentified PRC government officials," with the FBI's own comment undermining its TikTok-address mechanism. The handout shows HQ DADs Floris/Ugoretz "directly requesting it be recalled" within hours amid celebratory tone ("Crisis averted!", "Fact. Boom."), the "contradicts Director Wray's testimony" rationale appearing repeatedly in writing, an Albany analyst's principled dissent calling that "a political reason," the pre-release stripping of the "veracity … questionable" caveat, FITF's block of re-issue on "Authoritative" DRAIN grounds, and deletion of the redraft. Countervailing facts in the same record: the subsource stream included COVID conspiracy content ("underground bases in Republican states to disperse Coronavirus"); CBP attributed the seized counterfeit licenses "overwhelmingly" to under-21 drinkers; recalls are routine (1,130 since 2019; 154 in 2020); and the IIR reached the entire community — including the White House Situation Room — before recall, with "The Director has the original in his book" on 9/28. **Both narratives are documentable from this record: aggressive HQ intervention on politically sensitive raw reporting with at least one explicitly political-consistency rationale, and defensible tradecraft grounds for pulling thin, partly contradicted single-source reporting.** The Grassley connection: SJC Bates numbers show this exact IIR was produced to Chairman Grassley and publicly released in 2025 — the WH's "kept the information hidden" framing is undercut by both the 2020 community-wide dissemination and the 2025 congressional disclosure; the genuinely new 2026 material is the Inspection Division review artifacts.

**(e) Summary PART 1/2/3:** three 1-page "Summary:" sheets with masked banners and **no serials, dates, classification blocks, or sourcing** — sanitized summaries of unidentified underlying report(s); author agency undeterminable from the release. They describe Chinese **plans/capabilities/options** ("The plans addressed several scenarios, including military conflict between the U.S. and China"), not observed operations, and contain no voter-data content. As released, they are the weakest-provenance items in the collection.
