## Executive summary

The release contains **genuine primary records with real disclosures** — an actual PDB page, NIC assessments and memoranda, raw FBI/NSA/ODNI emails, FBI investigative files — alongside **three newly written 2026 documents** presented under a "previously-classified" banner. On every load-bearing claim, the page's framing **overstates what the documents say**: quotes are truncated mid-sentence, a headline figure is an unlabeled arithmetic sum from a catalog of already-circulating breach data, acquisition verbs are upgraded ("purchased"/"downloaded from commercial websites" → "bought, stolen, or hacked"), a declined-and-closed FBI case is framed as awaiting investigation, and the IC's own caveats — including its warning that *false claims of voting-machine compromise* would be used to undermine confidence — go unmentioned.

At the same time, the documents give real ammunition to the narrower claims: the IC did judge election infrastructure vulnerable; a genuine analytic dispute over China 2020 did occur, with an NSA analyst writing "We have deliberately massaged our one pending PDB to avoid any direct links to the election"; FBI HQ did recall the Albany IIR within 11 hours with "contradicts Director Wray's testimony" in writing; canvassers in Michigan did admit forging registrations; and PRC entities demonstrably analyzed, purchased, and downloaded large volumes of US voter data 2018–2023. **The honest story is "vulnerability, dispute, and data-brokered exposure — documented"; the page's story is "manipulation, cover-up, and theft — proven," which the documents do not support.** No document in the release states that any US vote total was altered.

## Collection verdicts

### 1. Voting-system vulnerabilities (8 docs, 37pp)
Both marquee page quotes are real, from NICM 2020-003 (15 Jan 2020) — truncated to drop "for the 2020 presidential election" and the same paragraph's "we do not know whether any of them have specific plans." The same memo judges at-scale manipulation "difficult" and says "postelection audits and paper trails very likely would uncover such an effort," and warns adversaries could make *false* compromise claims that would be "difficult… or impossible… to disprove." The IC's Aug 2020 matrix marks **Russia** as the only actor with "Observed" activity against election systems (2016 cycle). The Venezuela "plot" document is a **new 29 June 2026 CIA summary** (declassified 1 July — a two-day classification lifetime; uncoordinated with the IC; all ~22 endnotes redacted) whose own limitations section concedes limited sourcing, in-theory capability, and that the regime "did not need to resort to gross fraud" in the boycotted Dec 2020 election. The "CISA Election Report" was **never classified** — an authorless Word→PDF finalized 7/13, modified 12:14 ET on release day; its remedies are CISA's standard prescriptions (paper ballots, manual audits).

### 2. China voter data (23 docs, 167pp — the largest)
"220 million voter files" appears in **no document**: it is the sum (220,039,860) of four rows on a 2019 *list of likely leaked/compromised datasets* a PRC entity possessed — dominated by a 2016-vintage 204.8M-record national file matching datasets publicly circulating since 2015–2017, cataloged alongside Exploit.in credential dumps and torrent sites. Unredacted acquisition verbs: "purchased" (2023), "downloaded" from "commercial websites" (Jan 2022 — a SUBSTANTIVE REVISION issued specifically to make that clarification), "likely leaked" (2019). "Data exploitation unit" appears nowhere in unredacted text. The suppression emails document a real, sharp analytic dispute — but one escalated same-day to the IC Analytic Ombudsman and litigated publicly in Jan 2021 (Zulauf report, Ratcliffe letter, March 2021 ICA minority view). The Albany IIR is the **recycled 2025 Grassley/SJC production** (Bates FBI-SJC-IIR-000001-6); the genuinely new item is the FBI Inspection Division's July 2025 Strategic Review handout, which documents both aggressive HQ intervention ("Fact. Boom."; "the reporting will contradict Director Wray's testimony") *and* defensible tradecraft grounds (uncorroborated CHS, COVID-conspiracy subsource content, CBP attributing the license seizures to underage drinkers). A released PDB page (25 Jun 2020) shows China-threat reporting *was* reaching the President — in tension with the "hidden from the President" frame.

### 3. Michigan / GBI Strategies (25 docs, 53pp)
Real FBI case file (56D-DE-3407960) with genuine canvasser forgery admissions — one witness: ~100 fake applications, quotas, a mass-meeting "just fill them in" instruction — and a documented chronology of DOJ PIN scope-gating (2021 blue-cards-only), case-agent dissent in writing, full grand-jury authorization only Feb 2023, a 5-week election-sensitivity pause, an 8-month FBI lab backlog, and a 2024 no-prosecution muddle with no written declination. **But the release's own terminal document is a 25 Sep 2025 closing LHM — current administration, Patel-era FBI — finding the investigation "did not identify a criminal violation or a priority threat to national security," with AUSA concurrence.** The page does not disclose the closure while stating Patel "is being directed to ensure that the matter is fully investigated." The page also reverses the referral causality (the Muskegon **city clerk** flagged the forms; MSP briefed FBI two days *before* the raid), assigns a party affiliation the documents never establish, converts payroll debit cards to "gift cards," and implies votes where the documents show only registrations (91 of 107 box applications had no database hits — but that box was intercepted *unprocessed*; no document records a fraudulent vote cast).

### 4. Noncitizens on voter rolls (2 docs, 12pp)
Neither document was ever classified. The "approximately 278,000" is a merger of two incompatible tracks from an unsigned, undated one-pager: "OVER 250,000" from matching four states' *public* voter files against unstated federal data (no methodology, no match keys), plus 28,000 from SAVE checks across 25 states — flag rates differing ~15x. "Democrat states refused to share their voter files" appears **nowhere in the release** (the 250k came *from* Democrat-led states' public files). Naturalization false positives — the failure mode that collapsed TX-2019/FL/VA predecessors — are never addressed. Neither document claims anyone voted. The summary attacks a named federal judge ("activist Judge Sparkle Sooknanan") on DHS letterhead. The companion "Voter Registration Database Threats" report (Word, 2026-06-24) contains zero noncitizen content and overstates its own citations.

## Cross-cutting provenance

- **Declassification authorities**: three patterns — President Trump (3 Jul 2026), DNI Gabbard (16 Mar 2026, one doc), and **Counsel to the President Warrington (10 Jul 2026)** on the CIA WIRe, key emails, and all FBI files.
