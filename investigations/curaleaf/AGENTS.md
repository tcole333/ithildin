# Curaleaf Investigation Context

This investigation began with two August 2026 articles by The Newsground. Treat
the articles as a commission and discovery map, not evidence of wrongdoing.

## Critical distinctions

- The bank in the Tangela share-repo story is **Credit Bank of Moscow (CBoM / MKB)**,
  not Sberbank. Resolve exact legal names and identifiers for every Russian bank,
  broker, affiliate, custodian, and sanctions entry.
- A securities repo can transfer legal title at inception. Tangela's accounts say
  CBoM purchased 15.5 million shares in 2020 and later "realized" them in December
  2023. Do not shorthand that as a 2023 seizure without explaining the mechanics.
- Sanctions analysis is transaction-specific: establish the entity, ownership/control,
  designation date, jurisdictional nexus, currency, payer, recipient, intermediary
  banks, licenses, and transaction date before suggesting a violation.
- FBI FOIA **1579784-000** proves investigative activity involving Curaleaf-related
  facts. It does not publicly prove Curaleaf was a formal target, that a named person
  committed a crime, why the matters ended, or that either grand jury formally closed
  without charges.
- FD-302s memorialize statements; they are not FBI findings. Preserve tentative
  language and the exculpatory passages in both released witness interviews.
- The alleged Abramovich identity in Pennsylvania ownership discussions and the
  Nicholas Jordan identity in the redacted FBI file are reporter reconstructions.
  The Nicholas profile match is strong but remains an inference until primary records
  name him.
- Do not conflate Cetus Investments, Meliastove Investments, Tangela Holdings,
  Universal Growth Capital, Lifebrook Investments, Gociter, MedTech, Measure 8, or
  Sputnik vehicles. Resolve jurisdiction, registration number, directors, beneficial
  ownership, and dates for each.

## Immediate contradictions and unknowns

1. Curaleaf says Cetus debt was repaid in full on 2019-12-20, while an
   article-published audit-confirmation excerpt reports $90,795,144.42 due at
   2019-12-31. Obtain full confirmation, payoff, wire, lien-release, and closing records.
2. Identify Tangela's unnamed affiliate/surety and reconcile the 15.5 million repo
   shares, 10.2 million additional pledge, RUB 3.1685 billion partial payment, interest,
   final settlement, custodians, and ultimate buyer/holder.
3. Trace Tangela's receivable from its BVI parent, reported at approximately
   $53.85m (2019), $57.06m (2020), $85.14m (2021), $122.45m (2022), and
   $130.42m (2023).
4. Test Lifebrook's "unaffiliated" status and identify its Curaleaf, Inc. board nominee
   and the jointly selected nominee. Obtain the subscription, shareholders, protection,
   and put/call agreements.
5. Reconstruct ownership at every Pennsylvania application date and compare the exact
   disclosure thresholds across other licensed states.
6. Seek FBI opening/closing material, disposition codes, additional 302s, and segregable
   non-grand-jury pages using the identifiers above. Expect Rule 6(e) and privacy limits.

## Evidence handling

- Certified registry and regulator copies outrank DocumentCloud copies.
- Article-published images support only the exact visible excerpt; do not treat them as
  the complete agreement or account.
- Published leaked material is a lead source. Minimize incidental PII and require
  independent primary corroboration before promotion.
- Record absence claims only with a saved, date-bounded search artifact and the exact
  legal reporting duty. No SEDI/SEDAR+/EDGAR hit is not by itself proof of omission.
- No present evidence authorizes allegations of bribery, money laundering, visa fraud,
  sanctions evasion, intentional false filing, or nominee ownership by any named party.
