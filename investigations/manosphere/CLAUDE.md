# Manosphere Investigation Addendum

This profile is about the commercial ecosystem, not the rhetoric by itself.

## Research Priorities

- Prefer entities, products, payment flows, domains, and refund terms over personality drama.
- Treat a creator handle, legal name, and company name as separate until resolved.
- Focus on falsifiable claims: income claims, investment claims, business-opportunity promises, product claims, refund promises, and customer counts.
- Archive-first: compare current pages to Wayback snapshots, domain history, and certificate timelines before inferring rebrands.
- Entity-first: whenever a target looks promising, resolve the legal entity, DBA, merchant stack, and registered agent quickly.

## Evidence Discipline

- Trust primary records first: registries, court records, archived offer pages, terms pages, payment/platform artifacts, 990s, and enforcement documents.
- Treat Reddit, Trustpilot, BBB, YouTube callouts, and social-media threads as lead generators, not as stand-alone evidence.
- Separate `what is sold` from `how it is marketed` from `who gets paid`.
- Do not mistake social extremity, misogyny, or grift aesthetics for fraud evidence. The point is to find documentary contradictions or structurally risky business conduct.

## Good Tier 0 Targets

- Paid communities, academies, masterminds, coaching funnels
- Supplement brands and physical-product stores
- Crypto, forex, gambling, or investing education products
- Recurring checkout vendors, fulfillment firms, affiliate agencies, and platform-migration patterns

## Good Lead Signals

- Entity churn or frequent rebranding
- Deleted or rewritten claims on archived pages
- Shared officers, agents, addresses, or domains across nominally separate brands
- Refund disputes, chargeback language, forced arbitration, or no-refund policies
- Investment or earnings claims that collide with disclosures, court records, or product reality
