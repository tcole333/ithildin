# Smartbird / Allbirds / NewBird AI — Investigation-Specific Context

## The Core Question

On **April 15, 2026**, Allbirds, Inc. (NASDAQ: BIRD) — now **Smartbird,
Inc.** in current SEC submissions — was a cash-burning,
going-concern-flagged sustainable footwear company that had closed all its
full-price U.S. stores — announced a rebrand to "NewBird AI" with a $50M
convertible financing from an undisclosed institutional investor, stating
it would become a "fully integrated GPU-as-a-Service (GPUaaS) provider."

The stock closed up **~582% in one day**, from ~$3 to $16.99.

**The investigation is not about whether NewBird AI succeeds.** It is about:
1. Is this pattern-identical to a known pump-and-dump playbook?
2. Who benefited from the price move — and were they in position to do so?
3. What is the conversion/dilution structure of the $50M facility?
4. Does the company have the operational substance to justify the narrative?

## The Comparable — Read This First

**Long Island Iced Tea Corp → Long Blockchain Corp (December 2017)**:
- Iced tea company announces blockchain pivot; stock +380%, volume +1,000%
- Never executed on blockchain business
- February 2021: SEC delists
- July 2021: SEC charges 3 individuals (Eric Watson, Oliver Barret-Lindsay,
  Gannon Giguiere) with insider trading; Watson controlled ~30% of the float
  and tipped off a broker about the pivot before announcement

Every analytical frame for BIRD should reference this playbook and ask
**"where is the equivalent here?"**

## Analytical Rules of Engagement

1. **Primary-source-first.** Stock commentary, retail-investor forums, and
   "analyst" blogs are sentiment signal only. EDGAR, FINRA, and the PREM14A
   proxy are the evidence corpus.

2. **The shoe sale and the AI pivot are two transactions, announced
   15 days apart, voted on at the same special meeting.** Treat them as a
   coupled structure, not independent events. Any stockholder net-proceeds
   distribution from the $39M sale will be diluted by the convertible —
   the economic equities of existing holders depend on both pieces.

3. **"Going concern" is the key financial substrate.** BIRD's 2025 10-K
   was filed around the same window as the AXNY announcement, flagged
   substantial doubt about ability to continue operating, and reported
   negative $55.1M operating cash flow. A distressed issuer is the canonical
   habitat for toxic-convertible structures; structure scrutiny is
   proportional to distress.

4. **Distinguish fact from inference rigorously.** That the pattern
   resembles Long Blockchain is a **hypothesis for structural comparison**,
   not an allegation. The SEC charged specific individuals in that case on
   specific conduct. Do not project those allegations onto current BIRD
   actors without evidence; do use the pattern to know where to look.

5. **Insider trading allegations require evidence of trading on material
   non-public information.** "Insiders sold shares" is not insider trading
   if the trades were under established 10b5-1 plans adopted well before
   material events. Plan adoption dates matter.

  independently.

  corroborate via third-party placement-agent-tracker databases
