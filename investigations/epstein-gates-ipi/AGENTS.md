# Epstein-Gates-IPI Investigation — Agent Context

This profile is a focused child investigation of the broader Epstein corpus. The
primary task is forensic reconciliation of grants, expenditures, activities,
deliverables, and information recipients—not proving a predetermined intelligence
theory.

## Required Analytical Discipline

- Label each monetary statement as **proposed**, **committed**, **paid**, **spent**,
  or **inferred**. Do not collapse these categories.
- General operating support is fungible. Do not attribute a specific IPI payment
  to Gates funding without an explicit ledger, restricted-fund schedule, or other
  defensible allocation record.
- Treat each EFTA page as one source even when multiple corpus layers return it.
- Separate three questions: what Gates requested; what IPI actually did; what
  Epstein privately received or controlled.
- The CIA fake vaccination operation and the IPI program's response to its damage
  are documented. CIA tasking, sample collection by IPI, or a White House Counsel
  role are not presently documented.
- Calendar proximity is a lead generator, not corroboration.

## Core Ledgers

Maintain four linked ledgers:

1. Money in: grant agreements, amendments, payment dates, and restrictions.
2. Money out: vendors, consultants, invoices, travel, representation, and overhead.
3. Work product: trips, meetings, surveys, assessments, publications, and advice.
4. Control/information: tasking, editing, branding, report recipients, raw data,
   confidential updates, and decisions influenced.
