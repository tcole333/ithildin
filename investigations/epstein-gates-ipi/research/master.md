## Scope and central question

This investigation asks what Jeffrey Epstein actually did in the relationship
between the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation and the International Peace Institute
(IPI), how Gates money was ultimately used, what information and access the program
produced, and who controlled or received those products.

The BCCI comparison is a functional model, not a claim that Epstein literally
operated a bank. The question is whether he supplied some of the same useful
infrastructure on a smaller and more personalized scale: moving patrons toward
institutions and advisers, shaping the resulting structures, seeking private
economics, remaining outside public-facing materials, and retaining access to
information produced with other people's money. That model must be tested against
ordinary donor cultivation, legitimate philanthropic consulting, IPI's independent
program value, and Epstein's familiar self-aggrandizement.

### Strong inferences still requiring records

- Epstein held an undisclosed—or at minimum donor-unaware—parallel recipient-side

- The program had a legitimate polio purpose while also producing political,
  security, access, and influence intelligence with private utility to Epstein and
  Rød-Larsen beyond the public-health deliverable.
- Across the IPI grant, the Gates/JPMorgan donor vehicle, the Ruemmler recruitment
  pitch, and the later Edmond de Rothschild matter, Epstein repeatedly positioned
  himself between principals who did not necessarily share the same understanding
  of his role, compensation, or information access.

### Higher-risk branches that remain open

- Whether an intelligence service influenced or made use of the IPI program.
- Whether IPI or one of its local partners performed undisclosed collection beyond
  the recovered reports, including any biological or identity-related collection.
- Whether Epstein converted the CIA-created vaccination scandal, the program's
  security sensitivities, or the resulting private reports into reputational or
  relational leverage over Gates. Gates's knowledge or complicity is not required
  for that theory; concealed access could itself be the source of leverage.
- Whether Ruemmler participated in the CIA vaccination-cover policy decision or
  later connected any part of that portfolio to Epstein, Gates, or IPI.
- Which specific IPI disbursements came from Gates money. The aggregate grants and
  activities are established, but grant-level attribution still requires the
  underlying accounting.

The absence of proof for the literal endpoint—such as Gates knowingly financing a
CIA DNA operation—does not resolve these branches and should not be used as a
screening rule. The investigation instead separates the evidence into the
following claim ladder:

1. **Documented fact:** Gates funded IPI to perform politically and operationally
   sensitive polio-access work in countries where the CIA vaccination scandal had
   damaged public-health programs; IPI privately routed resulting reports to
   Epstein; Gates says he did not know this continued routing was occurring.
2. **Strong inference:** Epstein's hidden position created an information and
   relationship asymmetry. It allowed him to observe, package, and potentially
   exploit work financed by Gates while presenting himself to different parties as
   introducer, adviser, allocator, or problem-solver.
   The same confidentiality, non-attribution, oral elaboration, and discreet-access
   practices that made the work defensible in hostile settings also made it
   reputationally leverageable if selectively disclosed without context.
3. **Testable higher-risk hypothesis:** Epstein or another actor used the legitimate
   grant as cover, infrastructure, or a source of leverage for purposes not stated
   in the grant. This can be tested through native grant accounts, consultant files,
   raw field notes, recipient logs, government records, and Epstein's downstream
   use of the reporting; it does not depend on proving Gates knew of such a purpose.

## CIA vaccination-cover timeline and the Ruemmler question

### Established timeline

- **July 2011:** The press exposed the CIA-backed fake hepatitis-B vaccination
  campaign used in an attempt to obtain DNA from the bin Laden compound in
  Abbottabad.
- **January 6, 2013:** Deans of leading U.S. public-health schools asked President
  Obama to pledge that health programs would not be used for intelligence.
- **March 27, 2013:** Gates heard IPI's presentation in Strasbourg. An April 23
  follow-up from Robert Rosen called the conversation “invaluable” and said Gates
  planned to formalize an engagement.
- **August 2013:** According to the later White House letter and CIA statement,
  CIA Director John Brennan internally directed the agency to make no operational
  use of vaccination programs or vaccination workers. In a May 19, 2014 on-record
  statement later released through CIA FOIA, the agency said Brennan established
  the policy after considering “a variety of views, including those from outside
  the Agency.” The statement did not describe an interagency clearance process.
- **September–October 2013:** IPI circulated large proposals and Gates made the
  first $2.5 million general-support commitment.
- **April 21, 2014:** The White House announced Neil Eggleston would replace
  Kathryn Ruemmler. Contemporary reporting said Ruemmler had been trying to leave
  for months and Obama had persuaded her to remain until spring.
- **May 16, 2014:** Lisa Monaco, Assistant to the President for Homeland Security
  and Counterterrorism, wrote the public-health deans disclosing the August 2013
  policy. The letter also said the CIA would not seek or exploit DNA or other
  genetic material acquired through such programs. The two-page signed letter is
  preserved as DocumentCloud 1164764; it names neither Ruemmler nor the White
  House Counsel's Office.
- **May 19–20, 2014:** The policy became public through press reports and the
  schools' publication of the response.
- **May 30, 2014:** IPI's Pakistan update, forwarded to Epstein, discussed local
  reception of the CIA policy and IPI contacts relevant to polio access.

### Initial read

The CIA policy timeline does not by itself prove government direction of the IPI
program, but it is more than atmospheric coincidence. The recovered IPI work
explicitly treated the CIA operation as an operational obstacle, proposed discreet
access and influence strategies in the affected countries, and generated private
political-security reporting that flowed to Epstein without Gates's knowledge.
That makes the policy context relevant to what the grant produced and what private
value Epstein could derive from it, even if no intelligence-service tasking is
ever found.

The following points limit the narrower claim that Ruemmler personally connected
the White House policy decision to Epstein, Gates, or IPI:

1. The operative CIA decision is dated to August 2013, roughly nine months before
   the public disclosure and Ruemmler's exit.
2. The public communication came from Lisa Monaco's homeland-security and
   counterterrorism channel, not from the White House Counsel's Office.
3. Ruemmler's successor was announced before the policy was made public, and
   contemporary reporting says her departure had been in progress for months.
4. Targeted Epstein-corpus searches for “Ruemmler vaccination” and “Ruemmler DNA
   Brennan” returned no responsive documents. “Ruemmler polio” produced two hits
   in an unrelated December 2014 donor-advised-fund exchange with Gates, not the
   CIA policy. This is a corpus-coverage result, not proof that no White House
   record exists.
5. The Obama Library's 579-asset release of Lisa Monaco's Outlook calendar also
   contains no visible event fields naming vaccination, immunization, polio, DNA,
   the public-health deans, Ruemmler, or White House Counsel. That release is too
   incomplete to be dispositive: 169 assets are withheld in full, only two
   released August 2013 events are present, and there are no released May 2014
   events. The August entries are generic “SVTC” holds without useful subjects.

The unresolved issue is whether White House Counsel records show advice, clearance,
or awareness concerning the policy; if so, whether that was routine legal review
or something more. No present evidence links Ruemmler, Epstein, or IPI to the
decision.

### Ruemmler's CIA medal and attorney-general withdrawal

Two later events sharpen the Ruemmler records question without establishing a
connection to the vaccination policy.

  Ruemmler/Brennan confirmation is located. Ruemmler's “highest award” shorthand

### Edmond de Rothschild comparator: tax program, counsel transition, and fees

The available primary record does not support describing the 2015 Edmond de
Rothschild matter as a conventional prosecution opened specifically against the
bank. DOJ announced the Swiss Bank Program on August 29, 2013. Attorney General
Eric Holder, Deputy Attorney General James Cole, and Tax Division Assistant
Attorney General Kathryn Keneally were its publicly identified U.S. principals.
The program was available only to Swiss banks that were not already under a DOJ
criminal investigation of their offshore activity.

The executed EdR non-prosecution agreement says Banque Privée Edmond de
Rothschild SA and its Lugano subsidiary submitted separate Category 2 letters of
intent on **December 27, 2013**. Their matter therefore began as voluntary entry
into a department-wide tax program, followed by disclosure, penalty calculation,
negotiation, and a non-prosecution agreement. The DOJ release identifies the
officials handling the final resolution as Acting Tax Division AAG Caroline
Ciraolo, counsel Kevin F. Sweeney, Swiss Bank Program coordinator Thomas J.
Sawyer, Senior Litigation Counsel Nanette Davis, and Tax Division Attorney
Kimberle Dodd, with assistance from IRS Criminal Investigation and IRS Large
Business & International.

The presently documented counsel sequence is:

## Forensic work plan

### 1. Version-control the grant record

Create a table for every proposal, budget, agreement, amendment, and report with:
document date, effective date, amount, scope, restrictions, incorporated exhibits,
signatories, and superseded/superseding document.

### 2. Reconcile money in

Map Gates commitments and payment dates to IPI bank receipts and restricted-fund
accounting. Preserve the distinction between the $2.5 million general-support
grant and the project grant that grew from $2.5 million to $5.5 million.

### 3. Reconcile money out

Obtain or identify invoices, consultant contracts, travel claims, local office and
representation costs, meeting expenses, survey vendors, and country partners.
Search exact amounts ($40,146; $16,408; $11,890) and descriptive phrases across
the corpus. Use IPI 990s and audited statements to constrain salaries, contractors,
foreign expenditures, travel, conferences, occupancy, and related-party items.

### 4. Link expenditure to activity and output

For each country, build:

`grant/payment → payee → trip/meeting → deliverable → recipient → operational use`

An expenditure is not fully explained merely because an annual report names a
program. The investigation should identify the actual payee, service, output, and
recipient where records permit.

### 5. Map control and information

Record who proposed the task, approved it, edited language, controlled branding,
selected consultants, attended meetings, received drafts, received raw or
confidential reporting, and made decisions from it. Compare what Gates received
with what Epstein received.

### 6. Seek external accounting records

Potential public-record sources include the Norwegian Ministry of Foreign Affairs,
Norad, other public IPI funders, audited cost-allocation schedules, archived IPI
board materials, 990 workpapers where obtainable, and grantmaking records held by
partner institutions.

## Tests that would move the assessment

Highest-yield discriminators for the leverage model:

- Build a complete attachment-routing and version graph: original recipients,
  first-send and forward times, edits, replies, oral-briefing references, and later
  versions. Drafts sent to Epstein before Gates, adopted Epstein edits, or products
  withheld from Gates would materially strengthen the control-through-context
  hypothesis.
- Recover Gates-side recipient, tasking, security, briefing, and risk records. The
  key questions are who was authorized to receive the work, what the foundation
  understood about non-attribution and non-state contacts, and whether it assessed
  conspiracy or reputational exposure from the CIA vaccination scandal.
- Search for downstream use rather than possession alone: Epstein quoting or
  paraphrasing IPI material to Gates, Nikolic, donors, banks, lawyers, government
  officials, or media; offering to solve problems identified in the reports; or
  referring to the ability to disclose Gates-related operational details.
- Establish a forwarding baseline. If Rød-Larsen indiscriminately sent completed
  IPI reports to many friends and donors, operational-control theories weaken,
  although the donor-unaware governance and exposure problem remains.
- Trace the provenance of Epstein's instruction to omit Gates's name and compare
  it with the completed Somalia report's explicit non-attribution. Determine
  whether each decision reflected source safety, donor anonymity, institutional
  branding, reputational protection, or a common strategy.
- Recover Ruemmler origin and billing records. A Gates/DAF matter opening, time
  entry, draft, invoice, or direct tasking would strengthen the intended-fixer
  hypothesis; a clean absence would leave the documented recruitment pitch but
  weaken a completed-placement theory.

Evidence strengthening the intelligence-adjacent or covert-routing hypothesis:

- Consultants or partners with documented intelligence or military functions who
  performed undisclosed tasks beyond the approved scope.
- Tasking or reporting chains in which Epstein received raw operational information
  unavailable to Gates or the public.
- Undisclosed pass-throughs, unsupported representation costs, or vague consulting
  payments without matching deliverables.
- Documents showing that program design was responsive to a government or
  intelligence requirement not disclosed in the Gates scope.
- White House, CIA, or NSC records directly connecting the IPI program or its
  principals to the vaccination-policy deliberation.

Evidence weakening the higher-risk operational-control or intelligence-tasking
branches, without erasing the documented information asymmetry:

- Actual expenses reconcile to identifiable public-health, security, survey, and
  peacebuilding professionals with deliverables matching approved milestones.
- Gates staff received the same reporting and directly tasked the political and
  security analysis.
- The program continued under direct Gates oversight and produced comparable work
  after Epstein's involvement ceased.
- White House records show the CIA policy was developed and cleared through normal
  public-health and national-security channels with no IPI, Gates, Epstein, or
  Ruemmler connection beyond official responsibility.
