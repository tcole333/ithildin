## The October Wire

On October 15, 2013, Black Family Partners — the investment vehicle managing approximately $1.95 billion in Leon Black's personal wealth — initiated a wire transfer of $15,000,000 to Deutsche Bank account 44129244. The receiving account belonged to Southern Trust Company Inc., a single-client trust company incorporated in the U.S. Virgin Islands at 6100 Red Hook Quarter B3, St. Thomas. The sole beneficiary of Southern Trust Company was Jeffrey Epstein, who at this point had been a registered sex offender for five years. [EFTA02339486]

The wire wasn't unusual in any mechanical sense. It moved through ordinary banking channels, was processed by Deutsche Bank's standard wire transfer system, and landed in an Elite Money Market Deposit account. The compliance department at Deutsche Bank did not flag the transaction. No Suspicious Activity Report was filed. $15 million moved from a billionaire's family office to a convicted sex offender's trust company, and the banking system treated it as routine.

This was not the first such transfer, nor the last. And it was not only Leon Black.

## The Three Founders

Apollo Global Management was founded in 1990 by Leon Black, Marc Rowan, and Joshua Harris. By the mid-2010s it managed over $400 billion in assets. All three founders had direct, documented financial relationships with Jeffrey Epstein — a fact that would not become public until 2019, and whose full scope is still being reconstructed from internal documents released through litigation.

The Dechert LLP report, filed as an Apollo 8-K exhibit in January 2021 [SEC ADSH 0001193125-21-016405], attempted to frame Epstein's relationship as primarily a Black affair. The documentary record tells a different story.

### Leon Black: $158M+ in Direct Payments

The complete payment schedule, per Deutsche Bank records and the Dechert report:

- **February 2013**: $15,000,000 to STC [EFTA02339486]
- **May 2013**: $16,500,000 to STC [EFTA02339486]
- **August 2013**: $8,500,000 to STC [EFTA02339486]
- **October 2015**: $15,000,000 to STC + $4,750,000 to J. Epstein VI Foundation = $19,750,000 [EFTA02339486, EFTA02348528]
- **Additional transfers through 2017**: cumulative total exceeds $158,000,000

That October 2015 payment is worth pausing on. The initial negotiation between Richard Kahn (Epstein's accountant) and Brad Wechsler (Black's financial manager at Elysium Management) specified $15M to STC plus $4.75M to the J. Epstein VI Foundation — a total of $19.75M. But Black personally intervened: "full 19.75mm shld go to stc," he instructed Wechsler. He wanted the entire amount routed through the trust company rather than split between entities. [EFTA02348528]

Why would a client care about the internal routing of his own payment? Because the STC pathway offered fewer reporting requirements. The J. Epstein VI Foundation, as a 501(c)(3), would need to disclose the donation on its Form 990. Southern Trust Company, as a private trust, had no such obligation.

### Marc Rowan: Tax Adviser to the Network

Marc Rowan's engagement with Epstein was not a simple financial relationship — it was a professional advisory arrangement that embedded Epstein into Apollo's corporate architecture.

Rowan personally introduced Epstein to Perry Shwachman, a partner at Sidley Austin LLP, in the context of Apollo's "Liquid Funding" vehicle. [EFTA02454291] He served as Epstein's financial adviser on the Apollo Tax Receivable Agreement (TRA) valuation — the mechanism through which Apollo's IPO generated tax benefits worth billions for its founders. When Epstein asked Rowan "the tra liability amount is on apollo's balance sheet. can you send me," Rowan provided the information. [EFTA02454291]

Rowan sought Epstein's advice on IRS Form 8865 (Return of US Persons With Respect to Certain Foreign Partnerships), the kind of filing that touches cross-border tax structuring. Epstein replied: "Happy to explain my thinking on the 8865 issue." [EFTA02454291]

On April 9, 2014, Marc and Carolyn Rowan wrote a personal check (#000013326, US Trust/Bank of America) for $838,675 to Leon Black, tagged "2013HERITAGE." The accompanying TRA breakdown documents show this was part of Apollo's internal founder settlement structure. [EFTA02576529]

The check itself is notable because it demonstrates that Epstein had visibility into inter-founder financial settlements at Apollo — the kind of information that is ordinarily tightly held within a private equity partnership.

### Joshua Harris: Eight Meetings in Four Months

Joshua Harris's engagement is documented through scheduling records rather than wire transfers, but the pattern is no less revealing.

Between October 2013 and February 2014, Harris had at minimum eight confirmed in-person meetings with Epstein: a 7:30 AM meeting with Harris, Black, and Epstein together on October 22, 2013; additional meetings documented through calendar entries and email confirmations. [EFTA02576529]

Their email relationship was casual and direct. On November 26, 2014, Harris wrote from his Apollo email: "Hey Jeff. Sorry i missed you. Crazy week? U around any time this weekend?" [EFTA02576529]

Epstein named Harris as a party who needed to sign off on his 25% advisory fee proposal for the Athene restructuring — a transaction worth billions. In a January 18, 2016 email to Leon Black's assistant Melanie Spinella, Epstein described terms that "marc and josh" would need to approve. [EFTA02576529]

Michael Wolff's unpublished Epstein profile, recovered in multiple draft versions from the document corpus [HOUSE_OVERSIGHT_024239, 022905, 022753, 022850], describes Harris attending dinners at 9 East 71st Street — the same townhouse where Epstein hosted his most important social contacts.

## The Plumbing: How Money Moves to a Sex Offender

Understanding the Apollo pipeline requires understanding the infrastructure Epstein built to receive funds. This wasn't a man taking cash payments. It was a financial architecture.

### Southern Trust Company

STC was incorporated in the U.S. Virgin Islands — a jurisdiction that offers trust companies significant privacy advantages over mainland incorporation. Its Deutsche Bank account (44129244) was the primary receiving vessel for large transfers. By December 2015, the account held $109,981,919.36. [EFTA02347813]

In August 2015, Epstein sent himself a memo revealing he was "sitting on over 130 million in CASH" — a figure that aligns with the STC balance plus holdings across other accounts. [EFTA02347813]

STC's June 2016 Deutsche Bank statement shows $56,542,688.38 in the Elite Money Market Deposit account alone. [EFTA02347813] The address: 6100 Red Hook Quarter, St. Thomas, USVI — the same address used by dozens of Epstein-controlled entities.

### The Five-Tier Architecture

The full corporate structure reveals at least five tiers of financial intermediation:

1. **Source**: Black Family Partners LP, managed by Elysium Management LLC (445 Park Avenue)
2. **Conduit**: Wire transfer through Deutsche Bank standard channels
3. **Receiving entity**: Southern Trust Company Inc. (USVI)
4. **Trust layer**: The 2017 Caterpillar Trust, various GRATs and annuity trusts
5. **Distribution**: Disbursements to operating entities, foundations, and personal accounts

The Caterpillar Trust is a particularly instructive example of how this architecture was used. Created as a Grantor Retained Annuity Trust (GRAT) in 2017, it held accounts at Deutsche Bank Trust Company Americas. On February 6, 2018, when asked to sign trust documents, Epstein or an associate responded: "what is this for? Am I supposed to know?" [EFTA02576529] The beneficial owner of a trust did not know what his own trust was for.

On March 18, 2019, approximately $2.16 million was transferred from the Deutsche Bank Caterpillar Trust account to fund a newly opened "2013 Butterfly Trust" brokerage account at Morgan Stanley. Richard Kahn managed the transfer. [EFTA02576529] Two months later, the Caterpillar Trust's Deutsche Bank accounts were closed entirely.

When Deutsche Bank's due diligence team ran the Caterpillar Trust through AML screening on February 5, 2018, the system flagged Caterpillar Inc. — the heavy equipment manufacturer — as a false positive. The actual beneficial owner, a registered sex offender, did not trigger an alert. [EFTA02576529]

### The Escrow Mechanism

On March 3, 2016, Brad Karp — chairman of Paul, Weiss, Rifkind, Wharton & Garrison — emailed partner Bruce Birenboim: "I have been asked by Leon if we can have a $2m escrow fund established at Paul Weiss." [EFTA02470157]

The chairman of one of the world's most prestigious law firms was being asked to hold funds in escrow for a transaction between his client (Leon Black) and a convicted sex offender. Paul, Weiss — a firm whose compliance department would reject a fee arrangement with a sanctioned entity — apparently processed this request through internal channels.

## The Rothschild Channel

The pipeline extended beyond Apollo. Edmond de Rothschild Holding S.A. signed a $25 million consulting agreement with Southern Trust Company on October 5, 2015. The contract specified "risk analysis and the application and development of mathematical quantitative database," language broad enough to describe essentially any form of financial advisory. [EFTA02718592]

This was not an arm's-length corporate arrangement. Ariane de Rothschild, CEO of the Edmond de Rothschild Group, was in regular personal communication with Epstein. Their correspondence reveals that Epstein was receiving real-time intelligence about one of the largest banking scandals of the decade.

On June 24, 2016, Ariane told Epstein she was "preparing meeting for next week at CSSF about 1MDB" — the Luxembourg financial regulator investigating the bank's role in the Malaysian sovereign fund fraud. [EFTA02459040]

Five days later, on June 29, she sent an email with the subject line "Luxembourg": "Shit is hitting the fan. Police throughout the bank — full bk search with people locked and not allowed to get out." [EFTA02459040]

Epstein immediately forwarded this to Kathy Ruemmler — the former White House Counsel to President Obama — with the single word "go." [EFTA02459040] Ruemmler had been retained as Ariane's outside counsel, introduced by Epstein. A sex offender was brokering the crisis management of a major European bank's 1MDB exposure.

Two days before the police raid, on June 27, Ariane had told Epstein: "Brexit will give me the excuse to downsize & clean deeper. Get rid of those people who have kept bad values. We're ready for Lux remediation." [EFTA02459040] She was using the Brexit vote as cover for a compliance housecleaning.

The 1MDB connection was not abstract. Mohamed Badawy Al-Husseiny, CEO of Aabar Investments and a key 1MDB co-conspirator, appeared on a guest list shared with Epstein on November 9, 2014. [EFTA02593759] Approximately $472.5 million in suspicious 1MDB funds had flowed through Edmond de Rothschild accounts. In May 2025, Edmond de Rothschild (Europe) became the first bank ever convicted of money laundering in Luxembourg, paying EUR 25 million to settle. [EFTA02593759]

## What Should Have Happened

Deutsche Bank processed these transactions under a compliance framework that was, on paper, robust. The Bank Secrecy Act requires financial institutions to file SARs for transactions that appear suspicious. The Office of the Comptroller of the Currency's guidelines specifically flag large wire transfers to entities in jurisdictions known for secrecy — which the USVI qualifies as.

The failures were systemic:

**Know Your Customer**: Deutsche Bank maintained accounts for STC despite the fact that Epstein's sex offender status was public knowledge. A basic Google search would have surfaced his 2008 conviction. The bank's KYC process for the Caterpillar Trust caught a false positive on an equipment manufacturer but missed the actual beneficial owner.

**Suspicious Activity Reporting**: Transfers of $15M, $16.5M, and $8.5M from a family office to a single-client trust company in the USVI — all within a six-month period, totaling $40M in 2013 alone — should have triggered SAR filings. They did not.

**Enhanced Due Diligence**: As a Politically Exposed Person's close associate (Epstein maintained relationships with multiple government officials across several countries), accounts held by Epstein-controlled entities should have been subject to enhanced monitoring. They were not.

In 2020, the New York State Department of Financial Services fined Deutsche Bank $150 million for its failures in monitoring Epstein's accounts. The penalty was less than one year's management fees on the accounts in question.

## The Consulting Fiction

The official justification for Black's payments was "consulting" and "advisory services" — primarily tax, estate planning, and philanthropic advice. The Dechert report endorsed this framing.

But the documentary evidence reveals what these "advisory services" actually consisted of:

**Charity routing**: A December 23, 2014 document [EFTA02371754] titled "Leon Black charities commitment list" itemizes charitable donations directed or advised by Epstein. Recipients include institutions across academia, the arts, and public policy. This was not financial advice — it was social access brokered through philanthropic giving.

**Art inventory management**: EFTA01919638 documents a Southeast Asian art inventory for Leon Black maintained by Epstein's operation. The document lists bronze sculptures with stock numbers and valuations. A registered sex offender was managing a billionaire's art collection.

**Political donation coordination**: FEC records show Black made 1,185+ political contributions. The pattern of giving — particularly timing and recipient selection — aligns with Epstein's documented political influence strategy.

**Foundation management**: Both the Debra and Leon Black Family Foundation (EIN 13-3947890) and the Leon D. Black Foundation (EIN 20-5117734) operated from 445 Park Avenue, c/o Elysium Management. Epstein advised on grant timing and recipients. The Family Foundation's grant pattern shows a massive spike in 2014 — from roughly $100K in 2011 to over $1.7M in 2014 — coinciding with the period of heaviest Epstein engagement.

None of these services required a consulting agreement worth $158 million. A Big Four accounting firm would charge perhaps $2-3 million annually for comparable estate and tax advisory work. The gap between the market rate for the services described and the amounts actually paid is where the story lives.

## The Operational Security Failure

On May 24, 2019 — six weeks before Epstein's arrest — a journalist identified only as "Nova" called Southern Trust Company's USVI office. When the receptionist Una answered with the standard greeting, "Southern Trust Company," the journalist began asking questions. [EFTA02347813]

This was the first sign that the infrastructure was compromised. Within weeks, Epstein would be arrested. Within months, his death would freeze the investigation. But the corporate architecture he built — entities like Cypress Hill Advisors LLC (still active as of 2024), Southern Financial LLC, and the dozens of LLCs registered at the 6100 Red Hook address — would outlive him.

## What Remains Unknown

Several questions remain unanswered by the available documentary evidence:

**The $25M Rothschild consulting fee**: Southern Trust Company's consulting agreement with Edmond de Rothschild Holding was signed on October 5, 2015. Whether the full $25 million was ever paid, and through what channels, is not documented in the corpus.

**The "Heritage" payments**: The Rowan-to-Black check tagged "2013HERITAGE" suggests a structured settlement mechanism within Apollo's founding partnership. The full scope of these inter-founder settlements, and Epstein's role in them, has not been fully mapped.

**The Athene restructuring fee**: Epstein proposed a 25% advisory fee on the Athene transaction to both Rowan and Harris. Whether any payment was made under this proposal — and what 25% of the Athene transaction would have been worth — remains undocumented.

**The post-arrest liquidation**: Between December 2019 and early 2020, seven HBRK Associates wires totaling $9,238,007.10 liquidated subsidiary entity accounts into STC at FirstBank (the successor to Deutsche Bank for these accounts after the bank exited the relationship). The destination of these consolidated funds is not clear from available records.

The machinery that moved $158 million from a billionaire to a sex offender was not exotic. It was American trust law, standard wire transfers, ordinary corporate entities, and a compliance system that functioned exactly as designed — which is to say, it deferred to wealth.
