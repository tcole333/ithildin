## 1. Confidence-to-probability mapping — retain as a narrow P2 documentation/design finding

**Do not report the confidence caps themselves as a defect.** They implement the explicit root policy at `AGENTS.md:220-228`, and the methodology describes them as maximums, not measured probabilities (`research/INVESTIGATIVE_METHODOLOGY.md:579-587`). The claim ladder also deliberately assigns low ceilings to intent/motive (`research/INVESTIGATIVE_METHODOLOGY.md:626-635`). These are conservative safeguards; changing them would weaken an intentional audit boundary.

The narrower issue is that `research/INVESTIGATIVE_METHODOLOGY.md:715-723` labels the same field “Kent-style probability / approved prose,” mapping `medium` to “likely; probably.” Yet `tools/findings_tracker.py:937-940` defaults to inference/medium, `tools/findings_tracker.py:550-558` caps by claim type, and `research/INVESTIGATIVE_METHODOLOGY.md:517` instructs orchestrator synthesis findings to be medium. None of these operations assesses how likely a proposition is. The distinction is operational because `.codex/skills/review-article/SKILL.md:261-263` makes this mapping a blocking editorial check. A tentative synthesis that survives provenance checks can therefore be treated as eligible for “probably” because its administrative tier is medium, despite no explicit assessment that the proposed explanation is more likely than its alternatives. Conversely, a demonstrable cross-record count remains medium due to inference syntax (`research/INVESTIGATIVE_METHODOLOGY.md:629`), so the tier is not a calibrated likelihood measure.

**Mitigations checked:** The weakest-link rule is a ceiling, so it does not force an upgrade from “possibly”; competing hypotheses, diagnosticity, assumptions, premortem, and authenticity-versus-truth rules (`research/INVESTIGATIVE_METHODOLOGY.md:699-711,725-744`) all reduce the risk. Therefore avoid claiming the system necessarily overstates every medium finding or that this is a P1 code error. They do not establish a probability assessment for every ordinary synthesis or make evidence confidence and event likelihood interchangeable.

**Smallest useful remedy:** Keep existing claim types, confidence caps, and provenance intact. Replace the automatic probability equivalence with evidence-strength/attribution wording and state that a confidence tier never independently authorizes a probability phrase. Require “likely/probably/very likely” to have an explicit claim-specific assessment of rival explanations in the article/review context. Update the article review rule to match. A separate database probability field is optional future design work, not a prerequisite.

**Forward verification:** Review three fixtures: (a) default-medium tentative pattern with equally plausible rivals cannot become “probably”; (b) an exact arithmetic summary of several independently documented records is described precisely without implying that its medium cap means a probability estimate; (c) a confirmed quotation of an allegation establishes that the allegation was made, not that its contents are true. Preserve existing cap tests.

## 2. Pivot false-positive closure — dismiss the strong missing-stop/bias claim; optional P3 clarification

`research/INVESTIGATIVE_METHODOLOGY.md:307-316` does narrowly say to close *as false positive* only when the pivot is positively explained and the entity has no other suspicious characteristics. However, it does **not** require every investigation to conclude “false positive” before stopping. The same methodology explicitly adopts the pursue-lead stop policy (`research/INVESTIGATIVE_METHODOLOGY.md:591`). `.codex/skills/pursue-lead/SKILL.md:293-300` permits completion for consistent negatives or diminishing returns and explicitly forbids speculative rabbit holes; `:302-315` supports completed, dead-end, and blocked outcomes. `research/INVESTIGATIVE_METHODOLOGY.md:676-693` additionally allows routine/peripheral targets to remain at their tier or be deprioritized. Counter-evidence and innocent alternatives are mandated at `:476-502` and `:699-707`.

Consequently, do not present “the platform has no neutral stopping condition” or “absence always forces investigation escalation” as established findings. Existing linked instructions resolve that stronger failure path.

A small wording inconsistency remains: the truth of a particular linkage should not depend on whether the entity has *other* suspicious characteristics. If a shared-number match is disproven, unrelated concerns do not make that match true. Optional P3 correction: close the disproven pivot with the evidence and a specific reason; create or retain separately evidenced leads for independent concerns. Add a direct cross-reference from the pivot protocol to the existing completion/stop policy. Verify using two examples: a disproven pivot with an unrelated concern, and an unresolved pivot with exhausted relevant sources. The former closes the linkage; the latter completes with uncertainty rather than claiming disproval.
