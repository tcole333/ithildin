## Silence Periods

### Silence 1 — Macintyre's Identity Concealed: 94 Days
**Nov 21, 2025 → Feb 23, 2026 | Finding #7725**

The most significant silence: Macintyre received 10% of the deal value but was not named in the 6-K press release, not named in the half-year financial statements (Dec 31, 2025), and not named in the half-year 6-K filed March 13, 2026. He appeared only in the F-3 (Feb 23, 2026) — the registration statement filed to enable his own share sales. Even after his identification in the F-3, the financial statements continued to attribute all shares to "the Seller."

**Significance:** This is not inadvertent omission. A person receiving ~$1.47-2M in shares from a public company deal is a related party that must be disclosed. The 94-day concealment, and the continued omission in subsequent filings, suggests deliberate structuring to obscure his role until the shares were ready to sell.

### Silence 2 — No Form 144 Filings: 19+ Days Post F-3 Effectiveness
**Mar 4 → Mar 24, 2026**

As of March 23, 2026, no Form 144 has been filed by Swiss Commodity Re, Macintyre, or Deayton. However, because the shares were registered under the F-3 (not restricted under Rule 144), no Form 144 is legally required. The absence of Form 144 filings does not mean no sales occurred — the registered resale mechanism was specifically designed to avoid this disclosure requirement.
