## Learnings

- **[Timing]** FINRA registration dates are high-precision signals for career transitions. Blumenthal's June 2025 join date radically changes the structural analysis of who was at Jett Capital during which CRML deal phase.
- **[Methodology]** The "bridge firm" pattern (Amvest Capital, 2 months) is worth noting — it's consistent with someone re-establishing US brokerage credentials before moving to their target firm. The 121 Mining Investment conference may be a common recruiting ground for Perth-network advisors.
- **[Source quality]** FINRA BrokerCheck API returns only JS headers via WebFetch on the public page, but the raw API endpoint at `api.brokercheck.finra.org/search/individual/<CRD>` returns structured data. Record this for future FINRA pulls.
- **[Process gap]** ABR query tool (`tools/query_abr.py`) missing. Australian registry lookups for persons require paid ASIC ConnectOnline or InfoTrack — this should be documented in the infra gap list.
- **[Negative result significance]** Zero EDGAR hits for Blumenthal means he's not named in any US SEC filing — contrasting with Macintyre (F-3 footnote) and Kenneth Deayton (multiple beneficial ownership disclosures). His role in the network is indirect: through Jett Capital as institution, not through personal shareholding.
