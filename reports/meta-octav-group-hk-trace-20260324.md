## Executive Summary

META OCTAV GROUP (HK) LIMITED is the legal successor to **International Fiduciaries Limited** (IFL), a Hong Kong TCSP-licensed fiduciary firm founded in 2011. The HK Companies Registry formalized the rename on **January 22, 2026** (per weekly report RNC063_2026.01.19-2026.01.25). The transformation was operational by approximately August 2025 — management had already changed hands, the firm had relocated to (or newly registered at) Wyndham Place, and a sister entity (Meta Octav Group SA, Geneva) had been incorporated on July 2, 2025.

The rename carries investigative significance because IFL appeared as the **NNC1 nominee subscriber** for **Swiss Commodity Re Limited** — the Deayton-controlled HK shell that received 1,800,000 CRML shares under the disputed copper powder transaction (November 21, 2025 Asset Sale Agreement). At the time of Swiss Commodity Re's incorporation (October 30, 2025), IFL was still using its old legal name. Deayton is now confirmed as a **director of META OCTAV GROUP (HK) LIMITED** — placing him on both sides of the Swiss Commodity Re formation.

## 3. Relationship to Swiss Commodity Re Limited

Swiss Commodity Re Limited (HK Co. 79047415) and META OCTAV GROUP (HK) LIMITED are **legally distinct entities**. Their connections are structural, not organizational:

### A. NNC1 Nominee Subscriber (Oct 30, 2025)
When Swiss Commodity Re was incorporated on October 30, 2025 — 22 days before the copper powder deal was announced — "International Fiduciaries Limited" (the pre-rename name of META OCTAV GROUP (HK)) was listed as the **sole founder-member/shareholder on the NNC1**. This is standard TCSP nominee practice: a licensed firm subscribes the founding shares, then immediately transfers them to the actual client (Deayton/Swiss Commodity Re's beneficial owner). IFL's presence on the NNC1 does not establish ongoing ownership — only the transactional act of incorporation.

### B. Shared Controller — the structural conflict
Kenneth Raymond Deayton is **simultaneously**:
1. The person with "voting and investment control" over Swiss Commodity Re Limited (per CRML F-3)
2. A **director of META OCTAV GROUP (HK) LIMITED** — the successor to the TCSP that incorporated Swiss Commodity Re

This creates a structural conflict-of-interest problem: the TCSP that provided nominee services for Swiss Commodity Re's formation is now directly led by the very person who controls Swiss Commodity Re's share position. Under standard fiduciary independence norms, the nominee/TCSP is supposed to be independent from the beneficial owner.

### C. Shared Address (Wyndham Place)
Both entities operate at 6/F Wyndham Place, 40-44 Wyndham Street, Central Hong Kong — which is HKCS Group's registered office. Swiss Commodity Re uses this as its business address per the CRML F-3. META OCTAV GROUP (HK) uses it as its TCSP registered office. The address is a shared virtual office hub with 12+ TCSP entities.

### D. SEC Filing Status (as of March 24, 2026)
The CRML F-3 (Feb 23, 2026) and 424B3 prospectus (Mar 4, 2026) **still name "Swiss Commodity Re Limited"** as the selling securityholder. No amended filing has been filed updating the shareholder name to META OCTAV GROUP (HK) — consistent with them being distinct entities. The 1,800,000 CRML shares remain registered for resale under Swiss Commodity Re's name.

## 7. Assessment: What This Means for the CRML Investigation

### The Key Structural Finding
Deayton simultaneously controls (a) the TCSP that nominally incorporated Swiss Commodity Re, (b) the shell that holds 1.8M CRML shares, and (c) HKCS Group, which provides the registered office for all of these entities. The January 22, 2026 rename adds a fourth link: Deayton is now formally a director of the post-IFL entity, cementing his governance role across the full cluster.

### What META OCTAV's Rename Does NOT Change
- Swiss Commodity Re Limited remains the selling securityholder in CRML's SEC filings
- The 1.8M CRML shares remain registered under Swiss Commodity Re's name
- Deayton's voting/investment control over Swiss Commodity Re is unchanged
- The underlying copper powder transaction valuation anomaly is unchanged

### What the Rename DOES Change (Investigative Relevance)
- The TCSP (formerly IFL) that provided nominee subscriber services for Swiss Commodity Re is now formally under Deayton's directorial control — eliminating any claim of TCSP independence in that transaction
- The META OCTAV brand is now a vehicle with independent European (Geneva) presence and professional wealth management credentials (Vuillier/Viy), potentially repositioning the entity for a broader European client base
- If META OCTAV GROUP (HK) is used to nominate future CRML-related entities, the renamed entity will now directly appear in HK registry records under a brand associated with Deayton's directorship

### Open Questions
1. **When exactly was Deayton appointed director?** Before or after the January 22, 2026 rename? If before, he was director of IFL when it was the NNC1 subscriber — directly linking him to Swiss Commodity Re's incorporation act.
2. **Who is the beneficial owner of Swiss Commodity Re?** Deayton is described as having "voting and investment control" — standard SEC language for a controller who may not be the ultimate beneficial owner. The true beneficial owner has not been disclosed.
3. **Will META OCTAV GROUP (HK) serve as NNC1 subscriber for future CRML shell companies?** Deayton's directorship makes this now a Deayton-controlled TCSP, which would be more transparent about his role in future formations.
