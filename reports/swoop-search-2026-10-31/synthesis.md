## Bottom line

- **Who they are.** **Swoop Search LLC, now Swoop Technologies LLC**, is a
  Delaware LLC whose housing-search website launched in Madison, Wisconsin on
  2012-10-10 (the company dates its founding to December 2011). Its early
  website named founders Quinn, Michael and Paul Bottum; contemporary reporting
  described Quinn's co-founders as his father and uncle. Minnesota's
  foreign-LLC record identifies Swoop Search LLC as the prior Minnesota and
  home-jurisdiction name of Swoop Technologies LLC; Minnesota recorded the name
  amendment on 2025-02-12 (the Delaware effective date is unverified). Paul
  William Bottum signed the 2014 Form D as CEO, is Minnesota's listed manager
  and is the SAM point of contact; a 2001 SEC-filed biography describes a
  medical-device and investment career. The product is SwoopOS, marketed as
  "the operating system for defense and industry" (#16573, #16577, #16548,
  #16596, #16539, #16541, #16542, #16585).
- **How they got into Golden Dome C2.** Only partly visible. Guetlein described
  the coalition as beginning with six firms that organized themselves and
  approached him; January 2026 reporting said his team had worked with the six
  since July 2025, and March reporting put the start about six months earlier
  and said the firms each held independent Pentagon contracts, without saying
  when those contracts were awarded. The Pentagon
  then added Lockheed Martin, RTX and Northrop Grumman, and Guetlein told the
  Senate on 2026-04-27 the group had 11 members (#16523, #16525, #16522,
  #16563, #16554, #16558). **Swoop may have been one of the six founders; no
  dated record establishes when it joined** (inference, #16527). Before the
  coalition formed, Swoop held direct prototype OTs with CDAO (Joint Staff J6,
  2024-09) and the Air Force C3BM battle-management office (Project Two-Step,
  sole source, 2025-03). Separately, awards to Systems Intelligence LLC bought
  a "Swoop Operating System" demonstration and test report for MDA (later
  described as AN/TPY-2 hardware-in-the-loop work) and a Swoop OS
  demonstration on Space Force's PARCS radar; the product name suggests a Swoop
  connection, but those records do not disclose Swoop's role, revenue or
  whether the demonstrations were completed (#16531, #16530, #16560, #16561).
  **The reviewed public records do not identify the agreement funding Swoop's
  coalition role, who selected it, or its deliverables** (#16595, #16597).
- **Scale and relevant prior work.** Before July 2025 the retained records show
  $12.865M in direct prime obligations to Swoop, plus $2.580M in separately
  reported subawards; awards to Systems Intelligence mentioning Swoop OS are
  excluded because Swoop's share is unknown. Swoop and Aalyria are both far
  below Anduril, Palantir and Scale AI in recorded prime obligations. Two-Step
  carries the exceptional-circumstances (DEC) code; none of the 107
  pre-July-2025 OT agreements in the four-peer sample does, although that
  sample mixes prototype and production instruments. Swoop's direct C2-related
  OTs and the separately purchased radar demonstrations give a plausible
  capability-based explanation for its later reported membership; they do not
  establish unique capability, completed demonstrations, founding status or the
  selection reason (facts: #16630, #16633; inference, medium: #16632, #16634).
- **One administration tie.** Navy Secretary John Phelan's January 2025
  nominee disclosure listed a $500,001–$1,000,000 interest in "Swoop Search, LLC
  (cybersecurity company)" under Black Marlin Investments LLC. His February 14
  ethics agreement did not name it among the required divestitures but imposed
  general recusal and conflict-resolution obligations (#16599; verified against
  the OGE PDF). The disclosed company appears to be this Swoop, based on its
  historical legal name and description; the disclosure gives no UEI.
  Continued ownership during his tenure (confirmed 2025-03-24 and sworn in the
  next day per a Navy release seen through indexed text; departed 2026-04-22)
  has not been established. The sources reviewed did not show him
  participating in a Swoop award or coalition decision. Guetlein's reported
  reporting line ran to Deputy Secretary Feinberg, not through the Navy
  Secretary; that does not exclude Navy participation in the wider program.
- **No visible influence operation.** The reviewed federal lobbying
  disclosures, FEC committee records, selected earmark tables and
  congressional searches did not identify one. Identified employee donations
  were small and Democratic; CEO Paul Bottum's only matched itemized federal
  contribution was a 2003 Bush-Cheney gift. Rep. Betty McCollum, whose district
  SAM assigns to the company, has publicly opposed Golden Dome funding. These
  bounded results do not rule out ordinary business-development contacts or
  other access (#16601–#16606, #16608).

"Consistent with" is not evidence that one cause beats another; no row
establishes a selection mechanism.
