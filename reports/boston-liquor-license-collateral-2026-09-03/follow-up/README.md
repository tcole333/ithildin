# Boston license transfers and unannotated UCC records

Checked September 3, 2026, America/New_York. The roster’s comments omit documented license pledges. An independent, fixed sample of **12 holders without pledge markers** returned **eight legal-name-matched holders with Massachusetts UCC records**, including **two with a specifically identified liquor license as collateral**. Another sampled holder has a documented corporate-successor connection to a terminated filing. Three searches returned no matching current-database records.

Separately, **nine selected license transfers** received local board approval. Eight now show the transferee in the supplied roster; four also include a license pledge, and three of those four have no roster pledge marker. These are selected records, not a citywide estimate of loans, liens or transfers. Loan balances, enforceability, completed sales and sale prices remain unverified.

## Method and limits

The sample was fixed before its UCC searches. The eligible frame contained **750 distinct normalized legal holders**: 522 in `CV7 All Alc.` and 228 in `Retail All Alc.`. Each had an Active roster label, expiration on or after September 3, 2026, a legal name, and no pledge/collateral/lien/loan marker in comments. Prior reviewed license numbers were excluded. Case and punctuation were normalized for deduplication, while corporate endings were retained. Where multiple eligible licenses shared a holder, the first ascending license number represented that holder; the selection file preserves the other numbers.

Six holders per license-type stratum were selected by the lowest SHA-256 ranks of a fixed seed, stratum and normalized holder name. `sample-plan.json` records the seed and exact selections; `sampling-frame.csv` preserves the ranked frame. This is a small exploratory sample with equal allocation to two strata, **not a population-weighted estimate**. Other license classes and other states’ UCC systems were not sampled.

All twelve current-database debtor searches were read to completion, using the planned names, begins-with matching, all cities/states and no date cutoff. Selected original histories and needed attachments were inspected; the result JSON records exactly which histories were reviewed. Generic all-assets language, conditional references to liquor licenses, specifically identified licenses, and unread attachments were classified separately. No lapsed-archive search was performed. The source retains records until at least a year after lapse, so “current database” does not mean an active lien; termination and continuation events remain separate from any conclusion about outstanding debt.

## Sale prices and further records

No selected decision supplied a sale price or proof of closing. The [current ABCC transfer form](https://www.mass.gov/doc/application-for-a-transfer-of-license-retail-112024/download) requires a purchase-and-sale agreement and financing documentation. PDF page 7 records real-estate costs, business-asset costs, other costs, lenders, amounts and financing type; it has **no separate license-only price field**. The agreement and any allocation schedule are needed to separate the license consideration from a bundled business purchase.

The saved sales-access report identifies the [ABCC records-request route](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/submit-a-public-records-request-to-the-alcoholic-beverages-control-commission) and a concrete, unsent scope for the Jana transaction file. No public-records request, paid order or third-party message was submitted. No findings or leads were added to the unrelated active investigation profile.
