# Harvard Street legal-entity resolution

The **corporation-to-LLC chain is explicitly resolved** by the Massachusetts Corporations Division. It is not an inference from shared addresses.

An entity-name search beginning `144 HARVARD` returned two records:

| Exact official name | Entity ID | Type | Relevant history |
|---|---|---|---|
| 144 HARVARD STREET LIQUORS, INC. | 043067400; old ID 000314736 | Domestic Profit Corporation | Organized October 3, 1989; merged into the LLC July 25, 2006. |
| 144 HARVARD STREET LIQUORS, LLC | 000928025 | Domestic Limited Liability Company | Organized July 3, 2006; reciprocal merger link to the corporation July 25, 2006. |

The [corporation's official summary](https://corp.sec.state.ma.us/CorpWeb/CorpSearch/CorpSummary.aspx?sysvalue=Hm2zztuxHQ5xaindlBvE2umNOWnRK.93d_fXguW519w-) says: **“Merged into 144 HARVARD STREET LIQUORS, LLC on 07-25-2006.”** Following that direct link opened the [LLC summary](https://corp.sec.state.ma.us/CorpWeb/CorpSearch/CorpSummary.aspx?sysvalue=DDVHeJCm4cBj3WZujSCeNQ--), which reciprocally identifies the merger with the Inc. Both give 144 Harvard Street, Dorchester MA 02124 as the business location. The LLC displays a historical involuntary-dissolution date of June 30, 2015 and a subsequent revival date of June 2, 2016; those should not be collapsed into a current dissolved-status claim.

The parent sample's Boston roster spells the name `144 HARVARD STREET LIQUOR, INC.` (singular LIQUOR), DBA GIGI'S, license LB-101870. The official registry spells both legal names `LIQUORS` and establishes a **merger**, not merely a change of suffix. The roster therefore appears to retain the predecessor name with an additional spelling discrepancy. Treat “stale roster legal name” as an inference, rather than a directly recorded explanation by Boston.

For the sample, the LLC UCC can be retained as **documented corporate-successor evidence**, with this qualification. This subtask did not independently inspect the UCC history supplied by the parent (original 202415588360; termination 202517092990) and did not find a separate Boston/ABCC approval explicitly linking LB-101870 to the LLC. It should not be reported as an outstanding lien: the parent has identified a termination, and legal-entity resolution does not change that filing event.
