Three are conventional restaurant concepts; three are concert or recreation venues with food and beverage. Keep the sectors separate before comparing license ownership concentration.

## Evidence and interpretation

**200 Boylston / A Street.** The [Massachusetts corporate summary](https://corp.sec.state.ma.us/CorpWeb/CorpSearch/CorpSummary.aspx?sysvalue=XQZFQPRXKJi1P56yMOTpQejjoSC9qMymMgiAolSbRZI-) identifies managers Josephine Adams and Iraklis N. Papachristos. The [group leadership page](https://www.astreethospitality.com/leadership) identifies Eric Papachristos, Jody Adams and Jon Mendez, and the [company press index](https://www.astreethospitality.com/press) includes its new Four Seasons project. This supports group affiliation, not a complete ownership schedule. Do not substitute the Four Seasons hotel's ownership for the restaurant's.

The [group portfolio](https://www.astreethospitality.com/) lists La Padrona, Trade, Porto, The Venetian and Saloniki. [Saloniki's locations page](https://www.salonikigreek.com/locations) is more current than older biographies: six locations are open, while its new Fenway home at 1330 Boylston is pending. Its old alcohol-license roster entry remains at 132 Brookline Avenue / 4 Kilmarnock. Four other Boston Saloniki rows are Common Victualler-only licenses. La Padrona has no exact DBA match in the supplied roster; a standalone license cannot be assumed. Trade's site gives 540 Atlantic Avenue, while its roster entry uses 530.

**Legends.** The [company's February 2021 closing announcement](https://legendsglobal.com/legends-announces-closing-of-majority-investment-from-sixth-street/) documents Sixth Street's majority investment; the [sponsor's May 2026 page](https://sixthstreet.com/video_gallery/institutional-investing-in-pro-sports/) still includes Legends Global among its investments. The [Massachusetts record for Legends Music](https://corp.sec.state.ma.us/CorpWeb/CorpSearch/CorpSummary.aspx?sysvalue=NWE_45PMwpSf.45RerN_onWfkUS2AVsldfjEJGN.aXc-) identifies Daniel Smith as manager and a New York office matching the Legends group's address. Boston's records name the same LLC at View Boston and Leader Bank Pavilion. [View Boston's privacy notice](https://viewboston.com/privacy-notice/) links that operation to Legends Hospitality Parent Holdings and affiliates. The precise membership chain remains a gap, so the strict count of fully traced PE-controlled licensees should leave these as pending rather than pretending that the shareholding chain has been retrieved.

This is principally a venue/concessions affiliation. The [Pavilion venue site](https://www.leaderbankpavilion.com/) uses Live Nation contacts. Neither the naming-rights bank nor the venue operator should automatically be treated as the owner of the food-and-beverage licensee. **Exclude LB-99331, DBA “Legends,” at 100 Legends Way** from this match: its legal holder is Massachusetts Sportservice Inc., a different entity.

**PickleBOS.** The [operator's own page](https://www.picklebos.com/) identifies two open sites: 379r Dorchester Avenue and 440 Rutherford Avenue. [Alex Karsos's own profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/akarsos) identifies him as founder, consistent with Alexander Karsos as manager in the licensing record. No retrieved source establishes a PE investor or a full ownership schedule. A developer's ownership of the property would not establish ownership of PickleBOS.

**Ballers.** A [company-issued June 2025 announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/wellness-pioneers-bet-big-on-the-racquet-sports-boom-with-ballers-a-hospitality-driven-social-sports-concept-with-ambitious-growth-trajectory-302475994.html) disclosed a $20 million Series A led by Sharp Alpha and RHC Group. It describes RHC as a family office and identifies Good City Studio and Vero Capital as creators. [Sharp Alpha's current portfolio](https://sharpalpha.vc/) lists Ballers, and [Vero's history](https://www.verocapital.com/story) confirms co-founding. Vero operates across real estate and private equity, but the specific Ballers vehicle and control percentage are not established. Code this as verified institutional private capital, with VC, family-office and Vero participation retained separately.

The [current Ballers site](https://www.ballers-us.com/about) has two open locations and marks Los Angeles as pending. Do not use its 50-plus growth ambition as an existing location count. [The Boston venue page](https://www.ballers-us.com/location/bostonseaport) confirms operations at 25 Pier 4 Boulevard, but LB-99537 remains absent from the supplied roster. Operating status does not itself prove final issuance of that transferred license.

**All’Antico Vinaio.** The [US operator biography](https://www.allanticovinaiousa.com/about-us/) identifies the Mazzanti family and Bastianich collaboration; the [Italian brand's New York account](https://www.allanticovinaio.com/new-york/) explicitly identifies Tanya Bastianich Manuali and Joe Bastianich as partners at the 2021 opening. The [US location list](https://www.allanticovinaiousa.com/store-locator/) has 18 entries, including [Washington Street](https://www.allanticovinaiousa.com/location/washington-st-boston/) and [Boylston Street](https://www.allanticovinaiousa.com/boston/). These justify a multi-state operator category. Historical European ventures involving Percassi must not be projected onto the Boston LLCs. Exact current beneficial ownership is still unknown.

**Shy Bird.** The [operator's location list](https://www.shybird.com/) identifies three outlets; [Fenway](https://www.shybird.com/location/fenway/) and [South Boston](https://www.shybird.com/location/shy-bird-south-boston/) match the two Boston license records. [Eli Feldman's first-person account](https://shybird.substack.com/p/the-hngry-summit) identifies Andrew Holden as his business partner. [Cambridge's 2020 minutes](https://www.cambridgema.gov/-/media/Files/licensecommission/meetingminutes/minutes2020/011320lcmeetingminutes.pdf) identify Holden as a principal of the Kendall entity and describe another stockholder's family gift. These records are not a current Fenway cap table. Shared restaurateur history with Branch Line or Eastern Standard is insufficient to collapse them into one ownership group.

## Reusable matching output

The candidate CSV preserves 12 alcohol-license rows associated with these five roster-present operators, plus an explicit missing row for Ballers. It is a reviewed candidate map, not a certified ownership register. Exact legal membership, stock interests and any indirect entity chain remain the stronger next evidence for assigning ultimate control.

Use independent dimensions: operator size, geographic footprint, venue sector, public/private ownership, disclosed institutional capital type, ownership/control percentage, and evidence date. “No sponsor found” belongs in the unknown category. A UCC creditor is not an equity investor, and a license pledge is not evidence of PE backing.
