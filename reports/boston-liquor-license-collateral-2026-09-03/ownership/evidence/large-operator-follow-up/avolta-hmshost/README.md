A Manchester-Boston Regional Airport Sam Adams result was rejected because it is in New Hampshire.

HMSHost currently [identifies itself as an Avolta subsidiary](https://www.hmshost.com/about/who-we-are) and reports more than 1,000 operating locations in nearly 80 North American airports. This scale measures locations, not liquor licenses. The saved current roster names the legal HSI entities; venue and terminal comparisons connect them conservatively to the operator's current disclosures.

[Autogrill's June 30, 2020 financial report](https://www.autogrill.com/sites/default/files/relazione_finanziaria_semestrale_-_h1_2020_-_inglese.pdf) gives an additional historical legal-entity link: printed page 80 lists HSI MCA BOS FB, LLC at 80% held by Host Services, Inc. Printed page 77 gives the wholly owned Host Services → Host International → HMSHost chain. Both original pages were rendered and visually checked. Those percentages belong to **2020**; current HSI MCA and HSI PHE equity percentages remain unresolved.

[Avolta's investor disclosure](https://www.avoltaworld.com/en/company/our-stakeholders/investors) supports a **public-company** parent classification and reports **Advent International Corp. among holders above 3%, as of December 31, 2025**. The page gives no exact Advent percentage; it reports Edizione as the largest holder at 22.17%. [Advent describes itself as a private-equity investor](https://www.adventinternational.com/). Accordingly, public ownership, a disclosed PE minority shareholder, and PE control are separate fields. PE control is not established, and the historical shareholding is not presented as a verified September 2026 stake. These licenses should not automatically add to a strict PE-controlled license total.

All six alcohol rows have the exact roster type `CV7 All Alc. Airp.` and Logan premises. They belong in a separate airport/concession cohort for the license-price hypothesis. Neither license purchase prices nor individual legal transferability are verified here. The consumer brand, concession operator, local licensee, public parent and any local joint-venture investors are different analytical levels.

Public source HTML/PDF files and hashes are retained in `download-manifest.json`; the city minutes reuse the already downloaded transfer corpus.
