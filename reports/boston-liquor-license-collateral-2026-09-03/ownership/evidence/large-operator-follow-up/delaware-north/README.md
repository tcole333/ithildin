# Delaware North / Areas Boston license review

Review date: September 4, 2026. Proposed evidence only; main owner mappings were not changed.

The 11 exact roster IDs yield **three Delaware North operator matches and eight Areas successor candidates**. All were absent from the current mapping’s matches when selected. The airport acquisition is complete, but the current registered identity and equity chain of each license holder are not established by the sources reviewed.

All three occupy the arena/North Station complex at 100 Legends Way. TD Garden’s [December 2021 announcement](https://www.tdgarden.com/news/detail/td-garden-hires-exec-chef) explicitly identifies Massachusetts Sportservice as Delaware North’s arena provider. The [current food-and-drink page](https://www.tdgarden.com/plan-your-visit/food-drink) corroborates Delaware North Sportservice’s role. These are named-holder operator affiliations, not proven ownership percentages. Viva Victory Den’s current trading name remains unverified.

The [official Legends page](https://www.tdgarden.com/premium-seating/premium-dining/legends) places the restaurant on level three, matching the roster. Do not join it to Legends Global or Legends Music LLC. Three licenses at this complex are not three independent restaurant locations.

Delaware North [describes itself as family owned and operated](https://www.delawarenorth.com/who-we-are/company-history/); full current capitalization and outside equity remain unresolved. Its [restaurant operations page](https://www.delawarenorth.com/what-we-do/full-services-restaurants/) supports the 20+ scale band by reporting more than 100 managed restaurants; this undated threshold is not a current census.

Delaware North’s [August 2018 Logan contract announcement](https://media.delawarenorth.com/delaware-north-to-begin-food-beverage-and-retail-operations-at-bostons-logan-international-airport/) identifies all these concepts by terminal, including Wpizza as Wolfgang Puck’s concept. This is historical operator evidence. Later search-indexed official Massport directories corroborate several locations; those observations are individually labeled in the JSON because some direct pages returned an access challenge or only an iframe. Gate numbers are kept separate from roster space codes. Monica’s lacks independently current terminal-specific corroboration, and the combined Pei Wei/Wolfgang Puck roster row omits its terminal.

Areas [announced completion of the THS acquisition on December 2, 2025](https://us.areas.com/news/areas-officially-takes-over-delaware-norths-travel-hospitality-services-becoming-the-largest-privately-held-travel-hospitality-company-in-the-u-s), adding 237 outlets across 22 airports. Its [May 26, 2026 policy](https://us.areas.com/kiosk-privacy-policy) expressly includes **AREAS BOSTON FLIGHT LLC** among the defined Areas entities. Together these support a successor-operator inference. Neither is a registered name-change filing or an exact subsidiary/license transfer schedule; all eight remain candidates for strict current ownership counts.

Areas [identified PAI Partners as majority shareholder in February 2023](https://us.areas.com/news/areas-reinforces-its-global-growth-with-a-strong-focus-on-the-u-s/). PAI still publishes an [Areas investment page](https://www.paipartners.com/investment/areas/), but its retrieved text gives no current investment status or percentage. The proposal therefore records dated PE backing and leaves present control unverified. The completed acquisition establishes very large operator scale; its reported 2,200+ global restaurants and stores is a mixed retail/restaurant count.

Delaware North’s [2019 Bruins Bar opening](https://media.delawarenorth.com/bruins-bar-lands-at-bostons-logan-international-airport/) names Chase Concessions, Green Clay Concessions and City Fresh Foods as JV partners. Exact stakes, present membership and which entities each holds remain unknown. Areas’ completion statement also acknowledges JV and brand partners. Concession operation, local JV equity and ownership of restaurant brands must remain separate; these are not eight proven wholly PE-owned licenses.
