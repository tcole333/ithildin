# S03 — Tavern in the Square South Station ownership

Observed **September 3, 2026**. Subject: **Tavern in the Square South Station, LLC**, Boston license **LB-98812**, roster address **1 South Station**.

**Classification: regional restaurant group with documented equity-sponsor backing.** The operator is **Broadway Hospitality Group (BHG)**. The disclosed parent platform is **Authentic Restaurant Brands (ARB)**, backed by **Garnett Station Partners**. Garnett's current Tavern portfolio entry specifically identifies **GSP 4.0**, investment year **2025**, and active equity status. This is direct sponsorship evidence; the UCC lender names are not the basis for this classification. [Sponsor portfolio](https://garnettstation.com/partner/tavern-in-the-square/)

ARB's March 3, 2025 company announcement states that Tavern joined through a recapitalization of BHG. Transaction counsel also reports the acquisition. The announcement names Stephen DeSousa as BHG CEO; BHG's current team page still lists him in that role. These executive titles do not establish individual shareholdings. [ARB announcement republished by Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/authentic-restaurant-brands-adds-tavern-square-its-latest-fortress-regional-brand), [transaction counsel](https://www.riw.com/2025/03/riw-client-tavern-in-the-square-acquired-by-authentic-restaurant-brands/), [BHG team](https://www.broadwayhospitalitygroup.com/team)

**The ownership trace stops at the disclosed sponsor/fund affiliation.** Reviewed sources do not establish equity percentages, the complete chain of intermediate legal entities, management rollover stakes, fund investors, or ultimate individual beneficial owners. Do not describe Garnett as the sole owner or DeSousa as the equity owner.
