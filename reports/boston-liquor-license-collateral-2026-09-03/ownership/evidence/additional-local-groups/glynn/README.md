# Glynn Hospitality ownership and Boston license mapping

Research date: September 3, 2026.

The current [Glynn portfolio](https://www.glynnhospitalitygroup.com/our-locations/) lists seven concepts. Six distinct Boston alcohol-license IDs match those venues. D16 and Dillon’s share the 955 Boylston Street address; D16 is retained as a candidate under the Dillon’s license rather than counted as another license. This supports a local group with at least six premises, in the 6–19 scale band.

Glynn [describes itself as family-run](https://www.glynnhospitalitygroup.com/about-us/). A [vendor case study featuring Glynn’s operations manager](https://www.slang.ai/customers/glynn-hospitality) describes family ownership. Neither establishes an exclusive family equity structure or disproves PE participation. Institutional-equity status is **unknown** after bounded searches; it must not be treated as confirmed independent or non-PE.

Clerys uses 113 Dartmouth Street on its own website, which [places it on the Dartmouth/Columbus corner](https://www.clerysboston.com/). The [April 9, 2026 City Licensing Board agenda](https://content.boston.gov/public-notices/16574241) identifies Clerys, A.T.G., Inc., and LB-98998 at 331–335 Columbus Avenue, consistent with the roster’s identity. That is a brand and government license match with an alternate address, not an exact-address string match.

All six matched roster licenses have type `CV7 All Alc.`. This label alone does not establish transferability, license purchase price, collateral, or financing terms. The operator affiliation does not prove that Glynn directly owns the equity in every license-holding entity. No transfer, loan, or price conclusion is made in this ownership mapping.

Brownstone, Boston Sports Grille, Sterling’s and Hurricane O’Reilly’s were not added from historical lists or third-party directories because they are absent from the current official portfolio reviewed.
