# Official Massachusetts UCC data access options

Researched September 4, 2026 using official informational sources only. No UCC portal searches, purchases, subscriptions or contacts were made for this review.

**A published bulk-records route exists.** Massachusetts General Laws chapter 106, §9-523(f) requires the Secretary to offer bulk records for sale or nonexclusive license at least weekly, in available media. This supports obtaining an existing data product for local analysis instead of resuming 1,444 serial website searches. It does not establish the contents or price of a historical snapshot. [Official statute](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXV/Chapter106/Article9/Section9-523).

| Route | Published offering | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| UCC bulk index | 950 CMR 140.11 describes a “Full Extract,” supplied monthly in weekly increments, at $4,800/year or $100/week. | Obtain the existing schema, sample, minimum order and total quote. Confirm whether a week contains changes only or a complete baseline. |
| Bulk images | The rule lists images at an additional $1,200/year or $150/month; bulk PDFs of filings since February 19, 2001 are described as available on CD-ROM. | Ask about current electronic delivery, historical availability and matching identifiers. |
| Individual information/copy order | UCC-11 accepts one debtor per form; its ALL option includes retained lapsed records. Published fees are $10 for a listing or $30 with copies. | A fallback for selected records; obtain a quote before treating the roster as an order. |
| Existing public records | The Secretary's Records Access Officer accepts written requests covering Corporations Division records. | Request existing catalog, license/order terms, schema and electronic records. |

Sources: [950 CMR 140.11](https://www.sec.state.ma.us/divisions/corporations/pdf-html/950_CMR_140.htm), [UCC-11, revision July 1, 2023](https://www.sec.state.ma.us/divisions/corporations/download/ucc11.pdf), [Corporations fee schedule](https://www.sec.state.ma.us/divisions/corporations/general-information/corporations-filing-fees.htm), [Secretary's request guidelines](https://www.sec.state.ma.us/divisions/public-records/prepra/access-guidelines.htm).
