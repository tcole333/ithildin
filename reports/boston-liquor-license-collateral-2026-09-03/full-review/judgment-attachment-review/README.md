# Judgment, attachment, seizure, and discharge notices

This separate supplement records **13 historical source observations** from **153 saved Licensing Board texts** (2020–2026), including **six dollar-bearing observations describing five identified monetary matters**. All 15 relevant PDF pages were visually reviewed. It does not establish current liens, loan principal, sale prices, current debt, priority, enforceability, or completed auctions.

The observations are not additions to transfer/pledge application counts or distinct main-action totals. Fire & Ice appears twice with the same entity, license, execution date (May 6, 2024), and $33,429.27 amount: the 2024 attachment notice and 2025 seizure/announced-auction notice share one matter ID. No dollar total is calculated.

Eight observations join seven main-review license IDs; one joins only a non-alcohol roster record; one explicit ID is absent from the roster; three items omit a license ID and remain unjoined. All seven main-review IDs have no financing marker in the checked roster comments, location comments or premises description. This is a saved-source keyword review, not a claim that every historical encumbrance has been located.

## What the records support

- Fire & Ice explicitly concerns attachment/seizure of the liquor license. Its auction was announced; a completed sale or proceeds are not recorded in these items.
- Woody’s Grill and Tap identifies an execution against Essenex, Inc. and a license number, without saying the license was attached. Pastoral and Curry Quarters use general personal-property attachment wording. Auditorium Cafe’s writ notice does not specify the property or license ID.
- Six tax-related notices expressly describe license seizure; three also announce auctions. Their debt amounts are not stated. The two Olives/Figs items identify different licenses and premises. Both literally say “7/30/24” in an August 2025 source; that unexplained date is preserved without correction or assignment to seizure versus auction.
- The Traffic Restaurant/Stella item is a **discharge** notice. It is not a newly imposed encumbrance and does not establish payment or settlement.
- Curry Quarters/Shanti Express LB-100379 joins R03484, an excluded non-alcohol Common Victualler record. It remains in this supplemental ledger and is not forced into the 1,520-ID main review.
- Historical license IDs LB-99668, LB-99674, and LB-99536 currently appear under different roster holder names. The joins connect license history only; they do not attribute historical obligations to those roster holders.
