# Unsent inquiry about Massachusetts bulk records

Prepared September 4, 2026. This combines the UCC and corporate product questions for the same division. No message, attachment, order or payment has been sent. It requests product information and a quote, not a certificate or individual search by email.

To: Corporations Division — corpinfo@sec.state.ma.us  
Subject: UCC and corporate bulk records — existing products, schema and quote

Hello,

I am researching the entities behind Boston's published alcohol-license roster and their documented financing and management relationships. Your published regulations describe separate bulk-data programs for UCC records (950 CMR 140.11) and corporate records (950 CMR 113.15).

Could you provide the existing catalog, schema or data dictionary, any public sample, license/reuse terms, current price schedule and order instructions for each program?

For both products, please clarify whether a weekly purchase includes a complete baseline or only changes; the available historical coverage; minimum order; current delivery format; and total cost of a one-time existing baseline plus the necessary updates and images through an agreed cutoff, preferably September 3, 2026. Please quote the two products and their image components separately.

For UCC records, we would like to understand the available filing identifiers, original-to-amendment links, dates, debtor and secured-party names, continuations, assignments, terminations, corrections, retained lapsed records and image/index linkage. Please identify any limits on earlier records, including filings before February 19, 2001. If the division maintains separate lien records outside the UCC product, please identify the relevant catalog and access route.

For corporate records, please identify covered entity types, including LLCs, partnerships, nonprofits and foreign registrations, and whether inactive entities, former names and historical filing records are included. We are interested in existing entity identifiers and dates, agents, officers, directors, managers and members where expressly reported, with their original role labels. Please identify any fields available only in images and any limits on records before August 27, 2001.

We have a roster of 1,444 normalized holder-name groups with published name variants, DBAs, license identifiers and source-row references. These are candidate names, not confirmed registry entities. We can provide it if useful, but a standard existing statewide product is suitable for our own matching. We seek existing records and field definitions; we are not asking the division to create an ownership analysis or resolve identities.

Please provide a quote and terms before any charge or order. No purchase, subscription or paid search is authorized by this inquiry. If an existing-records request should instead go through the Secretary's Records Access Officer, please identify the appropriate process.

Thank you.
