Source: [https://www.boston.gov/departments/licensing-board/licensing-board-information-and-members](https://www.boston.gov/departments/licensing-board/licensing-board-information-and-members). April 8, 2021 has three anchors linking two PDF variants, with one repeated blank anchor. September 10, 2020 has two anchors that split the word “Voting” and link two PDF variants. These are two dated entries containing four distinct URLs; no file identity or additional-meeting inference was made.

The 2023 April 27 label has a stray unlinked trailing “1.” The May 25 anchor title says 5-23-23, unlike its visible label and filename. The April 20, 2021 filename says Emergency Hearing. All raw labels, title attributes, list-item HTML, and URLs are retained in the JSON.

The earliest linked year drawer is 2020, with an earliest dated label of April 23. The saved index has no link to a pre-2020 archive or catalog collection. Its generic Public records footer link is preserved as an access option but was not opened. Absence of an archive link does not establish absence of older records.

These are index coverage counts, not a meeting-completeness audit. The 2020–2021 links are labeled “Voting agenda” inside the Voting Minutes section; individual documents must be inspected before treating them as decisions.
