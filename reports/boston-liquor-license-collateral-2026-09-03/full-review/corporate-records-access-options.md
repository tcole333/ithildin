# Massachusetts corporate-records delivery options

Verified September 4, 2026 from official informational sources. No corporate/UCC portal searches, OpenCorporates credits, orders or external contacts were used.

Saved provenance: [evidence package and limitations](history-access-evidence/corporate-records/README.md), [URL/date/path/SHA-256 manifest](history-access-evidence/corporate-records/manifest.json). These are preserved web-tool excerpts and metadata, not downloaded original PDFs or HTML; missing content is explicitly identified.

**A separate corporate bulk product is published.** 950 CMR 113.15 offers a Corporations Information Management System extract monthly in weekly increments at $4,800/year or $100/week. Images cost an additional $1,200/year or $150/month; bulk corporate PDFs filed since August 27, 2001 are described as available on CD-ROM. The data-element list and file layout are available on request. This authority is separate from the UCC program. [950 CMR 113.15, PDF p. 7](https://www.sec.state.ma.us/divisions/corporations/download/950113.pdf#page=7).

These are published prices, not a confirmed quote. The Secretary's PDF bears December 29, 2006; the [Mass.gov catalog](https://www.mass.gov/regulations/950-CMR-11300-the-massachusetts-business-corporation-act-mgl-c-156d) is dated January 10, 2007. The document remained publicly available on September 4, 2026. Confirm current fees, delivery, minimum commitment, license and whether a weekly unit includes a complete historical baseline or only changes. The rule concerns the Business Corporation Act; coverage of LLCs, partnerships, nonprofits and foreign registrations in the offered extract needs explicit confirmation.

For product/schema/quote inquiries: **Corporations Division, corpinfo@sec.state.ma.us, 617-727-9640**, One Ashburton Place, 17th floor, Boston, MA 02108. Section 113.04 permits general email inquiries but says email cannot submit filings or request certificates/searches. The draft therefore requests product information and the proper order process, not a paid search. [Official contact](https://www.sec.state.ma.us/divisions/about-us/contact-us.htm), [950 CMR 113.04](https://www.sec.state.ma.us/divisions/corporations/download/950113.pdf#page=4).

An alternative existing-records route is **Sofya Gray, Records Access Officer, Sec.RAO@sec.state.ma.us, 617-878-3660**, One Ashburton Place, Room 1711, Boston, MA 02108. The published guidelines cover Corporations Division records. Request existing electronic records or separable data, schema and retention documentation; no new dataset or ownership analysis is demanded. [RAO guidelines](https://www.sec.state.ma.us/divisions/public-records/prepra/access-guidelines.htm), [950 CMR 32.07](https://www.sec.state.ma.us/divisions/public-records/download/950-CMR-32-00-Official.pdf).

**Requested export fields—not a verified schema:** stable state entity identifiers; exact current and former names with dates; entity type and jurisdiction; formation, registration and status dates; registered agents; reported officers, directors, managers and other named roles; and document identifiers, filing/effective/execution dates and image links. Ask for historical records, not only the latest profile. Original formation/registration filings, annual reports, amendments, supplemental changes, mergers, conversions, resignations and agent changes would support dated relationship analysis. The 1,444-row [request roster](ucc-request-roster.csv) is an optional selection aid, not a verified registry match or submitted order.

For a small number of unresolved entities, individually identified copies and certificates are a documented fallback. Domestic corporate legal-existence certificates with officers are listed at $12, or with amendments and officers at $25. The latter includes an amendment chronology and the most recent officer/director filing; it is not a complete historical officer table. [Fee schedule](https://www.sec.state.ma.us/divisions/corporations/general-information/corporations-filing-fees.htm), [certificate descriptions](https://www.sec.state.ma.us/divisions/corporations/download/Fee_Schedule.pdf).

**Ownership interpretation:** keep officer, director, manager, agent, signatory and expressly disclosed member/shareholder as separate roles. A Freeman-name match, shared address or service agent is a research lead, not proof of identity, common ownership, equity percentage or beneficial control. Preserve filing/reporting dates and inspect attachments. Missing ownership fields mean ownership was not disclosed there; corporate records alone are not a complete ownership census.

No public bulk download, API, enrollment form, license document or sample schema was verified in this bounded review.
