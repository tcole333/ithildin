## Executive Summary

**Wise Business Consulting Limited** is a Hong Kong corporate services intermediary with an established presence in the offshore entity formation ecosystem. The ICIJ Offshore Leaks database (data through 2010) classifies it as an **intermediary** for **Portcullis Trustnet** — one of Asia's largest offshore shell company formation agents — operating from the China Insurance Group Building at 141 Des Voeux Road Central, Hong Kong. It intermediated the formation of at least 13 BVI and Cook Islands entities.

In its current posture, Wise Business Consulting acts as **company secretary** of **META OCTAV GROUP (HK) LIMITED** (formerly International Fiduciaries Limited), a TCSP-licensed trust and fiduciary services firm at **6th Floor, Wyndham Place, 40-44 Wyndham Street, Central, Hong Kong**. This is the same floor and building as **HK Corporate Services Group** (Kenneth Raymond Deayton's firm) and the address used by **Swiss Commodity Re Limited** as its registered/virtual office.

No direct organizational link between Wise Business Consulting and Deayton or HKCS Group was identified. The connections are structural (shared building) and potentially chain-mediated (through META OCTAV GROUP, which may or may not be the same "International Fiduciaries Limited" that appeared on Swiss Commodity Re's NNC1 incorporation form).

As a Portcullis Trustnet intermediary, Wise Business Consulting acted as the client-facing firm that sourced and managed offshore entity formation requests, while Portcullis provided the registered agent, nominee director/shareholder, and BVI/Cook Islands filing infrastructure.

**13 connected offshore entities:**

The entity cluster reflects two main client networks:

**Philippine business network:** SINO MILE DEVELOPMENT LIMITED (BVI, Feb 2002) has five Filipino directors and seven Filipino/Philippine-corporate shareholders including Ching Bee Trading Corp. and Izuparts Center Inc. SINO MILE is also a shareholder of Braimer International Ltd (Cook Islands, Jul 2001), which uses Portcullis's own nominee director chain (Directcorp → Directra → Mactox).

**Anonymous nominee-structured entities (2005–2006 vintage):** APEX MATRIX INVESTMENT LIMITED, KEYBEST HOLDINGS LIMITED, EVERLAND FAR EAST LIMITED, and ASIA STRATEGY MANAGEMENT LIMITED all use **Carissa Limited** (BVI, Portcullis nominee) as director and **Ribalta Holdings Inc** (BVI, Portcullis nominee) as shareholder — the beneficial owners are fully obscured. ASIA STRATEGY MANAGEMENT also used **TMF Secretarial Services Limited** (Hong Kong) as its company secretary.

**Individual-owned entities:** SANCTUM PACIFIC LIMITED (BVI, 1994) has **Guy Richard Chaplin** as director and shareholder. ELITE SIDEREAL GROUP LIMITED (BVI, 2005) has **Robert Lee Naii** (US citizen) as director and shareholder.

**Assessment:** None of the 13 connected entities show names or structures that cross-reference the Deayton/HKCS Group/Swiss Commodity Re network. The client base appears to be unrelated to the CRML investigation — primarily Philippine business structures and anonymous-nominee vehicles of unknown beneficial ownership.

### 2. Company Secretary of META OCTAV GROUP (HK) LIMITED

**META OCTAV GROUP (HK) LIMITED** is the entity formerly named **International Fiduciaries Limited**. It is led by **Cécile Civiale Vuillier** (TEP, Trust & Estate Practitioner), Founding Partner & CEO. Meta Octav operates parallel fiduciary and corporate structuring practices from Geneva and Hong Kong, serving private clients, family offices, and intermediaries. Vuillier chairs the Wealth Management & Estate Planning Focus Group of XLNC. The Hong Kong entity holds a TCSP licence (inherited from its IFL predecessor).

**Wise Business Consulting Limited** acts as its company secretary — the entity responsible for maintaining the statutory books, filing annual returns, and handling corporate governance administration.

### 3. Wyndham Place Address Cluster

The address **6th Floor, Wyndham Place, 40-44 Wyndham Street, Central, Hong Kong** is confirmed for three distinct entities:

| Entity | Role | Source |
|--------|------|--------|
| HK Corporate Services Group (Deayton) | CEO/MD and co-founder Ken Deayton | HKCS website; SEC F-3 |
| Swiss Commodity Re Limited | Client entity using HKCS Group virtual office | SEC F-3 (0001213900-26-019356) |
| META OCTAV GROUP (HK) LIMITED | Independent fiduciary firm (formerly IFL); company secretary: WBC | mo.com; xlnc.org |

This is the single most important structural finding. Three entities — one directly controlled by Deayton (HKCS Group), one formed and controlled by Deayton (Swiss Commodity Re), and one that was the NNC1 nominee subscriber for Swiss Commodity Re (IFL/META OCTAV GROUP) — all converge at the same 6th floor address.

If Wise Business Consulting Limited is the company secretary of META OCTAV GROUP (which is the rebranded "International Fiduciaries Limited"), and if that IFL entity is the same one that appeared as NNC1 nominee subscriber on Swiss Commodity Re's October 2025 incorporation, then Wise Business Consulting is one layer removed from the Swiss Commodity Re formation chain.

### 4. The IFL Identity Problem

**Two separate Hong Kong entities have used the name "International Fiduciaries Limited":**

| IFL Entity | Principal | Founded | Status | Connection to CRML |
|-----------|----------|---------|--------|-------------------|
| IFL (Carolyn Butler) | Carolyn Butler (former CEO, HK Trust Company) | 2011 | Active, HKTA member, interfiduciaries.com | Shared institutional lineage with Deayton via HKTC; appeared on Swiss Commodity Re NNC1 per prior research |
| IFL (now META OCTAV GROUP, Cécile Civiale Vuillier) | Cécile Civiale Vuillier (TEP, Geneva/HK) | Unknown | Active, rebranded to META OCTAV GROUP; Wyndham Place | Company secretary: Wise Business Consulting Limited; same address as HKCS Group |

**The NNC1 subscriber for Swiss Commodity Re Limited (Co. No. 79047415, Oct 2025) was identified as "International Fiduciaries Limited" in prior research, attributed to Carolyn Butler's firm.** However, the discovery that a second entity also used that name — and that this second entity now operates from the same floor as HKCS Group — raises a material question: **which International Fiduciaries Limited appeared on Swiss Commodity Re's NNC1?**

If it was Vuillier's IFL (now META OCTAV GROUP), the connection to Deayton's operational address is direct and organizational — both entities operated from the same 6/F Wyndham Place. The company secretary of that entity (Wise Business Consulting) would then be a direct participant in the Swiss Commodity Re formation infrastructure.

**This ambiguity can only be resolved by checking the HK Companies Registry company number on Swiss Commodity Re's NNC1 against the company numbers of both IFL entities.** This remains an open gap.

### 5. TMF Group Connection (Attenuated)

**Deayton's AFS → TMF Group (Feb 2007):** Deayton was CEO of Asian Financial Services Limited (AFS) when TMF Group acquired it in February 2007, forming TMF's Asia hub. TMF Secretarial Services Limited (HK) subsequently served as company secretary for ASIA STRATEGY MANAGEMENT LIMITED (BVI), one of the entities intermediated by Wise Business Consulting through Portcullis Trustnet.

**Assessment:** This connection is attenuated. TMF Group is a large global professional services firm (~$1B revenue) with thousands of clients. The appearance of TMF Secretarial Services in one Wise Business Consulting-intermediated entity does not establish a Deayton-Wise Business Consulting relationship; it reflects the small universe of professional company secretarial firms in Hong Kong from which all intermediaries drew.
