# State/Local Public-Records Channels — Verification Report (2026-07-28)

Read-only verification of submission channels, statutes/fees, and disclosure limits. Where an official site was bot-blocked, content was verified from a Wayback capture of the official page or the official PDF (noted inline). (Persisted by orchestrator; subagent file writes are hook-blocked.)

## 1. New York AG Charities Bureau — Schedule B (LEGAL GATE)

**Verdict: DEAD END for public-charity donor names. Only exception: private foundations, whose donor lists are public via the 990-PF itself.**

- **Was Schedule B ever public through the Charities Bureau?** No. NY collected Schedule B with the CHAR500 (13 NYCRR § 91.5) but treated public-charity Schedule B as exempt from disclosure — "Both the IRS and Charities Bureau protect Schedule B from public disclosure" ([Nonprofit New York FAQ](https://www.nonprofitnewyork.org/2020/02/nonprofit-donor-disclosure-faqs/)); the Second Circuit upheld NY's collection partly on its confidentiality ([PBWT on Citizens United v. Schneiderman](https://www.pbwt.com/exempt-org-resource-blog/federal-court-upholds-new-york-donor-disclosure-requirement)). Private-foundation donor schedules have always been public as part of the 990-PF.
- **2021 suspension:** Effective July 30, 2021, the Bureau suspended Schedule B collection in response to AFP v. Bonta (July 1, 2021); pending Schedule B deficiency notices were declared no longer operative ([Morgan Lewis](https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2021/09/ny-attorney-general-suspends-schedule-b-collection-in-response-to-us-supreme-court-decision); [TEO Bulletin](https://www.teobulletin.com/2021/08/new-york-charities-bureau-suspends-schedule-b-collection/)).
- **Current rule:** Amended 13 NYCRR § 91.5, effective March 16, 2022 — public charities file a **redacted** Schedule B (amounts + states only, no names/street addresses) or a NY gross-contributions statement; **private foundations still file complete unredacted Schedule B** ([Political Law Briefing](https://www.politicallawbriefing.com/2022/05/new-york-adopts-regulations-amending-its-donor-disclosure-rules/); [Lawyers Alliance alert](https://lawyersalliance.org/userFiles/uploads/legal_alerts/New_York_Charities_Filing_Requirements_Legal_Alert.pdf)).
- **FOIL for 2010–2020:** will be denied for public-charity Schedule B — held confidential when collected; post-Bonta disclosure of donor identities is constitutionally foreclosed. FOIL channel for other registry material: https://ag.ny.gov/i-want/make-foil-request (first 5 pages free, then 25¢/page). CHAR500 filing is online: https://charitiesfiling.ag.ny.gov/ (guidance: https://ag.ny.gov/resources/organizations/charities-nonprofits-fundraisers/charities-annual-filing-char500 — the page itself is silent on Schedule B; the reg controls).
- **Pivots:** 990-PF filers; donor-side filings (foundations' own 990-PF grant lists); litigation records.

**SUBMIT VIA:** https://ag.ny.gov/i-want/make-foil-request — for non-Schedule-B registry material only; treat public-charity Schedule B as closed.

## 2. Ohio Inspector General

- **Channel:** no portal — **watchdog@oig.ohio.gov**, (614) 644-9110 / (800) 686-1525, 8–5 M–F (OIG FAQ, watchdog.ohio.gov; the FAQ sits behind the state CMS at /wps/portal/gov/oig/about/faqs).
- **Legal gate — ORC 121.44 (eff. 10/31/1990, HB 588):** "the report of any investigation conducted by the inspector general ... is a public record, open to public inspection"; the IG may designate parts confidential (matters confidential by law, witness safety, investigative techniques); nonconfidential reports provided for a prescribed fee (https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-121.44). The statute opens the **report**, not the file. OIG FAQ: active-investigation records are wholly confidential; after closure "some records may be subject to review" and the complaint form is generally public; CI names withheld absent court order.
- **1988–1994 era:** office created by **Gov. Celeste executive order, 1988**; statutory via **Am. Sub. HB 588 (1990)** (watchdog.ohio.gov/about/about-the-office; 2021 Annual Report). Early files, if appraised historical, would be at the **State Archives of Ohio, Ohio History Connection** (executive-branch repository; Celeste EO series catalogued at https://aspace.ohiohistory.org/repositories/2/resources/11127). Steps: search OIG retention schedules in RIMS (https://das.ohio.gov/buying-and-selling/state-printing-and-mail-services/records-management), then reference inquiry via https://www.ohiohistory.org/research/archives-library/state-archives/.

**SUBMIT VIA:** watchdog@oig.ohio.gov (cite ORC 149.43 for closed files; ask for the final report by case number separately). 1988–94: RIMS + State Archives reference request.

([Caster PDF](https://www.supremecourt.ohio.gov/rod/docs/pdf/0/2016/2016-Ohio-8394.pdf); [Mash 2026 PDF](https://www.supremecourt.ohio.gov/rod/docs/pdf/13/2026/2026-Ohio-498.pdf); [Ohio AG CLEIR chapter](https://www.ohioattorneygeneral.gov/getattachment/b3886d69-30b4-41d3-b7a6-31669d598358/b3886d69-30b4-41d3-b7a6-31669d598358.aspx))
- **Retention:** survival of 1990s files depends on the sheriff's RC-2 schedules approved by the county records commission (R.C. 149.38); **schedules and RC-3 disposal certificates are themselves public** — request them first, then the surviving files. (OHC Local Government Records Program: https://www.ohiohistory.org/research/local-government-records-program/)

**SUBMIT VIA:** FCSOPublicRecords@franklincountyohio.gov (or mail Attn: Public Records, 370 S. Front St., Columbus, OH 43215) — request #1: RC-2 schedules + disposition logs 1988–2000; request #2: surviving case files.

## 4. Minnesota Department of Education — MGDPA

- **Channel (from MDE's official "Guide for Members of the Public Requesting Information," required by § 13.03 subd. 2(b); PDF retrieved from education.mn.gov):** written request to **Data Practices Compliance Official, 400 NE Stinson Blvd., Minneapolis, MN 55413**; **mde.datapractices@state.mn.us**; (651) 582-8200; TTY (651) 582-8201; form included in guide. Responsible Authority = Commissioner of Education. Requesters need not identify themselves or state a purpose. (Pages: https://education.mn.gov/MDE/Data/datapr/ and https://education.mn.gov/MDE/About/MDE086069)
- **Fees (§ 13.03 subd. 3(c) via the guide):** inspection free; no charge under $10; ≤100 B&W pages = 25¢/side (50¢ duplex); else actual cost — 10¢/page materials + $24.71/hr staff (first 2 hrs of program-staff time free; data-practices staff never charged; no fee for separating public from not-public).
- **Key exemption — Minn. Stat. § 13.39 (civil investigative data):** data collected in an *active* investigation for a *pending civil legal action* (expressly includes "judicial, administrative or arbitration proceedings"; the entity's chief attorney decides) = protected nonpublic / confidential. Once **inactive** (abandoned, SOL run, appeals exhausted), the data **become public** unless release would jeopardize another pending action or another classification applies; discretionary release allowed even while active (https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/13.39). So demand letters/collection ledgers about a federal-program sponsor org are withholdable during active litigation and public afterward; embedded student data stays redacted under § 13.32/FERPA.

**SUBMIT VIA:** mde.datapractices@state.mn.us — cite ch. 13, frame as data on the organization, and if denied ask for the written § 13.39 active/inactive determination.

## 6. Texas counties — TPIA

**Statewide rules (Texas AG requestor guidance, texasattorneygeneral.gov):** written request to the body's **officer for public information**, delivered per § 552.234 (US mail, email, hand delivery, or approved method). Production "promptly"; >10 business days requires certifying a date (§ 552.221); withholding requires an AG ruling request **within 10 business days** (§ 552.301); fees per § 552.261 + 1 TAC ch. 70 (~10¢/page; labor on large requests; itemized estimate if >$40, § 552.2615). Hotline (512) 478-6736. (https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/open-government/members-public/how-request-public-information)

- **(a) Montgomery County** (official County Attorney PIA page, mctx.org, via 2024-06-03 Wayback capture): "Each elected official is the officer for public information and is the custodian of the information created or received by that county official's office" (§ 552.201); requests "may also be sent to the County department that holds the records."
  - Jail/detention **contracts** → Commissioners Court (County Judge/County Clerk) + Purchasing; the County Attorney's civil division coordinates: **mcao.pia@mctx.org**, mail Attn: Open Records, 501 N. Thompson, Suite 300, Conroe, TX 77301.
  - **Fund 222 ledgers** → **County Auditor** (county financial ledgers custodian) via the county department directory, or routed through mcao.pia@mctx.org.
  - Sheriff-held jail records → Sheriff's Open Records Division: **mcsoopenrecords@mctx.org**, fax (936) 760-5826.
  (https://web.archive.org/web/20240603151810/https://www.mctx.org/departments/departments_a_-_c/county_attorney/public_information_requests.php)
- **(b) Val Verde County:** online **Open Records Request Form** on the Transparency page (https://valverdecounty.texas.gov/148/Transparency → forms.aspx?FID=63); County Clerk form: PO Box 1267, Del Rio, TX 78841-1267, (830) 774-7564, fax (830) 774-7608; county offices 400 Pecan St., Del Rio, TX 78840, (830) 774-7501. Detention (GEO/USMS-ICE IGA) contracts → County Judge/Commissioners Court (records with County Clerk); payments → County Auditor/Treasurer. MuckRock reports ~55-day average actual response for this county (https://www.muckrock.com/place/united-states-of-america/texas/val-verde-county/).

**SUBMIT VIA:** Montgomery — mcao.pia@mctx.org (contracts), County Auditor (Fund 222), mcsoopenrecords@mctx.org (sheriff/jail). Val Verde — online form at valverdecounty.texas.gov/148/Transparency or County Clerk, PO Box 1267, Del Rio, TX 78841.
