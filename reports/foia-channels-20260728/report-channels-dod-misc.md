# FOIA Channels & Disclosure Rules — DoD/Navy + SSA OIG, USDA FNA, FDIC/RTC, USCIS
Verified 2026-07-28. Method note: secnav.navy.mil, fna.usda.gov, and uscis.gov pages were live-fetched (bot-blocks bypassed with plain curl); portal endpoints liveness-checked (HTTP status). web.archive.org unreachable from this environment.

## 1. Department of the Navy FOIA (personnel records of a CURRENT Navy Reserve officer)

**Current submission channel (verified live on the SECNAV DON FOIA site, fetched 2026-07-28):** DON has moved to the **SecureRelease** portal. Page text: "To submit a FOIA/PA request to the Department of the Navy, please do so via our online portal at **www.securerelease.us** or through FOIA.gov." (https://www.secnav.navy.mil/foia/Pages/default.aspx). SecureRelease is a multi-agency portal (DON, NCIS, FDIC, others); you register an account, pick the agency/component, and track the case there. Note: DON is on SecureRelease, while OSD/JS runs a *different* portal (PAL, item 2) — do not cross-file.

**Custodians:**
- **(a) Officer personnel records (OMPF) of a CURRENT (active or Reserve) officer:** Navy Personnel Command / MyNavy HR, Millington TN — NOT NPRC. The SECNAV FOIA page routes OMPF matters to NPC's FOIA office **BUPERS-00J** (bupers-00j@navy.mil, subject line "FOIA Request" — listed for own-record requests; third-party requests go through SecureRelease addressed to the Navy with NPC as action office). NPRC/St. Louis only holds records of **retired/discharged/separated** members (https://www.archives.gov/veterans/military-service-records; NPC guide: https://www.mynavyhr.navy.mil/Portals/55/Career/RecordsManagement/How%20To%20Request%20Navy%20OMPF%20Records.pdf).
- **(b) Officer grade-determination / constructive-service-credit records:** the appointment/acceptance package, precedence and date-of-rank computations are filed in the OMPF and in NPC officer accession/promotion files (constructive credit for JAG-type direct commissions is determined at appointment under 10 U.S.C. 12207). Same NPC channel via SecureRelease. Expect most of the underlying computation paperwork to be consent-gated for third parties (below); the releasable skeleton (rank, date of rank via promotion sequence, source of commission, dates of service) usually lets you infer constructive credit.
- **(c) Reserve retirement point records & orders history:** points are the **ARPR/ASOSH** maintained by **NPC PERS-912** (member self-service via BUPERS Online; MyNavy Career Center 1-833-330-6622 can read a point record to the member) (https://www.mynavyhr.navy.mil/Career-Management/Reserve-Personnel-Mgmt/Reserve-Retirements/). Reserve order history sits in NROWS/Navy Reserve systems and the OMPF. Third-party FOIA: SecureRelease to DON, action office NPC (points/orders are personnel records — heavily consent-gated).

**CRITICAL third-party disclosure gate (living current member, no consent):** Only the limited Privacy Act/FOIA "public information" categories are releasable. NARA/NPRC's published list (https://www.archives.gov/personnel-records-center/foia-info): name, service number, dates of service, branch, final duty status, final rank, **salary**, assignments and geographical locations, **source of commission**, military education level, **promotion sequence number**, awards/decorations (eligibility), photograph, transcripts of courts-martial, place of entrance/separation (+ for deceased: place of birth, date/place of death, burial). DoD's own statement for currently serving members (Military OneSource, DoD): name/photo, dates and branch of service, duty status, rank, duty assignments, military education, awards, courts-martial transcripts, home of record (state only) (https://www.militaryonesource.mil/financial-legal/legal/service-member-privacy-versus-public-access-to-information/). DoD practice additionally withholds assignment/location data for personnel in sensitive or routinely deployable units (DoDM 5400.07 line). Everything else in the OMPF — fitreps, medical, grade-determination correspondence, full point statements — requires the member's signed authorization (DD Form 2870 or equivalent).
**DD-214:** NOT releasable to a third party without the veteran's (or, if deceased, next-of-kin's) written consent until the record becomes **archival 62 years after separation**; non-archival third-party requesters get only the limited list above (https://www.archives.gov/personnel-records-center/ompf-access-public; https://www.archives.gov/personnel-records-center/veterans-privacy). A current reservist's records are by definition non-archival.

**Senate-confirmation (PN) alternative — ANSWER: NO for O-5.** Under **10 U.S.C. 12203(a)** as amended by ROPMA (Pub. L. 103-337 §1632, effective 1996): "Appointments of reserve officers in commissioned grades of lieutenant colonel and commander **or below** ... shall be made by the President **alone**"; only grades **above** O-5 need Senate advice and consent (https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title10-section12203&num=0&edition=prelim). Current practice matches: 119th-Congress Navy Reserve PN packages under §12203 are "to be **Captain**" (O-6), e.g. PN1030/PN1031/PN1035/PN1043 (2025-26) (https://www.congress.gov/nomination/119th-congress/1030). So a **March 2025 recommissioning at O-5 (CDR) in the Navy Reserve generates no congress.gov PN record** — PN search is not a viable public verification path for this event (it would be only for O-6+; active-duty-list O-4+ promotions under 10 U.S.C. 624 do appear). Even where PNs exist they show only name/grade, not constructive credit.

**SUBMIT VIA:** SecureRelease portal — https://www.securerelease.us/ (register, select U.S. Navy; identify Navy Personnel Command/BUPERS-00J as record holder), or FOIA.gov; own-record OMPF requests: bupers-00j@navy.mil.

## 2. OSD/Joint Staff FOIA — and where Navy ethics/outside-employment approvals actually live

**Current channel:** OSD/JS FOIA Requester Service Center (Freedom of Information Division, WHS/ESD). Online portal = **OSD/JS Public Access Link (PAL): https://pal.whs.mil/app/MainPage.aspx** (liveness-verified HTTP 200 on 2026-07-28; account gives submission, status, correspondence). Info page https://www.esd.whs.mil/FOID/ (bot-gated to scripted fetches but live). Mail: OSD/JS FOIA Requester Service Center, Freedom of Information Division, **1155 Defense Pentagon, Washington, DC 20301-1155**; org box whs.mc-alex.esd.mbx.osd-js-foia-requester-service-center@mail.mil; phone (866) 574-4970. Scope: OSD, Joint Staff, and specified Defense Agencies/Field Activities only — NOT the military departments.

**SOCO records:** Yes — the Standards of Conduct Office is part of the DoD Office of General Counsel and serves **OSD-level** personnel; its records are OSD records requested through the OSD/JS RSC above. SOCO's own FOIA/Privacy pointer page: https://dodsoco.ogc.osd.mil/FOIA-Privacy-Act/ (bot-gated); office contact OSD.SOCO@mail.mil.

**Navy Reserve JAG officer outside-employment/ethics approvals — custodian is the NAVY, not SOCO.** The JER (DoD 5500.07-R) outside-activities rules (Chapter 3 — the 3-300-series; off-duty employment approval by the "agency designee," typically the CO/supervisor; see also 5 CFR 3601.106 prior-approval rule for financial-disclosure filers, https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/5/3601.106) are administered inside each component. For Navy JAG officers, **outside practice of law** is governed by **JAGINST 5803.1 (series)** (Rules of Professional Conduct, "prescribes limitations on, and procedures for processing requests to engage in, the outside practice of law"), processed through **OJAG Code 13 (Administrative Law Division; Branch 132 = ethics counselors)** (https://www.jag.navy.mil/about/organization/ojag/code-01/code-13/); the DON DAEO is the General Counsel of the Navy. So approval letters live with the member's command and OJAG Code 13 — request via **DON SecureRelease** routed to OJAG (OJAG FOIA: Code 20 FOIA Branch, 1254 Charles Morris Street SE, Washington Navy Yard, DC 20374; https://www.jag.navy.mil/foia/), not via OSD/JS. DoD SOCO would hold such approvals only for OSD-serviced personnel.

**SUBMIT VIA:** https://pal.whs.mil/ (OSD/JS + SOCO records); Navy JAG ethics/outside-employment approvals via https://www.securerelease.us/ addressed to DON/OJAG (Code 13 records; Code 20 FOIA branch) or the officer's command.

## 3. SSA Office of Inspector General FOIA

**SSA OIG does not operate its own public FOIA intake.** By regulation (20 CFR 402.35, https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/20/402.35) all SSA FOIA requests — including for OIG records — go to SSA's **Office of Privacy and Disclosure (OPD)**: (1) online portal linked from https://www.ssa.gov/foia/ — the **FOIAXpress Public Access Link at https://securefoia.ssa.gov/** (liveness-verified HTTP 200, 2026-07-28), (2) FOIA.gov, (3) email **FOIA.Public.Liaison@ssa.gov**, (4) mail: SSA, Office of Privacy and Disclosure, ATTN: Freedom of Information Officer, 6401 Security Boulevard, Baltimore, MD 21235. The OIG contact page routes "SSA and OIG FOIA requests" to ssa.gov/foia (https://oig.ssa.gov/contact/). The reg names no separate OIG FOIA officer. Do not confuse with OIG's **Touhy** channel (litigation subpoenas only): OIG Room 3-ME-1, SSA.OIG.Touhy.Requests@ssa.gov (https://oig.ssa.gov/touhy-requests/). Practical tip: caption the request "records of the Office of the Inspector General" so OPD tasks OIG; expect Exemption 7 screening on investigative files.

**SUBMIT VIA:** https://securefoia.ssa.gov/ (SSA PAL) or FOIA.gov, addressed to SSA/OPD, expressly identifying OIG records.

## 4. USDA Food and Nutrition Service — now Food and Nutrition Administration (FNA)

**Renaming:** official notice, live-fetched 2026-07-28: "As of **June 1, 2026** the Food and Nutrition Service (FNS) is now the **Food and Nutrition Administration (FNA)**" (site migrating fns.usda.gov → fna.usda.gov) (https://www.fna.usda.gov/foia).
**Channel — three options per the FNA FOIA office page:** (1) email the FNA FOIA inbox **FOIA-FNA@usda.gov** (FOIA Officer: Kevin Lynch); (2) **USDA Public Access Link (PAL) — https://efoia-pal.usda.gov/ — still current**, account required, and **Login.gov sign-in required effective Jan 9, 2026**; (3) FOIA.gov. USDA FOIA is decentralized by agency (https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/general-information/staff-offices/office-general-counsel/office-information-affairs/freedom-information-act-division), so select FNS/FNA in PAL.
**Regional office records (e.g., Midwest Regional Office):** submit **centrally** to the FNA FOIA office — there is no separate regional FOIA intake; the page lists regional contacts only for program matters. Operational caveat straight from the page: FNA "encourages you to consider scoping your request to electronic files only as analysts likely have reduced access to offices where paper files are maintained."

**SUBMIT VIA:** https://efoia-pal.usda.gov/ (select FNS/FNA; Login.gov account) or FOIA-FNA@usda.gov.

## 5. FDIC FOIA — and where RTC records ended up

**Channel:** the legacy electronic form **efoiarequest.fdic.gov is DEAD — the hostname no longer resolves (NXDOMAIN, checked 2026-07-28)**. FDIC now takes FOIA requests through the **SecureRelease portal, https://www.securerelease.us/** (register; support 844-919-0718 / SecureReleaseSupportTeam@deloitte.com). Alternatives per https://www.fdic.gov/foia: mail FDIC, Legal Division, FOIA/PA Group, 550 17th Street NW, Washington, DC 20429; fax 703-562-2797; email **efoia@fdic.gov**; status line 202-898-7021.

**RTC records:** the RTC Completion Act terminated RTC on **12/31/1995 and transferred all remaining assets, liabilities, and duties to the FDIC** — FDIC is the legal successor and records custodian (CRS RS22959, https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/RS/PDF/RS22959/RS22959.4.pdf). NARA allocated **Record Group 484** to RTC, but it sits on NARA's list of record groups **without descriptions** (https://www.archives.gov/research/guide-fed-records/groups/000.html), and NARA's posted RG-484 schedule note states: "The Resolution Trust Corporation (RTC), now part of the FDIC, is **not subject to the Federal Records Act**. Records schedules approved by [NARA] are non-binding on the RTC. For questions about RTC records management, please contact the FDIC directly." (https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/departments/department-of-the-treasury/rg-0484/RG-484-RTC_sf115.pdf). Net: **no wholesale transfer to NARA occurred — request RTC-era failed-thrift records from FDIC FOIA**.
**Finding aids/accession metadata for failed-institution records:** (1) ask FDIC FOIA (SecureRelease) for the records inventories/retention schedules and box/file indices covering the named institution and the RTC receivership; (2) browse FDIC's public digital archive https://archive.fdic.gov/ (RTC-era publications/reports); (3) sweep the NARA Catalog (https://catalog.archives.gov/) under RG 484 and predecessor RG 195 (FHLBB, https://www.archives.gov/research/guide-fed-records/groups/195.html) for the little that was accessioned.

**SUBMIT VIA:** https://www.securerelease.us/ (select FDIC) or efoia@fdic.gov / FDIC FOIA/PA Group, 550 17th St NW, Washington DC 20429.

## 6. USCIS FOIA — GEN- control number, status checks, A-file rules

**GEN-10321032 is NOT a standard FOIA control number — it is a USCIS Genealogy Program case ID.** USCIS FAQ (live-fetched 2026-07-28): online genealogy receipts carry "the case identification number/Case ID (such as **GEN-1000XXXX**)"; mail-filed genealogy requests get CMT- numbers (https://www.uscis.gov/records/genealogy/genealogical-records-help/request-status-frequently-asked-questions).
**Status check for GEN- cases:** the **Genealogy Request Case Status Inquiry** page — **https://genealogy.uscis.dhs.gov/#/cases/status/check** (portal liveness-verified HTTP 200) — or email **Genealogy.USCIS@uscis.dhs.gov**. Do not expect it in the FOIA status tool.
**Status check for regular FOIA (FIRST) cases:** https://first.uscis.gov/#/check-status with the FOIA control number — works "whether you made it online or another way"; new requests "must" be made online at first.uscis.gov (USCIS records page, live-fetched 2026-07-28: https://www.uscis.gov/records/request-records-through-the-freedom-of-information-act-or-privacy-act).
**Processing times:** Genealogy (official page, updated monthly, fetched 2026-07-28): **G-1041 index search 191 business days; G-1041A record request 300 business days** (https://www.uscis.gov/records/genealogy/genealogical-records-help/request-status) — so a 2025-11-25 filing plausibly runs into early-2027 at current averages. FOIA A-file requests: three tracks; Track 1 (specific documents from an A-file) is materially faster; Track 2 (full A-file) currently runs several months to over a year per 2026 practitioner reporting (https://monrreallaw.com/immigration-attorney/foia-your-immigration-file-a-file-eoir-cbp-and-timelines/); official quarterlies are on the USCIS FOIA pages/DHS Annual FOIA Report.
**Deceased subject (died 2000):** YES — releasable to a third party with **proof of death**. DHS FOIA/PA rule 6 CFR 5.21: third-party access requires the subject's written consent **or** "proof ... that the subject individual is deceased," defined as "a copy of a death certificate or an obituary" (https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/6/5.21). The Genealogy Program's accepted proof-of-death list adds: individual death record, printed obituary/funeral program/gravestone photo, religious death record, death-benefit payment records.
**A-file → NARA transfer:** under the **June 3, 2009 USCIS–NARA agreement**, A-Files "are eligible for transfer to NARA custody **100 years after the immigrant's year of birth**." Transferred files are held at the **National Archives at Kansas City** (1.5M+ files, subjects born ≤1925) and **San Francisco (San Bruno)** (300K+); search the National Archives Catalog first and request from NARA if listed; USCIS retains everything not yet transferred (https://www.archives.gov/research/immigration/aliens). For a person who died in 2000: if born before ~1926 the file may already be at NARA; otherwise it is still USCIS-held (FOIA with proof of death, or Genealogy Program).

**SUBMIT VIA:** status of GEN-10321032 → https://genealogy.uscis.dhs.gov/#/cases/status/check or Genealogy.USCIS@uscis.dhs.gov; new/other FOIA → https://first.uscis.gov/ (status: https://first.uscis.gov/#/check-status); NARA-transferred A-files → https://catalog.archives.gov/ then NARA Kansas City/San Francisco reference.
